**Dividend Paid (2024):** $1.97B

1. **Cut the dividend** (signals distress but preserves balance sheet)

3. **Reduce capex** (underinvests in future competitiveness)

DOW appears to be doing **(2) and (3) simultaneously**. Look at the data:

**The dividend is unsustainable.** At negative FCF, paying out $1.97B means the company is:

None of these are sustainable.
