## III. GROWTH DRIVERS ARE REAL AND ACCELERATING

AVY isn't a broken company waiting for a turnaround—it's a fundamentally sound business with genuine tailwinds:

### 1. **E-Commerce Secular Growth**
Packaging volumes have shifted permanently higher due to e-commerce adoption. This isn't cyclical—it's structural. Every Amazon delivery, every DTC brand shipment, every Shein package requires labels, pressure-sensitive adhesives, and tracking materials. AVY is the go-to supplier. This market is growing mid-to-high single digits *structurally*.

### 2. **RFID Solutions Expansion**
This is the hidden growth engine. RFID labeling and supply chain tracking solutions carry much higher margins than commodity labels. Retailers are mandating RFID compliance, and AVY has first-mover advantages. This business is growing 15-20% annually, and it's a tiny portion of total revenue today—massive upside.

### 3. **Margin Recovery**
Input costs (adhesives, films, chemicals) have normalized. Raw material inflation, which crushed margins in 2021-2022, is behind us. Every basis point of input cost deflation flows directly to the bottom line. Management has demonstrated they can maintain pricing even as input costs fall—that's margin expansion.

### 4. **International Exposure = Currency Tailwind**
With meaningful Europe and Asia-Pacific revenue, USD weakness—which appears likely given Fed pause expectations—is a direct EPS accretor. A 5-10% euro/yen rebound adds 2-3% to reported earnings.
