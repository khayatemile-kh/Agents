# **COMPREHENSIVE FUNDAMENTAL ANALYSIS REPORT: LIN (Linde plc)**

## **Executive Summary**

Linde plc is a global leader in industrial gases and specialty chemicals, operating across more than 100 countries with a market capitalization of approximately **$215.9 billion** as of March 31, 2025. The company demonstrates solid financial fundamentals with strong profitability metrics, though growth rates have moderated and the balance sheet carries significant debt levels that warrant close monitoring.

---

## **I. Company Profile & Business Overview**

**Company Name:** Linde plc  
**Ticker Symbol:** LIN  
**Exchange:** NMS (Nasdaq)  
**Sector:** Basic Materials  
**Industry Classification:** Specialty Chemicals  
**Market Cap:** $215.9 billion  
**Shares Outstanding:** ~473.2 million (ordinary shares)  

**Business Description:**  
Linde plc is one of the world's largest engineering and industrial gas companies, formed through the 2018 merger of Praxair Inc. and Linde AG. The company operates through three primary business segments:
- Industrial Gases (largest revenue contributor)
- Engineering & Construction
- Specialty Chemicals

The company generates revenue primarily through the production and distribution of:
- Oxygen, nitrogen, argon, and other industrial gases
- Hydrocarbon processing services
- Air separation units
- Specialty chemicals and cryogenic equipment
