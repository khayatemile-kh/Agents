## COMPANY PROFILE & BUSINESS OVERVIEW

### Business Model & Operations

Deere & Company is a world-renowned manufacturer of agricultural and construction machinery, with a diversified product portfolio including:
- **John Deere branded equipment** for farming operations
- **Forestry and landscaping equipment**
- **Construction machinery**
- **Financial services** (equipment financing)

The company operates globally with significant exposure to:
- North American agricultural markets
- International markets (Europe, Asia-Pacific)
- Emerging markets with growing agricultural mechanization

### Strategic Position

As of fiscal year 2024 (ending October 31, 2024), DE maintains its position as the largest agricultural machinery manufacturer globally, though facing headwinds from:
- Cyclical agricultural commodity prices
- Currency fluctuations
- Competitive pressures in construction equipment
- Rising input costs and supply chain volatility
