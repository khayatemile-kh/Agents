## The Technical Setup Is Actually Bullish If You Read It Correctly

What we're seeing is *normalization of a previously overbought condition.* That's healthy. That's consolidation settling into equilibrium. In a durable uptrend (and DE *is* in a durable uptrend—up 17.9% from the November 2024 low of $391), a pullback from overbought to neutral is a *buy signal for disciplined traders*, not a sell signal.

The MACD crossover below the signal line? Yes, it happened on March 31. But look at the context: the histogram width is -1.32, which is nowhere near the extremes we saw in February when MACD was at +13.87. This is a *gentle* crossover, not a capitulation. MACD crossovers in consolidation are notoriously noisy and frequently precede breakouts, not breakdowns.

Here's what the technicals are *actually* telling us: **DE is in a healthy consolidation after a 7.5% pullback from February's peak.** That's normal. That's how bull markets work. They don't go straight up; they breathe. And when they breathe, they create entry points for risk capital.

The price action between $450 and $475 is not indecision—it's distribution of shares between weak hands and strong hands.
