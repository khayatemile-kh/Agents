#### **2. The Iran Conflict Scenario Is Pure Speculation**

15% probability of US-Iran conflict. The bull treats this as a material catalyst.

- The fact that prediction markets show these probabilities doesn't mean they're reliable or that LMT's stock should re-rate based on tail risks

**The real problem with the geopolitical bull case:**
