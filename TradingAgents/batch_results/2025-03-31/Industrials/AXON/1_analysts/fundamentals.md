## COMPANY PROFILE & BUSINESS OVERVIEW

**Axon Enterprise, Inc.** is a leading provider of technology solutions, cloud services, and equipment to law enforcement agencies and government entities globally. The company specializes in:

- **Public Safety Technology:** Cloud-based evidence management, dispatch systems, and software solutions
- **Aerospace & Defense:** Advanced technology systems and equipment for government and defense applications
- **Law Enforcement Solutions:** Body cameras, vehicle cameras, digital evidence management systems, and AI-powered analytics

The company operates as a technology and software-as-a-service (SaaS) provider with recurring revenue streams, positioning it favorably for long-term cash flow generation. Axon has demonstrated aggressive acquisition strategy and expansion into adjacent markets.
