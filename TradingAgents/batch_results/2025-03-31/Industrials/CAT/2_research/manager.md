The stronger, better-evidenced argument in this round is the bear's — they used the company's own reported numbers (TTM vs FY2024 margin comparison, OCF/FCF YoY declines) to show the bull's growth/margin narrative is decelerating, not accelerating, right as the stock is also breaking down technically.
