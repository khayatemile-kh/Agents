Yet the Neutral Analyst offers the most useful reconciliation: the ~250bp EBITDA margin compression tracks closely with rising interest expense from higher leverage, meaning the operating business may not be as broken as the Conservative Analyst implies — this is a capital-structure problem with a fixable (if slow) solution via deleveraging, not necessarily a competitive-moat problem.
