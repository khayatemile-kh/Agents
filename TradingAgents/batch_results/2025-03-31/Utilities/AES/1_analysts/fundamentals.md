## Financial Performance Analysis

**Analysis:** Revenue has remained relatively stable in the $12.3-12.7 billion range over the past three years, indicating mature market operations with modest growth opportunities. The slight decline from 2023 to 2024 reflects challenging market conditions or portfolio adjustments.

**Profitability Metrics:**
- **Gross Profit (FY 2024):** $2.31 billion (18.8% margin)
- **EBITDA (FY 2024):** $3.64 billion (29.7% margin)
- **Operating Income (FY 2024):** $2.03 billion (16.5% margin)
- **Net Income (FY 2024):** $1.68 billion (13.7% margin)

**Key Observations:**
- Gross profit margins of ~19% are reasonable for utilities sector
- EBITDA margin of 29.7% demonstrates strong operational cash generation
- Operating margin compression visible (2022: 18.6% vs 2024: 16.5%) suggests rising operational costs or margin pressure
- **Critical Finding:** Net profit margin of 13.7% is healthy, but this masks significant non-operating charges

**Unusual Items & Special Charges:**
- FY 2024: $29 million in total unusual items (minimal impact)
- FY 2023: -$1.35 billion in unusual charges (significant headwind)
- FY 2022: -$1.62 billion in unusual charges (major impact)
- Asset impairments improved from $1.08 billion (2023) to effectively $0 (2024)

**Strong Recovery Signal:** The 2024 results show dramatic improvement with normalization of unusual items, indicating the worst of previous asset impairments and restructuring charges are behind the company.
