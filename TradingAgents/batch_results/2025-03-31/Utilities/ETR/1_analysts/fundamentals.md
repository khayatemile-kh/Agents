## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Entergy Corporation (ETR) is a major regulated utility operating in the southeastern United States with a focus on electric and natural gas distribution. As of March 31, 2025, ETR presents a complex fundamental profile characterized by significant infrastructure assets, consistent dividend payments, but challenged by elevated leverage, negative free cash flow, and a valuation that has expanded substantially since the 52-week low. The company is trading at elevated multiples (Forward P/E of 20.84, TTM P/E of 27.20) while facing headwinds from capital expenditure requirements and interest expense pressures in a rising rate environment.

---

## COMPANY PROFILE & BUSINESS MODEL

**Core Business:**
Entergy Corporation operates as a regulated utility providing electricity and natural gas service to approximately 2.9 million customers across Louisiana, Arkansas, Mississippi, and Texas. The business model is characterized by:
- **Rate-regulated operations** with predictable, contracted revenue streams
- **Infrastructure-dependent model** requiring significant ongoing capital expenditure
- **Dividend-focused investor base** expecting consistent income distribution
- **Regional concentration** in the southeastern United States

**Regulatory Environment:**
As a regulated utility, ETR operates under Public Service Commission oversight with rate-setting mechanisms that theoretically provide cost recovery and a reasonable return on investment. This structure provides stability but limits growth upside and creates regulatory risk.
