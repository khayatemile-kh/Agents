### **PILLAR 3: DIVIDEND SECURITY & DEFENSIVE CASH FLOWS**

Now, let me address the elephant in the room: **negative free cash flow**. Bears will absolutely hammer on this. But context is everything.

**The Real Story:**

DUK's negative FCF is a *feature*, not a bug, of the regulated utility business model:

- **Operating Cash Flow: $12.33B** — this is the true cash-generation engine, up 24.6% YoY
- **Capital Expenditures: $13.12B** — this represents infrastructure investment, most of which gets **amortized into the regulated rate base**
- **Result:** CapEx funded through debt issuance (at favorable utility-regulated rates) and cash flow, creating regulated assets that earn a *fixed* return

**Dividend Sustainability Metrics:**
- **Annual Dividend Payout: ~$3.2B**
- **Operating Cash Flow: $12.33B**
- **Dividend Payout Ratio: 26%** of operating cash flow

**This is extraordinarily safe.** A 26% payout ratio means DUK has a massive cushion. Even if operating cash flow dropped 40%, the dividend would remain secure. Compare this to S&P 500 companies averaging 40-50% payout ratios—DUK is conservative.

**And here's the kicker:** Regulated utilities have demonstrated dividend resilience through multiple recessions. DUK has a **long history of dividend increases**, even during downturns. That's not happening with a fragile balance sheet.
