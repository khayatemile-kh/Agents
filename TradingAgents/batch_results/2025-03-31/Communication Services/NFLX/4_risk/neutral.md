Because Netflix has achieved scale efficiency. They produce more content at lower per-unit costs due to volume leverage. For that trend to reverse, Netflix would need to break its production discipline *and* face margin pressure simultaneously. Possible? Yes. Probable? That requires more than speculation.

**Scenario 3 (Macro weakness):** He says Netflix is "discretionary spending" and will get pressured in a recession. True in theory. In a 2-2.5% growth scenario (85% probability per prediction markets), discretionary spending doesn't crater. It moderates.
