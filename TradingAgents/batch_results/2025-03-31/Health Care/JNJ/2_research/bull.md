2. **Equity Value vs. Accounting Value**: JNJ's equity book value of ~$35.28/share drastically understates economic value because:
   - Pharmaceutical patents and drug portfolios aren't carried at fair value on the balance sheet
   - Intangible value (brand, distribution network, R&D capability) is enormous but invisible
   - When you adjust for this, true economic equity is far higher than accounting equity

3. **The Real Metric**: **Net Debt/EBITDA of 0.17x** is exceptional. For context:
   - Investment-grade threshold: < 3.0x
   - Pharmaceutical sector median: ~1.5-2.0x

A D/E ratio is an accounting construct. Cash-to-debt coverage is the economic reality. **JNJ wins on the metric that matters.**
