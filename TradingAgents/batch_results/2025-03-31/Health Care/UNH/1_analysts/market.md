## Detailed Analysis Report: UnitedHealth Group (UNH)

### **Executive Summary**

UnitedHealth Group (UNH) is demonstrating a **strong recovery and uptrend** as of March 31, 2025. After experiencing a significant pullback in late February (closing at $449.13 on 2/21), the stock has mounted a compelling recovery, closing at **$506.49** on 3/31—a gain of approximately **12.8%** from the February bottom. The recovery is supported by strengthening technical indicators, particularly momentum metrics showing bullish positioning, though traders should remain mindful of the stock's trading below its 200-day SMA, signaling a longer-term bearish structural context despite near-term strength.
