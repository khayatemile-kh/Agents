Weighing it: the bull's valuation-support pillar (forward P/E, PEG<1) depends entirely on hitting an unverified 80% EPS growth number that the bear correctly interrogates as fragile and undocumented, while the balance sheet reassurance depends on estimated, not disclosed, debt figures. That combination of unresolved earnings-quality risk, liquidity fragility, and confirmed technical deterioration outweighs the bull's quality-metric and generic secular-tailwind arguments. This tips the recommendation toward the cautious side rather than a genuine 50/50 hold, but the underlying business quality (ROE, FCF generation) and non-cyclical secular demand argument are strong enough that outright Sell/capitulation isn't warranted — this reads as Underweight: trim exposure, don't add into a confirmed breakdown until debt maturity schedule, actual leverage figures, and the earnings-collapse explanation get clarified.
