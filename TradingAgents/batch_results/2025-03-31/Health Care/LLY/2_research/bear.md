3. **The historical default rate is telling.** Among drugs in Phase 3, the FDA approval rate is roughly 50-60%. Among *novel mechanisms* in competitive categories, it's lower. A 3% probability for Retatrutide in 2025 implies expected approval in 2026-2027 (60-70% probability combined). That's not bearish; that's reasonable.
