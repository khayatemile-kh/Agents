## SECTION 1: THE "RECOVERY" IS A STATISTICAL MIRAGE

### You're Comparing Against the Worst Possible Baseline

Here's the sleight of hand in the bull thesis: **it's celebrating a recovery from a trough year while ignoring the pre-trough baseline.**

| Year | Revenue | Net Income | EPS | Context |
|------|---------|------------|-----|---------|
| **2022** | $101.2B | $31.4B | $5.47 | COVID vaccine peak |
| **2023** | $59.6B | $2.1B | $0.37 | **Trough** |
| **2024** | $63.6B | $8.0B | $1.41 | Recovery? |

The bull says: "Look at the 278% EPS growth! It's a recovery!"

**But here's what actually happened:** Even after this "recovery," Pfizer's 2024 EPS of $1.41 is still **74% below the 2022 level of $5.47**. You're not recovering to normalized performance—you're recovering to *mediocre* performance against a destroyed 2023 baseline.

### Revenue Is Still Collapsing in Real Terms

Let's be precise: **$63.6B in 2024 revenue is 37% below the $101.2B peak.** The bull narrative says this is expected COVID normalization. Sure. But here's the uncomfortable truth:

**Pfizer's core pharmaceutical business (excluding COVID vaccines) was never generating $101B in annual revenue.** The company's historical pre-COVID revenue (2019-2021 baseline) was approximately **$50-55B**. 

So yes, stripping out COVID vaccines was inevitable. But here's what that means:

- **2024 revenue of $63.6B is STILL elevated above normalized core business levels**
- This suggests **the core business is only marginally better than pre-pandemic**
- Future revenue could contract further if remaining COVID revenue (now normalized to ~$2-3B annually) continues to decline
- The company hasn't demonstrated top-line growth in its core franchises

**The bear thesis:** You're celebrating a recovery that still leaves the company below historical norms and facing continued revenue headwinds.
