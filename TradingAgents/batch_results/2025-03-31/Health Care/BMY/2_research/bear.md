Let me show you why this matters:

**The Goodwill & Intangible Asset Problem:**

| Item | Value | % of Total Assets | Critical Issue |

This is not a sign of strength. This is a sign that **BMY has accumulated massive acquisition-related write-downs and is highly exposed to future impairments if market conditions deteriorate or competitive dynamics shift.**

### Why "Normalization" Is Misleading

The bull says: *"Strip away the $14 billion charge and BMY is a profitable company generating $17.1B in EBITDA and $2.06B in normalized net income."*

1. **The Celgene acquisition (2019) has underperformed expectations.** The company is now writing down the value of those assets after 5+ years of integration. This suggests BMY overpaid and/or failed to realize synergies.

2. **BMY is forced to restructure its operations**, which means the "normal" business model is broken. If the business was truly healthy, why eliminate so many jobs and close so many facilities?

3. **Once management acknowledges problems this severe, you have to ask: Are there more impairments coming?** Look at the gross margin collapse:
   - 2022: 78.0%
   - 2023: 76.3%
   - 2024: 71.1%
   - **Decline of 700 basis points in two years**

This margin erosion is NOT explained by one-time charges. It's evidence of **deteriorating underlying business economics.**

The bull is essentially arguing the market has misprice BMY by 30-40%, requiring a **multiple re-rating to 12-14x.** But a re-rating only happens if the company executes perfectly on restructuring, maintains margins, and delivers growth. **Given the 700 bps margin decline and impairment history, what evidence suggests that execution is likely?**
