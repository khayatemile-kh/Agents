## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Moderna, Inc. is experiencing significant financial challenges as of March 31, 2025. The company has transitioned from a profitable position in 2022 (when it generated $8.36 billion in net income) to substantial losses in both 2023 and 2024. The company reported a net loss of $3.56 billion for 2024 and continues to exhibit negative operational performance metrics, including negative gross profit, EBITDA, and operating margins. This represents a dramatic reversal from the company's pandemic-era profitability.
