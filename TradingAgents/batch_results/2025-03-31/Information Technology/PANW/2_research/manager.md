## Debate Adjudication

**Winner: Bear analyst, on balance — but not decisively.**

The bull built a legitimate fundamental case (margin expansion, deferred revenue base, relative peer positioning) but **the valuation section collapsed under its own weight**. The bull's own EV/EBITDA modeling — even using an aggressive 25x forward multiple and a 20% EBITDA growth assumption — produced a fair enterprise value of ~$67B against a **$274.6B market cap**. Rather than resolving this ~4x gap, the bull pivoted to "wrong comparables" and the argument was left unfinished/unsupported. When a bull's own math disproves the bull thesis and the rebuttal is a hand-wave, that is a critical structural weakness in the argument, not a data point I can ignore.

The bear capitalized on this directly and added three points I find persuasive using only the evidence in this debate:

1. **Quality-of-earnings problem is real and unrebutted.** The bear's observation that FY2024 net income growth (486%) was driven substantially by a **$1.59B tax benefit**, with normalized net income closer to $988M, directly undermines the EPS growth assumptions embedded in the bull's PEG (2.0x) and forward PE (69x → 62-65x) arguments. If normalized EPS is ~$3.50-3.70 rather than the guided $4.88, forward multiples are meaningfully worse than the bull presented, and this was never rebutted.

2. **The bull never produced retention/booking data.** Net retention (DBNRR) and new bookings growth are the actual leading indicators for a deferred-revenue-heavy subscription business — not the static $5.54B deferred revenue balance itself, which the bear correctly reframes as an *obligation* rather than a guaranteed profit stream. This is a real gap in the bull's evidentiary base, explicitly conceded by omission.

3. **Technical deterioration is severe and accelerating, not decelerating.** An 18.2% drawdown in six weeks, with the *steepest leg occurring in the final week* (-10.1% in 6 sessions), price below all major moving averages, RSI at 37.25, and negative MACD, is a coincident risk signal regardless of whether one accepts the bear's "leading indicator" causal claim.
