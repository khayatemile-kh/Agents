## COMPANY PROFILE & BUSINESS OVERVIEW

**Company Name:** Advanced Micro Devices, Inc.  
**Ticker:** AMD  
**Exchange:** NMS (NASDAQ)  
**Sector:** Technology  
**Industry:** Semiconductors  
**Market Cap:** $825.61 billion

AMD is a global semiconductor company specializing in high-performance computing and graphics processors. The company designs and develops processors for data centers, gaming, professional visualization, and embedded systems. AMD operates through a fabless model, designing chips and outsourcing manufacturing to foundries, which provides operational efficiency and scalability.
