## The Leverage Problem Is Not Symmetric with the Upside Case

Here's the core asymmetry the aggressive analyst missed: 

**On the upside**, leverage amplifies equity returns if the business delivers growth and multiple expansion. The equity holder captures outsized gains. This is true.

**On the downside**, leverage amplifies equity losses if business deteriorates or macro conditions weaken. And crucially, the equity holder bears 100% of the downside risk while only capturing a share of the upside (debt holders get priority claims on assets).

We're positioned at exactly the wrong part of the leverage cycle for aggressive positioning. If we were early in an earnings growth cycle with confirmed management guidance and clear visibility, the leverage would be less concerning. But we're at a bottom bounce with **no recent guidance confirmation**. That's precisely when leverage creates tail risk.
