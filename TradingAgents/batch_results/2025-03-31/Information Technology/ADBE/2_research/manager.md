## Adjudicating the Debate

The bear's point on competitive erosion (Figma, Firefly commoditization, content-aware fill precedent) is a reasonable qualitative check on the "margin expansion via AI" narrative that the bull asserts but doesn't substantiate with actual margin trend data.

**Where the Bull Retains Some Ground:**

The bull is right that 89.4% gross margins, 35% operating margins, and $9.2B FCF are genuinely strong absolute figures — this is a profitable, cash-generative business, not a distressed one. The technical oversold conditions (RSI 34.63, capitulation volume) are real data points suggesting the *selloff itself* may be overdone in the near term, even if the bear's structural concerns about growth and leverage are valid over a longer horizon.

**Critical Gap:** Neither analyst provided verified debt figures, actual historical revenue growth trend data, or margin trend history — this dispute over "is 61x D/E dangerous" and "is 57.5% EPS growth credible" cannot be fully resolved with the evidence given. I flag this explicitly rather than guessing.

## Verdict

The bull's core valuation thesis rests on an unverified, unbridged EPS growth number, and their rebuttal to the leverage concern contains an unreconciled math error rather than a real answer. The bear's skepticism is better evidenced and more internally consistent, even though their own debt-figure estimate is also a guess. Given decelerating growth signals, unresolved leverage questions, and a growth forecast lacking a credible mechanism, this tilts toward caution — but the underlying cash generation and margin profile are too strong to justify a full Sell.
