## THE MACRO TAILWINDS: WHY NOW MATTERS

### **AI Chip Design Cycle is in Early Innings**

The current semiconductor design cycle is fundamentally different from prior cycles. Here's why:

1. **AI Infrastructure CPUs vs. Legacy Chips:** Legacy semiconductor designs evolve incrementally. AI accelerators require *radical redesigns*:
   - Larger die sizes to accommodate more tensor cores
   - Complex power delivery networks
   - Novel cooling requirements
   - Advanced packaging (chiplets, 3D stacking)
   
   Each of these design challenges requires more Cadence tool usage, more man-hours, and longer design cycles. **More design activity = more EDA revenue.**

2. **Multiple Simultaneous Design Efforts:** Unlike historical cycles where one company (e.g., Intel) dominated the roadmap, today's AI chip market has *dozens* of concurrent designs:
   - NVIDIA (multiple GPU families)
   - AMD (EPYC, MI series)
   - Google TPU (custom)
   - Meta (custom accelerators)
   - Microsoft (custom AI chips)
   - Apple (Neural Engine evolution)
   - Plus hundreds of edge AI chip startups

   **This creates a multiplicative effect on EDA demand.** It's not just "more chips"—it's more *unique* chips, each requiring heavy tool usage.

3. **Geopolitical Tailwind:** U.S. semiconductor restrictions on China are creating incentives for *both* Western chip companies (building new designs to replace restricted exports) and Chinese companies (designing indigenous alternatives). Both scenarios drive EDA spending.
