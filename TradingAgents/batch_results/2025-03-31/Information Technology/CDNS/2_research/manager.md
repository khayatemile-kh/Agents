The bear's FCF-multiple cross-check (48.6x trailing FCF vs. mid-30s/40s for mature large-cap software comps) is a legitimate, uncontested data point showing the stock is pricing in a premium that requires flawless execution.

Neither side successfully lands a knockout blow: the bull didn't adequately defend against the margin-compression data, and the bear didn't dispute the underlying AI-driven demand thesis or the technical support level holding. Given that valuation already prices in significant EPS growth that recent trends don't clearly support, but the fundamental demand story and technical support remain intact, the balanced call is to hold current exposure rather than add or trim aggressively. This is a case where price already reflects a lot of optimism, so new buying is not warranted, but the deteriorating margin trend and full valuation aren't severe enough (no fundamental breakdown, support intact) to justify selling down a position either.
