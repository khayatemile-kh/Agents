## 3. GROWTH THAT JUSTIFIES THE PREMIUM: THE PEG RATIO STORY

Here's where valuation gets interesting. Yes, 145x P/E sounds insane in isolation. **But let's use the PEG ratio (Price-to-Earnings-Growth):**

**PLTR's PEG Ratio: 1.68x**

What does this mean? If the market prices in, say, 35%+ annual earnings growth over the next 3-5 years, a PEG of 1.68x is *actually reasonable*.

### Why Is 35%+ Growth Achievable?

1. **Total Addressable Market (TAM) Expansion:** Palantir's government business is proven. The commercial business—where the *real* growth lives—is still in early innings. Enterprise AI adoption is accelerating. Palantir's platforms (Gotham for defense/intel, Apollo for commercial, AIP for AI-native development) are increasingly central to how enterprises handle data.

3. **Market Expansion:** The commercial segment revenue is still small relative to government. As commercial scales 2-3x, total earnings could easily compound at 30-40% annually for the next 3-5 years.

**The Bull Case:** If you believe PLTR can grow earnings at 30-40% annually over the next 3-5 years—which the business model and market position suggest it can—then **145x P/E is not expensive; it's actually fair to slightly cheap.**
