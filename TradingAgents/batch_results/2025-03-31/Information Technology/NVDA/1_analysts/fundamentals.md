# COMPREHENSIVE FUNDAMENTAL ANALYSIS REPORT: NVDA (NVIDIA CORPORATION)

**Analysis Date:** March 31, 2025 | **Company:** NVIDIA Corporation | **Sector:** Technology / Semiconductors | **Exchange:** NMS

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

NVIDIA Corporation (NVDA) has demonstrated extraordinary financial performance through fiscal year 2025 (ended January 31, 2025), showcasing exceptional growth trajectory driven by AI and data center demand. The company has evolved from a severe cyclical downturn in 2023 to a hypergrowth phase, with revenues more than doubling and profitability expanding significantly. However, current valuations reflect this optimistic outlook, and the company faces strategic considerations including capital allocation priorities and sustainability of growth rates.

---

## COMPANY OVERVIEW

**Business Classification:** Semiconductors / GPU Design & Manufacturing  
**Primary Markets:** Data Centers (dominant), Gaming, Professional Visualization, Automotive

NVIDIA is a fabless semiconductor company that designs and markets Graphics Processing Units (GPUs) and system-on-chip (SoC) units. The company does not manufacture chips directly but partners with foundries (primarily TSMC). NVIDIA's business has been transformed by the AI revolution, particularly through demand for its flagship CUDA architecture processors used in AI training and inference.
