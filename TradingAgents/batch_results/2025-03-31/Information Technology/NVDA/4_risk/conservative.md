The real danger here is confusing *statistical* support with *structural* support. A stock at the lower Bollinger Band is statistically oversold. But if the trend is down and the 10, 50, and 200 EMAs are all declining, then "oversold" may simply mean "not down *enough* yet."

Prediction markets are useful for understanding base-case scenarios, not for timing tactical entry points during periods of elevated volatility.
