- **Q1 2025 showed -$27M FCF**, but let's examine the composition:
  - Prepaid assets changed by -$147.95M (cash outflow)—but this is a *timing* item, not recurring

**If we normalize for one-time items and prepayments, the underlying operational cash generation is likely positive or breakeven.** The negative FCF headline is distorted by Q1 timing and working capital movements.

**Why this matters:** A company with $25B in revenue, 46.6% gross margins, and $1.55B EBITDA isn't in a free cash flow crisis. It has refinancing optionality and operational flexibility that the current -$27M FCF doesn't reflect.
