## Company Profile & Business Model

PayPal is a global financial services and payments technology company operating in the credit services industry. The company provides digital payment solutions, including online transfers, merchant services, and financial products to consumers and businesses worldwide. PYPL's business model generates revenue through transaction fees, merchant services, and financial services offerings.

**Key Business Segments:**
- **Merchant Services:** Processing payments for e-commerce, point-of-sale, and invoicing
- **Consumer Financial Services:** Money transfers, direct debit services, and credit products
- **Digital Wallets & Payment Technology:** Branded payment solutions and platform services
