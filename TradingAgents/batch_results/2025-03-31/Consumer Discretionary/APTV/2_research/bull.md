That's a **7.9% FCF margin**—extraordinary for a manufacturer. More importantly:

**The leverage structure reflects past M&A and restructuring, not operational decay.**

Here's the critical point the bear will miss: A company with 7.9% FCF conversion doesn't face a debt crisis—it faces a *capital structure optimization* challenge. And those get solved through:
- Refinancing (APTV's scale and investment-grade profile enable this)

- Earnings growth (which forward multiples suggest is coming)

The 64.94x debt-to-equity is a **lagging indicator** that will compress as earnings recover. By next year, with EPS at $6.51, that ratio will look far less alarming.
