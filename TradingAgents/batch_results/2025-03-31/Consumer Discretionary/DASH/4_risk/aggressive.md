## The Profitability Inflection They're Ignoring

The trader's sell thesis rests heavily on this claim: "core operating income remains negative ($38M loss in 2024) despite two years of optimization." This is **technically accurate but dangerously misleading** when you read the full picture.

Here's what actually happened in 2024:

- **2024 Operating Income:** -$38 million (on $10.72B revenue)
- **2023 Operating Income:** -$577 million 
- **2022 Operating Income:** -$1,032 million

That's a **$539 million improvement from 2023 to 2024**—a 93% swing toward profitability in a single year. Operating margin went from -6.7% to -0.4%. The company is *literally at breakeven on an operating basis*, and the analyst wants to call this "deterioration"? 

But wait—there's more nuance here. **Net income tells the real story:**

- **2024 Net Income:** $123 million (1.1% net margin)
- **2023 Net Income:** -$558 million
- **2022 Net Income:** -$1,365 million

DoorDash posted its **first profitable year** after consecutive years of losses. That's not deterioration—that's a company that has crossed the Rubicon from growth-at-all-costs to sustainable profitability.

Now, I'll grant the bear case this concession: the company is still burning cash on an operating basis by the narrowest of margins. But the trajectory is unmistakable. The question isn't "Is DASH profitable today?" but rather "Is DASH on the path to sustainable profitability?" The answer is an emphatic **yes**. And the market hasn't priced this in yet—hence the weakness.

- **Operating Cash Flow:** $5.84 billion (54.5% of revenue)

The sell thesis claims "the bull case rests on margin expansion claims" as if this is aspirational. It's not.
