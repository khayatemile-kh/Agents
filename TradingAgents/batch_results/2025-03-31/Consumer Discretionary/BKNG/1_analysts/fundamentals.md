Booking Holdings is the world's leading online travel company, offering a comprehensive portfolio of travel services including:

- Flights and airline tickets
- Car rentals

The company's core business model is commission and advertising-based, generating revenue from suppliers (hotels, airlines, car rental companies) through a digital marketplace platform.
