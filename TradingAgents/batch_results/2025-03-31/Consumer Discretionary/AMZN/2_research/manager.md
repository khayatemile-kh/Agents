**Recommendation**: Overweight

**Rationale**: Weighing both sides, the bull case rests on solid, verifiable fundamentals: a 50.8% gross margin, 17.4% net margin, $135B TTM net income, $94.6B in liquid assets, and a still-dominant AWS franchise (32% share) plus a fast-growing, high-margin advertising business. The bear case raises legitimate points—AWS growth has decelerated from its historical 30-40% pace to the 20% area, working-capital swings inflate headline OCF in any given quarter, and competitive pressure from Azure/Google Cloud is real—but none of these amount to a thesis-breaking structural impairment. AWS at ~20% growth on an $100B+ run-rate is still a very large absolute dollar growth engine, and the bear's argument that this is 'decelerating' doesn't change the fact that it remains oligopolistic with high switching costs; the multi-cloud narrative is a real but incremental risk, not evidence of share collapse. On valuation, the bear's attempt to inflate the PEG to '3.0+' relies on an assumed 6-8% earnings growth rate that isn't substantiated in the data provided—if AWS and advertising (both growing double-digits with expanding margins) make up a rising share of the mix, blended earnings growth is plausibly higher than the bear's charitable assumption, which undercuts their overvaluation argument. The balance sheet is more nuanced than the bull's dismissal or the bear's alarm: net debt of roughly $38.7B against $135B of net income and $17B/quarter of operating cash flow is manageable, not fragile, even if capex intensity is elevated. The technical setup (RSI 35, price below the 50-day, MACD stabilizing) supports the bull's framing of this as a pullback within a structurally sound business rather than the start of a fundamental breakdown, but the bear's caution about decelerating growth and margin risk from cloud competition argues against a full-conviction Buy. Net: the fundamental compounding story (AWS + ads margin mix shift, fortress-like liquidity, entrenched moat) outweighs the bear's decelerating-growth and competitive-erosion concerns, but the elevated capex, working-capital-driven OCF volatility, and real multi-cloud competitive risk mean this isn't a maximum-conviction Buy — it's a constructive, gradually-add position.
