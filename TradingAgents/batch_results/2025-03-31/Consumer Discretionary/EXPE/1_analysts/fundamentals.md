### EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Expedia Group, Inc. (EXPE) is a leading global online travel agency and metasearch platform operating in the Consumer Cyclical / Travel Services sector. The company demonstrates strong recovery momentum post-pandemic with improving profitability metrics, though it faces structural challenges related to high leverage and significant intangible asset impairment risk.

---

## SECTION 1: COMPANY PROFILE & BUSINESS OVERVIEW

**Company Name:** Expedia Group, Inc.  
**Ticker:** EXPE (NMS Exchange)  
**Sector:** Consumer Cyclical  
**Industry:** Travel Services  
**Market Capitalization:** $32.95 billion (as of analysis date)

### Business Model & Operations
Expedia operates as a vertically integrated travel services platform providing:
- Online accommodation bookings
- Flight reservations and ticketing
- Vacation rental platforms (Vrbo)
- Cruise and activities bookings
- Travel insurance and payment solutions

The company generates revenue primarily through commissions on booking transactions, advertising partnerships, and direct supplier commissions. This asset-light business model enables high gross margins with substantial operating leverage.
