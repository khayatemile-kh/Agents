### **REITs Are Different Animals**

Iron Mountain is a specialty REIT, not a traditional corporation. The REIT structure is *designed* to have high leverage and operate on thinner equity cushions than industrials or tech companies. Here's why:

**REIT Math 101:**
- REITs **must distribute 90% of taxable income** to shareholders—this is by law, not choice
- This distribution requirement means REITs *intentionally* retain minimal retained earnings
- The capital structure evolves to have lower equity, higher debt—this is **optimal for REIT shareholders**, not a sign of distress
- What matters isn't stockholders' equity; what matters is **asset value, cash flow generation, and debt serviceability**
