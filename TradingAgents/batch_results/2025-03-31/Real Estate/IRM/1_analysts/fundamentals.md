## Executive Summary

Iron Mountain Incorporated is a leading global provider of storage and information management services, operating as a specialty Real Estate Investment Trust (REIT). The company owns and operates an extensive portfolio of storage facilities, data centers, and document management centers across multiple continents. As of the latest available data (FY 2024), the company exhibits mixed financial characteristics with significant scale but concerning capital structure metrics that warrant careful consideration.

## Company Profile & Business Overview

**Primary Business Segments:**
- Data Management: Document storage, scanning, and archival services
- Physical Storage: Secure storage facilities for business records and assets
- Data Centers: Specialized facilities for enterprise IT infrastructure
- Digital Transformation Solutions: Cloud-based information management services

**Global Presence:**
Iron Mountain operates facilities across multiple continents with a focus on major business hubs and financial centers. The company serves enterprise clients, government agencies, and healthcare providers requiring secure information storage and management.

**Competitive Positioning:**
As the world's largest provider of storage and information management services, IRM maintains a dominant market position with recurring revenue streams and high customer retention rates typical of the storage industry.
