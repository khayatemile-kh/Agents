**But this metric is meaningless for REITs when viewed in isolation.**

**REIT Accounting is Designed to Maximize Debt Leverage:**
- REITs are legally required to distribute **90% of taxable income to shareholders**
- This means retained earnings are negative by design (~$5.6 billion deficit)
- Debt-to-equity ratios for REITs **look terrible** on spreadsheets but function efficiently operationally
- What matters is **not debt/equity, but debt service coverage**
