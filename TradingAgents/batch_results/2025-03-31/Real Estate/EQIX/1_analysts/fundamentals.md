## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Equinix, Inc. (EQIX) is a leading global provider of interconnection and data center infrastructure services, classified as a Real Estate Investment Trust (REIT) specializing in data center operations. As of March 31, 2025, the company demonstrates strong operational performance with substantial revenue growth, robust cash generation, but faces notable leverage challenges. The company's market capitalization stands at $102.74 billion, reflecting strong investor confidence in the strategic importance of data center infrastructure in the AI and cloud computing era.

### Business Operations
Equinix operates a global network of data centers and provides interconnection services that enable organizations to establish and maintain direct connections with customers, partners, and cloud providers. The company's platform facilitates the digital transformation of enterprises across various industries by providing secure, efficient infrastructure for compute, cloud, storage, and connectivity services.
