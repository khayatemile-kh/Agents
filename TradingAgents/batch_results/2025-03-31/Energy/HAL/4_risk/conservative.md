## **THE "BAKED-IN" EARNINGS GROWTH IS EXACTLY THE PROBLEM, NOT THE SOLUTION**

During the 2020 downturn in energy, we saw E&P companies slash capex by 30-50% *overnight*. Contractors like HAL didn't gradually decline—they cratered. **Second, the current environment is more fragile than it appears.** Yes, oil majors have strong cash flows today.
