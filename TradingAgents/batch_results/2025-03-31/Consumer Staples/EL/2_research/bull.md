1. **Premium brands have pricing power:** EL's 75.4% gross margins reflect the fact that affluent consumers—EL's core market—are far less price-sensitive than mass-market consumers. A 1-2% slowdown in GDP does **not proportionally impact luxury spending.**

3. **China exposure is a feature, not a bug:** Yes, China is slowing. But EL's established distribution there provides **structural optionality**—if China recovers (which it will), EL captures upside. If China remains soft, the other 60%+ of revenue continues to flow.

During the 2008-2009 recession (peak-to-trough -37% decline in S&P 500), EL's revenue remained relatively stable and the company returned positive earnings. Luxury goods proved more defensive than broad equities because **affluent consumers maintained spending on prestige beauty.**
