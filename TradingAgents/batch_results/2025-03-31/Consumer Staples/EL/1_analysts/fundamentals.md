# Comprehensive Fundamental Analysis Report: EL (The Estée Lauder Companies Inc.)
## Analysis Date: March 31, 2025

---

## Executive Summary

The Estée Lauder Companies Inc. (EL) is a leading global beauty and personal care conglomerate operating in the Consumer Defensive sector. As of March 31, 2025, the company presents a mixed fundamental picture with significant challenges balanced against its strong brand portfolio and market position. Key concerns include elevated debt levels, compressed profitability margins, and recent asset impairments, though the company maintains solid revenue generation and operational cash flows.

---

## Company Profile & Business Overview

**Company Name:** The Estée Lauder Companies Inc.  
**Ticker:** EL  
**Exchange:** NYQ (New York Stock Exchange)  
**Sector:** Consumer Defensive  
**Industry:** Household & Personal Products  
**Market Capitalization:** $36.79 billion (as of analysis date)

### Business Description

Estée Lauder is a multinational manufacturer and marketer of prestige skin care, makeup, fragrance, and hair care products. The company operates through a portfolio of globally recognized brands including Estée Lauder, Clinique, MAC, Avon, Bobby Brown, DKNY, and many others. The company serves consumers across multiple channels including department stores, specialty retailers, travel retail, and direct-to-consumer platforms. Its business model emphasizes high-margin luxury and prestige beauty products, with significant international exposure.

### Revenue & Profitability Overview

**Trailing Twelve Months (TTM) Revenue:** $15.05 billion

**EBITDA (TTM):** $2.46 billion

### Critical Profitability Concerns

The most striking observation is the severe compression in net profitability despite robust revenue and gross margins. The TTM net profit margin of 1.21% represents significant deterioration from historical levels. This is driven by:

1. **Significant Impairment Charges:** Annual data reveals $471 million in impairment charges for fiscal year 2024 (ended June 30, 2024) and $207 million for fiscal 2023, indicating challenges in asset valuation and market conditions.

2. **Restructuring Costs:** The company incurred $122 million in restructuring and merger/acquisition-related charges in FY2024, reflecting organizational realignment efforts.

3. **Operating Expense Burden:** SG&A expenses of $9.62 billion represent ~61.7% of revenue in FY2024, reflecting the high marketing and distribution costs inherent in the prestige beauty business.

### Fiscal Year 2024 Performance (Ended June 30, 2024)

- **Total Revenue:** $15.61 billion (down 1.9% from $15.91 billion in FY2023)
- **Operating Income:** $1.56 billion
- **Operating Margin:** 10.0%
- **EBIT:** $1.15 billion
- **Net Income:** $390 million
- **Diluted EPS:** $1.08 (down 61% from $2.79 in FY2023)
- **Basic EPS:** $1.09

The dramatic decline in EPS reflects both lower net income and special charges. FY2023 benefited from more favorable comparisons, with net income of $1.01 billion and EPS of $2.79.

### EBITDA Deterioration

EBITDA declined from $2.40 billion in FY2023 to $1.98 billion in FY2024, a 17.6% decrease. This indicates operational performance challenges beyond just special items. Normalized EBITDA of $2.57 billion in FY2024 (vs. $2.66 billion in FY2023) shows underlying operational pressure even after adjusting for unusual items.
