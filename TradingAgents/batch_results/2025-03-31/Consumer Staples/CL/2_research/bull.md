## **PILLAR 1: DOMINANT COMPETITIVE ADVANTAGES & MARKET POSITION**

### **1. Unassailable Brand Moat**

CL controls **iconic, multi-generational brands** that dominate their categories:

- **Colgate:** Global oral care leader with ~45% market share in developed markets
- **Palmolive:** Household care staple recognized for over 100 years
- **Tom's of Maine:** Natural personal care segment (premium positioning)

**Data Point:** Gross margins expanded from 57.0% (2022) to 60.5% (2024)—a 350-basis-point improvement. This reflects pricing power overcoming cost inflation. Competitors with weaker brands cannot achieve this.
