**Recommendation**: Underweight

**Rationale**: The bull case rests on real strengths: dominant brand portfolio, genuine margin/FCF quality (FCF yield ~4.9%, FCF covers dividend 2.4x), and a deleveraging balance sheet (net debt/EBITDA down from 2.37x to 1.41x). Those are hard facts and matter for downside protection and dividend safety.

But the bear case lands the more decisive blows on the forward-looking, decision-relevant points: (1) the margin expansion story is largely a commodity-tailwind/comp effect rather than durable pricing power — only 140 of 350bps of gross margin gain made it to operating margin, implying opex is eating the rest and that the easy wins from palm oil/oil normalization are behind us, not ahead; (2) revenue growth decelerated sharply from 8.3% to 3.3%, which is the tell that price increases are outrunning volume/demand — exactly the elasticity risk the bear flags, and the bull never actually rebuts this deceleration; (3) EM 'growth engine' framing is undercut by FX translation drag and rising local competition/private label, so the 5-7% headline growth is not what shows up in reported USD results; (4) valuation is stretched — forward P/E ~22x and trailing ~25.7x versus staples peer average ~20-22x, which is a premium for a business now growing topline in the low-single-digits with margin tailwinds fading.

Net: the quality-of-balance-sheet and cash-generation points support HOLDING existing exposure and collecting the dividend, but they don't justify paying a premium multiple for a business whose organic growth is decelerating and whose recent margin gains look more cyclical than structural. The bear's rebuttal on margin math and revenue deceleration is the most concrete, verifiable weak point in the bull thesis and wasn't adequately countered. That tips this toward a cautious/underweight stance rather than an outright sell, since the dividend, cash flow quality, and deleveraging still provide a real floor — but it argues against adding to the position or paying up here, and favors trimming into any strength given the valuation premium versus decelerating growth.

**Strategic Actions**: 1) Trim CL exposure modestly (e.g., reduce position by 15-25%) rather than adding at current levels near $90, given a ~22x forward / ~25.7x trailing P/E that is running ahead of a business now growing revenue only 3.3% (down from 8.3%).
2) Do not chase the margin-expansion narrative for further upside — treat the 2022-2024 gross margin gains as largely commodity-tailwind driven; watch gross-to-operating margin flow-through (only ~40% of gross margin gains reached operating margin) as an early warning that SG&A/marketing spend is offsetting pricing gains.
3) Retain a core position sized around the dividend/cash-flow quality (FCF yield ~4.9%, dividend covered ~2.4x by FCF, payout ratio normalizing to ~70%) — this supports holding a base position for income and defensiveness rather than exiting entirely.
4) Monitor EM revenue in local currency vs. reported USD growth each quarter; if the FX/organic growth gap persists (organic ~5-7% but reported ~3%), treat EM as a stabilizer, not a growth catalyst, and resist re-rating the stock higher on EM optimism.
5) Set a re-entry/add trigger: only increase weight if forward P/E compresses toward the ~18-20x staples peer average, or if quarterly organic volume growth (ex-price) reaccelerates, confirming pricing isn't destroying demand.
6) Use any rally toward prior highs as an opportunity to further reduce, given limited multiple re-rating upside and decelerating top-line momentum; keep stop/trim discipline rather than treating dips as buying opportunities until growth data improves.
