## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

PepsiCo, Inc. (PEP) is a global leader in the beverage and food industry with a diversified portfolio spanning soft drinks, juices, water, sports drinks, and snack foods. As of March 31, 2025, PepsiCo demonstrates solid fundamentals with consistent revenue generation, strong cash flow generation, and a reliable dividend yield. The company operates in a defensive sector, making it attractive for income-focused investors, though it faces typical headwinds such as changing consumer preferences toward healthier beverages and inflationary pressures on input costs.
