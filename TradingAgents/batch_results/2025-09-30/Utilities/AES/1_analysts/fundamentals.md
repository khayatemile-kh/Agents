## COMPANY PROFILE & BUSINESS OVERVIEW

**The AES Corporation** is one of the world's largest independent power producers and energy utilities, with operations across multiple continents. The company's business model includes:

1. **Generation Assets**: Owns and operates power generation facilities using diverse fuel sources (renewables, thermal, hydro)
2. **Distribution Utilities**: Operates regulated utility distribution businesses in key markets
3. **Global Footprint**: Significant presence in Latin America, Asia, and North America

The company's strategic focus includes:
- Energy transition and renewable energy expansion
- Grid modernization and digitalization
- Improving operational efficiency across its portfolio
