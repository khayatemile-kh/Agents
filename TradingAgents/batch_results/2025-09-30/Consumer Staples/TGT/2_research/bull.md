- **Expansion: +360 basis points**

And net margins? **Up from 2.55% to 3.83%—a 128 basis point improvement.**

This isn't a fluke. This is **disciplined operational excellence.** Target has:
1. **Optimized its product mix** toward higher-margin categories
2. **Reduced promotional intensity** (proof: margins are expanding despite flat/declining traffic)
3. **Improved inventory management** (reducing markdowns and obsolescence)
4. **Negotiated better supplier terms** (leveraging scale)

**The Bull Interpretation:** The company is deliberately trading lower volume for substantially higher profitability. This is a *strategic shift*, not a decline. The net result? **Net income remains robust at $4.09B despite lower revenues.** That's operating leverage at work.
