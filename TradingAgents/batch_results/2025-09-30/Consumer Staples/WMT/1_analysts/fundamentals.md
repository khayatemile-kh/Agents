# COMPREHENSIVE FUNDAMENTAL ANALYSIS: WALMART INC. (WMT)

## Executive Summary

Walmart Inc. (WMT) is a Consumer Defensive/Discount Stores retailer with a market capitalization of approximately **$841.6 billion** as of September 30, 2025. As the world's largest retailer by revenue, WMT generates over **$735.8 billion** (TTM) in annual revenue with consistent profitability and strong operational efficiency. The company demonstrates resilience typical of defensive consumer stocks with a relatively low beta of 0.595, making it suitable for risk-averse investors.

---

## COMPANY PROFILE & BUSINESS OVERVIEW

**Company Name:** Walmart Inc.  
**Ticker:** WMT  
**Exchange:** NMS (Nasdaq Capital Market)  
**Sector:** Consumer Defensive  
**Industry:** Discount Stores  
**Headquarters:** Bentonville, Arkansas, USA  
**Business Model:** Omnichannel retail with physical stores and e-commerce operations

Walmart operates a comprehensive discount retail business spanning general merchandise, grocery, and various consumer goods across multiple formats including supercenters, neighborhood markets, and Sam's Club warehouse clubs. The company has successfully diversified into e-commerce, advertising services, and financial services to supplement its core retail business.
