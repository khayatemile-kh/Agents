**This isn't stabilization. This is *sequential deterioration.***

The bull frames FY 2023 as an "anomalous peak" that shouldn't be the benchmark. But that's convenient narrative-building. Here's what actually happened:

1. **FY 2023 wasn't an anomaly—it was the result of extraordinary margin expansion** driven by:
   - Commodity supercycle (corn, soybeans hit record prices)

   - Supply-constrained markets allowing pricing power

3. **FY 2025 is the validation of the downtrend:** Management's forward guidance of $22.69 EPS represents a **34.4% cumulative decline from the FY 2023 peak.** This isn't cyclical normalization—**this is a structural loss of earnings power.**

## **2. UNPACK THE "STRONG MARGINS" CLAIM—IT'S AN ILLUSION**

The bull points to Q3 FY 2025 operating margins of 18.3% and gross margins of 35.7%, claiming "pricing power persists."

**I'm going to show you why this metric is misleading:**

### **The Gross Margin Trap**

Yes, gross margins are 35.7% in Q3. But look at the trajectory:

- **FY 2023:** ~38-39% gross margins (estimated from strong net profitability)
- **FY 2024:** ~38% gross margins (company's general disclosure)
- **Q3 FY 2025:** 35.7% gross margins

**That's 200-300 basis points of gross margin compression year-over-year.**

The bull interprets this as "pricing power persists." I interpret it as: **Pricing power is breaking down.** 

Here's why: If DE had genuine pricing power, gross margins would be *expanding* as it passes through inflation to customers. Instead, margins are *contracting*, which means:
1. Input cost inflation is outpacing price increases to customers, OR
2. Product mix is shifting toward lower-margin segments, OR
3. Competition is intensifying, forcing price concessions

**All three scenarios are bearish for forward earnings.**

### **Operating Margin Context is Missing**

The bull claims Q3 operating margin of 18.3% is "up from 17.6% run-rate expectations." Let me reality-check this:

- **FY 2023 Operating Margin:** ~24.2% (from full-year data)
- **FY 2024 Operating Margin:** ~22.6%
- **Q3 FY 2025 Operating Margin:** 18.3%

**This is a 380-590 basis point deterioration from recent history.** The Q3 margin looks "good" only if you compare it to a depressed expectation, not to historical reality. The fact that bulls are celebrating 18.3% as "improvement" when the company historically operated at 22-24% reveals how much the narrative has shifted downward.
