The technical picture cited by the bull (RSI recovering from oversold, MACD histogram turning) is a weak, low-conviction argument for a re-rating catalyst — it's consistent with a bounce, not proof of a trend reversal, and the stock remains below both the 50- and 200-day moving averages.

Net: the fundamental story here is 'good business, but the balance sheet just got materially more levered and near-term-risky, at the same time growth is decelerating, and the market is paying a premium multiple that assumes neither of those things.' That combination argues for trimming/dialing back exposure rather than adding here, but the underlying moat and recurring-revenue quality keep this from being an outright sell — the debt is serviceable (interest/EBITDA ~11%) and the business isn't broken, just priced richly against a slightly worsening risk profile. I'm landing on Underweight: the bear's balance-sheet and growth-deceleration arguments carry the round, but the moat/cash-generation quality argues against a full Sell.
