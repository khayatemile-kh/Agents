### The Hidden Advantage: Structural Demand That Doesn't Care About Economic Cycles

Leidos derives the vast majority of its revenue from **government contracts with the DoD, intelligence agencies, and civilian government** (census, infrastructure, etc.). This is mission-critical work—cybersecurity, systems integration, engineering services, IT infrastructure modernization.

**Why This is Bullish:**
1. **Demand is Non-Discretionary:** Unlike commercial software or consumer tech, government IT spending doesn't get cut when the economy slows. If anything, defense budgets expand during geopolitical tension.
2. **Multi-Year Contracts Lock in Revenue:** Leidos isn't hunting for new deals every quarter. Many contracts span 3–5 years, providing predictable revenue visibility.
3. **Barriers to Entry are Massive:** To win government contracts, you need security clearances, compliance certifications (CMMC, NIST, etc.), and relationships built over decades. Leidos has all three.
4. **Pricing Power:** Government contracts are often cost-plus or negotiated fixed-price with escalators. This isn't a race-to-the-bottom market like commercial SaaS.

**The Implication:**
While the S&P 500 worries about recession risk, Leidos' revenue is virtually **recession-proof.** This defensive quality alone justifies a valuation premium, yet the market is pricing LDOS at a **discount** to its government contractor peers (RTX, GD, LMT) on P/E and EV/EBITDA multiples.
