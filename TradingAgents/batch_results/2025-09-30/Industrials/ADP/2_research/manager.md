Net-net: the bull's structural moat/margin-profile/balance-sheet-strength arguments are credible reasons to keep exposure, but the bear has exposed real analytical gaps in the growth extrapolation and valuation justification (19.95x forward P/E) that make an Overweight/Buy premature without more information on the durability of the margin move and actual NRR/retention data.
