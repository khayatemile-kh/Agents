Baker Hughes is a leading global energy company providing oilfield services, products, and solutions to the upstream, midstream, and downstream sectors of the oil and gas industry. ## COMPANY PROFILE & BUSINESS OVERVIEW

Baker Hughes operates across multiple business segments serving the energy industry:

1. **Oilfield Services & Equipment** - Drilling solutions, well completion, and production equipment
2. **Digital Solutions** - Advanced technologies and software for energy operations
3. **Industrial Equipment** - Turbomachinery and power generation equipment
4. **Support Services** - Maintenance, repair, and operational support

The company benefits from strong underlying demand dynamics driven by:
- Global energy demand resilience
- Increased capital expenditures by major oil & gas operators
- Technological advancement in extraction and processing
- Energy transition opportunities in emerging technologies
