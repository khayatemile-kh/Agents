- Revenue growth: **+3.2%** (modest)
- **Other non-interest expense declined -5.8%** — this is the key driver of pretax income outpacing revenue

**The problem?** This expense decline likely reflects *lower loan loss provisions*, not operational improvements. In a credit cycle, **declining provisions is a leading indicator that credit quality is *already priced in*, and the next move is typically *higher* provisions as delinquencies rise.**

Let me highlight the critical data point the bull glossed over:

- **Net income was essentially flat: +0.6% QoQ** (from $2.885B to $2.902B)

**What this tells us:** The operational "beat" is being masked by tax normalization. The actual earnings power—net income—is *stagnating*, not accelerating. That's not a company firing on all cylinders; that's a company showing signs of maturity.
