**Analysis Date: September 30, 2025**

---

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Moderna, Inc. (MRNA) is experiencing a critical transition period characterized by significant profitability challenges and dramatic revenue contraction. The company has shifted from a highly profitable position in 2022 (when it generated $18.9B in revenue and $8.4B in net income) to substantial operating losses and negative free cash flow in 2024-2025. The fundamentals reveal a company in distress, requiring significant strategic repositioning to return to profitability.

Q3 2025 revenue of $1.002B still represents a 94.5% decline from the 2022 quarterly average of $4.7B, indicating the company's inability to meaningfully replace COVID revenue with other product sales.
