IQV collects cash from pharma clients upfront (deposit models) but pays researchers and trial site payments on net-60/90 terms. This creates a natural negative working capital that actually *generates cash flow*. This is a feature, not a bug.

Look at the cash position: **$1.81B and growing.** That's not the behavior of a company in distress.

**c) Debt service is well-covered:**

With $3.6B EBITDA and presumably $250-300M in annual interest expense, the interest coverage ratio is **12-14x**. That's fortress-level debt service capacity.

IQV has investment-grade ratings and access to capital markets. The debt maturity profile is laddered, and in a "no rate cuts" environment, the company locks in certainty (vs. a cutting cycle creating refi uncertainty).
