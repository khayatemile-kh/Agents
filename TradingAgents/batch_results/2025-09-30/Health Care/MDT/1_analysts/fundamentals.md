**Analysis Date: September 30, 2025**

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Medtronic plc (MDT) is a global leader in medical devices, headquartered in Ireland and traded on the NYSE. As of September 30, 2025, MDT presents a mixed fundamental picture characterized by steady revenue growth, solid profitability, but elevated leverage and moderate valuation concerns. The company maintains a strong dividend yield of 3.13% and demonstrates resilient cash flow generation, though recent quarterly performance shows some working capital challenges.

**Company Name:** Medtronic plc  
**Ticker:** MDT  
**Exchange:** NYSE (NYQ)  
**Sector:** Healthcare  
**Industry:** Medical Devices  
**Market Capitalization:** $117.1 billion  

Medtronic is one of the world's largest medical technology companies, operating across multiple divisions including Cardiovascular Portfolio, Minimally Invasive Technologies, Restorative Therapies, and Diabetes. The company provides medical devices and therapies for cardiac conditions, chronic pain, movement disorders, cardiac arrhythmias, structural heart disease, and diabetes management.
