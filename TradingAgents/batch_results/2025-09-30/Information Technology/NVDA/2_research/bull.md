The answer is an emphatic **no**.

According to the data we have, NVIDIA's Q2 gross margin hit an astounding **72.4%**—remarkable for hardware. But this isn't because NVIDIA is charging unreasonable prices; it's because:

1. **Demand far exceeds supply** - Every hyperscaler (Microsoft, Google, Meta, Amazon, OpenAI) needs NVIDIA GPUs and has committed multi-year purchasing commitments
2. **No real competition exists at scale** - AMD's MI300X is a distant second, Intel's Gaudi chips are nascent, and custom silicon from hyperscalers won't ship in volume for 2-3 years minimum

The forward PE of **14.03x** on a 100%+ growth business is frankly, a gift. Compare this to:
