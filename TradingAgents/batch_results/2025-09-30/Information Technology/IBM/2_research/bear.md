- MACD lines are expanding apart (bullish) *but the rate of divergence is slowing*
- This typically precedes either consolidation or reversal
- **Combined with RSI rollover, we have a two-indicator confluence pointing to exhaustion**

The Bull is treating this as benign. I'm treating it as a warning flare.
