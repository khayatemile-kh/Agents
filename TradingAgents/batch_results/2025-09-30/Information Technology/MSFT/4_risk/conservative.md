## **The Bollinger Band Argument is Backwards—They're Actually Signaling Exhaustion**

The aggressive analyst claims that price approaching the upper Bollinger Band means "the next leg higher targets the band itself and beyond." This reveals a fundamental misunderstanding of how Bollinger Bands function in extended moves.

Bollinger Bands are volatility envelopes, not trend-following tools. When price approaches the upper band during a strong uptrend, yes, sometimes it breaks through. But here's what the aggressive analyst conveniently ignores: **price touching the upper band while RSI is still below 70 typically indicates the move is losing steam, not accelerating.**

Why? Because if momentum were truly strong, RSI would already be *approaching* overbought (70+) as price approaches the upper band. The fact that RSI is at 58 while price is near the upper band tells you that price has moved far faster than momentum indicators suggest is sustainable. That's a **warning sign**, not a continuation signal.

In practical terms: when price reaches an upper Bollinger Band but momentum oscillators haven't confirmed with overbought readings, the next move is typically **mean reversion back toward the middle band**—not breakout higher. The $503.95 middle band (20-SMA) is where this consolidates, not $517.
