Let's address the elephant: **Can Google be disrupted?**

**Market Reality:**
- **Google Search: 92% global search market share** (per industry estimates)
- **Google-owned properties: 88% of U.S. digital ad spend flows through Google ecosystems** (Search, YouTube, Display Network)
- **Switching costs are *structural*, not accidental:**
  - Users don't switch search engines because of inertia (habit, integration with Chrome/Android)
  - Advertisers can't leave because the ROI is unmatched; Google Ads' AI bid optimization targets users *at moment of purchase intent*
  - No other platform achieves this with comparable precision

**AI Threat? Not yet real.**

The bear will claim: *"ChatGPT threatens Google Search; GenAI will disrupt the search moat."*

**My counter-argument with data:**
- OpenAI/ChatGPT have NOT captured meaningful search traffic. Google Search volume remains stable at ~5.6 billion searches/day (2025 estimates).
- ChatGPT's business model is *fundamentally different*: subscription revenue ($20/month Premium). Google's model is *ad-supported*—which scales infinitely with traffic.
- **Crucially: Google is integrating AI *into* Search (Gemini, SGE in search results).** Users get AI-powered summaries *while remaining in the Google ecosystem*, where ads are served.
- Google's AI training data advantage is *durable*: 25+ years of search queries, user behavior, and feedback loops that OpenAI cannot replicate.

**Verdict: Search moat is STRONGER in an AI world, not weaker.**
