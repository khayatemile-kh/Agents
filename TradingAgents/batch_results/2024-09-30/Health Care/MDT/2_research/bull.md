### Inflation is Manageable, Not Punitive

The market is pricing in inflation trending toward **3.3%-3.4%**—moderate, not recessionary, not hyperinflationary. For Medtronic, this is actually *favorable*:

- **Pricing Power:** A 65.3% gross margin in medical devices reflects decades of patent protection, high switching costs, and customer lock-in. When inflation hits 3.3%, MDT raises prices 4-5%. Customers absorb it because they have no choice—you don't switch cardiac pacemakers or surgical systems because of a 2% price increase.
- **Input Cost Absorption:** Unlike discretionary consumer goods, medical devices can pass through cost inflation to hospitals, insurers, and ultimately patients. Medtronic has proven this repeatedly through multiple economic cycles.

So while a commodity producer or retailer squeezes margins in this environment, Medtronic *expands* them. That's structural advantage, not luck.
