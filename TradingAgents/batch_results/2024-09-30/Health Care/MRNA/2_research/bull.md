### 3. **The Capital Deployment Story: Moderna is Investing, Not Failing**

**The Bear Will Claim:** "Negative gross profit of -$1.471B and EBITDA of -$2.195B prove the company can't operate profitably."

Once the pipeline programs start generating revenue (2025-2027), the company can dial down discretionary R&D spend and see **massive operating leverage**.

**The Free Cash Flow Signal:**

Despite -$3.151B net income, Moderna generated **+$299.2M in free cash flow**. This is crucial because:
- FCF is harder to manipulate than net income
- FCF of +$299M while losing $3B on income basis means one thing: **the company's actual cash burn is much lower than headline losses**
- This suggests working capital management and potential one-time charges inflating the loss figure

**Translation:** The company is *not* going broke. It's investing strategically and still generating positive free cash.
