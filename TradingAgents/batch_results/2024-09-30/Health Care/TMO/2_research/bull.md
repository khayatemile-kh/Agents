### 5. **Market Position & Competitive Moat**

TMO doesn't compete on price. It competes on **platform stickiness and integratedness**.

**Here's the defensive moat:**

- **Installed base:** Once a hospital, pharma company, or research institution adopts TMO's lab platforms, switching costs are astronomical. Retraining staff, validating new instruments, updating protocols—it's not worth the hassle.
- **Consumables model:** The real profit engine is reagents, assays, and consumables—**recurring revenue streams** that customers must repurchase.
- **Brand trust:** In diagnostics, accuracy and reliability are non-negotiable. TMO's reputation is unmatched.
- **Regulatory relationships:** FDA clearances, CE markings, and clinical validation take years. This creates regulatory moats other companies can't easily vault.

This is why TMO can maintain a **41% gross margin** and a **15% net margin**. Competitors can't undercut pricing because TMO's solutions are embedded in mission-critical workflows.
