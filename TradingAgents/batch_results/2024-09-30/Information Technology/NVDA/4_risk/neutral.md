Recoveries typically run to one of three destinations: they retrace to the 50% Fibonacci level, they test the previous resistance level, or they fail and roll over. NVDA at $121.25 has retraced 82% of the decline, which actually puts it near the exhaustion point of a typical recovery rally.

So yes, the MACD is positive and expanding—but that's normal in recovery phases. The real question is whether NVDA continues through the previous consolidation resistance near $130-135 or whether it rolls over and retest the 50 SMA at $115.73.
