### Liquidity Assessment
The current ratio of 0.751 warrants attention. While the company generates strong free cash flow of $9.22 billion annually, the low current ratio suggests potential challenges in meeting short-term obligations without operational cash flow.

### Leverage Concern
The debt-to-equity ratio of 61.44x is exceptionally high and represents a significant risk factor. This likely stems from Adobe's acquisition strategy (notably the Figma acquisition discussions and prior major acquisitions) and reflects aggressive financial structuring. This elevated leverage in a rising interest rate environment could pressure profitability and financial flexibility.
