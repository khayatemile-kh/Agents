### **SECTOR-SPECIFIC CONSIDERATIONS**

DOW Inc. operates in the **Basic Materials / Chemicals** sector, which is inherently cyclical and sensitive to:

1. **Global Manufacturing Activity:** Slowing capex and industrial production in China and Europe would reduce demand for polyurethane, silicones, and specialty chemicals.
2. **Oil & Gas Price Environment:** Feedstock costs remain tied to crude and natural gas; elevated energy prices compress margins.
3. **Currency Headwinds:** A strong U.S. dollar (likely given higher-for-longer rates) reduces international competitiveness and reported earnings from overseas operations.
4. **Demand from Downstream Users:** Auto production, construction, consumer durables all affect chemical demand.
