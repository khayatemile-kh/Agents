## III. THE EBITDA ARGUMENT EXPOSES THE CORE WEAKNESS

The bull argues: *"EBITDA is $3.96 billion—that's real cash-generative capacity. EBITDA matters more than GAAP earnings."*

**I agree with the first part. I violently disagree with the conclusion.**

Yes, EBITDA of $3.96B is real. But here's what the bull *conveniently ignores*:

**With $3.96B in EBITDA and debt that's 111.91x equity, what is DOW's total debt?**

Let me estimate: With a market cap of $21.36B and a debt-to-equity ratio of 111.91x, **DOW's equity is roughly $0.19B and debt is approximately $21B+.** 

That means **DOW's debt-to-EBITDA ratio is roughly 5.3x to 5.8x.** For a cyclical chemical company, this is not just elevated—**it's in distress territory.** Investment-grade thresholds for cyclicals are typically 3-3.5x.

Now, let's trace the cash flow math:

- **EBITDA:** $3.96B
- **Less: Capex** (chemicals are capital-intensive): ~$800M-$1B annually
- **Less: Interest expense** (at 5%+ on $21B debt): ~$1.05B annually
- **Less: Taxes** (when earnings turn positive): ~$200-$300M

**You get to roughly $600-700M in free cash flow available for principal reduction and dividends.**

The bull cited $237.6M in positive FCF. **Let me explain why that's the real nightmare scenario:**

If DOW is only generating $237.6M in FCF while carrying $21B+ in debt at distress-level leverage ratios, **the company has ZERO margin for error.** Any operational weakness—a 2-3% demand decline, a surge in feedstock costs, a recession—and that FCF turns negative, forcing either:

1. **Dividend cuts** (the market hates this; stock craters 15-20%)
2. **Asset sales** (value-destructive; signals desperation)
3. **Debt restructuring/default** (equity holders lose everything)

The bull frames the dividend cut as a "temporary pain" that leads to recovery. **That's faith, not analysis.** In reality, cyclical chemical companies that cut dividends rarely recover to prior levels—they enter a slow decay where the business is starved of capital while servicing legacy debt.

**The EBITDA story doesn't save DOW. It condemns it.**
