But constructive technicals alone, absent confirmed operating inflection, are an insufficient basis to add risk given negative core EBITDA and an unresolved impairment pattern. The valuation "reset" argument for 40-60% upside from multiple expansion is speculative — it assumes normalized EBITDA of $1.5-2.0B that does not yet exist in the data, and depends entirely on cost-savings and volume-recovery catalysts that remain unconfirmed. Net: reduce weighting now to reflect real balance-sheet tail risk and unconfirmed structural share loss, while retaining a small residual position to participate if the turnaround catalysts prove out, consistent with the Research Manager's underweight framing rather than a full sell (cash generation and debt paydown provide some downside cushion) or a passive hold (which lacks explicit triggers per the neutral analyst's critique).
