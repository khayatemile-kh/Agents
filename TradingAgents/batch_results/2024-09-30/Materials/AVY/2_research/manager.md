Net assessment: the bull's profitability and technical arguments are real and support not exiting the position, but the bear's leverage and growth-assumption critiques are substantive enough that they cap upside conviction. Neither side lands a decisive knockout — the debt picture is ambiguous without confirmed net-debt/EBITDA or coverage figures, and the growth outlook is plausible but unproven given the macro uncertainty cited by both sides. Given the genuinely balanced nature of the disclosed evidence, a Hold — maintaining current exposure without adding or trimming aggressively — is the appropriate stance until either (a) confirmed leverage/coverage metrics de-risk the balance sheet story, or (b) an earnings print confirms or disproves the double-digit EPS growth trajectory.
