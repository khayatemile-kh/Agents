### II. Macroeconomic Context & Implications for ABNB

#### A. **Monetary Policy Environment: Rate Cut Expectations**

**Key Finding from Prediction Markets:**
- Markets are pricing in **93% probability of NO Fed rate cuts in 2026**, with deep liquidity ($8.16M volume) suggesting high confidence.
- The prediction market data available shows forward guidance is focused on 2026, indicating:
  - Near-term (Q4 2024) rates likely to remain elevated or move modestly.
  - Delayed rate relief could extend into 2025, keeping borrowing costs elevated.

**Implication for ABNB:**
- **Negative Impact on Consumer Discretionary Spending:** Higher interest rates reduce consumer disposable income via increased debt servicing costs (mortgages, credit cards). Travel & leisure are highly sensitive to rate environments.
- **Headwind for Platform Growth:** Higher financing costs may pressure consumer willingness to spend on short-term rentals vs. traditional hotels, especially in price-sensitive segments.
- **Extended Cost Pressures:** Hosts with mortgage debt face higher carrying costs, potentially increasing rental prices to compensate, which could compress demand.

#### B. **Interest Rate Market Structure**

The prediction markets reflect a **hawkish-to-neutral stance**, suggesting:
- Central banks globally are prioritizing inflation control over growth stimulus.
- The ECB is expected to raise rates further (100% probability of 25 bps increase in Sept 2026), and the Fed is expected to maintain a higher-for-longer policy.

**Implication for ABNB:**
- A persistent high-rate regime favors traditional, lower-cost accommodations over premium short-term rentals.
- Emerging market travelers (key to ABNB's geographic diversification) may face currency headwinds if US rates remain elevated, reducing international demand.
