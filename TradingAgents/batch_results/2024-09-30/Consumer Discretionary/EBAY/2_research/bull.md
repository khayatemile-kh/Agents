- **Why high leverage?** Because marketplace businesses *can* sustain leverage. Unlike capital-intensive manufacturers, eBay converts revenue to cash efficiently. Wall Street capital structure theory says: use leverage when your business generates stable, predictable cash flows. eBay qualifies.

**Analogy:** High leverage on a stable, cash-generative business is different from high leverage on a volatile, asset-heavy business. eBay is the former.
