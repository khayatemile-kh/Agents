## Comprehensive Technical Analysis Report for EBAY (eBay Inc.)

---

### Executive Summary

EBAY is displaying **strong bullish momentum** as of September 30, 2024, with the stock posting a **+15.3% gain** over the last month (from $54.83 on 2024-08-19 to $63.28 on 2024-09-30). The stock has broken above key moving averages and is trading near overbought conditions, signaling potential pullback risks despite the powerful uptrend. Multiple technical indicators confirm the strength of the move, though caution is warranted at current price levels.

### Detailed Market Analysis

The price action reveals a **powerful uptrend across all timeframes**:

- **Long-term trend (200-SMA):** At 49.44, price is **+27.9% above the 200-day SMA** (as of 2024-09-30), indicating EBAY is in a sustained bull market with strong fundamental momentum.
- **Medium-term trend (50-SMA):** At 56.89, the 50-SMA is being used as a **dynamic support level**. Price closed at 63.28, which is **+11.2% above this critical average**, confirming the uptrend is intact.
- **Short-term trend (10-EMA):** At 62.02, the 10-EMA serves as a **short-term momentum driver**. Price at 63.28 sits +2.0% above this level, showing the uptrend remains in control but is becoming extended.

The **gap between moving averages** (10-EMA → 50-SMA → 200-SMA all in ascending order) is a textbook bullish alignment, confirming the hierarchical uptrend structure.
