Let me address the elephant in the room: **Aptiv's debt load.** The balance sheet shows $6.79B in total debt, which on first glance seems heavy for a ~$9.5B market cap company. **But this analysis misses the critical point:**

| Metric | 2023 | 2022 | Improvement |
|--------|------|------|-------------|
| **Net Debt** | $4.57B | $4.94B | **-$370M (-7.6%)** |
| **Operating Cash Flow** | $1.896B | $1.263B | **+$633M (+50.1%)** |
| **Free Cash Flow** | $990M | $419M | **+$571M (+136.3%)** |

The company is **generating nearly $1B in annual FCF and using it to reduce debt and return capital to shareholders** ($398M in buybacks in 2023).

#### **The Path to Sub-1.0x Net Debt/EBITDA is Clear**

At current FCF run rates ($1.6B+ annually), Aptiv can reach **1.0x net leverage within 12-18 months** while maintaining current capex and shareholder returns. This is a **peer-group best-in-class profile** and dramatically de-risks the equity.

**Why does this matter for stock price?** Credit rating agencies reward companies achieving sub-1.0x leverage with rating upgrades, which:
