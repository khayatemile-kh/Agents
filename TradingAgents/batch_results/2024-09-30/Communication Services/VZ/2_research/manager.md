Neither side fully invalidates the other. But the bear's capex/growth skepticism and technical exhaustion signals argue against adding aggressively at current levels near resistance ($40-40.45) with an elevated RSI. The prudent synthesis: this is a name to hold for income and stability, but not one to chase or aggressively add to right into resistance with decelerating volume. The lack of clear structural growth catalyst caps upside optionality, while the cash flow and dividend framework caps downside — that combination points to a balanced/neutral stance, tilted slightly defensive given the technical exhaustion signals are more immediately actionable than the bull's "breakout" thesis.

Net call: Hold. This is a case where the balance of evidence doesn't decisively favor either accumulating aggressively or exiting — it favors maintaining position size and letting the dividend do the work while monitoring the capex/growth trajectory and technical follow-through.
