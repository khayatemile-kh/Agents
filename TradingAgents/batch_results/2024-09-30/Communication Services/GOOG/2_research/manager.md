Net: the bear raises legitimate risks (Search disruption tail risk, rate-driven capex hurdle economics, cloud/YouTube margin mix) that argue against a maximum-conviction 'Buy' and argue for disciplined position sizing and monitoring — but they don't overturn the combination of dominant profitability, multiple monetization channels for AI, and a confirmed technical uptrend.
