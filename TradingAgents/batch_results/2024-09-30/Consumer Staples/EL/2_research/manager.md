Put together, the bull thesis rests on optimistic extrapolation (margin normalization, continued RSI/MACD momentum, debt being 'manageable') without a clear catalyst, while the bear thesis is grounded in the same data set showing distributional/divergence characteristics in the technicals and unresolved profitability/leverage issues in the fundamentals.
