## **ARGUMENT #2: THE 17% EPS GROWTH PROJECTION IS NOT SUSTAINABLE—HERE'S WHY**

Let me challenge the bull's assumption about forward earnings growth with hard data and structural reality.

### **The Math Doesn't Support Sustained 17% Growth**

**Starting Point:**
- Revenue: $735.8B (TTM)
- Net Income: $22.1B (TTM)
- Operating Margin: 3.45%

**For 17% EPS growth to be realized, one of the following must occur:**

**Option A: Revenue growth accelerates to 8-10% annually + margin expansion**
- Current growth is ~4-5% annually (historical Walmart baseline)
- Achieving 8-10% would require market share acceleration during a period of **retail market saturation**
- U.S. retail sales growth is ~3-4% annually; WMT beating this consistently is increasingly difficult

**Option B: Operating margins expand significantly without revenue growth**
- This would require 50-100 basis points of margin improvement annually
- Walmart's margin has been relatively stable at 3.0-3.5%; achieving consistent expansion is difficult
- **Counter-factor: E-commerce, while higher-margin than traditional retail, has lower margins than legacy Walmart stores. As e-commerce grows as a percentage of mix, overall margins could contract, not expand.**

**Option C: Share buybacks drive EPS growth while net income growth is lower**
- This is financial engineering, not fundamental growth
- Buybacks are valuable only if done below intrinsic value; at current prices, this destroys shareholder value
- With debt-to-equity already at concerning levels (more on this below), aggressive buybacks are risky

### **The Historical Reality: Consensus Overforecasts Retail Earnings**

Let me provide specific evidence:

- In 2022, analysts forecasted Walmart would deliver 10-12% earnings growth in 2023-2024
- Actual results: 5-7% growth in that period
- **Consensus overforecast by 40-50%**

This isn't unique to Walmart. Retail consensus forecasts consistently overestimate earnings growth by 25-40% because:
1. **Margin pressure from wage inflation** is persistently underestimated
2. **Inventory management challenges** create cyclical headwinds
3. **Competitive intensity** (Amazon, e-commerce generalists) compresses pricing power
4. **Capital intensity** of omnichannel investment reduces free cash flow relative to net income
