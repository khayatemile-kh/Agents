### **4. SECTOR DYNAMICS & CONSUMER OUTLOOK**

**Consumer Defensive Strength:**
- PepsiCo's classification as Consumer Defensive positions it favorably in a moderately uncertain environment.
- The current prediction market setup (moderate inflation, no imminent recession signal) suggests consumer staples remain steady performers.

**Potential Headwinds:**
1. **Consumer Price Sensitivity:** If higher inflation persists in specific categories (energy, transportation), lower-income consumers may trade down to private label or reduce discretionary snacking.
2. **Input Cost Pressures:** Agricultural commodities and packaging remain subject to geopolitical and seasonal volatility.
3. **Valuation Compression Risk:** If interest rates stabilize higher than pre-pandemic norms, dividend-paying stalwarts like PEP may face multiple compression.

2. **Margin Recovery:** If input costs flatten or decline, margin expansion becomes achievable.
3. **Portfolio Diversification:** Beverages + snacks + emerging market exposure provide multiple growth levers.
