- **32% probability of 3.3% annual inflation**

**What This Means for PEP:**
In a **moderate inflation environment (3.3-3.4%)**, PEP thrives:

1. **Inflation High Enough to Justify Price Increases** — PEP's pricing power is *validated*. When inflation is 3-3.5%, competitors also raise prices, so PEP doesn't stand out as a "price gouger."

2. **Inflation Low Enough to Protect Margins** — Input costs (sugar, aluminum, transportation) are no longer accelerating. This means **margin expansion** as pricing benefit exceeds cost increases.

3. **Consumer Remains Resilient** — Beverages and snacks are staples. Even if wage growth lags inflation, consumers still buy Pepsi and Lay's. This is *defensive* earnings.

**The Alternative (Bear Scenario):** If inflation resurged above 4%, PEP might face cost pressures and consumer pushback on prices. **But prediction markets price this at just 1% probability.** We're not betting on a tail risk; we're betting on the base case.
