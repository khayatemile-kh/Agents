In professional trading, declining volume on lower prices is a sign of **selling exhaustion, not capitulation acceleration.** If big institutional money were exiting, we'd expect sustained volume spikes, not declining volume. Instead, we're seeing consolidation—which is a bullish setup, not a bearish one.

If PEP were truly at risk of breaking below the 200 SMA, we'd expect:
- Accelerating volume on downside moves
- Negative catalysts in the news or earnings pipeline
- Deterioration in the macroeconomic backdrop
- Signs of demand destruction in the core business

We have none of these. We have technical weakness in an otherwise fundamentally sound business trading in a stable macro environment with no negative catalysts on the horizon.
