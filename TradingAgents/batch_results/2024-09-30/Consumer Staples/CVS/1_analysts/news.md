### III. SECTOR-SPECIFIC HEADWINDS & OPPORTUNITIES

#### **Headwinds:**
1. **Pharmacy Margin Compression** — Ongoing industry-wide reimbursement pressure from PBM consolidation and Medicare negotiated drug pricing.
2. **Healthcare Plans Uncertainty** — CVS's Aetna health insurance division faces ACA deductible/premium pressures and potential changes under 2025 administration.
3. **Retail Pharmacy Commoditization** — Competition from Amazon Pharmacy, Walmart, and telehealth threaten CVS's retail footprint despite its convenience.
4. **Labor Cost Inflation** — Wage pressures remain acute in healthcare.

#### **Opportunities:**
1. **Integrated Healthcare Model** — CVS's clinic + pharmacy + insurance convergence creates defensive barriers if executed well.
2. **Digital Transformation** — Investment in telehealth and digital health records positions CVS for long-term healthcare delivery shifts.
3. **GLP-1 Drug Distribution** — Growing demand for obesity/diabetes medications (Ozempic, Wegovy, etc.) via pharmacies is a tailwind for pharmacy volumes.
