Netting it out: the bull's cheapness argument is really a leverage/solvency risk discount hiding in plain sight, the dividend "coverage" argument doesn't survive contact with the actual free cash flow statement, and the technical setup is unconfirmed by fundamentals or catalysts. The bear's arguments are more rigorous, more evidence-based, and directly rebut the bull's key claims rather than talking past them. This tips the scale toward caution rather than balance — this isn't a "both sides have equally strong cases" situation.
