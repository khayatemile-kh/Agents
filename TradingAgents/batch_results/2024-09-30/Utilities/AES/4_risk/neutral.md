1. Utility earnings are highly stable and predictable (regulated, long-term contracts)
2. Revenue visibility extends 10-20 years into the future
3. Asset bases are hard assets with regulatory protections
4. Credit rating agencies understand utility cash flow dynamics and don't apply the same pressure as they would to cyclical businesses
