3. **Liquidity Concern:** Current ratio of 0.968 (below 1.0) indicates short-term liabilities exceed short-term assets, suggesting potential cash flow management issues.

4. **Below-Average Beta Adjustment:** While 1.173 beta indicates market correlation, combined with leverage, this could translate to amplified downside risk in market downturns.

5. **Premium Valuation (TTM):** Current P/B of 5.98x and P/E of 31.16x are elevated compared to many utilities, leaving limited margin for disappointment.
