- But has negative FCF of $6.15B

The aggressive analyst says the regulated return framework "guarantees" cost recovery—but that's not quite right. Regulators *allow* cost recovery *up to* a regulatory return. PCG is essentially betting that regulators will keep the cost-recovery spigot open wide enough to service both old debt and new capex while funding a 1.4% dividend. That's not a certainty. That's a bet.
