#### **Regulatory Environment**

Regulated utilities operate under state and federal oversight, where:
- **Rate Recovery**: Utility operating costs, capital investments, and financing costs are typically recovered through regulated rates approved by state public utility commissions (PUCs).
- **Capital Intensity**: Utilities like EXC require continuous capital investment in aging grid infrastructure, renewable integration, and resilience—financed at prevailing market rates.
- **Political Risk**: Energy policy, climate mandates, and grid modernization priorities vary by state and federal administration, creating both opportunities (green investments) and risks (stranded assets, rate caps).
