### Business Overview

Lockheed Martin is one of the world's largest defense contractors, serving the U.S. Department of Defense, intelligence agencies, and international customers. The company operates across four primary business segments:

1. **Missiles and Fire Control** – Advanced weapons systems, precision munitions, and fire control solutions
2. **Rotorcraft** – Black Hawk, Apache, and other military helicopters
3. **Aeronautics** – F-35 fighter jets and other advanced aerospace platforms
4. **Space** – Satellites, space launch systems, and advanced space technologies

The company benefits from long-term government contracts, multi-year appropriations, and recurring production orders that provide revenue visibility and stability. International defense budgets and geopolitical tensions (particularly in Europe and Asia-Pacific) are driving sustained demand for LMT's products and services.
