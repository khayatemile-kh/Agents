**MACD is a lagging indicator. It follows price; it doesn't lead it.** Here's what actually happened:

1. **Price broke $100 on September 20** (led)
2. **MACD crossover occurred on September 23** (followed, 3 days later)
3. **The histogram widened because price continued rallying** (not because buying was confirmed)

More critically, look at what a **real** institutional accumulation signal should look like:

**That's not what happened with CHRW.**

Instead, price ripped 9.8% and MACD tagged along. This is textbook **momentum chasing by retail and short-covering**, not institutional accumulation.
