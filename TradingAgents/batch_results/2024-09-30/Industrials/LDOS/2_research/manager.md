**Recommendation**: Overweight

**Rationale**: Weighing both sides: the bull case rests on LDOS's cheap valuation (10.15x forward PE vs. peers at 12.8-14.2x), a de-leveraging balance sheet (net debt/EBITDA down to ~1.63x), a large working-capital swing (+$1.3B), and a bullish technical setup (golden cross, MACD positive, breakout above Bollinger band). The bear also makes a sharper structural point: total defense budget growth doesn't automatically translate into LDOS revenue growth, since faster-growing, higher-multiple defense-tech upstarts (Anduril, Palantir) are capturing the more innovative, higher-margin contract awards, while LDOS's single-digit revenue growth (7-8%) plus margin-driven EPS growth (19%) looks more like cost-cutting than genuine expansion. That's a legitimate concern about the durability of the growth narrative.

On balance, I think the bull's valuation and balance-sheet arguments are the stronger, more verifiable points — the peer multiple discount is real, the deleveraging trend is real and quantifiable, and technical momentum supports near-term price action. The bear's competitive-displacement argument (Anduril/Palantir stealing high-growth contracts) is directionally plausible but somewhat speculative without concrete backlog/contract-loss data; it's more a risk to monitor than a fully proven thesis. The impairment argument has some merit as a red flag on capital allocation, but a single one-time write-down from a past acquisition doesn't necessarily indict all future earnings power, especially since operating income and margins are still expanding organically. Net: the valuation gap versus peers is too wide relative to the modest, but real, execution risks the bear raises. I'd lean constructive but not aggressively bullish, given the legitimate uncertainty around long-term competitive positioning in the defense-tech shift toward newer entrants. This points to a moderate Overweight rather than a full-conviction Buy, since the bear's competitive/structural concerns are real enough to warrant sizing discipline rather than an all-in position increase.
