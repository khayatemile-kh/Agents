## COMPANY PROFILE & BACKGROUND

RTX Corporation is a premier diversified technology company serving the civil and defense sectors globally. The company operates through integrated systems providing:
- **Aerospace Systems** (aircraft systems, services, and supply chain integration)
- **Collins Aerospace** (aerospace systems and components)
- **Raytheon Technologies** (defense & space systems, intelligence, and surveillance)

The company is a critical supplier to major government defense budgets, commercial airlines, and original equipment manufacturers (OEMs) worldwide.
