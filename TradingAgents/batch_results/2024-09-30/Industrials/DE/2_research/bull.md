**This is NOT the same as an overleveraged manufacturer.**

**The Real Leverage Story:**

**Evidence This Isn't a Problem:**
If DE's leverage were genuinely dangerous, we would see:
- ❌ Dividend cuts or suspensions — *Not happening*
- ❌ Credit rating downgrades — *Not happening*
- ❌ Weakening interest coverage — *Coverage remains exceptional*
- ❌ Covenant violations or refinancing pressure — *None evident*

The $4.52B in annual free cash flow is more than sufficient to service debt, fund capex, and reward shareholders. The financing operations generate profitable spreads that offset borrowing costs.

**Bear counter-point anticipated**: "But rising interest rates could squeeze DE's financing margins!"
