### INDUSTRY & BUSINESS MODEL ANALYSIS

**Nasdaq's Primary Revenue Drivers:**

1. **Listing Services** (IPOs, capital raises)
   - **Beneficiary of**: Lower rates (↑ VC deployments, IPO activity), broader market breadth
   - **Risk from**: Persistent recession fears, tech funding winter

2. **Trading & Connectivity Services** (transaction fees, market data)
   - **Beneficiary of**: Higher volumes, volatility-driven derivative trading
   - **Risk from**: Consolidation in asset management, lower fee pressure

3. **Data & Analytics Services**
   - **Beneficiary of**: Institutional demand for market intelligence, ESG/factor data
   - **Risk from**: Competitive pressure from Bloomberg, Reuters, indie data providers

4. **Market Technology** (Nasdaq's proprietary platforms)
   - **Beneficiary of**: Trading technology demands, clearing/settlement upgrades
   - **Relatively stable** revenue stream
