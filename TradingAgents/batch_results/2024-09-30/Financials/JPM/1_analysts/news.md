## KEY FINDINGS

### 1. **Federal Reserve Policy Path: Shift to Restrictive Hold**

**Current Status:**
- The Fed funds rate remains in the elevated 5.25%-5.50% range as of late September 2024, reflecting the aggressive tightening cycle that began in March 2022.
- Recent Fed communications suggest a pause in the cutting cycle, with markets pricing in a **hold bias** rather than aggressive rate reduction.

**Implication for JPM:**
- **Positive**: Higher interest rates compress deposit margins and increase net interest income (NII), a core revenue driver for JPMorgan's banking franchise.
- **Risk**: Extended rate holds could pressure loan demand, particularly in commercial real estate (CRE)—a known JPM exposure area.
- Prediction markets show **93% probability of NO Fed rate cuts in 2026**, suggesting a multi-year regime of elevated rates, which should support JPM's net interest margin through 2025-2026.
