## THE CORE BULL THESIS: THREE PILLARS

### **PILLAR 1: RECESSION-RESISTANT RECURRING REVENUE MODEL**

Here's what many investors miss: **FDS doesn't sell transactional products—it sells indispensable utilities.**

FactSet's business model is built on **subscription-based SaaS recurring revenue** from asset managers, hedge funds, investment banks, and institutional buy-side firms. These aren't discretionary purchases that get cut during downturns. They're mission-critical infrastructure:

- **$1.8B+ annual recurring revenue (ARR)** locked in via multi-year contracts
- **85%+ gross margins** on a subscription model, meaning cash generation is highly resilient
- **Customer stickiness:** When a portfolio manager or research team has 500+ workflows built on FactSet terminals, switching costs are astronomical

**What does this mean in practice?** During the 2008 financial crisis, FDS revenue actually grew. During COVID, demand *accelerated*. In recessions, financial institutions may cut headcount, but they don't rip out their core analytics infrastructure—that would cripple their operations.

This is fundamentally different from, say, advisory fees or trading commissions. FactSet gets paid whether markets are booming or crashing.

## GROWTH CATALYSTS: WHERE'S THE UPSIDE?

You'll hear bears say "revenue growth is slowing" (5.6% YoY). That's fair on the surface. **But they're missing the catalysts that can re-accelerate growth:**

### **1. AI/Analytics Integration — Still Early Innings**

The financial services industry is in the early stages of AI adoption. FDS is perfectly positioned:

- **FactSet's "Insight Platform"** integrates generative AI into research workflows
- **Natural language processing** on unstructured data (earnings calls, news, research notes)
- **Predictive analytics** for portfolio optimization

The company is monetizing AI capabilities *incrementally* through:
- **Premium pricing** on new AI-enhanced modules
- **Wallet share expansion** within existing clients (AI tools drive stickiness + usage)
- **New customer segments** attracted to AI-forward solutions

This is a *multiyear* TAM expansion opportunity. If FactSet can capture even 5-10% of the AI-driven incremental demand in financial services, that's $500M+ in *new* revenue opportunity.

### **2. Emerging Markets & Developing Client Base**

FactSet has historically been strong in North America and Europe. **Asia-Pacific and emerging markets remain underpenetrated:**

- Growing wealth management in Asia
- Regulatory compliance requirements driving analytics demand in APAC
- Expansion of index-based investing globally

As these regions mature, FactSet's installed base can grow, and pricing can rise.

### **3. Alternative Data & Third-Party Integration**

FDS is increasingly integrating alternative data (satellite imagery, credit card transactions, web scraping) into its platform. This is a **high-margin bolt-on** that increases customer stickiness and command higher fees.

### **4. Consolidation of the Vendor Ecosystem**

The financial data space has historically been fragmented. FDS could consolidate smaller vendors, quickly integrating their data into the main platform. This would:
- Reduce customer switching costs (one-stop-shop)
- Improve margins (eliminate duplicate infrastructure)
- Accelerate revenue synergies
