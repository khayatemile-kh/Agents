## **Detailed Technical Analysis Report: HAL (Halliburton Company)**
**Analysis Date: September 30, 2024**

---

### **Executive Summary**

HAL presents a **bearish technical setup** with multiple warning signals indicating continued weakness through September. The stock has declined approximately **8.3% from its August 26 high of $30.19 to $27.68** as of September 30, breaking key moving average support and generating persistent negative momentum readings. While the stock shows minor stabilization at month-end, structural headwinds remain intact, and a meaningful recovery would require a decisive break above the 50-SMA resistance near $29.22.

---

### **Price Action & Trend Structure**

**Current Price Level:** $27.68 (September 30, 2024)

The monthly price action reveals a **clear downtrend with three distinct phases:**

1. **Early September Capitulation (9/3–9/13):** Price collapsed from $28.28 to $26.55, a sharp 6% decline, establishing a swing low and confirming a breakdown below the 50-SMA. Volume surged during this phase (11M+ shares on 9/11), signaling institutional selling.

2. **Mid-September Recovery Attempt (9/16–9/24):** Price recovered to $28.56 (+7.6% from the low), nearly reclaiming the 50-SMA. However, this bounce stalled at $28.56 on September 24—**a clear rejection of higher prices**—and failed to close above $28.61.

3. **Late September Retest & Consolidation (9/25–9/30):** After a violent reversal on 9/25 (from $28.45 to $27.51) and severe single-day decline on 9/26 (to $26.50), the stock recovered modestly to $27.68. This bounce shows exhaustion and remains well below both the 50-SMA ($29.22) and 200-SMA ($32.85).

**Structural Observations:**
- **Below all key moving averages:** Price is trading $1.54 (5.6%) below the 50-SMA and $5.17 (18.7%) below the 200-SMA.
- **Downtrend sequence intact:** 10-EMA ($27.60) < 50-SMA ($29.22) < 200-SMA ($32.85) confirms a **fully aligned bearish structure**.
- **Lower lows and lower highs:** The swing low of $26.50 (9/26) is below $26.55 (9/13), and the swing high of $28.56 (9/24) is below $29.07 (9/3), confirming a **downtrend in process**.
