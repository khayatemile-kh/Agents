- **Tech mega-caps at 25x forward P/E** trade on *compounding growth assumptions*. If those assumptions break, multiples collapse 40-50%. But the *growth rate itself can recover* if the competitive moat holds.
- **XOM at 14.9x forward P/E** trades on *commodity-driven cyclicality*. If demand is declining structurally, multiples don't just compress—they compress AND stay compressed because the growth rate is negative. There's no recovery bounce.
