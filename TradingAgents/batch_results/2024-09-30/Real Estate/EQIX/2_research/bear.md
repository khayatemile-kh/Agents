- For comparison, the S&P 500 median P/E is approximately **20-22x**
- EQIX is trading at **3x the market average multiple**

**What would justify a 3x multiple premium?** Extraordinary growth rates that justify extraordinary multiples. But here's the problem:

**EQIX's Revenue Growth is NOT Extraordinary—It's Ordinary:**
- TTM revenue growth: **12.7% YoY**
- The S&P 500 average growth rate: **7-10%**
- EQIX's growth premium: **~2-5%** over the market average

**This is not a 3x multiple justification.** Rule of thumb: A company's P/E multiple should roughly equal its expected growth rate (the PEG rule). By this standard:
- **Fair P/E for EQIX:** ~13x (based on 12.7% growth)
- **Actual P/E:** 67x
- **Overvaluation:** **~5.2x fair value**

**But Wait, What About Forward EPS Growth?**

The bull cites forward EPS of $18.69 vs. TTM EPS of $15.54—a **20% projected increase**. This sounds impressive until you realize:

1. **This is *consensus* guidance, not guarantee.** Consensus estimates are frequently wrong, especially for cyclical infrastructure companies.
2. **Even if achieved, 20% growth doesn't justify 55x P/E.** Using PEG: A company with 20% growth should trade at ~20x P/E, not 55x.
3. **The forward multiple already prices in perfect execution.** There's zero margin for error.

**The Reality Check:** The bull is asking us to believe that in a $102.87 **billion** market cap REIT, the market is somehow *undervaluing* the AI opportunity. That's absurd. Institutional capital has already fully priced in AI demand into EQIX's valuation. The stock has already had its run—**$720.62 to $1,128.68 (56% gain in 52 weeks)**—driven by exactly this thesis.

**We're not buying the opportunity; we're buying the hangover.**
