If EQIX is growing at roughly 20-25% (their claim), it should trade at 20-25x earnings, not 55-67x. The Aggressive Analyst says regulatory moratoria (73% Polymarket odds) create "scarcity value for existing capacity." If California, Texas, and Virginia suddenly restrict power consumption or zoning for data centers, existing operators like EQIX have to absorb those constraints too.
