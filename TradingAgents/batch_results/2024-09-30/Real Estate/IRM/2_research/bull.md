**Wrong.** Here's why that argument misses the forest for the trees:

**Net income is a GAAP accounting artifact heavily distorted by:**
- **Non-cash charges**: Depreciation and amortization totaled $793 million in 2023

**Operating cash flow, by contrast, is real dollars the business generated.** And IRM's operating cash flow **grew 20%**—a powerful signal that the underlying business is actually getting more efficient, not less.
