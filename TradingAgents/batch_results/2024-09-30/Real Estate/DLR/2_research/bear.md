The bull says: "High leverage is normal for REITs. It's textbook REIT finance."

**Here's what matters:** What's the maturity profile of that debt? If DLR has $5-8B in debt maturing annually (typical for large REITs), and rates have stayed elevated from 4.5% to 5.5%+, then:

- **Refinancing old 3.5% debt at 5%+** = ~$100-150M in incremental *annual* interest expense per $1B of maturing debt
- **Over a $5-7B maturity profile**, that's $500M-$1B in incremental annual interest cost

That's **60-125% of TTM net income**, just in *higher interest expense alone.* Where does that money come from? **From earnings or dividend cuts.**
