**Recommendation**: Underweight

**Rationale**: Both sides make valid points, but the bear case identifies more structurally durable concerns that outweigh the bull's short-term technical/cash-flow optimism. Key bear points that carry the debate: (1) operating margin actually contracted 110bps even as gross margin rose 40bps, meaning SG&A/cost discipline is deteriorating, not improving as the bull claims - this directly undercuts the bull's 'operating leverage is coming' thesis; (2) negative tangible book value (-$2.01B) combined with 242.93% debt-to-equity is a real balance-sheet fragility, not just an accounting footnote - it materially raises the risk of future impairments and leaves little cushion if brand values erode further; (3) the cash flow 'surge' is substantially explained by a one-time ~$800M inventory drawdown and a capex cut, both of which are non-repeatable and could reverse or signal weak reinvestment/demand rather than durable FCF improvement; (4) revenue decline, while modest in aggregate, is concentrated in China/Asia and wholesale channels per both sides' framing, suggesting mix deterioration that the bull's 'flat is fine' framing glosses over. The bull's rebuttal that impairments are 'non-cash and one-time' and that cash flow is healthy doesn't adequately address why operating margins are shrinking even with gross margin expansion - that's the crux: if the core business's expense structure is worsening on a comparable basis, the 'restructuring dividend' the bull expects in FY25+ is speculative, not evidenced yet in the FY2024 numbers. The technical picture is genuinely mixed (recovery bounce off Nov lows but renewed MACD bearishness and RSI at 47 in December) - not compelling enough on its own to offset the fundamental concerns. Given the balance-sheet fragility (negative tangible equity, high leverage) combined with margin deterioration despite gross-margin gains and reliance on non-recurring working-capital cash flow, the weight of evidence tilts bearish/cautious rather than genuinely balanced. This isn't a full conviction Sell because the bull does have legitimate points about cash generation turning positive and a technical bounce, and forced liquidation isn't imminent, but the risk/reward skews toward trimming exposure rather than adding or holding steady until there's clearer evidence of margin stabilization and China/wholesale channel bottoming.
