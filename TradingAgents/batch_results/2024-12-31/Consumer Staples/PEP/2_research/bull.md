## **4. DEFENSIVE POSITIONING IN UNCERTAIN MACRO TIMES**

Here's a fact buried in the technical analysis: **PEP's beta is 0.361**. That means it moves about one-third as much as the market. In a year of macro uncertainty (2025 recession fears, Fed policy confusion, geopolitical risks), this is *precisely* where defensive capital belongs.

### **The Dividend Aristocrat Advantage:**

PepsiCo has increased its dividend **50+ consecutive years**. This isn't marketing—it's a binding commitment. Current dividend yield of ~2.8-3.0% combined with historical annual increases of 5-7% provides:
- **Income cushion:** If the market tanks 20%, PEP dividend likely holds and grows
- **Valuation floor:** Dividend investors create a natural bid
- **Inflation hedge:** Dividend increases outpace inflation over multi-decade periods
- **Tax efficiency:** 60% of PEP's shareholder base is institutional (pensions, funds) attracted to dividend stability

**The bear will argue:** "But this is 2025—growth stocks are returning; why own a slow dividend payer?"

**My rebuttal:** That's a recency bias argument. If 2025 brings the recession that many economists fear, PEP's 0.36 beta becomes a feature, not a bug. And if growth resurges, PEP's operating leverage (16.8% margins, 5-7% earnings growth) will deliver solid capital appreciation anyway. **PEP wins in both scenarios.**
