Given genuinely balanced, well-evidenced arguments on both sides and a stock sitting right at technical support with mixed momentum signals, this is a case where the evidence doesn't yet tilt clearly enough to justify adding or cutting exposure — the appropriate stance is to hold the current position and wait for confirmation, either margin durability through a full cost cycle or clearer volume/share data, before shifting weight.
