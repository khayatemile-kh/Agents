## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

American Tower Corporation (AMT) is a leading Real Estate Investment Trust (REIT) specializing in wireless communications infrastructure. The company operates a portfolio of tower and antenna systems that serve as critical infrastructure for wireless communications providers globally. As of December 31, 2024, AMT demonstrates strong operational fundamentals with improving profitability, robust cash generation, and a significant dividend yield, though it carries notable leverage consistent with its business model.

### Core Operations
AMT owns and operates wireless communications towers and antenna systems. The company generates revenue through:
- Long-term tenant leases from wireless carriers
- Multi-tenant relationships on single tower structures
- International tower portfolio
- Network engineering and installation services
