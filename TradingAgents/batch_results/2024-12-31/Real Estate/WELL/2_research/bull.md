## III. VALUATION IS REASONABLE (NOT STRETCHED)

Here's where many bears get it wrong. They see the 106.87x trailing P/E and declare the stock overvalued. **That's a category error.**

**Why P/E is misleading for REITs:**

REITs must distribute 90% of taxable income, not GAAP earnings. Taxable income is *lower* than GAAP earnings because depreciation (non-cash) is added back. The relevant metric is **FFO (Funds From Operations)** and **AFFO (Adjusted FFO)**.

With normalized EBITDA of $2.85B and interest expense of $574.3M, we can estimate FFO conservatively at ~$2.3-2.5B annually. 

**At current market cap of $170.96B:**
- **Price-to-FFO: ~72x** (still looks high, BUT...)
- **Dividend yield: 1.44%** with a payout ratio of ~60-65% of FFO leaves room for 5-7% annual FFO growth
- **Implied FFO yield + growth = ~7-8% total return**, which is competitive for a low-volatility, dividend-paying healthcare REIT

Moreover, the company's price-to-book of 3.67x reflects investor confidence in management's *capital deployment capability*—management has proven it can acquire properties, improve operations, and generate returns exceeding cost of capital.
