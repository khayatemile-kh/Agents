### SECTOR HEADWINDS & TAILWINDS FOR PCG

#### **TAILWINDS:**
1. **Regulated Utility Model**: PCG's cost-plus regulatory framework allows recovery of operating costs + approved return on equity (ROE). Rising rates → regulators typically increase allowed ROE → higher earnings potential
2. **Defensive Demand**: Utilities perform well during economic uncertainty; stable dividend-focused investors seek PCG's yield
3. **Infrastructure Investment Tailwind**: California's green energy transition (grid modernization, wildfire hardening, EV charging) drives long-term rate base growth
4. **Debt Deleveraging**: PCG emerged from bankruptcy in 2020; ongoing debt reduction improves credit quality and reduces financial risk

#### **HEADWINDS:**
1. **Rising Refinancing Costs**: PCG has ~$60+ billion in debt; higher rates make new issuances and refinancing more expensive
2. **Regulatory Lag**: California CPUC rate adjustments have multi-year cycles; costs rise before rates adjust, creating near-term margin compression
3. **Wildfire Liability Risk**: California faces elevated wildfire seasons; PCG's mitigation spending continues (capex drag) but cannot eliminate tail risks
4. **Customer Affordability Pressure**: Rising electricity costs face political resistance; regulators may limit rate increases, constraining margin expansion
5. **Inflation Persistence**: Operating costs (labor, materials) remain elevated; regulatory recovery may lag
6. **Federal Rate Policy Uncertainty**: While 2026 looks hawkish, 2025 Fed decisions remain fluid—surprise cuts could compress yields and hurt equity valuations
