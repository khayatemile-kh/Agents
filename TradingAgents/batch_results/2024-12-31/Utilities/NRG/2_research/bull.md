## III. SECULAR TAILWINDS: THE GROWTH STORY

This is where the bull case becomes truly compelling. NRG isn't just recovering from temporary weakness—**it's positioned in one of the strongest structural growth markets in the economy.**

### Why Electricity Demand is About to Surge

**1. Data Center & AI Power Consumption**
- Major hyperscalers (Meta, Google, Microsoft, Amazon) are in a **capex arms race** to build data centers for AI training and inference
- Data centers consume **enormous amounts of baseload power**—they run 24/7/365
- NRG's dispatchable generation (conventional + battery storage) is exactly what these operators need
- **Electricity demand from data centers is projected to grow 15-20% annually through the decade**

**2. Electrification of Transportation**
- EV adoption is accelerating; every EV sold adds a persistent load for charging
- This creates **predictable, growing demand** for grid capacity
- Utilities and IPPs like NRG benefit directly

**3. Industrial Reshoring & Manufacturing Growth**
- U.S. manufacturers are reshoring production (chips, batteries, automotive) due to geopolitical and supply-chain concerns
- These facilities require **massive, stable power supply**
- The Inflation Reduction Act incentivizes this trend with tax credits

**4. Capacity Market Strength**
- As demand grows, **capacity prices rise**—this directly benefits NRG's contracted generation
- Current capacity prices are elevated and expected to remain strong
- This creates **visible, contracted revenue streams** that reduce earnings volatility

From the prediction markets data:

- **California: 10%** (declining risk)

What does this mean? **The grid is under stress, especially in the Central US.** When grids are stressed, **capacity is scarce, prices are high, and generators with reliable assets print money.** NRG has substantial exposure to these markets.
