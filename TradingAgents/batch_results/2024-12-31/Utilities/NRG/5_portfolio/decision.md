Second, and more decisive, is leverage. The 483x debt-to-equity ratio (equity of just $2.48B against ~$11B debt) with $996M of current debt due is the single most concrete, quantifiable risk in the debate. Given the earnings-quality uncertainty makes an EPS/EBITDA normalization plausible, the combination of thin equity cushion, near-term refinancing needs, and un-reconciled forward guidance argues for reducing exposure now rather than waiting for price confirmation.

The genuine secular tailwinds (grid tightness, data-center demand, capacity pricing) justify not going to an outright Sell, but the unresolved leverage and earnings-quality overhang — un-rebutted by the bull case — keeps this at Underweight, not Hold.
