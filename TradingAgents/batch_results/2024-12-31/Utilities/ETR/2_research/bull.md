2. **Debt Maturity Profile:** ETR's debt is long-dated (average maturity 10-12 years) with staggered maturities. Refinancing risk is minimal—there's always a receptive debt market for utilities with investment-grade ratings (ETR: Baa2/BBB).

4. **Interest Coverage:** ETR's EBITDA of $5.04B against interest expense of $1.15B yields **4.4x interest coverage**—well above the 3.0x minimum for investment-grade utilities. This is healthy.

**Verdict:** The leverage is manageable within the utility regulatory framework. It's not a concern.
