### **PILLAR 3: THE VALUATION IS ATTRACTIVE, NOT EXPENSIVE**

Here's where I need to push back on a potential bear argument before it's even made: 

**"CHRW is trading at 28.65x TTM P/E—that's expensive!"**

No, it's not. Here's why:

**Forward-looking valuation tells the real story:**

- **TTM P/E: 28.65x** — This *looks* high, but it's inflated by 2023's depressed $2.72 EPS
- **Forward P/E: 20.02x** — *This* is the real metric. At 20x, CHRW is trading **in line with or below** typical industrials and logistics peers
- **PEG Ratio: 1.80** — This suggests fair-to-reasonable valuation *relative to growth*

**Here's the kicker:** If earnings grow at just **10% annually** (a conservative assumption given the operational improvements), the forward P/E compresses to ~18x. That's *attractive* for a market leader in logistics.

And if management's cost discipline continues and freight rates stabilize or improve (as they historically do post-cycle), **20-25% earnings growth is entirely achievable**, which would justify a 22-25x multiple—implying **$115-130 per share within 12-18 months.**

**Current price of $99.98 implies only ~$3.86 * 1.05 = $4.05 forward EPS at the forward multiple.** That's *underestimating* growth.
