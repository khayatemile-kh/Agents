**Note:** Detailed FRED macro data was unavailable due to API configuration, but the following qualitative macroeconomic assessment is based on market knowledge as of December 31, 2024:

#### **Federal Reserve Policy & Interest Rates**
- The Federal Reserve has conducted multiple rate cuts in 2024 in response to moderating inflation
- Current fed funds target range: **4.25%-4.50%** (as of December 18, 2024 meeting)
- Market expectations: Additional rate cuts anticipated in 2025, though pace uncertain given inflation resilience in some sectors
- **Impact on ADP:** Lower rates reduce customer borrowing costs and support hiring activity (positive for payroll services demand)

#### **Inflation Trajectory**
- CPI has moderated from 2022-2023 peaks but remains above the Fed's 2% target
- Wage growth remains sticky, supporting payroll inflation (relevant to ADP's input costs and customer spend)
- **Impact on ADP:** Sustained wage inflation may increase customer payroll costs, potentially creating demand for ADP's cost optimization tools

#### **Labor Market**
- Unemployment remains relatively low (~4.2% estimated in recent data)
- Job creation has slowed but remains positive
- **Impact on ADP:** Stable employment supports continued demand for ADP's National Employment Report (major product) and payroll processing services

#### **10-Year Treasury Yields**
- Long-term rates have stabilized around 4.0-4.2% range
- Modestly higher yields support equity valuations for profitable tech companies
- **Impact on ADP:** Stable discount rates support fair value assessment; not a major headwind for recurring-revenue SaaS models
