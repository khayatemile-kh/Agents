## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Johnson Controls International plc is a global industrial conglomerate specializing in building products and equipment. The company shows moderate growth momentum with improving profitability, but faces challenges related to elevated leverage and capital-intensive operations. The stock is trading at a significant premium valuation (P/E of 41.23x TTM), suggesting market confidence in future earnings growth, yet the forward P/E of 24.19x indicates expected normalization.
