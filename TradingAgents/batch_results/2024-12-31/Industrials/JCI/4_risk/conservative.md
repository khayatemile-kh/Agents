The analyst treats the 40% band compression as if it's a coiled spring that must release upward. But it can release downward with equal force. In fact, during the December 18-19 decline, the bands were already contracted, and price *still broke lower*, reaching $77.26. The compression didn't provide support then. Why should we assume it provides support now?

Second, RSI at 40.88 is not a "sweet spot for mean reversion." That's folklore masquerading as technical law. RSI at 40 simply means the stock is neither oversold (<30) nor overbought (>70). It's neutral. And neutral conditions can persist for weeks or reverse sharply. More importantly, **RSI at 40 with price below the 50-day SMA ($79.76) is actually a bearish alignment**—the stock is technically weak with momentum deteriorating. The fact that we haven't reached true oversold conditions (RSI <30) suggests the selling pressure has been *controlled and deliberate*, not panicked. That's institutional distribution, not capitulation.
