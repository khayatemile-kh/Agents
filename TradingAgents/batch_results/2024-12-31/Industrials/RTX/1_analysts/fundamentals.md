## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

**RTX Corporation** is a major aerospace and defense contractor with a diversified portfolio spanning missile systems, avionics, propulsion, and advanced technology platforms. As of 2024-12-31, RTX demonstrates strong operational momentum with improving financial metrics, significant backlog growth, and robust defense spending tailwinds. However, the company operates with elevated leverage ratios and margin pressures that warrant close monitoring.

### Business Segments & Operations

RTX is a diversified aerospace and defense leader operating across four primary business segments:
- **Collins Aerospace:** Commercial aviation systems and components
- **Raytheon Missiles & Fire Control:** Advanced missile and fire control systems
- **Raytheon Intelligence & Space:** Advanced technologies and intelligence systems
- **RTX Technology Group:** Emerging technologies and R&D

The company benefits from strong secular growth drivers including:
- Rising global defense budgets (NATO expansions, geopolitical tensions)
- Increased military modernization spending
- Commercial aviation recovery and fleet expansion
- Hypersonic and advanced weapons development programs
