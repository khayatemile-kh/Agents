## The MACD Narrative is Dangerously Backwards

Here's where I fundamentally disagree with the aggressive interpretation. They say MACD bearish crossovers after overbought rallies are "reversal signals." That's oversimplified and dangerous.

Let me provide the actual technical reality: **MACD bearish crossovers *can* precede reversals, but only when they occur in conjunction with specific confirmatory signals.** Those signals include:

1. **Divergence patterns**: Price makes a lower low while MACD makes a higher low (classic bullish reversal setup). We don't have this. Price made a lower low at $415.02, and MACD also made a lower low (more negative). This is *confirming* weakness, not presaging reversal.

2. **RSI oversold territory**: We established RSI is neutral at 43.51. It would need to drop below 30 to show true capitulation reversal energy. **We're nowhere near that condition.** A neutral RSI combined with bearish MACD crossover is simply momentum continuing downward—not bottoming.

3. **Volume exhaustion**: The volume during the Dec 20 bounce was insufficient to generate sustained recovery. True capitulation reversals show massive volume on down days, followed by equally massive volume on reversal days. We've seen neither pattern.

The aggressive analyst is committing a classic error: **assuming that because the MACD *was* extremely bullish, it *must* now reverse to extremely bearish, and therefore extreme bullish again.** But that's not how technical analysis works. MACD can stay negative for weeks or months during a prolonged downtrend. The current reading of -0.80 with an expanding negative histogram is consistent with *continued downside*, not imminent reversal.

Let me be specific about the risk here: **if we break below the 50 SMA at 415.36, the next technical level is the Bollinger lower band at 408.77, and then the November support zone at 395-400.** That's a 5% further downside from current levels.
