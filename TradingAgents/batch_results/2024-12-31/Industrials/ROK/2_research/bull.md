## PART 1: THE CYCLE THESIS — ROK AT THE INFLECTION POINT

### The Current Reality: Temporary Cyclical Trough, Not Structural Decline

Let me be crystal clear about what we're looking at:

**FY 2024 was a demand-side recession in industrial capex.** This is documented. Manufacturing customers pulled forward orders in 2022-2023, then delayed 2024 spending to preserve cash in an uncertain interest rate environment. ROK bore the brunt because:

1. **Discretionary nature of automation:** Factories can defer upgrading control systems and automation infrastructure when cash is tight—it's not like their production lines stop running.
2. **Customer destocking:** The supply chain normalized, inventory normalized, and customers worked through excess stock purchased during the 2021-2022 boom.
3. **Margin compression from volume, not pricing power loss:** The 430-basis-point operating margin decline was volume-driven (revenue down 8.8% while fixed costs remained high), not a collapse in profitability per unit sold.

**But here's the bull thesis:** This was cyclical, not secular.
