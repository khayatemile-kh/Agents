**Critical Assessment:**
VZ's RSI has **crashed from overbought (>68) to oversold (<35)** in just 20 days. An RSI below 30 typically signals exhaustion and potential reversal. However, **this must be interpreted cautiously**:

1. **Bearish Context:** In strong downtrends, RSI can remain in the oversold zone (below 30) for extended periods without triggering reversals.
2. **No Bullish Divergence:** The recent RSI low (28.23 on 12/30) does NOT form a divergence with the price low (36.42 on 12/16). Price made a lower low while RSI was not as low, which would support a reversal. Instead, RSI is tracking price lower—a **bearish sign**.
3. **Volume Confirmation:** The peak volume spike on 12/20 (47.6M shares) coincided with price breaking through support, suggesting **capitulation selling** rather than recovery bouncing.

**Verdict:** While the RSI is technically oversold and a bounce is **possible**, the lack of divergence and the concurrent MACD weakness argue against an imminent reversal.
