The fundamental trajectory is **decelerating profitability growth**, not accelerating. The market is pricing in some of this reality already. ## THE TECHNICAL SETUP IS NOT AN ENTRY SIGNAL—IT'S A FRAGILE CONSOLIDATION

Now let me directly challenge the Aggressive Analyst's technical framework, because this is where the staged entry approach reveals its true risk.

The Aggressive Analyst claims the RSI at 44 is "perfect entry timing" because we haven't hit capitulation (RSI below 30). This is technically correct but strategically misleading. Here's why:

**The MACD histogram is at -3.92, having just crossed below the signal line.** In technical analysis, MACD crossovers are momentum *reversal* signals, not reversal *confirmation* signals. When the MACD histogram turns negative, it signals that momentum is shifting from positive to negative. This is a *lagging* indicator—it confirms what has already happened, not what will happen next.

The Aggressive Analyst's trade plan waits for "MACD cross back above signal line or RSI reclaim above 50" before adding the second 50% tranche. But here's the risk: **What if the stock breaks below the 50 SMA at $584.54 before either of those confirmations occur?**

**Downside Scenario (Days 1-10):**

1. Stock rallies intraday on January 2 opening (first tranche at $584.54 is entered)
2. Institutional selling resumes; volume spikes as traders return from holiday
3. Stock fails to hold the 50 SMA; breaks down to $575-578 range
4. RSI hits 38 but does not yet drop to 30 (capitulation threshold)
5. MACD remains negative; no cross back above signal line yet
6. Stop-loss triggers at $575, and the first tranche is exited with a 1.75% loss

**What has the firm gained?** A 1.75% loss plus opportunity cost (the capital that was deployed could have remained dry until true capitulation or a clearer reversal confirmation).

The Aggressive Analyst will respond: "But the long-term fundamentals are still intact, so the stop is too tight." This is precisely the problem with conflating fundamental strength with near-term entry timing. **Strong fundamentals do not prevent 10-15% pullbacks.** In fact, strong-fundamentals pullbacks often become the most painful to traders because the false narrative of "buy the dip" causes position averaging-down into deteriorating technicals.
