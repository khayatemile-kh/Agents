Overall, the bear's arguments about margin compression, low-quality cash flow (working-capital driven), and peer-relative overleverage are more rigorously evidenced and more directly tied to forward fundamentals than the bull's oversold-bounce narrative and cross-sector D/E comparison. That tips the balance toward caution: this is not a strong "Sell" the fundamentals aren't collapsing outright (equity still growing, cash position up, dividend intact) but the risk/reward skews unfavorably enough versus the bull's optimistic assumptions that maintaining or adding to a full position isn't warranted. Given genuinely mixed signals—real cash generation and balance sheet resilience on one side, margin compression and low-quality FCF plus peer-relative overleverage on the other—the prudent stance is to trim exposure rather than aggressively add or hold at current levels awaiting confirmation that margins stabilize and working-capital-driven FCF proves durable.
