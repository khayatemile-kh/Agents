## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Tesla, Inc. (TSLA) is one of the world's leading electric vehicle manufacturers and energy storage companies. As of December 31, 2024, Tesla operates as a Consumer Cyclical company within the Auto Manufacturers industry, trading on the NMS exchange with a market capitalization of approximately **$1.45 trillion**. The company is undergoing a critical juncture marked by moderating growth, margin compression, and increased competitive pressures in the EV market.
