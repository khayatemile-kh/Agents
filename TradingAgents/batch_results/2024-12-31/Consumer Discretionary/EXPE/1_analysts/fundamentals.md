## Analysis Date: 2024-12-31

---

## **EXECUTIVE SUMMARY**

Expedia Group, Inc. (EXPE) is a leading global online travel services company operating in the Consumer Cyclical/Travel Services sector. As of 2024-12-31, the company demonstrates strong financial recovery post-pandemic with significant revenue growth, improving profitability, and robust cash generation. The company shows momentum heading into 2025, though elevated leverage and negative working capital warrant attention.

---

## **COMPANY PROFILE & BUSINESS OVERVIEW**

**Company Name:** Expedia Group, Inc.  
**Ticker:** EXPE  
**Exchange:** NASDAQ (NMS)  
**Sector:** Consumer Cyclical  
**Industry:** Travel Services  
**Market Capitalization:** $32.95 billion

### **Business Model & Operations:**
Expedia operates a diversified online travel platform ecosystem that includes:
- Expedia.com (accommodation, flights, car rentals, packages)
- Hotels.com
- Vrbo (vacation rental homes)
- Booking.com integration and partnerships
- Cruises, activities, and experiences
- Egencia (corporate travel management)

The company generates revenue through commission-based bookings, hotel accommodation merchant model, and ancillary services. It operates globally with exposure to multiple currencies and travel markets.

**Current Assets (2024):** $9.82B  
**Current Liabilities (2024):** $13.61B  
**Current Ratio:** 0.72 (concerning level)  
**Working Capital:** -$3.80B (deteriorated from -$2.55B in 2023)

**Cash Position:**
- Cash and Short-term Investments: $4.48B
- Restricted Cash: $1.39B
- Operating Cash Flow: $3.09B

**Key Concerns:**
- Working capital deficit has worsened, primarily due to current deferred revenue of $8.68B (advance customer bookings—a liability but also a cash-generating asset)
- Current ratio below 1.0 is typical for travel booking platforms due to the prepaid nature of the business model
- However, the negative working capital position should be monitored closely

**Mitigating Factors:**
- The $8.68B current deferred revenue represents customer prepayments and is ultimately converted to cash
- Strong operating cash flow generation of $3.09B demonstrates the business model's cash conversion capability
