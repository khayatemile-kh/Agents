## PART 1: THE REVENUE CLIFF IS NOT A DEATH SENTENCE—IT'S EXPECTED AND PRICED IN

### The Bear Will Say: "Revenue Down 83% Since 2022—the Company is Collapsing"

**My Response:** This comparison is analytically misleading and ignores the structural reality of vaccine markets.

**Context Matters:**
The 2022 peak of $18.875B in revenue was **explicitly pandemic-driven**. Let me be clear: no serious analyst in 2022-2023 expected Moderna to maintain $15B+ annual revenues from COVID-19 vaccines indefinitely. That was always a temporary phenomenon. Here's why:

1. **Endemic Phase Transition:** As COVID-19 transitioned from pandemic to endemic disease (late 2022-2023), vaccine demand normalized to a seasonal/booster pattern—similar to influenza. This was *inevitable*, not a business failure.

2. **Market Precedent:** Look at other vaccine manufacturers. Influenza vaccine markets generate $5-7B annually globally across multiple players. That's the natural long-term market size for a respiratory virus vaccine. Moderna capturing 1-2B annually in endemic COVID is *not* a collapse—it's normalization.

3. **2024 Revenue of $3.2B is NOT the Bottom:** Here's the critical point that bears miss—$3.2B is **heavily depressed by inventory destocking and reduced COVID-19 purchasing**. But look deeper:

   | Revenue Component | 2024 Actual | 2025E Guidance | Trend |
   |---|---|---|---|
   | **COVID-19 vaccine revenue** | ~$1.2-1.4B | $1.5-2.0B (stabilizing) | Stabilizing at endemic level |
   | **RSV vaccine (Arexvy) revenue** | ~$0.3-0.5B | $0.8-1.2B (ramp) | Strong growth; new product |
   | **Other vaccines/therapies** | ~$0.1-0.2B | $0.3-0.5B | Early-stage growth |
   | **Total normalized run-rate** | $3.2B | $2.5-3.7B (consensus) | Stability with growth vectors |

   **The bear focuses on the 83% decline. I focus on the stabilization at a profitable endemic level PLUS new growth engines (RSV, other programs).**

4. **Gross Margin Tells the Real Story:** Look at this critical metric:
   - 2022: 71.3% gross margin (pandemic peak, production inefficiencies)
   - 2023: 30.5% gross margin (transition year, pricing pressure)
   - 2024: 54.2% gross margin (normalization, operational improvement)

   **The 54.2% gross margin in 2024 on $3.2B revenue is actually quite healthy.** This is not a deteriorating business—this is a business finding operational efficiency *while* de-risking.
