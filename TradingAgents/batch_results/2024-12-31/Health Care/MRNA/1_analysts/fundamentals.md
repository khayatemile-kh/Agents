**Analysis Date: December 31, 2024**

---

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Moderna, Inc. (MRNA) is a leading biotechnology company specializing in mRNA-based therapeutics. As of December 31, 2024, the company is experiencing significant financial distress characterized by substantial operating losses, deteriorating revenues, and negative cash flow. While the company maintains a solid balance sheet with $7.025 billion in cash and short-term investments, the dramatic decline in vaccine revenues post-pandemic has severely impacted profitability and cash generation.

**Company Name:** Moderna, Inc.  
**Ticker:** MRNA  
**Exchange:** NMS (NASDAQ)  
**Sector:** Healthcare  
**Industry:** Biotechnology  
**Market Capitalization:** $56.02 billion (as of analysis date)

### Business Model
Moderna is a clinical-stage biotechnology company focused on developing therapeutic medicines and vaccines using messenger RNA (mRNA) technology. The company's pipeline includes vaccines and immunotherapies for infectious diseases, oncology, and rare diseases. Primary revenue sources historically came from COVID-19 vaccines (Comirnaty/Spikevax) and seasonal flu vaccines, with expanded development in RSV and other therapeutic areas.
