Net: the bear's near-term operational and valuation critique is more evidence-based than the bull's long-horizon demographic narrative, but the balance sheet strength and cash generation argue against an outright Sell. This nets to a cautious/trim stance rather than balanced Hold — the stalled top line combined with a premium multiple and leverage still above peer average tips the scale toward reducing exposure rather than adding or holding pat.
