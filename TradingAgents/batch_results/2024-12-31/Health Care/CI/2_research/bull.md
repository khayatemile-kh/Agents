### B. PEG Ratio of 0.77: The Ultimate Value Signal

The PEG (Price/Earnings-to-Growth) ratio is calculated as:
```
PEG = P/E Ratio / Expected Earnings Growth Rate
```

A PEG below 1.0 classically indicates undervaluation. CI's PEG of 0.77 signals that:

- The market is pricing in *zero* growth or *negative* growth expectations
- Even modest earnings recovery (5-10% annual growth) would warrant significant re-rating

Given that CI is growing revenue at 26.8% and operating income at 14.5%, a PEG of 0.77 is frankly absurd. This is the market panic-pricing in a depression scenario that the fundamentals don't support.

At 1.71x P/B, the market is pricing in significant writedowns or value destruction. For a company generating $8.96B in free cash flow and $8.44B in operating income, this is overly pessimistic.
