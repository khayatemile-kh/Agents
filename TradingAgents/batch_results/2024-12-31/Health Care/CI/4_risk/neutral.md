## Where the Aggressive Case Breaks Down Under Scrutiny

The Aggressive Analyst argues that a forward P/E of 8.24x doesn't matter because "if the underlying business is genuinely deteriorating, the stock can stay oversold by historical standards and still have significant downside." 

But here's the logical flaw: **a forward P/E of 8.24x already embeds an assumption about deterioration.** The forward multiple isn't calculated in a vacuum. It's the market's consensus forecast for 2025 earnings divided into current price. If analysts are forecasting $33.49 in EPS for 2025 and the stock is trading at $267.55, that's an 8.24x multiple. That means the market is already pricing in either significant earnings recovery *or* significant margin compression continuing.

The question then becomes: is the market right or wrong about 2025? The Aggressive Analyst doesn't engage with this question. They just assume deterioration will persist. But that's an assumption, not a fact. The market might be pricing in a return to normalized margins as integration headwinds ease. Or it might be pricing in continued compression. We don't know yet.

What we *can* know is when Q4 2024 earnings are released. That's when the fog clears.
