## Third: The Balance Sheet "House of Cards" Argument Misrepresents Insurance Industry Norms

Insurance companies collect premiums upfront (current assets/cash inflow) but pay claims over time (current liabilities). This creates a natural "float"—the difference between cash collected and cash paid out. That float is *free capital* that funds operations. A current ratio below 1.0 in an insurance company is completely normal and expected. Look at UnitedHealth Group, Anthem, or Humana—they all operate with current ratios below 1.0 because the float model is integral to their profitability.
