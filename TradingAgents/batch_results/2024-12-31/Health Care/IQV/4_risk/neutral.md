The fact that cash flow is still accelerating offsets some of this concern, but it doesn't eliminate it. So my challenge to you both: stop treating working capital as either "nothing to see here" or "time bomb." It's neither. It's a secondary indicator of demand softening, and we should weight it accordingly.
