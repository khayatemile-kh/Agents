More critically, you're treating the 8% recession probability from prediction markets as if it's a reliable floor on downside risk. It's not. Prediction markets are extraordinarily efficient at pricing in *base case* scenarios and terrible at flagging inflection points. The moment we see two weeks of softening labor data or a noticeable tick up in credit card charge-offs, that 8% number reprices to 15-20% almost overnight. And when it does, financial stocks don't hold steady—they gap down. You're building a strategy on the assumption that economic conditions remain static. They won't.
