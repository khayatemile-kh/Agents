## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Robinhood Markets, Inc. (HOOD) has undergone a significant operational and financial transformation, demonstrating a remarkable turnaround from losses to profitability. The company has shifted from a growth-at-all-costs strategy to a more disciplined, profitable business model. As of December 31, 2024, HOOD is trading at $114.28 with a market capitalization of approximately $102.6 billion, reflecting substantial investor confidence in its recovery and future prospects.
