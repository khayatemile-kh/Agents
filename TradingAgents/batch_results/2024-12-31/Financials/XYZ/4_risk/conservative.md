For comparison, traditional financial services companies often operate at 5-10x leverage. Capital-intensive infrastructure companies operate at similar or higher multiples.

More importantly, what matters for conservative risk management is not the leverage ratio itself, but the company's ability to service debt under stress scenarios. Let me look at the actual metrics:

- Block has $12.26B in cash and short-term investments
- Net debt is approximately -$4.34B (negative debt, meaning the company is a net creditor)
