**Analysis Date: December 31, 2024**

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Moody's Corporation is a leading global rating agency and provider of financial intelligence and analytical tools. Operating in the Financial Services sector as a Financial Data & Stock Exchange provider, MCO has demonstrated robust growth trajectory through 2024, with strong revenue expansion, margin improvement, and enhanced cash generation. The company maintains a market capitalization of approximately $81.5 billion, positioning it as a dominant player in credit rating and financial analytics.

---

## COMPANY PROFILE & BUSINESS OVERVIEW

**Company Name:** Moody's Corporation
**Ticker:** MCO
**Exchange:** NYQ (NYSE)
**Sector:** Financial Services
**Industry:** Financial Data & Stock Exchanges
**Market Capitalization:** $81.5 billion

**Business Model:**
Moody's operates primarily through two segments:
1. **Moody's Investors Service (MIS)** – Provides credit ratings and research on debt securities
2. **Moody's Analytics (MA)** – Delivers software, analytics, risk assessment, and data services

The company serves financial professionals, corporations, government agencies, and investors globally, generating revenue through subscription-based analytics platforms, rating fees, and transaction-based revenue.
