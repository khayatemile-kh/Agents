## THE VOLUME ANALYSIS IS BACKWARDS

The aggressive analyst claims that "volume on down-days exceeding accumulation volume is evidence of panic selling by weak hands, not institutional distribution." This is exactly backwards from what disciplined risk managers should conclude.

**When down-volume exceeds up-volume during a consolidation, that's institutional distribution.** That's literally the definition. Weak hands panic-sell in 30-minute bursts on bad news. Institutions distribute over weeks, using strength to unload size, and then using weakness to manage their exit so they don't crater their own exit price.

The fact that support held at the 200-SMA doesn't prove accumulation—it proves that the selling exhausted itself at a technically significant level. That's *normal.* But now we're in a phase where institutional sellers have exhausted their buying pressure, and the stock is caught in a grinding downtrend with intermittent bounces.
