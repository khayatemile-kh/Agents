## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Fidelity National Information Services, Inc. (FIS) is a leading information technology services provider operating in the financial technology and banking solutions space. As of year-end 2024, FIS exhibits a compelling turnaround narrative with significant debt reduction, improved operational efficiency, and stabilized profitability after years of integration challenges and asset write-downs.

### Key Takeaways:
- **Strong Valuation**: Trading at attractive multiples (PE 6.06, Forward PE 5.88) with 4.2% dividend yield
- **Debt Reduction**: Successfully reduced net debt from $18.6B (2023) to $10.5B (2024), a 44% decrease
- **Cash Generation**: Free cash flow of $1.25B (2024) vs. $3.6B (2023), reflecting strategic asset divestitures
- **Revenue Growth**: 3% year-over-year growth to $10.1B, with improving margins
- **Capital Allocation**: Returning $4.8B to shareholders through buybacks and dividends while strengthening balance sheet
