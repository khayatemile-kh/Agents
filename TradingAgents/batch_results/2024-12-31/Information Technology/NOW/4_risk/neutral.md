That's partially true, but it misses something crucial. When enterprises are under margin pressure—which is precisely when they're cost-cutting—workflow automation becomes *more* valuable, not less. It's defensive capital allocation, not discretionary. NOW's core value proposition is "do more with less." That's actually *more* valuable in a cautious macro environment, not less.

At 19%, the 26.8x P/E is defensible. At 15%, it's expensive. We don't actually know which one materializes without seeing Q4 guidance and forward estimates. Multiples are not stable if *expectations* about rates shift.
