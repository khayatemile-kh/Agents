The real problem with the aggressive case isn't the analysis—it's the confidence level. The aggressive analyst says "selling now is the only rational move." That's overstated. Selling 40% now is rational. Claiming certainty about what happens next is not.

But here's where I diverge from the aggressive analyst: the conservative analyst is correct that we don't know this for certain. And that's precisely why we should execute a measured reduction, not a panic sale.
