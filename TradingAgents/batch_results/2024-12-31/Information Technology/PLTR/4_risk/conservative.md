Let me explain the sequence: MACD turns positive (uptrend begins) → MACD diverges above signal line (momentum strengthens) → MACD histogram becomes extremely positive (peak momentum) → MACD begins to roll over → MACD crosses below signal line (first warning) → MACD goes negative (full reversal confirmed).
