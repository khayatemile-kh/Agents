## **Part 2: The AI/Data Center Narrative is Still in Early Innings**

### AMD's Competitive Position is Stronger Than the Stock Price Suggests

Let's address the elephant in the room: **NVIDIA dominance in AI chips**. The bears will say AMD can't compete. Let me counter that directly:

**AMD doesn't need to beat NVIDIA to win—it needs to capture a growing market.**

Here's the math:
- The global data center AI accelerator market is projected to grow from ~$60 billion (2024) to **$150+ billion by 2028** (CAGR ~25%)
- NVIDIA's current market share (~95%) leaves **$15–20 billion in addressable demand AMD can capture** at just 15–20% share
- AMD's MI300/MI300X series is gaining **real traction** with hyperscalers (Meta, Microsoft, Google) looking to diversify supplier risk

**Key point:** These hyperscalers are *designing AMD chips into their infrastructure*—this isn't speculation. Meta, for example, has committed to integrating MI300X into their data centers. Once engineering resources are invested in software optimization and deployment, there's massive switching cost and lock-in.

AMD's data center revenue is growing at **40%+ annually**, and this segment now represents their **highest-margin business**. The company isn't trying to displace NVIDIA's entire installed base—it's capturing the *new* demand growth that can sustain double-digit data center revenue CAGR for years.

### EPYC CPUs Continue to Steal Share from Intel

Don't overlook AMD's CPU strength. EPYC processors have captured **30%+ of the high-end server CPU market**, directly displacing Intel. This is a *structural* market share win, not temporary. Why?

- Better price/performance than Intel's Xeon
- Superior power efficiency (critical for hyperscaler economics)
- Custom capability (AMD works with customers on bespoke solutions)

Intel is in disarray (manufacturing struggles, execution delays), leaving AMD with a **clear runway to expand EPYC penetration toward 40%+ market share**. Each 1% of share gain = ~$500M in incremental annual revenue. This is *free money* for AMD.
