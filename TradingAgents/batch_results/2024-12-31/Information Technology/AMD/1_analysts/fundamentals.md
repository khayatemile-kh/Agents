## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Advanced Micro Devices, Inc. is a leading semiconductor designer and manufacturer specializing in processors for data centers, personal computers, and gaming systems. As of December 31, 2024, AMD presents a compelling profile of a company experiencing significant growth recovery with demonstrated operational improvements and strong market positioning. The company has recovered from 2023's challenging environment and is positioning itself for accelerated growth through AI and data center initiatives.

---

## 1. COMPANY PROFILE & MARKET POSITION

### Business Overview
AMD is a fabless semiconductor company that designs and manufactures microprocessors and graphics processing units (GPUs). The company competes in several key markets:
- **Data Center CPUs & GPUs** (highest growth driver)
- **Ryzen Consumer Processors** (personal computers)
- **EPYC Server Processors**
- **Radeon Professional & Gaming GPUs**

### Market Capitalization & Valuation
- **Market Cap:** $825.6 billion USD
- **Current Stock Price Range:** 52-week high $584.73, low $149.85
- **50-Day Average:** $499.27
- **200-Day Average:** $340.99

### Industry Standing
AMD has established itself as the primary competitor to Intel in CPUs and NVIDIA in discrete GPUs. The company's acquisition of Xilinx (2022) and Pensando Systems (2022) expanded its FPGA and data center networking capabilities, positioning it for enterprise and infrastructure markets.
