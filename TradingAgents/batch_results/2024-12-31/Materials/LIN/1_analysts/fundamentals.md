## COMPANY PROFILE & BUSINESS OVERVIEW

**Linde plc** is one of the world's largest industrial gas companies, providing atmospheric gases (oxygen, nitrogen, argon), process gases (hydrogen, helium), and specialty gases, as well as engineering services and equipment. The company serves diverse industries including:

- **Chemical & Petrochemical**: Major consumer of industrial gases
- **Refining & Energy**: Oxygen for combustion, hydrogen for processing
- **Food & Beverage**: CO2, nitrogen for preservation and processing
- **Healthcare**: Medical gases and supplies
- **Electronics & Semiconductors**: Ultra-high purity specialty gases
- **Metals & Mining**: Oxygen for combustion, nitrogen for inerting

The company operates globally with a strong presence in developed markets and growing operations in emerging markets.
