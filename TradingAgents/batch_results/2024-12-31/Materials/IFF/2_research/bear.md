Most leveraged companies operate with debt covenants (max leverage ratios, minimum interest coverage, etc.). If IFF violates these covenants, the company faces:
- **Accelerated debt maturity** (forced repayment)
- **Credit facility restrictions** (working capital constraints)

The bull gives zero acknowledgment of covenant risk. This is a massive blind spot.
