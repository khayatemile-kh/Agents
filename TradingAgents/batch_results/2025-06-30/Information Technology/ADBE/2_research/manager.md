Competitively, Figma's rise to a $20B+ private valuation and the proliferation of cheaper AI-native creative tools (Midjourney, Canva, Runway) are legitimate encroachments on Adobe's historically unassailable moat, even if Adobe's full-suite enterprise stickiness remains intact for now. The bear's claim that operating cash flow is inflated by deferred revenue prepayments is a fair flag on quality of cash flow, though it doesn't fully invalidate the $9.2B FCF figure since deferred revenue is a normal SaaS mechanic, not evidence of channel-stuffing.

Net-net: the balance sheet story (buyback-driven equity erosion, weak current ratio) and the competitive/pricing-power erosion themes are real risks that argue against adding aggressively here, while the low absolute valuation, cash generation, and technical stabilization argue against exiting a name with this quality. Given the debate is genuinely close-to-balanced with credible evidence on both sides and no fresh fundamental catalyst (no earnings miss, no guidance cut) explaining the selloff, the responsible stance is to hold the current position, not extend it and not cut it, while watching for confirmation (either a clean break above the 50-day MA on volume, or concrete signs of Firefly monetization/enterprise renewal strength) before upgrading, or evidence of accelerating churn/Figma share loss before downgrading.
