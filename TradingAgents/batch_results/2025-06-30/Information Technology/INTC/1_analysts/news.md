### 6. RISK FACTORS & CATALYSTS

**Downside Risks:**
1. **Competitive Pressure:** Continued GPU market dominance by NVIDIA in AI workloads; AMD's EPYC gaining share in data centers
2. **Foundry Execution Risk:** Delays or cost overruns in leading-edge fab construction (Arizona, Ohio) could undermine investor confidence
3. **Supply Chain Disruptions:** Taiwan geopolitical risks could benefit INTC as alternative onshore supplier, but also disrupt component sourcing
4. **Regulatory Uncertainty:** Antitrust scrutiny in Europe and US; potential trade restrictions on advanced chip exports

**Upside Catalysts:**
1. **CHIPS Act Funding:** Additional government subsidies could reduce capex burden and improve ROI on foundry investments
2. **AI Inference Growth:** If Intel's CPU-based inference products gain traction with hyperscalers, could drive CPU volume growth in data centers
3. **Geopolitical Decoupling:** US government preference for onshore semiconductor supply could drive orders to INTC's fabs
4. **Product Cycle Launches:** Arrow Lake CPUs and next-gen Gaudi accelerators could reignite competitive momentum if successful
