## **2. DECONSTRUCTING THE PAIN: Why Q2 2025 Losses Are **Temporary and Expected****

Now, let me address the elephant in the room: **-$2.92B net loss in Q2 2025, -22.7% net margin, negative operating income.**

This looks catastrophic. It's not. Here's why:

### **The Restructuring Charge Masking Real Business Performance**

Breaking down Q2 2025's $-2.92B net loss:

| Line Item | Amount | Nature |
|-----------|--------|--------|
| **Operating Loss** | -$1.29B | Core operations |
| **Restructuring Charges** | -$1.47B | **One-time** |
| **Asset Impairment** | -$0.533B | **One-time** |
| **Write-offs** | -$0.467B | **One-time** |
| **Total Non-Recurring** | **-$2.47B** | **85% of total loss** |

**What's the real operating picture?**

Strip out the non-recurring charges, and **core operating loss is only -$1.29B on $12.86B revenue**—a -10% operating margin. That's bad, but it's **not a broken business**. It's a company in transition, temporarily unprofitable while:
- Ramping new fab capacity (massive fixed cost burden)
- Transitioning to new product architectures (R&D heavy)
- Restructuring for efficiency
- Building foundry business (pre-revenue investment)

**Comparable restructuring periods:** Look at Apple (2007-2011 iPhone transition), AMD (2016-2018 EPYC ramp-up), or even TSMC (early 2000s foundry expansion). All experienced temporary margin compression before explosive growth. Intel is in year 2 of a similar transition.

### **Gross Margin Compression is Fixable**

Q2 2025 gross margin is 27.5%—down from historical 50%+. Bears scream: **"Permanent competitive loss!"**

Bull response: **Look at the actual cause.**

Intel's fab utilization is currently **sub-optimal** because:
1. New fabs in Arizona/Ohio are ramping (early-stage, low utilization = high cost per unit)
2. Legacy fab capacity is underutilized (COVID hangover + competitive losses)
3. Product transition is incomplete (Arrow Lake just launching)

As new fabs ramp utilization and foundry business scales:
- Fixed cost per wafer **drops significantly**
- Gross margin recovers toward 40%+ (not 50%+, but healthy)
- Operating leverage kicks in

**Precedent:** TSMC's fab ramp-ups show gross margins expanding by **10-15 percentage points** over 2-3 years as utilization improves. Intel's path is identical.
