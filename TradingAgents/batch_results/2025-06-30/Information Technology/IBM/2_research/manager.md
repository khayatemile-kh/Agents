The technical setup (Golden Cross, 14% rally, price above 200-DMA) is real short-term momentum, but technicals alone don't override a deteriorating balance sheet and liquidity trajectory, especially for a value/income-oriented mega-cap where the bull thesis leans heavily on fundamentals (FCF, dividend safety) rather than momentum trading.

Weighing both sides: the bear's arguments are more granular, cite specific balance-sheet deterioration (current ratio, debt growth, debt/equity vs peers) that the bull never adequately rebuts (the "strategic M&A" defense fails because no acquisition was actually identified), and use a more appropriately forward-looking cash flow lens. The bull's strongest points — dividend coverage, margin expansion, cheap absolute valuation — are real but do not fully offset the liquidity and leverage deterioration that has occurred in just the last six months. This tips the balance toward caution rather than full conviction on either side.
