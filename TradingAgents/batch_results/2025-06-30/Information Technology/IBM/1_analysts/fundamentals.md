## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

International Business Machines Corporation (IBM) presents a mixed fundamental profile as of Q2 2025. The company demonstrates solid operational profitability with consistent cash flow generation, but faces significant headwinds from high leverage, deteriorating liquidity metrics, and declining profitability trends. IBM's TTM metrics show a market capitalization of $221.3 billion with modest growth prospects, indicating the company is in a mature phase requiring strategic operational efficiency to maintain shareholder value.

---

## COMPANY PROFILE & BUSINESS OVERVIEW

**International Business Machines Corporation (IBM)** is a multinational technology conglomerate headquartered in Armonk, New York. Operating within the Information Technology Services sector, IBM provides comprehensive IT infrastructure, cloud solutions, software, and managed services to enterprise clients globally.

**Key Business Segments:**
- Infrastructure & Cloud Platform
- Software & Red Hat (open-source software)
- Consulting & Enterprise Transformation
- Managed Infrastructure Services
- Financing & Services (IBM Financial Services)

IBM's business model centers on subscription-based recurring revenue, managed services contracts, and enterprise software licensing, providing relatively predictable cash flows despite the competitive IT services market.
