## **PART 1: THE PROFITABILITY COLLAPSE IS NOT "INVESTMENT FOR SCALE"—IT'S OPERATIONAL DETERIORATION**

### **The Bull's Sleight of Hand**

The bull argues: *"CRWD is investing for growth! Operating losses are normal for scaling SaaS companies!"*

**This is misleading, and the data proves it.**

Let's look at the actual operational trajectory:

| **Metric** | **FY2023** | **FY2024** | **FY2025** | **Trend** |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Revenue Growth Rate** | 56.3% | 36.2% | 29.3% | **Decelerating** ✗ |
| **Operating Income** | $(190.1M)* | $(19.1M)* | $(116.4M)* | **Worsening** ✗ |

| **EBIT** | $(134.6M)* | $131.4M | $84.9M | **Declining** ✗ |

**Here's the critical point:** FY2024 was profitable. CRWD achieved **$72M in net income and positive EBIT of $131M** while growing revenue 36%. That proves the company *can* operate profitably at substantial growth rates.

Then FY2025 happened: Revenue growth slowed to 29%, but profitability *collapsed by 121%* into negative territory. **Operating margin didn't compress—it inverted.**

The bull claims: *"This is just aggressive investment spending!"*

Let me break down where the money actually went:

**The smoking gun:** R&D grew 37.8% and SG&A grew 33.6% while revenue only grew 29.3%. **Expenses are growing faster than revenue.** This is not "strategic investment"—this is **operational leverage going negative.**

In a healthy SaaS scaling company, you'd see:
- Revenue growing 25-30%
- Expenses growing 10-15%
- Operating leverage expanding
- Margins *improving*

Instead, CRWD shows:
- Revenue growing 29%
- Expenses growing 33-38%
- Operating leverage *compressing*
- Margins *deteriorating*

**This is the signature of a company losing control of its cost structure.**

Let me focus on the SG&A spend: **$1.52 billion, or 38.5% of revenue.**

| **Company** | **FY2024 SG&A % of Revenue** | **Revenue Size** |

CRWD's SG&A ratio is **the highest in peer group.** This is particularly concerning because:

**The bull's narrative breaks down here:** If this spending surge were truly "strategic investment for market dominance," we'd see corresponding market share gains. Instead, we see 29% revenue growth—solid, but not exceptional in the cybersecurity market.

## **PART 2: REVENUE GROWTH IS DECELERATING, NOT ACCELERATING**

### **The Deceleration Trap**

The bull emphasizes the 29.3% revenue growth as proof of strength. **But look at the trajectory:**

- **FY2023 → FY2024:** 56.3% growth
- **FY2024 → FY2025:** 36.2% growth
- **FY2025 ytd (annualized):** 29.3% growth

**Growth is collapsing.** In just two years, CRWD's growth rate has been cut by more than half—from 56% to 29%.

### **Why This Matters**

At current valuation—a forward P/E of 131x and PEG ratio of 6.82—CRWD requires continued acceleration to justify the multiple. Instead, growth is decelerating.

**Here's the math that should terrify investors:**

A typical SaaS company with 30% growth deserves a forward P/E of 30-40x, not 131x. That multiple assumes:
- Perpetual 30%+ growth for 5+ years, OR
- Explosive margin expansion to 20%+ operating margins
