**The Critical Problem:**

The bull is projecting that Q2 2025's $4.4B in quarterly net income will **persist for all of 2025**. This is not grounded in reality. Here's why:

1. **Q2 is seasonally strong for pharma.** Pharmaceutical companies typically see **peak revenue and earnings in Q2** due to:
   - Year-end inventory builds by distributors in late Q1/early Q2
   - Seasonal flu vaccine sales peaks (timing)
   - Pharmacy restocking patterns

2. **Historical Earnings Pattern for MRK:**
   - Q1 typically lags Q2 by 15-25%
   - Q3-Q4 show normalization or decline
   - TTM earnings of $3.2B suggests Q3-Q4 2024 was significantly weaker

3. **The Bull Cannot Have It Both Ways:** If Q2 represents "normalized" operational performance, then why was TTM earnings only $3.2B? The answer: **the other three quarters were substantially weaker**, and there's no evidence Q3-Q4 2025 will improve.

**A More Realistic Forward EPS Estimate:**

Using conservative seasonality adjustment:
- Q2 2025: $4.4B (actual)
- Q1 2025E: $3.0B (assuming 30% lower than Q2)
- Q3 2025E: $3.2B (assuming seasonal normalization)
- Q4 2025E: $3.1B (holiday season adjustment)
- **FY2025E Total: $13.7B Net Income ($1.70/share)**

**This implies a Forward P/E of 45.0x**, not the bull's magical 13-14x.

**Even if we assume Q2 performance repeats perfectly in all quarters** (an absurd assumption), you get $17.6B in net income, giving a P/E of 43.5x—still **3x higher than the sector average of 15-20x**.
