## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Moderna, Inc. (ticker: MRNA) is a biotechnology company operating in the healthcare sector, specifically focused on mRNA-based therapeutics. As of June 30, 2025, the company is undergoing a significant profitability crisis, transitioning from its peak COVID-19 pandemic revenues to a challenging post-pandemic environment. The fundamental analysis reveals a company facing severe operational headwinds with ongoing losses, declining cash generation, and eroded shareholder value—a stark reversal from its 2022 performance peak.
