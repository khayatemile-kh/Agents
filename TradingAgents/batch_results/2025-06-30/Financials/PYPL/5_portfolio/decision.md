The margin debate is the crux and the bear side wins it decisively. This is consistent with the Research Manager's framing that recurring restructuring signals defensive cost-cutting, not offensive efficiency gains.

That lesson applies directly here: the bull's catalyst (growth reacceleration/margin expansion) still has not materialized, and margin/restructuring dynamics look worse, not better, than in March.
