### **Indicator Selection Rationale**

The following **7 indicators** were selected for MA analysis:

1. **close_50_sma** — Identifies the medium-term trend pivot point; critical for determining if MA is in an uptrend or downtrend correction
2. **close_200_sma** — Establishes the long-term trend floor; ensures we don't confuse a correction with a structural trend break
3. **close_10_ema** — Provides short-term momentum confirmation; helps detect reversal entry/exit points
4. **rsi** — Flags extreme conditions (overbought >70 / oversold <30) and divergence patterns; shows current balance (49.74 = neutral)
5. **macd** — Confirms trend changes via crossovers; critical for detecting when momentum shifts from positive to negative
6. **macds** — MACD signal line for crossover confirmation and trend change validation
7. **boll** & **atr** — Together, they quantify volatility and establish dynamic support/resistance; useful for risk management and breakout detection

These indicators avoid redundancy (e.g., no dual momentum indicators like RSI + Stochastic) and provide diverse information across trend, momentum, and volatility.
