### **INVESTMENT THESIS**

#### **BULLISH FACTORS:**
1. **Defensive Profile:** Recurring revenue from ratings maintenance, market data subscriptions insulates earnings from cyclical downturns.
2. **Pricing Power:** Mission-critical data/analytics; high barriers to switching; pricing elasticity allows margin expansion in inflationary regimes.
3. **Capital Allocation:** Strong FCF generation supports dividends and buybacks; historically attractive shareholder returns.
4. **M&A Optionality:** Potential to acquire complementary data businesses if valuations contract.

#### **BEARISH FACTORS:**
1. **Cyclical Exposure:** M&A advisory revenues decline sharply in low-transaction environments.
2. **Equity Market Weakness:** Lower AUM in equity indices, reduced demand for indices licensing in down markets.
3. **Refinancing Risk:** If credit spreads widen sharply, ratings volatility may reduce issuer demand for new ratings (perverse feedback loop).
4. **Regulatory Pressure:** Ratings agency regulations (Dodd-Frank legacy, ESG disclosure rules) create compliance cost headwinds.
