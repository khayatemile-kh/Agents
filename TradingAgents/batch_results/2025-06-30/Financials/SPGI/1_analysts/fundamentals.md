### COMPANY PROFILE & BUSINESS OVERVIEW

**S&P Global Inc. (SPGI)** is a leading financial services company operating in the Financial Data & Stock Exchanges sector. The company is a dominant provider of transparent and independent ratings, benchmarks, analytics, and data to capital and commodity markets worldwide. SPGI operates through multiple business divisions including:

- **Ratings**: Credit rating services and opinions
- **Market Intelligence**: Market data, research, and analytics
- **S&P Global Platts**: Commodity pricing and energy market data
- **S&P Global Market Intelligence**: Financial information services

The company serves institutional clients including asset managers, insurance companies, financial institutions, corporate issuers, and government entities globally.
