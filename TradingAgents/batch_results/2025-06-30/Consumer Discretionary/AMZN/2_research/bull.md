- **AWS customers aren't discretionary—they're structural.** A bank, healthcare provider, or e-commerce competitor doesn't cut cloud spending when rates are high; they *increase* it because on-premise infrastructure capex is even more expensive and harder to finance. The macro headwind argument ignores Amazon's structural position as the beneficiary of cloud consolidation during economic uncertainty.
