## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Amazon (AMZN) presents a robust fundamental profile as of June 30, 2025, demonstrating strong operational momentum, impressive profitability expansion, and healthy cash generation. The company has significantly improved its financial position compared to prior years, with Net Income reaching $18.2 billion in Q2 2025 and operating margins at healthy 12.9%, up from 8.5% in the prior year period. While the company's valuation remains elevated at 20.3x forward P/E ratio and 24.2x forward-looking metrics, this is supported by sustained revenue growth, capital efficiency improvements, and strategic investments in high-margin cloud infrastructure.

## COMPANY PROFILE & BUSINESS OVERVIEW

**Amazon.com, Inc.** is a multinational technology company and leading e-commerce retailer operating across multiple segments:
- **Retail:** Online and physical retail operations globally
- **AWS (Amazon Web Services):** Cloud computing and infrastructure services (primary profit driver)
- **Advertising:** High-margin third-party seller advertising services
- **Other:** Digital streaming, marketplace services, and emerging technologies

The company operates across North America, International, and Amazon Web Services (AWS) segments, with AWS now accounting for the majority of operating income despite retail generating the largest revenue share.
