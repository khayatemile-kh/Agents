## COUNTERARGUMENT 1: THE "PROFITABILITY RECOVERY" IS A MIRAGE—IT'S NOT SUSTAINABLE

You've highlighted the impressive 68.6% EPS growth from 2023 to 2024. This is where I have to ask: **From what baseline was this measured, and can it persist?**

### **The Earnings Growth Story Hides a Fragile Foundation:**

Let me walk through the actual math:

**2023 was a "recovery year" for a reason.** Here's the context you glossed over:
- **2022 Net Income: $352M**
- **2023 Net Income: $797M** (+126% YoY)
- **2024 Net Income: $1,234M** (+55% YoY)

Notice the *deceleration*? EPS growth went from **+126% to +55%**. That's a **56% *slowdown* in growth rate year-over-year.** This is the first red flag: **growth is already normalizing.**

### **Why Q2 2025 Isn't as Strong as It Appears:**

You cited Q2 2025's 14% operating margin as evidence of momentum, but let me contextualize:

- **Q2 is peak travel season.** Hotels, airlines, and OTAs *always* perform best in Q2-Q3 when leisure travel peaks
- The 14% operating margin is **seasonal, not structural.** It won't persist through Q4 when travel demand drops
- You're annualizing a **single quarter** during the strongest period of the year—this is methodologically flawed

**The Actual 2024 Full-Year Picture:**
- 2024 Operating Income: $1,670M (12.2% margin)
- Q2 2024 would have been similarly elevated
- Yet full-year came in at 12.2%, not 14%

**So the question is: What changed in Q2 2025 to justify 14% margins?** The data suggests nothing has structurally improved—we're just seeing normal seasonal strength.

### **The Real Problem: Margin Sustainability is Deteriorating**

Look at EBITDA margins:
- **2024: 19.2%**
- **2023: 16.5%**
- **2022: 15.1%**

Great story, right? But here's the issue: **EBITDA margin expansion came entirely from reducing share count and financial engineering, NOT from operational efficiency.**

When you break it down:
1. **Gross margin is flat** at ~90% (this makes sense for an asset-light OTA)
2. **Operating leverage is muted** because SG&A spending as a % of revenue has remained sticky at 61-62%
3. **The margin expansion is a rounding artifact** of operating leverage on a growing but not rapidly expanding top line

**Revenue growth is slowing:**
- 2023 revenue growth: +10.0% YoY
- 2024 revenue growth: +6.6% YoY
- This is a **34% deceleration in topline growth**

When revenue growth decelerates this sharply, **operating leverage disappears.** Fixed costs (tech, personnel, infrastructure) don't shrink with revenue. At a 6.6% revenue growth rate, expecting sustained 14% operating margins is optimistic at best, delusional at worst.

- Cash on Hand: $6,304M

**On the surface, this looks okay—debt is nearly covered by cash.** But here's the trap:

1. **$2,151M is "restricted cash"** — this isn't freely available. It's tied to partner settlements, escrow requirements, and regulatory holds. **Unrestricted cash is only ~$4.2B.**

2. **Working capital is massively negative:**

   - Current Liabilities: $19.6B

   - Deferred revenue evaporates

**The debt service picture is concerning:**
