**Investment-grade leverage works for stable, non-cyclical businesses** (utilities, telecom, etc.). **Automotive suppliers are HIGHLY cyclical.** APTV cannot operate at 2.0x net debt/EBITDA during an economic downturn—the company will be forced into emergency deleveraging that destroys shareholders.

Let me walk through what a realistic downturn scenario looks like:

**2025 Baseline (Current):**
- EBITDA: $3.23B
- Net Debt: $6.34B
- Net Debt/EBITDA: 1.96x

**2026 Recession Scenario (Realistic given macro backdrop):**
- Vehicle production down 15-20% (not unprecedented during rate shock cycles)
- EBITDA declines ~20% to $2.58B (fixed costs don't move proportionally)
- Net Debt: $6.34B (doesn't shrink quickly)
- **Net Debt/EBITDA: 2.46x**

**At 2.46x leverage in a downturn, APTV loses investment-grade rating, refinancing becomes expensive, and covenants come into play.**
