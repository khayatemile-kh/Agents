The bull's own numbers imply the stock is already fully priced (arguably overpriced) even in the favorable 73%-probability scenario, and the stock's 41% pullback from highs is a signal the market has already begun repricing these risks. That argues against adding exposure here, but the durable dividend and integrated business model don't justify an aggressive Sell/exit either.
