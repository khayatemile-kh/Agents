**The Deleveraging Trajectory:**

With normalized EBITDA of $7.38 billion and forward earnings of $3.23 per share (on 1.44B shares = ~$4.7 billion net income), the company is in a **deleveraging cycle**:

Within 18-24 months, I expect SLB to target Net Debt-to-EBITDA below 1.0x, moving into pristine financial territory.

**Why Debt Matters Less Than You Think in This Cycle:**

- Oilfield services demand is supported by **fundamental economics**, not speculation
- Interest rates, while elevated, aren't spiking—and SLB's debt is already on the books at fixed rates

The technical report flags the MACD bearish crossover as a "confirmed trend reversal." Let me push back on this.

**Context Matters:**
- MACD is a *momentum* indicator, not a *trend* indicator
- It's most reliable in **ranging markets**, not during corrections in longer-term uptrends
- A single 7-10 day divergence doesn't override the **fundamental demand cycle** that's just getting started

**The Real Story:**
- June saw typical summer softness (lower trading volumes, holiday periods)
- The technicals compressed because the market was thin, not because demand fundamentally changed
- Historically, MACD crossovers that occur *during corrections in bull markets* are **whipsaw signals**, not trend confirmations

Once we move past the July 4 holiday and summer trading resumes, I expect volume to normalize, and these technical signals to reverse.
