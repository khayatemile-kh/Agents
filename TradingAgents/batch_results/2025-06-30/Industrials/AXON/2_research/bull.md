| Metric | Value | Assessment |
|---|---|---|
| **Net Debt-to-EBITDA** | ~5.0x | Elevated but not catastrophic |
| **Interest Coverage Ratio** | ~10.0x (EBITDA-based) | Serviceable; company can pay interest 10x over |
| **Debt maturity profile** | Staggered | No cliff; refinancing risk is minimal |

**Key insight:** The debt-to-equity ratio is inflated because **Axon is carrying substantial net cash in investments ($1.61B in short-term investments) that could theoretically pay down debt immediately.** The company has the financial flexibility to reduce leverage if desired.

Moreover, **government contractors have implicit credit quality.** Axon's contracts with municipalities and federal agencies are backed by public budgets and multi-year appropriations. Refinancing risk is virtually zero.

**Interest expense of $28.7M is easily manageable within operating cash flow once the working capital cycle normalizes.** By 2026, I expect net debt-to-EBITDA to drop below 3.0x as EBITDA expands.
