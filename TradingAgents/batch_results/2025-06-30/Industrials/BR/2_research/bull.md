## PART I: THE GROWTH THESIS

### 1. **Accelerating Revenue Growth from a Strong Base**

The data speaks for itself:
- **TTM Revenue:** $7.48 billion
- **Q2 2025 Quarterly Run Rate:** $2.065B annualizes to **$8.26 billion**

That's **+10.4% sequential acceleration** compared to the TTM baseline. This isn't noise—this is a company that's inflecting positively.

**Why this matters:**
- BR operates in financial services technology—a sector that's *structurally required* to keep spending on compliance, data, and infrastructure regardless of economic cycles
- The company benefits from multiple growth vectors:
  - **Regulatory complexity expansion** (post-2008, post-MiFID II, ongoing Dodd-Frank enforcement)
  - **Digital transformation** in wealth management and retail investing
  - **M&A activity** correlation (financial services M&A drives demand for integration and trade infrastructure)
  - **Data proliferation** (more data = more demand for BR's analytics and risk solutions)

### 2. **Recurring Revenue Provides Earnings Visibility**

Here's a critical point that bears often miss: **~60% of BR's revenue is recurring subscription-based.** This isn't transactional software—it's infrastructure that financial firms *cannot live without.*

What does this mean?
- **Revenue visibility:** When BR signs a multi-year contract with Goldman Sachs or Morgan Stanley, that's locked-in revenue. It's far more predictable than selling perpetual licenses.
- **Pricing power:** Once embedded, BR can gradually increase prices with inflation. Switching costs are astronomical—you don't rip out your entire trade processing system lightly.
- **Downside protection:** In a recession, compliance and risk software spending holds up far better than discretionary IT capex. Firms cut other things first.

The bear case always assumes growth stalls. The reality? BR's recurring revenue base provides a durable floor.

## PART III: THE PROFITABILITY & EFFICIENCY STORY

### 5. **Operating Leverage & Margin Expansion Potential**

Current margins:
- **Operating Margin:** 24.6%
- **Net Margin:** 15.0%
- **EBITDA Margin:** 24.2%

These are fortress-level margins. But here's the bull thesis: **there's room to expand.**

Why?
- **Software is inherently scalable.** As BR grows revenue, the incremental cost of serving customers is minimal (think: one extra server vs. hiring 100 people).
- **Operating leverage:** If BR grows revenue 5-7% annually while keeping headcount growth at 2-3%, margins automatically expand 50-100 basis points per year.
- **Cloud migration:** Moving customers to SaaS reduces on-premise delivery costs, improving margins.
- **AI/automation:** BR is investing in AI-driven analytics and workflow automation—this will reduce service delivery costs while increasing customer value.

A conservative scenario: Operating margins expand from 24.6% to 26-27% over 3 years = **$150-190M additional EBITDA** on stable revenue.
