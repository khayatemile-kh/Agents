- **That's a ~57% *decline* in earnings on a forward basis**

You're framing this as "the market expects earnings recovery." I'm reading it as **"the market knows HON's earnings are about to get worse, and even at depressed valuations, the stock is expensive relative to what's coming."**

This isn't theoretical. Let me cite the data:

**Q2 2025 TTM Net Income: $8.27 billion**
**Q2 2025 Annualized Revenue: $37.3 billion**
**Implied Profit Margin: 22.2%**

But here's what you glossed over: **Q2's 16.8% net margin is already down from the 21.6% TTM average.** The trend is *deteriorating* in real time. This is a classic sign of a company in managed decline. **Divestitures disguised as portfolio optimization.**

**3. Working capital deterioration is real**

The "sustainable" $4.4B annual FCF is actually **$1.3B minus $475M working capital drain = $825M**, or roughly **3.0x EBITDA debt paydown capacity—not 2.2x as the bull case suggests.**
