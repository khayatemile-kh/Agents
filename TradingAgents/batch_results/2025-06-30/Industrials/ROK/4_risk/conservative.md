The fact that a single quarter came in at 17.6% (which is still a solid margin, mind you) is not evidence of structural deterioration. 4. **Their math ignores forward growth rates.** The forward EPS of $14.85 isn't derived purely from flat margin assumptions. It accounts for revenue growth. If ROK is growing revenue at, say, 6-8% annually (reasonable for an industrial automation leader in a favorable capex cycle), and margins hold at or slightly above 20%, then forward EPS growth of 38% becomes achievable through a combination of organic growth, operating leverage, and potential M&A contributions.

So the Aggressive Analyst's 14-15% "overvaluation premium" is actually based on a counterfactual assumption: that margins will remain depressed at Q2 levels indefinitely, despite historical precedent and favorable macro conditions suggesting otherwise.

**For a conservative risk manager, betting that a company will underperform relative to TTM margins when the macro setup is improving is a losing proposition.**
