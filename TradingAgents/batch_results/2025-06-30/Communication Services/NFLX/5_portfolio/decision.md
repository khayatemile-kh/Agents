The Emmy prediction-market shift (-13.2pp in one week, Apple TV+ 96% vs. NFLX 2%) was the most contested data point. However, the Neutral Analyst explicitly rejects this dismissal: Emmy voters "don't vote randomly," and unlike HBO Max, Apple TV+ carries the structural bundling and balance-sheet advantage HBO Max never had — making this a more credible leading indicator this time, even if not yet confirmed in subscriber data.

On capital allocation, the Conservative Analyst's read that aggressive buybacks over accelerated content/international investment can signal capital-deployment constraint rather than abundance went largely unrebutted with hard data; the Aggressive Analyst's EPS-accretion argument is mathematically valid but doesn't resolve whether the underlying growth optionality is actually expanding.
