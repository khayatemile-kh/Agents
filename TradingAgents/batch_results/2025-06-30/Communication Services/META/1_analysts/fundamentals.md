### COMPANY PROFILE & BUSINESS OVERVIEW

**Business Model:**
Meta Platforms, Inc. operates a digital advertising and social media ecosystem spanning three primary segments:
- **Family of Apps (FoA):** Facebook, Instagram, Messenger, WhatsApp
- **Reality Labs:** Virtual/Augmented reality development and metaverse initiatives
- **Platforms & Infrastructure:** Advertising technology, content delivery, and platform services

The company generates approximately 98% of revenue from advertising across its family of applications, with massive user engagement (2.1B+ daily active users globally) creating substantial advertising inventory.

**Recent Business Developments:**
- Continued AI integration across recommendation algorithms and advertising systems
- Strategic shift toward "Year of Efficiency" operational improvements
- Increased capex for AI infrastructure and data center expansion
- Monetization improvements in emerging markets
- Strong recovery in ad demand following industry headwinds in 2022-2023
