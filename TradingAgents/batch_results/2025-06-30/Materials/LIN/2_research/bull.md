- **EBITDA margin of 39%** across a $35.45B revenue base is massive. That's $13.82B of cash generation before considering taxes and capex. For context, that's more EBITDA than entire Fortune 500 companies generate in revenue.

The business model is reminiscent of utilities: once you're embedded in a customer's supply chain, switching costs are extraordinarily high. A steel mill or semiconductor fab cannot simply switch oxygen suppliers mid-production without catastrophic disruption.
