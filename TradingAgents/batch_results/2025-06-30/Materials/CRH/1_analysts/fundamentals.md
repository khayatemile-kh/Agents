### EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

CRH plc is a leading global building materials company with a significant presence across North America and Europe. As of June 30, 2025, the company demonstrates solid operational performance with a market capitalization of **$58.1 billion**. The first half of 2025 reflects continued demand for construction materials, though some macroeconomic headwinds are evident in working capital dynamics. The company maintains a reasonable balance between growth investments and shareholder returns while managing a moderately elevated debt position.

---

### COMPANY PROFILE & BUSINESS OVERVIEW

CRH plc operates as a diversified building materials supplier with operations spanning:
- **Cement and aggregates** (primary revenue driver)
- **Asphalt and readymix concrete**
- **Architectural products**
- **Distribution and logistics**

The company operates globally but derives the majority of revenue from:
- **United States** (~60% of revenue)
- **Ireland and Europe** (~40% of revenue)

CRH's vertically integrated model provides exposure to multiple points in the construction value chain, providing resilience across different market cycles and customer segments.
