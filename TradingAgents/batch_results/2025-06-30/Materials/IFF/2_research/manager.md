This isn't a full Sell because the balance sheet isn't in immediate distress - interest coverage, while overstated by the bull, is still solidly positive on a TTM basis (~7.8x), and gross margins do reflect real product differentiation. But the debt overhang, weak ROE/ROA, and reliance on a non-repeatable cash inflow to fund the "turnaround" narrative make this a name to reduce exposure to rather than add to at current levels, pending clearer evidence of organic FCF improvement in Q3/Q4.
