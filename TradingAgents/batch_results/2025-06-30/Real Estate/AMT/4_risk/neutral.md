For a 3.98% yield REIT with stable cash flows and inflation protection, normalization is acceptable.

The Conservative Analyst has the mechanics backward.

The leverage that concerns the Conservative Analyst is dangerous in *rising* rate environments or in *deteriorating operational* environments, not in slowing growth environments.
