1. **Real Estate Control**: $28.18B in net PP&E represents actual tower locations in high-demand markets. You can't build a competitive network without land. Acquisition costs to replicate AMT's footprint would exceed $50B+—prohibitive for any new entrant.

2. **Customer Lock-In**: Wireless carriers have invested billions in network integration with AMT's towers. Switching costs are enormous. Once a carrier commits to a tower site, they're locked in for years.

4. **Intangible Value**: $27.21B in goodwill and intangibles ($12.25B goodwill + $14.96B other intangibles) reflects customer relationships, non-compete agreements, and brand equity built over decades. These aren't paper assets—they represent real pricing power and contract stability.
