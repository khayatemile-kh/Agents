### COMPANY PROFILE

**American Tower Corporation (AMT)** is a leading Real Estate Investment Trust (REIT) specializing in tower and data center real estate. The company owns, operates, and develops wireless and broadcast communications real estate infrastructure across the United States and internationally. As a specialty REIT, AMT provides critical telecommunications infrastructure essential to 5G deployment, wireless connectivity expansion, and data transmission networks.

**Key Identifiers:**
- **Ticker:** AMT
- **Exchange:** NYQ (NYSE)
- **Sector:** Real Estate
