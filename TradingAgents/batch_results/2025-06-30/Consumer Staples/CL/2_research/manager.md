Neither side has landed a knockout blow. The bull's margin/FCF quality argument is real but the bear's point about the *rate of change* decelerating and the multiple already pricing in continued strength is equally real. This is a case where the underlying business quality isn't in dispute — the debate is really about whether you're overpaying today for that quality, which is a valuation/timing question rather than a fundamental deterioration thesis. Given the genuinely balanced evidence — quality moat and cash generation on one side, stretched valuation and decelerating growth/margin cracks on the other — I'm calling this a genuine Hold rather than forcing a directional call.
