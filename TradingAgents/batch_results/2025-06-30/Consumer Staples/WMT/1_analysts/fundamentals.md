### EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Walmart Inc. (WMT) continues to demonstrate robust financial performance as of Q2 FY2025, with strong revenue growth, improving profitability, and solid cash generation. The company has successfully navigated inflationary pressures and competitive dynamics in retail, positioning itself as a defensive investment in the consumer discretionary sector. With a market cap of approximately **$842.4 billion**, Walmart maintains a strong competitive moat through its scale, operational efficiency, and diverse business model.

### COMPANY PROFILE

**Business Overview:**
Walmart Inc. is the world's largest retailer by revenue, operating a diversified retail portfolio that includes:
- **Walmart U.S.** - Discount retail stores and Sam's Club
- **Walmart International** - Stores in multiple countries
- **Digital and E-commerce** - Growing online and marketplace capabilities
- **Advertising Services** - Expanding high-margin ad business

**Key Competitive Advantages:**
- Unmatched scale and supplier leverage
- Diverse revenue streams (retail, membership, advertising)
- Strong supply chain and distribution network
- Low-cost operational model
- Growing digital/e-commerce capabilities

### DETAILED FINANCIAL ANALYSIS

#### INCOME STATEMENT ANALYSIS (Annual Fiscal Years)

**Revenue Growth & Trajectory:**
- FY2025: $681.0 billion (+5.1% YoY)
- FY2024: $648.1 billion (+6.0% YoY)
- FY2023: $611.3 billion

Walmart's revenue growth has moderated slightly from 6.0% to 5.1%, reflecting normalization post-pandemic and market saturation in domestic retail. However, this remains solid growth for a mature retailer of Walmart's size.

**Gross Profit Analysis:**
- FY2025 Gross Profit: $169.2 billion (24.8% margin)
- FY2024 Gross Profit: $158.0 billion (24.4% margin)
- FY2023 Gross Profit: $147.6 billion (24.1% margin)

Gross margins have expanded by 70 basis points over three years, indicating:
- Improved product mix (higher-margin categories like apparel and electronics)
- Enhanced pricing power
- Better supply chain efficiency
- Growing high-margin segments (advertising, marketplace commissions)

**Operating Income & EBIT:**
- FY2025 Operating Income: $29.3 billion (4.3% margin)
- FY2024 Operating Income: $27.0 billion (4.2% margin)
- FY2023 Operating Income: $20.4 billion (3.3% margin)

Operating income grew 8.5% YoY, outpacing revenue growth and demonstrating strong operational leverage. This expansion reflects:
- SG&A leverage (selling/general/administrative expenses growing slower than revenue)
- Depreciation as a percentage of revenue declining
- Improved store productivity

**EBITDA Progression:**
- FY2025: $42.0 billion (6.2% margin)
- FY2024: $36.4 billion (5.6% margin)
- FY2023: $30.1 billion (4.9% margin)

EBITDA has shown exceptional growth of 15.4% YoY, indicating strong underlying cash-generating capability.

**Net Income & Earnings:**
- FY2025 Net Income: $19.4 billion
- FY2024 Net Income: $15.5 billion (+25.1% YoY)
- FY2023 Net Income: $11.7 billion (+32.7% YoY)

Net income growth of 25.1% significantly outpaces revenue growth, indicating substantial operational improvements and leverage.

**Tax Efficiency:**
- Effective tax rate FY2025: 23.4% (down from 25.5% in FY2024)
- Lower tax rate contributed to stronger bottom-line results
