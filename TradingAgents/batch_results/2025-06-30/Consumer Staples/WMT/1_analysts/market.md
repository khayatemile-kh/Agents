**Interpretation**: WMT is in a confirmed uptrend (price > 50 SMA > 200 SMA hierarchy maintained), but the narrow margin between current price and the 10/50 EMAs suggests the trend is consolidating rather than accelerating.

#### **Mid-June Correction: A Healthy Pullback or Warning Sign?**
- The stock declined from a June 3 close of **$98.91** to a June 17 low of **$93.25**, representing a **-5.72% pullback** within the month.

**Interpretation**: The mid-June dip appears to be a healthy consolidation rather than a reversal, with the 50 SMA confirming its role as a support zone.
