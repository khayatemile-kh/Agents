The real risk isn't earnings normalization alone. It's the combination of three things hitting simultaneously:

Second, the advertising and marketplace businesses face margin pressure as competition intensifies. Amazon already owns ~50% of US e-commerce ad spend. Target and other retailers are building out competing platforms. Walmart's high margins in these segments *aren't* guaranteed to persist.

Third, valuation multiples compress simply because the market reprices growth expectations downward *without* an actual recession. This is where tail risk lives. A 31x PE could easily compress to 26-27x if guidance turns cautious, even if earnings are still positive and growing.

In that scenario, WMT goes from $96.74 to somewhere in the $80-83 range. That's a 15% downside.
