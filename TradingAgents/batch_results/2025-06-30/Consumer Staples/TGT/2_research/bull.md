Target **successfully implemented pricing strategies**, optimized inventory (down 5.9% from FY2023 while maintaining sales), and cut costs. This is the hallmark of excellent management execution.

**The Leverage Play:**
Here's the critical point: if TGT can stabilize revenue at current levels (even at $106.6B) while maintaining these expanded margins, **the operating income compounding opportunity is massive**. Every incremental dollar of sales falls through to the bottom line at a much higher rate than before. This is **operating leverage in action**, and it's a hallmark of undervalued turnarounds.

## **ADDRESSING THE BEAR CASE BEFORE IT'S MADE**

Let me preemptively address the arguments the bears will likely raise:

### **Bear Claim #1: "Revenue is declining; TGT is a mature, shrinking business"**

**Bull Rebuttal:**
Revenue contraction in absolute dollars is less meaningful than **profitability expansion**. Consider:
- A company can shrink revenue but expand margins and EPS (value creation)
- A company can grow revenue but contract margins and EPS (value destruction)

TGT is the former. Over two years:
- Revenue: -2.3% (minor)
- EPS: +48% (substantial)
- Operating Margin: +170 bps (significant)

This is a **quality improvement**, not a deterioration. Moreover, the revenue decline reflects:
1. Normalization post-pandemic (consumers moved back to experiences vs. goods)
2. Conservative inventory management (not overstocking to chase top-line growth)
3. Strategic focus on profitable sales, not volume

The bears will focus on the decline; I'm focusing on **what matters: profitability and cash generation**. And by that measure, TGT is excelling.
