No analyst rebutted this. (3) Leverage remains structurally heavy: net debt/EBITDA of 3.3x-4.8x and interest expense consuming over half of TTM net income, with a $1.2B refinancing tranche due within 12 months. The Conservative analyst's refinancing-cost stress test (100-200bps higher spread = $111-222M in incremental annual interest) is directionally sound and unrebutted in substance — the Aggressive analyst's counter-claim that rate cuts are "already priced in" is internally contradictory with their own upside case built on rate cuts materializing, a flaw the Neutral analyst correctly calls out. (4) The $11.20 forward EPS / 10x forward P/E support requires a ~$1.7B earnings swing from a -$0.62 quarterly loss; even the Neutral analyst's most generous scenario modeling tops out near $10.80-12.00 EPS only in a 35%-probability best case, while the Neutral analyst's own probability-weighted scenarios show two of four cases (55% combined probability) producing 37-71% downside to $45-99/share. That asymmetry — thin, speculative upside versus large, quantifiable downside — is the crux. Technicals (golden cross, RSI 65.55, single-day volume spike) are real per all three analysts but are explicitly flagged by the Aggressive analyst himself as showing bearish MACD divergence (-0.21) even as price makes highs, meaning the technical picture is already softening under the surface. Consistent with the prior NRG playbook (Sept 2024 and Dec 2024 Underweight trims, both validated by subsequent alpha), the discipline of trimming into unconfirmed-catalyst strength while retaining residual optionality has proven the correct structure for this specific stock's recurring pattern of speculative forward-EPS reratings colliding with real leverage/cash constraints. But the balance of evidence — unrebutted cash-burn math, an unrebutted buyback-during-burn red flag, and a forward EPS bridge that no analyst could credibly construct — keeps this at Underweight rather than Hold or Sell.
