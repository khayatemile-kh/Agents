**Company Name:** Duke Energy Corporation
**Ticker:** DUK
**Exchange:** NYSE (NYQ)
**Sector:** Utilities
**Industry Classification:** Utilities - Regulated Electric

Duke Energy is one of the largest electric utility companies in the United States, operating as a regulated public utility. The company's business model centers on generating, transmitting, and distributing electricity to millions of customers across the Southeast, Midwest, and other regions. As a regulated utility, DUK's revenue is largely protected through regulatory frameworks, providing stable and predictable earnings.
