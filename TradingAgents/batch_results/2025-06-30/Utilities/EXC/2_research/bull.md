### The Utility Economics Model: CapEx → Regulated Returns

Here's what bears misunderstand about regulated utilities:

**Capital Expenditure is NOT consumption; it's *investment that earns regulated returns*.**

This is **sustainable leverage**, not reckless borrowing. The debt is financing productive assets that earn regulated returns—exactly what rating agencies and investors expect from utilities.

**Bull Point:** EXC's $7.1B annual CapEx, financed partly through regulated debt, generates contractual 8-10% returns. This translates to ~$640M incremental annual earnings.
