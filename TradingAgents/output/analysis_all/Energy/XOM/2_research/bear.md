3. **"Interest Coverage of 144x Proves It's Safe"—This Is Cherry-Picking At Its Finest**

The bull calculated: Net Income ($32.76B) / Interest Expense ($227M) = 144x

- With normalized OCF of $12B and ~$5B annual debt service, coverage is only **2.4x**—not alarming, but not fortress-like either
