The bull says: "1.16x Net Debt/EBITDA is healthy investment-grade leverage."

**Q1 2026 (Pre-Acquisition):**
- Total Debt: ~$6B (estimated)
- EBITDA: ~$3.5B (estimated)
- Net Leverage: ~1.7x

**Q2 2026 (Post-Acquisition):**
