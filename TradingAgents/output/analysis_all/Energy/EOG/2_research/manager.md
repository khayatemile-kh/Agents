So this isn't a pure sell case—EOG isn't distressed, and cutting the position entirely would ignore real balance-sheet quality and cash-flow strength today. But given oil price cyclicality is the single biggest swing factor for this name and the bull never adequately quantifies downside protection beyond 'the balance sheet is strong,' the prudent stance is to trim exposure into current strength rather than add or hold at full size. This lets the trader capture upside via retained partial position while respecting cyclical downside risk the bear articulated more rigorously.
