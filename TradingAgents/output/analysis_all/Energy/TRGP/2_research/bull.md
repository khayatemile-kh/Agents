## PILLAR 1: THE EXXONMOBIL CATALYST – A TRANSFORMATIONAL AGREEMENT

### Why This Deal Matters

On August 17, 2026, Targa announced a **20-year integrated midstream agreement with ExxonMobil**, covering natural gas gathering, processing, NGL transportation, and fractionation across the Delaware and Midland basins through **2046**.

This isn't just another contract. Let me explain why:

**A. Revenue Stability vs. Commodity Cycles**

Traditional midstream companies face cyclical pressures tied to energy prices and producer activity. But this deal is **fee-based**, not margin-based. That distinction is everything.

- **What does this mean?** Targa gets paid for services rendered *regardless of whether oil/gas prices are $50 or $150 per barrel.* 
- **Historical context:** During the 2014-2016 oil crash, midstream companies that relied on margin-based contracts saw profitability collapse. Companies with higher fee-based exposure weathered the downturn substantially better.
- **TTM EBITDA of $5.5 billion with $16.74 billion revenue** already demonstrates the company's cash-generation machine. This ExxonMobil contract extends that machine's runway through 2046.

**B. Lock-in of Volumes & Acreage Dedications**

The agreement includes "extensive acreage dedications" from ExxonMobil. Translation: **ExxonMobil commits to shipping material volumes through Targa's infrastructure for two decades.** This is not a handshake—it's a binding commercial framework.

- ExxonMobil is one of the world's largest integrated energy companies, with a market cap exceeding $400 billion.
- Counterparty credit risk is negligible.
- Volume predictability allows Targa to *plan capital spending* with confidence and optimize its asset base.

**C. Strategic Positioning in the Permian**

The Permian Basin is where the energy industry's capital is concentrated. It's the highest-return onshore acreage globally, with sub-$30/BOE development costs for many operators.

- RBC Capital Markets specifically validated Targa's "growth runway extension" via the ExxonMobil deal and three new Permian projects.
- **Analyst Commentary:** The institutional sell-side views this as de-risking Targa's growth profile.
- Three new projects are already in motion to handle ExxonMobil volumes, demonstrating management's confidence in execution.
