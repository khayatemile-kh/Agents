Unlike a typical industrial company, **KMI's competitive moat is almost impossible to replicate:**

- **Network effects:** Producers and refiners MUST use KMI's pipelines because they're the most efficient, economically rational paths for energy movement. Switching costs are prohibitively high.

**Translation:** KMI has a 30+ year structural moat that protects its cash flows from competitive pressure. This is why the company can raise dividends reliably—the business is defensible.
