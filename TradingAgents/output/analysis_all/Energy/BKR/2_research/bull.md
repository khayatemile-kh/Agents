Meanwhile, BKR's *margins* actually improve in higher rate environments because energy companies prioritize efficient, proven technologies over unproven alternatives. That favors established players like BKR.

And again—**the turbine business is insulated from this.** Data centers are raising unlimited capital for AI infrastructure; rates don't matter. That's the whole point of the diversification.
