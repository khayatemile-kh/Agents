**Divergence Interpretation:**

This is a classic **retail-leads-institutions** setup. Retail traders on StockTwits are discussing data-center power demand and technical support, while institutional news flow remains focused on near-term underperformance. This mismatch suggests either:

1. **Retail is ahead of the narrative curve** — The data-center energy demand story (a structural, multi-year thesis) has not yet been formally validated or integrated into sell-side research or news cycle. Retail traders are positioning ahead of institutional recognition.

2. **Retail is chasing a thesis without institutional validation** — The data-center angle may be accurate but is not yet material enough to move stock price or drive headline coverage. Risk that retail expectations exceed near-term catalysts.

3. **Price action is signaling institutional buying despite negative headlines** — The "nibbling" observation by @Radiodude60 (2026-09-01) aligns with technical support; if true, institutions are quietly accumulating despite the negative 6-month performance narrative.
