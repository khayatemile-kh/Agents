What does this tell us? Either:

1. **Q1 was truly anomalous** (one-time events, working capital swings, project completion), or
2. **Q2 signals the real normalized run-rate**, which is substantially weaker than the trader or aggressive analyst want to admit.

The aggressive analyst wants to split the difference and assume $1.0–1.2B quarterly FCF going forward. But there's a problem: **we have no basis for that assumption other than hope.** The most recent hard data point is Q2 at $398M. That's the only current-quarter number we have.
