### KEY DEVELOPMENTS

#### 1. **Major Portfolio Transaction: $925M Chobani & Asset Sale**

**What Happened:**
- KDP sold its **entire equity stake in Chobani for $800 million** back to the yogurt maker
- Concurrent sale of **Allentown, Pennsylvania manufacturing facility for $125 million**
- **Total proceeds: $925 million**
- Transaction announced Tuesday and covered extensively across financial media (WSJ, Barron's, Quartz, Food Dive)

**Strategic Significance:**
- **Debt reduction focus:** The company explicitly stated proceeds will reduce debt ahead of the planned 2027 coffee business spinoff
- **Portfolio streamlining:** Moves align with broader strategy to rationalize operations and focus on core beverage brands (Dr Pepper, Snapple, etc.) and coffee (before spinoff)
- **Asset optimization:** Pennsylvania plant acquisition by Chobani ($1.2B investment planned) signals facility was non-core for KDP but valuable to buyer

**Investor Implications:**
- **Positive:** $925M in cash reduces financial leverage, improves balance sheet flexibility for spinoff
- **Neutral-to-Negative:** Signals limited organic growth optionality; company relying on asset sales rather than operational expansion
- **Timeline:** Transaction likely completes Q4 2026, supporting spinoff execution in early 2027
