## 1. VALUATION: THE PREMIUM IS UNJUSTIFIED & VULNERABLE TO COMPRESSION

### The Bull's ROE "Moat" Argument is Deeply Misleading

**The Bull's Claim:** "PG's 30.29% ROE proves competitive moat strength."

**The Bear's Rebuttal:** This is the **most dangerous misreading of PG's financials** I've seen. Here's the reality:

#### A. ROE Inflation from Massive Share Buybacks = Accounting Illusion

Let me break down what's actually happening:

- **Total Stockholders' Equity:** $54.08 billion
- **Treasury Stock (Cumulative):** ($143.01 billion)
- **Net Equity:** ~$54B (after buybacks)

PG has repurchased **$143 billion in treasury stock**—a staggering amount relative to market cap of $343B. This creates a **mathematical illusion of high ROE**:

**ROE = Net Income / Shareholders' Equity**

When you *continuously shrink the denominator* through buybacks while earnings are *flat to declining*, ROE artificially inflates. **It's not that PG's business is generating 30% returns—it's that PG is dividing earnings by a dwindling equity base.**

**Compare to competitors:**
- **Kimberly-Clark (KMB):** 25.1% ROE (also buyback-heavy, but with 3.42% yield)
- **Reckitt Benckiser (RBGLY):** 14.3% ROE (lower, more honest)

KMB has a *higher* dividend yield (3.42% vs. 2.98%) and comparable ROE with less leverage. If ROE truly signaled moat strength, why accept PG's lower yield? **The answer: ROE metrics are misleading for heavily leveraged, buyback-heavy companies.**

#### B. The Real Story: Earnings Per Share Growth is Anemic

Let me look at what actually matters—**real EPS growth**:

| Period | Net Income | Diluted Shares Outstanding | Diluted EPS |
|--------|-----------|---------------------------|------------|
| FY2024 | $14.2B | 2,525M | $5.63 |
| FY2025 | $15.4B | 2,435M | $6.33 |
| FY2026 | $16.05B | 2,425M | $6.62 |
| **3-Year CAGR** | **6.1%** | **(-2.0% annually)** | **8.3%** |

Here's the critical insight: **PG's EPS "growth" of 8.3% CAGR is entirely attributable to share count reduction, not business growth.**

- **Net Income Growth:** 6.1% CAGR
- **EPS Growth (with buybacks):** 8.3% CAGR
- **Difference:** 2.2 percentage points = the *buyback boost*

**So what's the actual organic business growth?** ~6%. That's being valued at 22.3x P/E, or **implying a 4.5% yield on growth**—barely above the 10-year Treasury.
