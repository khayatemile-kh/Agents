When technicals are contested but fundamentals show severe, worsening balance-sheet stress with negative FCF for multiple consecutive quarters and 90%+ cash depletion, the fundamental red flags should dominate the call. This tips the scale toward a cautious stance rather than a genuine 'balanced' hold - the bear's cash/leverage argument is more concrete and less speculative than the bull's momentum argument, and the bull hasn't neutralized the liquidity risk, only offered a hoped-for resolution.
