**Ticker:** DG (NYSE)

---

## Executive Summary

DG has entered a **bullish sentiment environment** characterized by strong post-earnings momentum, broad institutional analyst upgrades, and notably positive retail trading activity. The earnings beat on 2026-08-27/28 triggered a narrative shift: the market is now treating DG's turnaround as **credible and sustainable**. However, there is a subtle but important cross-source divergence: **retail traders are more exuberant than news-flow framing**, suggesting late-stage momentum enthusiasm alongside legitimate operational improvements.
