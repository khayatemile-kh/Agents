1. **Margin expansion has limits.** The company already showed margin expansion from 2023-2025:

**Critical realization:** The forward EPS of $1.24 is based on **assumptions that have not materialized** and likely won't. It's a management guidance number, not a market consensus. And it's priced into a stock that has already compressed the multiple.
