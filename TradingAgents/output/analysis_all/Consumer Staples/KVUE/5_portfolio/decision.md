But the neutral analyst's synthesis is decisive here: tail risk to the dividend is real (a plausible 15-20% cut, not the aggressive analyst's overstated 30-40%) specifically in the deal-break scenario, since a guidance miss would pressure management to rebuild balance-sheet credibility.

Sentiment (3.5/10, low confidence, minimal retail engagement) was read by all three analysts as genuinely neutral-to-uncertain rather than a directional signal — it confirms the market has not yet priced in either deal outcome, which is consistent with holding a reduced position rather than making a binary bet either way.

Net synthesis: the risk-reward is asymmetric to the downside (12-20% deal-break downside vs. 8-10% deal-close upside per the neutral analyst's own framing), technicals confirm near-term momentum deterioration, and the bull/conservative case for re-rating upside rests on an unproven multiple expansion and a deal-contingent EPS number.
