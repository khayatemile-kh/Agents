2. **Pricing Power**: Peers (REYN) are successfully implementing price increases to offset commodity inflation.
3. **Economic Resilience**: Low recession risk (8%) supports consistent demand.

**Headwinds Facing CLX:**

3. **Structural Margin Pressure**: Without productivity gains and pricing discipline, CLX risks margin compression.
4. **No Near-Term Rate Relief**: Absence of Fed rate cuts through Q3 2026 removes a potential catalyst for consumer confidence uplift.
