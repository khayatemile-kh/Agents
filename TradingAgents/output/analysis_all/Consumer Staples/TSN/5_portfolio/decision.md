Where the bull is legitimately right — and this keeps the call from being an outright Sell — the antitrust settlement genuinely removes tail risk, the forward P/E (~12.5x) is cheap versus the 34x trailing multiple, the 3.71% dividend yield offers real income while waiting, and technicals show ATR compression (from $1.77 to $1.30) and a modest RSI bounce (35.80→38.85) consistent with stabilization rather than further breakdown. But the neutral analyst's key caveat applies: this stabilization setup only supports mean reversion "if fundamentals don't deteriorate further," and volume during the recent decline reflects distribution, not accumulation. Net-net, the preponderance of granular evidence — unchanged guidance midpoint, three-quarter sequential margin decline, thin absolute margins, sequential earnings deterioration, tangible leverage well above sector norms, and depleting cash reserves — outweighs the bull's valuation-optionality arguments, which are about de-risking and cheapness rather than an actual inflection in fundamentals.
