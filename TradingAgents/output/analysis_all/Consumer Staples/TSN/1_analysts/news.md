### Focus: Tyson Foods, Inc. (TSN) — Consumer Defensive / Farm Products

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Tyson Foods (TSN) is navigating a complex operating environment shaped by **structural livestock constraints, geopolitical trade tensions, and shifting consumer demand dynamics**. While the company has demonstrated pricing power and raised full-year revenue guidance to 2.5%–3.5%, it faces headwinds from beef market softness, recently resolved antitrust litigation, and weak consumer demand in the broader protein sector. Market sentiment remains cautiously optimistic, with TSN included in Goldman Sachs' Conviction List as a dividend-paying value play.

---

## KEY FINDINGS

### 1. **TYSON FOODS OPERATIONAL UPDATES (Past Week)**

**Positive Developments:**
- **Guidance Raise**: TSN revised FY2026 revenue guidance upward to **2.5%–3.5%** (from prior 2%–4%), reflecting successful price realization across protein categories
- **Chicken Strength Offset**: Poultry (chicken) remains a bright spot, offsetting beef market weakness
- **Goldman Sachs Recognition**: TSN appears on Goldman Sachs' current Conviction List as a dividend-paying value pick, appealing to income-focused investors
- **Broiler Antitrust Settlement**: **Final court approval** of landmark broiler chicken settlement reduces long-term legal risk and clarifies compliance obligations

- **Record Ground Beef Prices**: Ground beef prices are approaching $7/lb, driven by feed costs and livestock scarcity—not corporate "greed" or tariffs
