Net assessment: the preponderance of unrebutted, quantifiable evidence (flat/negative profitability, repeated impairments, below-peer margins, and a fully-consumed FCF profile against a near-term debt wall) tips this toward reducing exposure into the rally, while the absence of an imminent, confirmed default/dividend-cut catalyst (acknowledged even by the Aggressive analyst) argues against a full Sell/exit at this stage. This is a trim-and-monitor situation, not a durable buy-the-breakout or full-exit scenario.
