**What This Reveals:**

1. **Q2 is seasonally strong**—FCF spiked 67% QoQ to $344.8M. The bull is cherry-picking a peak quarter.
2. **Underlying trend is flat to negative**—Excluding seasonal variation, OCF is declining. Q1 2026 at $255.1M was dramatically weak.

- But Q4 2025 elevated CapEx suggests the trend is **stabilizing or declining**, not strengthening

**Year-over-year growth from Q2 2025 to Q2 2026:** Only **+2.3%** ($699.0M to $715.3M)

This capital return strategy only works if revenue is **growing robustly**. At 2.3% YoY revenue growth, SBAC is returning capital as if it's a mature, shrinking business—which is exactly what it appears to be.

### The 5G "Tailwind" is Not Materializing

Here's where the bull's secular thesis breaks down: **If 5G densification was driving material lease growth, we'd see it in revenue.** We're not.

**Reality Check:**
- Q2 2026 revenue ($715.3M) vs. Q2 2025 ($699.0M) = +2.3% growth
- This is **below GDP growth** and well below typical tower operator expectations (4-6% organic growth pre-2020)

**Why the disappointment?**

1. **5G buildout peaked in 2022-2023**—Most carriers completed initial 5G deployment. The "densification" phase is incremental and slow.
2. **Fiber is cannibalizing tower demand**—AT&T, Verizon, and others are shifting to fiber small cells, which bypass SBAC's towers.
3. **Carrier capex is maturing**—After years of heavy 5G spending, carriers are now optimizing existing networks, not building new ones.
4. **No new major lease wins announced**—If 5G densification was real, we'd see new tenants or material lease expansions. The silence is deafening.

**Verdict:** The bull is relying on a secular trend that peaked 2-3 years ago and is now **normalizing into single-digit growth rates**.
