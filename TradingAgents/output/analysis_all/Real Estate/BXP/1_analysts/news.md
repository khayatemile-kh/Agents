### SECTOR-SPECIFIC HEADWINDS & TAILWINDS

#### Headwinds for Office REITs (including BXP):
1. **Structural Shift to Remote/Hybrid Work:** Long-standing trend reducing corporate demand for traditional office space
2. **Higher Interest Rates (89% no-cut probability):** Directly impacts REIT financing costs and tenant willingness to expand occupancy
3. **Post-Earnings Sell-off:** Market may be pricing in limitations to earnings recovery despite positive guidance

#### Tailwinds:
1. **Accelerating Earnings Guidance (UBS):** Forward earnings growth suggests operational improvements (rent growth, occupancy recovery, or cost discipline)
2. **Low Recession Risk:** Reduces probability of severe tenant defaults or demand collapse
3. **Portfolio Quality:** Boston Properties is among the highest-quality office REIT platforms with premium Boston, NYC, San Francisco, and D.C. markets
