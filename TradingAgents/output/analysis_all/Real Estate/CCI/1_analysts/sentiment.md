2. **Macro sensitivity** — as a yield/dividend play, CCI is sensitive to rate expectations. Any jump in Treasury yields or economic slowdown could reverse the mild bullish tilt.
3. **Sample-size fragility** — with only 3–4 bullish retail messages, a single negative catalyzer (missed guidance, dividend cut speculation, etc.) could shift the tone rapidly.
4. **Satellite disruption not definitively resolved** — the news reframes it as manageable, not eliminated. Unexpected technological progress by SpaceX could reignite fear.
