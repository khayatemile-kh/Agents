## THE AGGRESSIVE ANALYST'S FATAL FLAW: THEY'RE TREATING A COMPLEX REIT AS A SIMPLE LIQUIDATION STORY

Let me walk through why their "fraudulent earnings" claim requires serious pushback. Yes, Q2 2026 reported $162M net income includes a $71M PPE gain. Q1 2026 includes $78M in special items. Q4 2025 had unusual items. But the aggressive analyst conveniently excludes one critical fact from the data: **this is how REITs operate.** Real Estate Investment Trusts generate income from two sources: operations AND capital recycling. Selling mature timberland parcels and redeploying capital into higher-yielding properties is not a "broken business model"—it's the standard playbook for REITs across the entire sector.

Timber REITs, specifically, have a harvest-and-replant cycle. When you harvest mature timber, you record a gain. That's not fraud. That's forest management. Strip out all realization gains from any REIT—Prologis, Realty Income, STORE Capital—and you'd paint every one of them as a collapsing business. But that's not how the sector functions.

The dividend payout ratio of 129% of GAAP earnings? Yes, that's elevated. But it's not 129% of cash earnings from operations. So the aggressive analyst has conflated GAAP earnings (which include non-cash items and realization gains) with cash sustainability. That's a category error, and it's led them to a conclusion that doesn't survive scrutiny.
