## II. STRUCTURAL HEADWINDS INVALIDATE THE BULL'S NARRATIVE

### A. Interest Rate Environment Is a REIT Killer—And It's Locked In

**The Bull's Blind Spot:**

The bull addresses interest rates only briefly, asserting that "size creates advantage in capital access." This is dangerously naive.

**Here's the Reality:**

From the macroeconomic data:
- **Probability of zero Fed rate cuts in 2026:** 89%
- **Prediction market pricing:** No rate cuts likely through year-end

But here's what matters more: **even if the Fed cuts rates in 2027, we're starting from a higher rate environment than historical norms.**

**Why This Matters for REIT Valuations:**

REITs trade on **cap rate expansion.** When long-term interest rates are elevated:

1. **Cap Rates Rise:** Property valuations compress because the required yield increases
2. **Debt Becomes Expensive:** WELL's $19.98 billion debt becomes more expensive to refinance
3. **Dividend Yields Compress:** Investors demand higher yields, pushing WELL stock down
4. **Capital Deployment Slows:** The company can't deploy capital as efficiently if cap rates are elevated

**The Math:**

If WELL's properties were valued at a 4.5% cap rate in 2022 (low-rate era) and are now valued at a 5.5% cap rate (normalized rate era), that's an **automatic 18% valuation compression** on the property base, regardless of occupancy.

**WELL's response?** Aggressive acquisition. The company deployed $5+ billion to buy properties in a rising-rate environment. This is **precisely the wrong time to be aggressive with leverage.** The bull calls this "hitting the gas on growth." I call it **catching a falling knife.**
