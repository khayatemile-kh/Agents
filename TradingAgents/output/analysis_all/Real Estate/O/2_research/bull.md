REITs are required to distribute 90%+ of taxable income. They do this by **deploying leverage efficiently** to amplify returns on equity. A healthy REIT typically operates at 50-80x leverage on an equity basis—O is squarely in this range.

What matters more:

1. **Refinancing Flexibility:** O just amended its **$1.85B revolving credit facility** with Wells Fargo and Toronto Dominion. This signals:
   - Banks are willing to extend credit (confidence vote)

3. **Current Ratio:** 1.528 indicates adequate short-term liquidity. The $2.76B in current debt maturing soon has clear coverage from operating cash flow.

**Translation:** O has the balance sheet strength to refinance comfortably and maintain dividend growth even in a higher-rate environment.
