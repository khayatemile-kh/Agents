Net assessment: the long-term fundamental income story (dividend safety, defensive NNN model) is intact and supports NOT selling out of the name, but the debt trajectory, narrowing coverage under refinancing risk, and the accelerating negative technical momentum argue against adding to or holding full-size exposure right now. That combination — durable long-term business but rising near-term financial and technical risk — is best captured by trimming exposure now (Underweight) rather than adding (Buy/Overweight) or leaving unchanged (Hold), while not going as far as an outright Sell given the multi-decade cash flow durability and defensive tenant base.
