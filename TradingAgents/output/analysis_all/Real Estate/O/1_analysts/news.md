### SECTOR & COMPANY-SPECIFIC RISKS & OPPORTUNITIES

#### **Opportunities:**

1. **Dividend Aristocrat Status:** O's 32-year dividend growth streak attracts ESG-conscious, income-focused capital; tax-deferred retirement accounts prioritize it.
2. **Financing Flexibility:** $1.85B amended credit facility provides optionality for accretive acquisitions or refinancing at favorable terms.
3. **Defensive Business Model:** Convenience stores, grocery, and drug retail are recession-resistant tenant sectors.
4. **Valuation Divergence:** If intrinsic value models are correct, there is embedded margin of safety below current prices.

#### **Risks:**

1. **Valuation Ambiguity:** Richness relative to market multiples may cap near-term appreciation unless intrinsic value thesis plays out.
2. **Interest Rate Sensitivity:** While no cuts are priced in for 2026, a future Fed easing cycle would likely favor equities over high-dividend REITs, potentially causing outflows.
3. **Retail Sector Headwinds:** E-commerce and off-mall retail trends persist; O's tenant mix (especially convenience and drugstores) has some structural pressures, though the company has navigated these well.
4. **Tax Drag:** Ordinary dividend treatment outside tax-deferred accounts erodes after-tax returns for high-net-worth individuals.
