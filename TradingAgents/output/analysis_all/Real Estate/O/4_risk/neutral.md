## WHERE THE AGGRESSIVE ANALYST OVERSTATES THE CASE

But here's where you go off the rails: you're claiming this debt accumulation signals "unsustainable" leverage without acknowledging that O's refinancing just happened at better terms. - Leverage is elevated but manageable in current environment

- Valuation is premium relative to market, fair relative to intrinsic value
- Technical setup shows exhaustion but not capitulation

The question isn't whether to sell 20-25% or hold. The question is: **What is O now, and what will it be?**

- Multiple compression (5.30% yield becomes less exotic as alternatives improve)
- Dividend growth deceleration (from 4-5% to 2-3% as growth CapEx declines)
- Stock price stability with lower volatility (income stability over appreciation)

This isn't a crisis narrative or a "buy forever" narrative. It's a repositioning.
