- Q2 2025: $244M (peak seasonal)
- Q3 2025: $218.7M (-10.3% sequential)
- Q4 2025: $203.9M (-6.7% sequential)

Now here's the critical question: is that Q1 decline consistent with normal seasonality, or is it worse than it should be? The Aggressive Analyst assumes the worst; the Conservative Analyst assumes it's normal. The reality is we need historical Q1 2024 and Q1 2025 numbers to actually know.
