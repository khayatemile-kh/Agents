**The Math:**
- A buyer today needs roughly **$450,000 in purchasing power** to afford the median US home at 7.0% rates

- **Translation:** Middle-income renters are being priced out of ownership **permanently** — at least until rates fall meaningfully

**The Result for INVH:**
- **Tenant Duration Extension:** Renters forced into longer tenancies (7+ years) = more stable, predictable cash flows
- **Pricing Power:** Scarcity of affordable rental inventory = annual rent growth of 3-5% (vs. 1-2% historical), which INVH has demonstrated in Q2 2026 with revenue growth of 9.1% YoY

**My response:** Yes, **short-term** financing costs are elevated. But INVH's long-term competitive moat—owning thousands of rental properties that generate inflation-protected cash flows—is *strengthening*, not weakening, as housing becomes unaffordable.
