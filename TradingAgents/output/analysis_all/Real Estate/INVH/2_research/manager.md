So this isn't a broken business, but it is one where near-term earnings quality and leverage trends argue against adding exposure until we see acquisition spreads and core (ex-gains) FFO stabilize. Net: the bear's forensic dissection of earnings quality (60%+ of net income from property-sale gains) and the operating-income deceleration versus revenue growth are the more decisive, evidence-backed points in this round, tipping the scale toward caution rather than accumulation. That argues for trimming/capping exposure rather than adding, but the still-positive FCF generation, dividend coverage (even if thinning), and intact long-term technical trend keep this short of an outright sell.
