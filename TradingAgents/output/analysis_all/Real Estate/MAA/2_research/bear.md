You argued: *"At 7% mortgage rates, renting becomes economically superior for millions of Americans, strengthening multifamily demand."*

Here's the economic chain you missed:

1. **Higher mortgage rates → Home prices must compress or buyers disappear**
2. **Home price compression or stagnation → Household wealth destruction for existing homeowners**
3. **Wealth destruction → Reduced consumer spending and credit confidence**
4. **Reduced credit confidence → Potential renters have weaker employment/income trends**
5. **Weaker employment trends + Higher rents = Deteriorating renter credit quality and delinquency rates**
