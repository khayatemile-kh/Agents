Let's be concrete about the math:

If HST has approximately $1.4-1.5B of debt rolling over in the next 6 months (a reasonable assumption for a REIT), and if refinancing rates have moved up 150-200 basis points from the historical average, then:

- Old debt at ~3.5% = $52-53M annual interest on $1.5B
- New debt at ~5.0-5.5% = $75-82M annual interest on $1.5B
- **Incremental cost: $23-30M per quarter, or $92-120M annually**

That's not trivial. That's roughly 9-12% of TTM net income. And that's *before* we account for any operational softness in Q4 when hotel occupancy can decline due to holiday seasonality and travel unpredictability.

- Q2 2027 (estimated): $240M (normalization)

But subtract the refinancing impact of $92-120M in incremental interest, and we're looking at **approximately $1.06-1.09B** in normalized, forward-looking net income. We need to be protecting against that tail risk, not dismissing it.
