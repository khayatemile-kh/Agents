The bear didn't present a credible alternative valuation case showing GD is expensive on an absolute basis—only that the growth assumptions might not fully materialize, which is a reason for tempering the size of conviction, not for going bearish. Given the balance of a strong, low-leverage balance sheet, genuine backlog-driven revenue visibility, reasonable (not stretched) valuation, and only a moderate, near-term technical pullback with real but not thesis-breaking risks (congressional funding risk, fixed-price margin risk, competitive pressure), the appropriate stance is constructive but not maximum-conviction Buy.
