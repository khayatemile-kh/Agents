## **COMPANY PROFILE**

Norfolk Southern Corporation is one of the largest freight railroad companies in the United States, operating an extensive network for the transportation of goods across major corridors. The company generates revenue from freight services across multiple commodities including intermodal, coal, merchandise, and auto carriers. NSC operates strategically positioned rail networks connecting major economic centers, making it a critical infrastructure provider in North American supply chains.
