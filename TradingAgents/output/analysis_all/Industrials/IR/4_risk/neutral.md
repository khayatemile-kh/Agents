Q2 2025 gross profit: $1.888B × 43.7% = $824.7M
Q2 2026 gross profit: $2.049B × 42.1% = $862.6M

Okay, so gross profit dollars grew from $824.7M to $862.6M—that's $37.9M or about 4.6% growth. But here's the problem: the revenue grew 8.5%, and gross profit only grew 4.6%.
