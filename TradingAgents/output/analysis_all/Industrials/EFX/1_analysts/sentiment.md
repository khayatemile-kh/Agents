## Dominant Narrative Themes

1. **Settlement & Liability Risk** (Primary):  
   The $100M credit score error settlement is the headline event. Framed as a "line under past issues" in recovery language, but it underscores **execution risk and reputational wear** for a business (credit reporting) where trust is paramount.

2. **Long-Term Underperformance** (Secondary):  
   27.6% decline over 5 years is material and suggests either structural headwinds (competitive pressure, regulatory tightening, client concentration) or cyclical weakness. No deep commentary provided on root causes.

3. **Valuation Disconnect** (Tertiary):  
   Despite the 5-year decline, the stock is deemed "fully valued," implying limited margin of safety and suggesting the market has already priced in recovery expectations. This is a **contrarian red flag**: institutional buyers expect limited upside, even after a long drawdown.

4. **Dividend Maintenance** (Supportive but Minor):  
   Management continues to pay, signaling confidence in cash generation, but it does not offset the three bearish narratives above.
