This isn't a situation where oversold RSI is the *only* signal. We have **multiple trend deterioration signals converging**: the break of the 50 SMA, the collapse of the 10 EMA, the widening MACD histogram. When trend-following indicators all break in the same direction simultaneously, that's not capitulation—**that's a trend reversal from bullish to bearish.**

The "low volume on the selloff" argument also doesn't hold up. Volume is *below* the 60-day average, yes—but that's true of many trading days. What matters is whether volume is **increasing or decreasing relative to the price move.** If price is falling on below-average volume, that can mean either of two things: (1) a small group of sellers is pushing price down (tactical opportunity), or (2) there's a lack of buyers at any price level (structural weakness). We don't have real-time market microstructure data to distinguish between these. In the absence of clarity, assuming capitulation is optimistic.
