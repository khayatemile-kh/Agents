## COMPANY PROFILE

**Johnson Controls International plc** is a multinational conglomerate headquartered in Dublin, Ireland, with significant operations across North America, Europe, and Asia-Pacific. The company operates three primary business segments:

1. **Building Solutions (HVAC, Controls, Security)** - Core building automation and climate control systems
2. **Global Products** - Industrial equipment and components
3. **Energy Storage & Power Solutions** - Battery and renewable energy systems

The company serves diverse end-markets including commercial real estate, residential construction, industrial manufacturing, and utility customers.
