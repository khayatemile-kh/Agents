**Recommendation**: Hold

**Rationale**: Both sides make legitimate points that roughly offset each other, so I can't justify a strong directional call here. The bull is right that the current fundamentals are genuinely strong: revenue growth in the 20%+ range, gross/operating margin expansion of several hundred bps YoY, healthy FCF conversion (~64%), and a real oligopoly moat in single-crystal/directional-solidification casting that isn't trivially replicated. The SpaceX threat, even in the bear's more aggressive 18-24 month framing, is still a segment-level risk (space/defense turbines are a minority of revenue) rather than an existential one today—Howmet's core commercial aviation and defense volumes are intact and backed by multi-year OEM backlogs.

That said, the bear's pushback is credible and can't be waved away. A trailing P/E near 55x and forward P/E near 39x leave very little room for error, and the bull's own PEG argument only holds if 30-40% forward earnings growth is durable—which is exactly what's in question given (a) the cyclical nature of the current aerospace/defense upcycle, (b) the risk that customers use the mere credible threat of SpaceX in-housing to renegotiate pricing well before any blades actually ship, and (c) margin expansion that has been running on tight-supply pricing power that could compress quickly if that leverage erodes. The bear's point that pricing damage can precede actual competitive shipment by quarters is a real dynamic in aerospace supply chains and deserves weight.

Given the debate produced two well-supported but conflicting narratives—strong current execution and moat versus stretched valuation and a credible (if uncertain-timing) competitive/pricing risk—the prudent stance is to stay balanced rather than commit capital aggressively in either direction. This is a case where the evidence genuinely cuts both ways: the fundamentals don't support an outright Sell, but the valuation and SpaceX overhang don't support adding aggressively into a name already priced for near-perfect execution.

**Strategic Actions**: Maintain current HWM exposure rather than adding or trimming meaningfully until there is more clarity. Specifically: (1) Do not chase the post-selloff move or add new capital at current elevated multiples (trailing P/E ~55x, forward ~39x) given how much flawless execution is already priced in. (2) Do not exit the position either, since near-term fundamentals (20%+ revenue growth, margin expansion, strong FCF, buyback support) remain intact and the SpaceX in-house threat is not yet operationally material. (3) Set a watch-list trigger on customer commentary/contract renegotiation signals from GE, Rolls-Royce, CFM, and Blue Origin regarding SpaceX-driven pricing pressure — an early warning that would justify moving to Underweight. (4) Also watch gross/operating margin trends next 1-2 quarters: continued expansion supports upgrading toward Overweight, while a reversal toward the low-30s gross margin range confirms the bear thesis and warrants trimming. (5) Size the position at current levels (no incremental buys or sells) and revisit the rating after the next earnings print and any concrete SpaceX capability milestones.
