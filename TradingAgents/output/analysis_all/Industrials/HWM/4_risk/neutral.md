And here's where your logic really breaks down: you're using a *lagging indicator* (MACD) to justify ignoring *current momentum reality*. Yes, MACD lags. But that means when it finally flips positive, the move will already be partially played out. The smarter question is: why hasn't momentum at least *started* to stabilize if this is truly a capitulation bottom? A stock genuinely reversing off a panic low shows MACD flattening at minimum, often showing early histogram expansion toward zero. HWM is showing neither. An RSI spike to 28 followed by a bounce to 37 in a *single day* isn't capitulation confirmation—it's *volatility noise*. True capitulation happens when RSI *stays* in the oversold zone (below 30) for multiple days while price consolidates or makes higher lows. One-day spikes and bounces are exactly what you see in bear traps. The real risk scenario is RSI falling back through 30 into the 20s—potentially much lower—on the next leg down. Your confidence is based on a single day's action. That's not a foundation.
