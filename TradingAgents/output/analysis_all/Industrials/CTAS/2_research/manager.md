The asymmetric risk/reward the bull cites (1.4% downside to Bollinger support vs 7-10% upside to highs) is a reasonable near-term technical setup, but it's thin support for a full conviction Buy given the bear's structural concerns about labor cost inflation, mix shift toward lower-margin segments, and PE-backed competitive pressure eating into pricing power over the next 12-24 months. Given genuinely strong current fundamentals colliding with a valuation that already prices in continued outperformance and a bear case that credibly explains why the next catalyst may underwhelm, the right call is to stay balanced rather than lean hard either direction. This isn't a stock to add aggressively to at these multiples given decelerating guidance raises, but it's also not a name to cut given real margin and earnings quality trends and defensive characteristics - existing holders should stay put and reassess after the next earnings print resolves the beat/raise magnitude question.
