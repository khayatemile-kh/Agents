## PART II: THE MARGIN "EXPANSION" MIRAGE — OPERATIONAL DETERIORATION DISGUISED AS IMPROVEMENT

### Gross Margins Are Improving, But Net Margins Are Collapsing

The bull makes a big deal about +180 bps of gross margin improvement (40.6% → 42.4%). I'm not going to dispute the math—margins did improve. But here's what the bull carefully avoided highlighting:

#### Operating Leverage Is Working *Against* PNR, Not For It

| Metric | Q2 2026 | Q2 2025 | Change | Implication |
|--------|---------|---------|--------|-------------|
| **Revenue** | $932.6M | $1,123.1M | -$190.5M (-17.1%) | ⚠️ Demand weakness |
| **Gross Profit** | $395.2M | $455.8M | -$60.6M (-13.3%) | ⚠️ Volume decline |
| **Gross Margin %** | 42.4% | 40.6% | +180 bps | ✓ Improved |
| **Operating Income** | $165.9M | $217.9M | -$52.0M (-23.9%) | ⚠️⚠️ Earnings collapsed |
| **Operating Margin %** | 17.8% | 19.4% | -160 bps | ⚠️ Leverage failure |
| **Net Income** | $128.6M | $148.5M | -$19.9M (-13.4%) | ⚠️ Bottom-line pressure |
| **Diluted EPS** | $0.80 | $0.90 | -$0.10 (-11.1%) | ⚠️ Per-share erosion |

**Read this carefully:** Despite gross margins *expanding* by +180 bps, **operating income collapsed by -$52M (-23.9%),** and **operating margin contracted by -160 bps.**

This reveals the ugly truth: **The margin expansion is being overwhelmed by fixed cost deleverage.** Pentair has significant fixed operating expenses (SG&A, R&D, manufacturing overhead), and a -17% revenue decline is forcing these costs to absorb the entire hit. The result:

- Gross margins up → Operating margins down
- Profitability declining in absolute dollars
- Per-share earnings down -11.1% despite "operational improvements"

**This is the opposite of a healthy business trajectory.** This is a company with bloated fixed costs struggling to maintain profitability during a demand contraction.
