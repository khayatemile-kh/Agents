The neutral analyst's independent math (2025 EPS $7.44 to implied ~$9.80) reinforces that this growth rate is an outlier without an identifiable catalyst (M&A accretion is typically 5-10%, not 20%+).

Sticky-inflation-driven high rates encourage capex deferral on discretionary/marginal projects, which is exactly ALLE's exposure.

Net: the pullback has already priced in some risk (stock down ~10% off highs on 6.5x volume), which argues against an outright Sell, but the combination of unconfirmed FCF deceleration, static-to-rising leverage, an unproven 31.8% growth assumption embedded in the multiple, fragile technical support, and seasonal Q3/Q4 guidance-cut risk into CRE softness argues against holding full size or adding here. This supports trimming exposure and waiting for one more data point (Q3 2026) before re-underwriting the bull case — consistent with the Research Manager's Underweight call and the trader's cautious stance, but with a bias toward proactive de-risking rather than passive holding given the specific, quantified deterioration in FCF that the aggressive analyst never rebutted with hard numbers.
