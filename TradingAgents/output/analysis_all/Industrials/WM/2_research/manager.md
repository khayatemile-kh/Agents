Net: the qualitative moat and cash-generation story are real and support a floor under the stock (this is not a broken business), but the elevated, above-peer leverage combined with a decelerating dividend trend and margin plateau are structural, not transitory, and directly cap the near-to-medium-term upside the bull is underwriting. The debate doesn't support a full Sell — WM is not distressed, coverage ratios are healthy, and cash flow is real — but it also doesn't support Buy/Overweight given the leverage disadvantage vs peers and lack of clear catalysts for margin expansion or faster deleveraging. The bear's rebuttal on capital allocation math and peer leverage comparison was more rigorous and evidence-based than the bull's rebuttal (which was cut off before addressing it), so I'm tilting cautious rather than staying purely neutral.
