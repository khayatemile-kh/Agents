**Status:** MIXED SIGNALS

#### **C. Tariff Environment**
**Status:** UNCERTAIN/MIXED

- **SWK Impact:** As a tools/accessories manufacturer with both domestic manufacturing and supply chain exposure, tariff pressures likely remain a headwind. Potential for margin benefit if refunds materialize, but timing and allocation are unclear.
