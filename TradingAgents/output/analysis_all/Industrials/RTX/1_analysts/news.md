### I. COMPANY-SPECIFIC DEVELOPMENTS (RTX)

#### **1. Massive $22.9 Billion Tomahawk Missile Production Contract**

- **Scope**: Accelerates Tomahawk missile production from 60 to 1,000 units annually
- **Strategic Significance**: Addresses depletions in U.S. and allied stockpiles following Iran conflict
- **Impact on RTX**: Substantial multi-year revenue visibility and capacity expansion; demonstrates sustained demand for RTX's core defense products
- **Share Performance Context**: RTX shares trade at $212.08 with strong momentum (90-day return: +18.05%, YTD return: +13.26%, 1-year total return: +33.96%)

#### **2. Record $289 Billion Backlog – Majority Commercial**
- **Total Backlog**: $289 billion (largest in RTX's history)
- **Composition Shift**: Notably, the majority is **commercial aerospace**, not defense
- **Significance**: Provides durable revenue streams across multiple business cycles and economic conditions; reduces dependency on defense spending volatility
- **Commercial Drivers**: Post-pandemic aviation recovery, replacement of aging aircraft, increased passenger demand

#### **3. Additional B-52 Modernization Contract**
- **New Win**: RTX secured $766 million contract (Boeing also received allocation) for B-52 bomber modernization
- **Implication**: Demonstrates RTX's diversified defense portfolio beyond missiles; indicates sustained modernization investments by the U.S. Air Force

#### **4. Recent Stock Performance**

- **Context**: Normal consolidation after strong run; recent surge driven by contract wins and geopolitical factors
