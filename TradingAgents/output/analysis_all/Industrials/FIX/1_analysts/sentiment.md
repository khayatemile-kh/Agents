## Executive Summary

FIX sentiment shows a **bullish retail overlay against moderately cautious institutional framing**, marked by near-term price weakness but persistent fundamental confidence. **Institutional sentiment inference:** Fundamentals remain strong (balance sheet, earnings upgrades, segment growth); however, recent price action and sector volatility suggest near-term caution or valuation reset risk. Institutions are not calling for a sell, but neither are they uniformly aggressive on new positions.
