Leverage is the second pillar: net debt +49.3% in nine months to $5.28B, net debt/EBITDA climbing to ~2.3x. The conservative analyst's peer comparison (Northrop >2.5x, Lockheed 1.8-2.2x) shows this isn't extreme for the sector, but the neutral analyst correctly flags the compounding risk - if margins compress another 100-150bps while debt stays flat, leverage drifts toward the 2.5-2.6x upper boundary of sector comfort. Net assessment: two of three margin lines and the debt trajectory are moving against the thesis with data confirmed by all three analysts, while the bull's strongest points (contract pipeline, valuation) are supportive but not yet sufficient to offset the profitability and leverage deterioration. This is not a Sell - there's no fraud, no thesis-breaking event, and the contract pipeline plus valuation discount argue against exiting entirely - but it is too fragile a fundamental picture to Hold unchanged.
