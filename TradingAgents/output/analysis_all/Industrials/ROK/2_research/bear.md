## **PART 2: THE MACRO CASE IS SCREAMING SELL**

### **2A. The Fed "Hawkish Hold" Is a Capex Killer, Not a Non-Event**

The bull wants to minimize the macro headwinds. Let me quantify why they're actually catastrophic for ROK:

**Prediction Market Signal: 89% probability of ZERO Fed rate cuts in 2026**

Translate this: **The Fed is holding rates steady because inflation is sticky or growth is less certain.** Either way, this environment creates a capex freeze in industrial companies.

Here's why:

#### **The Capex Sensitivity Math**

Rockwell's revenue is **highly dependent on manufacturing capital spending**. When capex cycles slow, ROK's business follows within 1-2 quarters.

| Rate Environment | Capex Response | ROK Revenue Impact |
|------------------|-----------------|-------------------|
| **Falling rates** | Capex accelerates (lower financing costs) | Revenue +5-8% |
| **Stable/high rates** | Capex cautious or deferred | Revenue +0-2% |
| **Rising uncertainty** | Capex cancellation | Revenue -5-10% |

**Current environment: STABLE/HIGH RATES + UNCERTAINTY**

What does this mean? Manufacturers with $50M capex budgets are saying: *"We'll wait and see if rates fall or demand stabilizes before we commit."* This pushes orders out 2-3 quarters. ROK's Q3-Q4 2026 bookings are likely already weak.

#### **ISM Manufacturing PMI: The Canary in the Coal Mine**

The market prices a wide distribution of September 2026 ISM outcomes:
- **7% probability below 50** (contraction)
- **26% probability 53.0-53.9** (modest growth)
- **71% probability above 53** (growth)

Bull takeaway: *"71% probability of growth—that's bullish!"*

Bear takeaway: **This is extraordinarily wide uncertainty for early September.** In normal times, ISM forecasts converge within 1-2 points. A 5+ point spread indicates the market has **no conviction** on manufacturing demand.

And here's the critical point: **Even with ISM at 54, that's barely above the 50-point expansion threshold.** Manufacturing growth at 54 ISM is: sluggish, vulnerable to reversal, and **insufficient to drive double-digit capex growth**.

ROK needs robust capex cycles (ISM 55+) to deliver 35-40% earnings growth. **The market prices only 50% probability of that.**
