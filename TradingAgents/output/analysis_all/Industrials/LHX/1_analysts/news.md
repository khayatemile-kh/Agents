### **EXECUTIVE SUMMARY**

L3Harris Technologies (LHX) is currently trading at an **11% discount year-to-date** amid significant organizational turbulence, creating a critical decision point for investors. However, underlying business fundamentals remain solid, with strong growth drivers in electronic warfare systems.
