**FCF Trend Analysis:**
- **FY 2024:** ~$1.50B (estimated)

- **H1 2026 Annualized:** ~$1.20-1.30B (potentially -10% from 2025)

The bull is cherry-picking a snapshot. **The trend is deteriorating.** The H1 2026 free cash flow of $603M annualizes to roughly $1.2B—a **meaningful decline** from the $1.44B the bull keeps citing.

Why? Because **service revenue growth is slowing,** not accelerating as the bull claims. Here's the proof in the actual financials:

**Q2 2026 Operating Cash Flow:** $267M (down from $413M in Q1 2026)  
**Q2 2026 FCF Conversion:** Only 52.1% (vs. 78.5% in H1 average)

This isn't seasonal noise—this is a deterioration in cash generation **in the middle of 2026.** The company is working harder to generate the same cash.

### **B. The "Non-Discretionary" Service Business Argument Is Overstated**

Yes, building codes require elevator maintenance. But let's be honest about what that means in a slowing economy:

1. **Price increases become difficult:** When real estate activity slows, customers have more bargaining power. OTIS can't easily raise service contract prices when commercial real estate owners are tightening their belts. **Pricing power deteriorates.**

2. **Mix shift happens:** If new equipment sales (higher margin) decline faster than service revenue (lower margin) holds, the blended margin profile *compresses*. The bull is implying that service replacing new sales is neutral—it's not. It's margin-accretive negative.

3. **The $3.02B deferred revenue is NOT the moat the bull claims:** This $3B isn't free cash—it's *committed* future work at contracted prices. In an inflationary environment, doing work at prices locked in 2-3 years ago is a *margin headwind*, not a tailwind. OTIS has already committed to deliver services at margins that don't reflect current cost inflation.

**Reality check:** If the service business is truly non-discretionary and recession-proof, why is H1 2026 FCF on pace to be DOWN 10% from FY 2025? The answer: **the business is facing headwinds the bull refuses to acknowledge.**
