Here's the uncomfortable truth: **HUBB appears to be playing catch-up in data center infrastructure, not leading.** When a company has to lever up significantly to acquire capabilities that competitors have already built organically or through earlier, more strategic deals, it suggests HUBB was late to the party. **The secular tailwind is real. HUBB's competitive positioning to capture it? That's far less certain.**
