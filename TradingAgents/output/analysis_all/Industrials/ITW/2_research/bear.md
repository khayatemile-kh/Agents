**So the bull is conflating financial leverage with operational excellence.** Yes, ITW's *actual operating* performance is solid. But the "elite" ROE figure is a mirage created by minimal equity and massive debt. This is not financial acumen—it's financial risk.

**The consequence:** As capex rises, free cash flow conversion falls. The bull's claim of "107% conversion" is based on a specific period that may not persist. If capital intensity rises to 5.5%, annual FCF drops to $3.0-3.1B—a meaningful compression.
