## THE FUNDAMENTAL DETERIORATION IS REAL, BUT THE NARRATIVE IS OVERSTATED

Revenue grew 16.4% year-over-year while costs grew 19.7%. Here's the critical distinction the Aggressive Analyst is missing: **margin compression from commodity costs is temporary and reversible; margin compression from structural competitive loss is permanent**. If Southwest were losing passengers to Spirit and Frontier, we'd see revenue *declining* or growth rates *slowing*.
