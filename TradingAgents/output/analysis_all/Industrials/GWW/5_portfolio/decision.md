But paying 32x for a business whose own margin trend just decelerated, with no rate tailwind and technical momentum breaking down, means adding to or holding the full position uncritically is not prudent. Underweight — trim into strength, keep a core position, and use the next two quarterly prints as the explicit re-rating trigger in either direction.
