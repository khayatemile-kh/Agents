**JBHT's response?** Unlike competitors, JBHT has **kept drayage operations in-house** rather than outsourcing to third parties. According to EVP Darren Field (reported August 25, 2026), this is a **"meaningful competitive advantage"** in the current tight labor environment.

What does this mean in practice?

- **Service Reliability:** JBHT can guarantee drayage capacity when competitors cannot. In logistics, reliability = pricing power.
- **Cost Control:** Outsourced drayage exposes carriers to wage inflation and vendor margin compression. JBHT internalizes this risk and captures the upside.
- **Market Share Capture:** As competitors struggle with outsourced drayage delays and cost inflation, JBHT steals accounts. We're already seeing this in their strong Q2 2026 earnings.
- **Margin Protection:** While the industry faces drayage labor cost pressures, JBHT's vertical integration acts as a natural hedge.

**The bull case:** This competitive advantage is **self-reinforcing**. The longer the drayage shortage persists (which the news cycle suggests will happen through 2026 and beyond), the more valuable JBHT's in-house capability becomes. This is a **multi-year tailwind**, not a one-quarter phenomenon.
