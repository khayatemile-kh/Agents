- **Tangible Book Value per Share: -$6.88 (NEGATIVE)**

**What does this mean?**

1. **Zero Margin for Error:** If anything goes wrong—recession, competitive disruption, customer churn spike—the company can't absorb losses without:
   - Cutting dividends
   - Suspending buybacks
   - Breaching debt covenants

3. **Debt Refinancing Risk:** When PAYX's debt matures (likely in 2027-2028 given current rate environment), the company will need to refinance at **higher rates than the current blended cost of debt.** This will further compress earnings and limit financial flexibility.
