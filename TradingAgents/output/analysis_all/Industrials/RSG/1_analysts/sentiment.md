## Dominant Narrative Themes

1. **Insider Confidence as Contrarian Signal:** The most prominent positive theme across sources is insider buying post-weakness. This is institutional money betting on value and signals management confidence in intrinsic value.

2. **"Boring but Makes Money":** RSG is characterized as steady, profitable, and low-volatility—a utility narrative, not a growth narrative. This attracts yield-focused investors and stability-seekers, not momentum traders.

3. **Capital Allocation Skepticism:** One institutional source questions whether RSG's cash generation translates into shareholder value—implying dividends/buybacks may not be aggressive enough or returns on invested capital are modest.

4. **Technical Overbought Condition:** RSI 84.4 and a bearish 7D market bias suggest near-term pullback risk or consolidation.

5. **Unexplained Volatility Event (2026-08-31):** The after-hours dump requires explanation; its absence from news sources suggests either (a) the event is too recent for news indexing, (b) it was driven by broader market or sector dynamics, or (c) a company event not yet widely reported.
