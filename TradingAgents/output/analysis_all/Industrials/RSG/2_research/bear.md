### **The Recycling Division: A Commodity Trap, Not a Growth Engine**

The bull highlights RSG's recycling division as a growth beneficiary of e-commerce. Let me expose the flaw in this logic:

**Recycling is a commodity business with margin compression dynamics:**

- Paper, cardboard, and plastic prices are **volatile and determined by global commodity markets**, not by e-commerce volume
- When commodity prices decline (as they have periodically), recycling margins collapse
- RSG doesn't control input pricing—it's a price-taker in the commodities market
- E-commerce packaging (lightweight cardboard, plastics) is often LOWER-VALUE than traditional industrial waste streams

**The Real Problem:** As e-commerce grows, the mix shifts toward **lower-value packaging materials**, not higher-value industrial waste. This is a **margin headwind, not a tailwind.**
