**Free Cash Flow Generation (TTM): $3.11 billion**

This means:
- **Debt-to-FCF ratio: 6.9x** (a reasonable multiple for a capital-light industrial business with recurring revenue)
- **Interest coverage:** ETN's EBITDA of $6.64B TTM easily covers debt service costs
- **FCF yield:** At a $151.8B market cap, the company generates $3.11B in free cash flow annually—a **2.05% FCF yield**

The $9.9 billion debt raise in Q1 2026 funded a major acquisition that expanded the company's exposure to higher-margin, faster-growing segments. This is **accretive leverage**—debt deployed to acquire higher-return assets, not debt incurred to cover operational shortfalls.

Compare the financial profile:
- **Net Income (TTM): $3.83B**
- **Operating Income (TTM): $4.97B**
- **Operating Margin: 16.56%**

These are *strong* profitability metrics. The company is not in financial distress; it's in financial optimization mode—using leverage to accelerate growth in higher-return segments (data centers) while maintaining stable core operations.

### Dividend Sustainability is Secure

- **Dividend Payout Ratio (TTM):** ~44% of net income ($1.7B / $3.83B)
- **Dividend Yield:** 1.13% (conservative for a diversified industrial)

The dividend is **completely safe**. In fact, the low payout ratio provides room for dividend growth as earnings expand—a catalyst often missed in valuation.
