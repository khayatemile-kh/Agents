The technical picture is genuinely mixed: oversold conditions can precede either a bounce or further capitulation, and neither side can decisively prove which this is from the data given. I don't think this debate is balanced enough to justify a flat Hold, though. The bull wins on the two data points that matter most in the near term: (1) actual, current-quarter growth and raised guidance versus a speculative call that this is "peak cycle" with no concrete Q4 booking data to support it, and (2) analyst upgrade momentum (Zacks #2) into apparent technical oversold conditions. Net: the near-term fundamentals (raised guidance, 17% aftermarket growth, peer confirmation) outweigh the bear's speculative deceleration call, and technical oversold conditions provide an attractive entry price relative to trend.
