## COMPREHENSIVE NEWS & MARKET ANALYSIS REPORT
### Deere & Company (DE) – Week Ending September 1, 2026

---

### EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

**DE is in a strong momentum phase driven by a major analyst upgrade and positive sector expectations.** Baird upgraded Deere to "Outperform" on August 31, 2026, raising its price target from $640 to $800—implying **25% upside**. The upgrade catalysts are compelling: anticipated agricultural recovery in North America for 2027, supported by improving corn futures and row-crop demand dynamics.

---

### KEY FINDINGS & TRADING IMPLICATIONS

#### **1. MAJOR ANALYST CATALYST: BAIRD UPGRADE (August 31, 2026)**

**Trigger & Price Target:**
- Baird analyst Mircea Dobre upgraded DE from **Neutral → Outperform**
- New price target: **$800** (up from $640), representing ~25% upside potential
- Stock immediately rallied ~3–4% on the news
- Upgrade applies to the broader agricultural machinery complex (also upgraded AGCO, CNH Industrial, Titan Machinery)

**Rationale:**
- **North American agricultural recovery expected in 2027** — the core thesis
- **Mid-2027 corn futures expected to improve** — a key leading indicator for farmer purchasing power and margin recovery
- **Cyclical trough hypothesis** — Baird believes the market has already priced in the worst of the farming downcycle, setting up a recovery trade

**Trading Implication:** The upgrade reflects institutional conviction in a cyclical recovery. The 25% price target upside is substantial but contingent on:
- Corn/commodity price stabilization
- Farmer spending returning
- No adverse labor outcome that disrupts production

---

#### **2. LABOR UNION NEGOTIATIONS – ACTIVE BUT MANAGEABLE RISK**

**Status as of Late August 2026:**
- **Boeing, Deere, and US Steel** are all navigating labor union contract negotiations
- US Steel and Cleveland-Cliffs extended their steelworker contracts by **30 days** (a holding pattern indicator)
- Deere negotiations timeline: **Labor Day is Sept 1, 2026** — Boeing plans to resume talks post-Labor Day (Sept 8+)

**Risk Assessment:**
- Labor costs and disruption are a **moderate near-term tail risk**
- The 30-day extension at US Steel suggests these talks are protracted but not currently in crisis mode
- An extended or contentious Deere labor settlement could delay production ramps or increase COGS, undermining the recovery thesis

**Trading Implication:** Watch for September–October labor developments. Negotiations could become a **volatility catalyst** but are not immediately threatening the bullish case.

---

#### **3. MARKET ENVIRONMENT & MACROECONOMIC BACKDROP**

**Fed Policy & Rate Expectations:**
- **Prediction market consensus: 89% probability of NO Fed rate cuts in 2026** (Polymarket, high volume confidence)
- This is a significant shift—the crowd now expects rates to remain higher for longer
- Only 1-week move: +1.4pp, suggesting this view is stabilizing

**Recession Risk:**
- **US recession by end of 2026 probability: 8%** (Polymarket, down 1.0pp week-over-week)
- This is a **low recession probability**, supporting the cyclical recovery narrative
- Global sentiment: UK recession 7%, Japan recession 7% (all modest)

**Implication for DE:**
- Higher rates increase farmer debt service costs and borrowing friction—a **headwind to capex**
- However, the low recession probability + no rate cut expectation suggests **economic resilience**
- This creates a mixed backdrop: rates remain a drag, but growth remains intact
