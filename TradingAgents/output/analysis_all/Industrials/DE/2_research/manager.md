The fundamentals haven't changed; only sentiment and a speculative 2027 catalyst have. That combination - decent fundamentals, uncertain/technical extended price action, and a catalyst that's more promise than proof - is exactly the kind of genuinely balanced setup that warrants a Hold rather than forcing a directional call.
