Neither side has fully rebutted the other's strongest point: the bulls haven't addressed why growth is decelerating and capex is so low if the long-term compounder story is intact; the bears haven't addressed why a fortress balance sheet and strong FCF generation should be discounted so heavily, especially with technicals this oversold. Given genuinely balanced, unresolved arguments on both quality-of-growth and valuation-support fronts, and no incremental evidence available beyond what's in this debate, a neutral stance is the appropriate call rather than forcing conviction into either camp.
