A stock can be technically oversold and still expensive on a multi-quarter view — those are different time horizons, and the trader needs to size positions accordingly.

Net-net: the fundamentals are genuinely strong (cash generation, margins, interest coverage in the base case) so this isn't a Sell — the bear's downside scenarios require assumptions (35-50% EBITDA decline) not supported by current macro data. That argues against adding aggressively here. I'm landing on a constructive-but-cautious Hold: maintain existing exposure to benefit from the underlying quality and likely technical bounce, but don't chase the dip aggressively given the peer-relative valuation gap and balance-sheet fragility that the bear correctly flagged.
