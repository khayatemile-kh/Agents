CCC Intelligent Solutions is *central* to that ecosystem—it's the software platform that processes insurance claims and guides vehicles into the salvage pipeline. Currently, CPRT sits at the *end* of that pipeline (the auction). With CCC, CPRT would control *both the beginning and the end*.

**The strategic logic is bulletproof:**

1. **Vertical integration creates a competitive moat.** IAA (CPRT's primary competitor) cannot easily replicate this. If CPRT owns CCC, it controls the claims workflow *and* the auction platform. That's a pincer move.

2. **Recurring software revenue diversifies the model.** Today, CPRT is transaction-fee based. CCC software represents **recurring, high-margin SaaS revenue**. Analysts will re-rate the company on a higher multiple once this segment is materialized.

3. **Pricing power improves.** When you control both claims and auctions, you can capture incremental value at each step. Insurance companies and claims adjusters won't *want* to use CPRT+CCC—they'll *need* to.
