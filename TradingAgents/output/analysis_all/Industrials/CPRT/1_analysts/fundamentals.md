## **EXECUTIVE SUMMARY**

Copart, Inc. (CPRT) is a leading specialty business services company operating in the industrials sector, primarily engaged in online auction services and solutions for salvage vehicles and other assets. As of September 1, 2026, CPRT demonstrates solid fundamental strength with a market capitalization of approximately **$29.77 billion** and established profitability metrics. The company operates in a mission-critical segment (vehicle recycling/salvage) that provides stable revenue streams and strong cash generation capabilities.

## **COMPANY PROFILE & BUSINESS MODEL**

**Industry & Market Position:**
- **Sector:** Industrials / Specialty Business Services
- **Primary Business:** Online auction platform for salvage vehicles and related assets
- **Geographic Presence:** Multi-national operations with significant international footprint
- **Market Position:** Dominant player in the vehicle auction and salvage market, providing essential infrastructure for insurance, financial institutions, and automotive recyclers

**Business Characteristics:**
- High-margin, asset-light business model with platform economics
- Recurring revenue streams from vehicle consignments, seller fees, and buyer fees
- Mission-critical service reducing friction in salvage vehicle distribution
- Economies of scale with network effects in bidder participation
