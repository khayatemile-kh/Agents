### B. **$85 Billion Defense Backlog: The Snowball Effect**

Beyond the F-15 win, Boeing has an **$85 billion defense backlog** with programs moving into **low-rate initial production (LRIP)**. What does that mean in plain English?

LRIP is the transition phase where a military platform moves from development into actual production. It's the inflection point where:
- Engineering costs decrease (design is finalized)
- Production volume ramps up
- Gross margins typically expand significantly
- Cash conversion improves

**Historical precedent from the defense industry:**
- Programs in LRIP typically see margin expansion of 200-400 basis points over 3-5 years
- Volume ramps drive fixed-cost absorption, improving operating leverage
- Customer commitment deepens (they're now buying, not just contracting)

With $85 billion sitting in the backlog and programs entering LRIP, Boeing is positioned for a **multi-year earnings expansion cycle**—not just next year, but through 2028-2030.
