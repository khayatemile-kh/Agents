- **No Real-Time Price Data:** Cannot correlate sentiment to intraday or week-over-week price action; difficult to assess whether "weak" is relative or absolute.

**Recommendation for Confidence Upgrade:**
- Collect StockTwits data over the next 2–4 weeks; a sample of 20+ messages provides more robust sentiment.

- Track news flow for deal updates, contract wins, or guidance revisions.
