### Business Description
Trane Technologies is a diversified industrial company focused on climate control and indoor air quality solutions. The company operates through:
- **HVAC Systems:** Commercial and residential heating, ventilation, and air conditioning equipment
- **Refrigeration:** Commercial and industrial refrigeration products
- **Building Management Services:** Controls and monitoring solutions for building efficiency
- **Parts & Service:** Aftermarket replacement parts and maintenance services

The company serves commercial, residential, and industrial end markets globally, with strong exposure to energy efficiency trends and climate control demand.
