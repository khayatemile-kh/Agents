### **SECTORAL & THEMATIC DRIVERS**

#### **1. AI Data Center Buildout – PRIMARY TAILWIND**
- **Market Signal**: Polymarket shows 73% probability that **at least one US state will enact a data center moratorium by end-of-2026**.
  - This regulatory risk underscores the *intensity* and *geographic concentration* of data center deployment.
  - TT's thermal management solutions are **critical infrastructure** that operators will prioritize even under regulatory constraints.
  - **Implication**: Regulatory friction may slow *marginal* growth but will not arrest core AI capex; in fact, it may *increase* demand for sophisticated thermal management (more efficient cooling = less energy footprint = regulatory advantage).

#### **2. Commercial Real Estate Softness – SECONDARY HEADWIND**
- No dedicated prediction markets for CRE in 2026, but macro context suggests:
  - Persistent interest rate environment (no rate cuts) keeps refinancing costs elevated.
  - Moderate GDP growth (2%) is insufficient to drive speculative new construction.
  - **Likely impact on TT**: Lower-margin HVAC retrofit and commercial building segment may see volume compression; this is offset by AI data center premium-margin work.

#### **3. Global Outlook: Controlled Weakness**
- UK and Japan recession probabilities both at **7%** (extremely low), consistent with soft but non-recessionary trajectories.
- International demand for TT's products (HVAC, water systems, thermal management) remains supported by low global recession risk.
