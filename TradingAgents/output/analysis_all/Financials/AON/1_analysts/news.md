### **I. AON-SPECIFIC DEVELOPMENTS**

#### **Major Transaction: $17 Billion USI Acquisition**

Aon plc (NYSE: AON) announced a **definitive agreement to acquire USI Insurance Services from KKR and other shareholders for $17 billion in an all-cash transaction**, with expected close in Q4 2026, subject to regulatory approvals. This represents one of the largest insurance-brokerage acquisitions in recent years.

**Deal Highlights:**
- **Valuation:** ~$17 billion ($16.7 billion net of tax attributes); approximately **6x sales** on USI's ~$3 billion annual revenue
- **Strategic Rationale:** Expands Aon's middle-market reach and specialty capabilities, positioning Aon as a "premier U.S. middle market platform" when combined with Aon's 2024 NFP acquisition
- **Scale:** USI has 10,500+ employees, operates ~200 U.S. offices, and generates recurring revenue
- **CEO Commentary:** Aon CEO Greg Case characterized the deal as potentially "the greatest value-creation opportunity of his career" in CNBC remarks

**Market Reaction & Concerns:**
- **Stock Performance:** AON shares declined **6.6%** on deal announcement, reflecting investor concerns
- **Valuation Pressure:** At nearly 6x sales, the deal is considered expensive with one analyst (Zacks) calling it "a costly bet on middle-market growth"
- **Debt & Execution:** The all-cash nature freezes shareholder buybacks, adds significant debt, and puts near-term execution pressure on the company
- **Risk Assessment:** Execution risk is elevated given the integration complexity and current macro uncertainty
