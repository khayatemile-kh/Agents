**Recommendation**: Hold

**Rationale**: Both sides land real punches, and the evidence genuinely cuts both ways rather than clearly favoring one side. The bull is right that AMP screens cheap on absolute multiples (13.3x trailing, ~10.7x forward), generates excellent operating margins (~30.7%) and near-100% cash conversion, and operates with modest leverage relative to market cap (debt/assets ~3.3%) despite the scary-looking D/E ratio, which is a normal artifact of financial-services balance sheets. The buyback program ($779M in the quarter) is real capital return and the company clearly has the FCF ($2.9B TTM) to support it.

But the bear's rebuttal on the earnings trend is the more rigorous read of the same numbers. The bull's 'accelerating momentum' story relies on a sequential Q1-to-Q2 comparison, while the proper YoY comparison shows net income down ~5% and EPS down ~11.8% in Q2, with Q1 down a steeper ~15.8% YoY. That's two consecutive quarters of YoY earnings contraction even as revenue grew ~12.9% YoY — a margin compression signal the bull never really addresses. If earnings are shrinking, buying back stock at 13.3x is a much less clearly accretive story than the bull frames it, and the bear's point about funding some of that return via net debt issuance ($708M) deserves weight rather than being waved away as "strategic refinancing."

The technical picture also matters here: a 65% MACD collapse, a bearish signal-line crossover, and RSI cooling from overbought to neutral after a ~5% pullback from highs is a legitimate near-term momentum concern, not just noise — though it's also not by itself a fundamental sell signal.

Net-net: the valuation multiples are genuinely low and the balance sheet is not distressed, which argues against an aggressive Underweight/Sell. But the YoY earnings deterioration and margin compression are real and unaddressed by the bull, which argues against chasing the stock higher or adding aggressively right now. This is a case where the two theses roughly offset — cheap valuation and strong cash generation versus deteriorating YoY earnings trend and negative momentum — so I'm not comfortable committing capital in either direction with conviction.

**Strategic Actions**: Maintain current position size in AMP without adding or trimming meaningfully until the earnings trend clarifies. Specifically: (1) Wait for the next quarterly print to confirm whether YoY earnings turn positive (would validate the bull's acceleration thesis) or continue contracting (would validate the bear's margin-compression thesis) before sizing up or down. (2) Do not add on the current technical setup — the MACD bearish crossover and momentum collapse argue for patience on new buying even if you like the valuation. (3) If you must act now, consider trimming a small (5-10%) portion of the position into the recent bounce to reduce risk while the YoY earnings/margin picture is unresolved, rather than adding into weakening momentum. (4) Watch AUM/net flow disclosures and next-quarter margin trends as the key catalyst that would tip this from Hold to Overweight (margins stabilize, YoY earnings inflect positive) or to Underweight (further YoY earnings/margin deterioration, continued debt-funded buybacks). (5) Set a stop/review level around a further breach of recent lows (below the -4.9% pullback zone) to reassess if technical deterioration accelerates.
