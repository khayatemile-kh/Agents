**The Math the Bull Won't Show You:**

If AMP buys shares at 13.3x earnings, it's paying **$13.30 for every $1.00 of annual earnings.** But if those earnings decline by 5% YoY (as we're seeing), the company is:

1. **Overpaying for those earnings** (13.3x for declining cash flows)
