**Why this matters for GL:**

1. **Net Interest Income (NII) Compression Risk Eliminated:**
   - In a low-rate environment, life insurers face NII compression (reinvestment risk)

   - This is a **permanent structural advantage** vs. 2020-2021 when rates were pinned at zero

2. **New Issue Volume (NIW) Remains Healthy:**
   - Elevated rates don't kill insurance sales immediately; they slow growth modestly
   - The industry is expecting ~4.9% premium growth (per Morgan Stanley), which GL is tracking
   - Importantly: **Customers don't exit life insurance because of higher rates**—they face lock-in effects and persistency remains strong

3. **Pricing Power:**
   - Elevated rate environment allows GL to reprice products with improved margins
   - In a low-rate environment, competitive pressure forces margin compression
   - Current environment favors incumbents like GL

**Persistency & Lapses:**
- In a recession, policy lapses can spike (customers drop coverage to preserve cash)
- With only 8% recession probability through end-2026, GL can assume benign lapse rates
- **This supports earnings stability and revenue predictability**

**Employment Stability:**
- Life insurance lapses correlate with unemployment
- Current macro backdrop suggests stable employment through 2026
- **GL's policyholder base should remain intact**

**Sales Momentum:**
- In a soft-landing scenario (59% probability), consumer confidence remains healthy
- This supports new policy sales and persistency
- GL benefits from both retention *and* growth

**Claims Inflation Contained:**

- GL's reserves remain adequate; no surprise reserve strengthening needed

**Investment Return Environment:**
