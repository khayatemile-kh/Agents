### **CRITIQUE 3: The Macro Environment Argument Ignores the Real Risk**

The bull says: "89% probability of zero Fed rate cuts supports IBKR."

Let me challenge this flawed logic:

#### Higher Rates Are NOT a Tailwind for Trading Volumes

Yes, IBKR earns on idle cash deposits when rates are high. That's ~$300-400M annually in today's environment. But the bull is ignoring the **velocity effect**:

**When rates are higher, retail investors have *less incentive to trade actively* because:**
1. **Savings accounts and money market funds become attractive** (4-5% yields)
2. **Opportunity cost of equity investing increases** (why take market risk for 8-10% expected returns when you get 4-5% risk-free?)
3. **Bond portfolios outperform equities** (less demand for stock trading, more competition for investor dollars)

This is *Economics 101*, and the bull is ignoring it.

The periods of **highest retail trading volumes historically occur during:**
- Low rate environments (2020-2021)
- Periods of equity market FOMO and momentum (like today, temporarily)

The current elevated volumes are due to **geopolitical volatility and equity market momentum**, NOT the rate environment.

#### The Geopolitical Risk Argument is Backwards

The bull says geopolitical risk "widens bid-ask spreads and increases fixed-income trading."

**That's true for a 2-4 week window.** Then what happens?

The market reprices, volatility subsides, and we're back to normalized volumes. But here's the real risk:

**If US-Iran tensions escalate into something more serious, we don't get higher trading volumes—we get a *market crash.***

In a crash scenario:
- Retail traders panic and *reduce* trading (not increase)
- Margin calls spike (IBKR faces counterparty risk)
- Volatility spikes (good for spreads, bad for volumes and sentiment)
- The equity markets enter a bear phase (dead money for brokerages)

**The bull is cherry-picking the 2-week scenario where volatility helps IBKR while ignoring the tail risk where volatility destroys IBKR.**
