Now, the aggressive analyst correctly flags this as concerning in a "higher for longer" rate environment. But they miss something crucial: the company's ability to refinance or service this debt hasn't actually deteriorated yet. The Debt-to-EBITDA ratio at 1.92x, while elevated, is still *serviceable* for a financial services company. It's not like we're looking at 4-5x leverage in a cyclical business.

Here's the honest assessment: the leverage trajectory is a *yellow flag*, not a red flag.
