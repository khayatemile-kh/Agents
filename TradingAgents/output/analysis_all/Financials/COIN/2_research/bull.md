The TTM loss of -$0.99B and -16.34% profit margin demand context:

**Q4 2025 included a $1.14B loss on investments/securities**, which was a one-time mark-to-market event, not operational deterioration. **Excluding this:**

**This is not a broken business model—it's a cyclical business in a crypto bear/consolidation phase.**
