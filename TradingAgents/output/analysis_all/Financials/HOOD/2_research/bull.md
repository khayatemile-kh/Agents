Here's where I think the bear case falls apart. Let me show you something remarkable that happened on **September 1, 2026**:

- **Coinbase (COIN)**: Down 6%
- **Strategy (STRAT)**: Down 6%
- **Bitcoin**: Down 0.4%

**What caused this divergence?**

The bond market sold off (yields rose). For fintech *lenders* like SoFi and Affirm, rising yields are a **negative**—their funding costs increase, net interest margins compress, and consumer lending demand weakens. For crypto holdings and crypto-correlated stocks, risk-off meant selling.

But **HOOD decoupled upward**.

Why? Because:

1. **HOOD's business model is commission-based, not balance-sheet based.** Rising volatility = higher trading volumes = higher HOOD revenue. HOOD doesn't lend money; it makes money on transaction flow.

2. **The upgrade narrative (prediction markets, tokenization) is *independent* of rate movements.** This is why the upgrade announcement drove the stock higher despite macro headwinds.

3. **HOOD has no balance-sheet duration risk.** Unlike SoFi, HOOD doesn't need to worry about liability repricing or NIM compression.

**The Bear Will Say**: "But HOOD is a leverage play on crypto and volatility—it'll crash in a risk-off environment."

**My Response**: That's the 2020-2022 playbook, and it's outdated. Yes, HOOD has crypto exposure. But look at today's evidence: crypto is down, HOOD is up. Why? Because the story is no longer "crypto trading volumes." It's "prediction markets + tokenization + record profitability." The September 1 decoupling *proves* the narrative has evolved.
