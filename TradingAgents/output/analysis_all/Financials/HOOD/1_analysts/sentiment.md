1. **Prediction market adoption surge** — regulatory environment in U.S. improving; TAM expanding.
2. **Tokenization ecosystem lock-in** — Robinhood Chain fees hitting records; increasing switching costs.
3. **Q2 earnings beat** — recent track record supporting upgrade rationale.
4. **Yield-resilient model** — Q2 record + product diversity allowed HOOD to shrug off 2026-09-01 bond selloff while balance-sheet-heavy competitors fell.

### Downside Risks
1. **Retail participation cliff** — if trading volumes collapse (e.g., on macro shock or market pause), subscription/tokenization growth insufficient to offset commission revenue loss.
2. **Regulatory headwinds** — prediction markets and tokenization remain politically contested; adverse regulatory action could halt growth narrative.

4. **Valuation reset** — $150 PT at 43% upside implies near-term re-rating already priced; surprise misses could snap momentum.
