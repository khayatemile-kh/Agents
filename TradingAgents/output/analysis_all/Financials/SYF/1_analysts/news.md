### KEY FINDINGS BY CATEGORY

#### **1. Credit Sector Stress Signals**

**Critical Issue: Delinquency Concentration**
- Credit card balances 90+ days late have **nearly doubled since 2022**, per recent Motley Fool reporting
- New delinquencies **have NOT significantly increased** — indicating this is a *deepening* problem, not a widening one
- This bifurcation suggests that:
  - A subset of consumers in material financial strain is deteriorating rapidly
  - SYF's loan portfolio may face increased severity (larger losses per default) even if default rates stabilize
  - Collections and charge-offs could accelerate for the distressed cohort

**Implication for SYF:**
- As a major credit card provider and consumer finance lender, SYF is directly exposed to this delinquency trend
- Provision for credit losses (PCL) could face upward pressure
- Net interest margins (NIM) may face headwinds if competitive pressure forces yield concessions
