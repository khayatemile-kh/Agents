Book value is *not* a hard asset floor; it's a balance sheet line item. In a market downturn or credit event, the realized value of those investments could compress significantly. The market is discounting AIG's stated equity because it understands that financial leverage and market-sensitive asset bases create embedded volatility that simple P/B ratios obscure.

- Q3 2025: $519M net income

This is *not* consistent growth. This is extreme volatility. The Q2 2026 quarter showed recovery from a depressed Q1, but we're still below Q2 2025 levels. - Q2 2026 operating cash flow: $1.716B
