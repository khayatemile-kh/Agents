**Higher rates are a *direct benefit* to insurance companies:**

1. **Investment Income Boost:** AIG holds $82.2 billion in investment assets. When rates are elevated, the portfolio reinvests at higher yields. Net investment income (NII) rises directly. 3. **Competitive Moat Strengthens:** Larger, more capitalized insurers like AIG benefit disproportionately in rising-rate environments because they have better access to capital and more diversified funding sources. Smaller competitors get squeezed.

- **Commercial Insurance Demand Remains Strong:** A stable economy = stable commercial property, liability, and workers' comp premiums. AIG's core business thrives in this environment.
