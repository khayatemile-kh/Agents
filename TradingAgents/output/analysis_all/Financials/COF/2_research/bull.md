This sounds terrifying. It's also **misleading without context.**

### **3.1 Delinquencies Are Rising, BUT Originations Are Tightening**

The fact that **90+ day delinquencies are rising while new delinquencies have NOT increased** tells us:

1. **COF is originating to higher-quality borrowers** (new delinquency rates flat = better underwriting standards)
2. **Existing stressed borrowers are taking longer to cure or default** (aging problem, not new problem)
3. **The company is being MORE selective,** not less

**This is actually a sign of disciplined credit management, not deterioration.**

If COF were originating recklessly and seeing a surge in *new* delinquencies, that would be a red flag. Instead, we see:
- **New delinquencies flat**
- **Older balances aging out** (means fewer accounts are curing; suggests higher defaults ahead, but this is priced into charge-off reserves)
