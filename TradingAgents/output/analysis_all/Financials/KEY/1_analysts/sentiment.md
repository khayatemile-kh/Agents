### **Identified Catalysts (Positive):**
- **Execution under new strategy chief:** Chris Doll's appointment is a near-term focal point for investors; successful integration and strategy clarity could drive near-term re-rating.
- **Earnings season:** Upcoming quarterly results will test the "strong revenue growth" narrative cited in Zacks article.

- **Momentum cooling:** Simply Wall St. notes short-term weakness; if this continues, institutional optimism may be tested.
- **Retail remain disengaged:** Lack of retail enthusiasm could limit upside if the stock relies on broader market participation.
- **Competitive & macro headwinds:** Typical for regional banks; no specific threats mentioned, but not discussed in current news either.
