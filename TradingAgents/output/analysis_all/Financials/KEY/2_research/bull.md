## SECTION 1: THE MACRO SETUP IS IDEAL FOR REGIONAL BANKS

### The "Higher for Longer" Regime is Locked In

Let's start with the most important macro tailwind: **89% probability of ZERO Fed rate cuts in 2026**, according to prediction markets. This isn't speculation—this is real money backing this outcome.

**Why this matters for KEY:**

1. **Net Interest Margin (NIM) Stability**: In a prolonged higher-rate environment, regional banks like KEY benefit from sticky deposit bases. Customers don't immediately flee to money market funds; deposits remain at the bank, earning competitive but still-below-market rates. This creates a **natural margin cushion** that expands bottom-line earnings.

2. **No Rate-Cut Compression Risk**: The bear narrative often centers on rate cuts compressing margins. That risk is essentially **off the table for 2026**. Why would you sell now on a risk that won't materialize?

3. **Lending Demand Remains Robust**: In a stable, higher-rate environment, businesses continue to borrow (they have no incentive to delay—rates won't fall), and consumers maintain steady demand for mortgages, auto loans, and credit products. KEY's lending portfolio will continue to grow.

**The Numbers**: KEY's Net Interest Income grew from $1.096B (Q1 2025) to $1.222B (Q1 2026)—an **11.5% year-over-year increase**. This is *exactly* what we'd expect in a "higher for longer" regime, and it's happening in real time.
