3. **Investment income** (elevated due to higher rates—but we're at the *peak* of the rate cycle)

### A. Premium Pricing Moderation = Margin Compression

### B. Claims Frequency is Artificially Suppressed

The 8% recession probability you're citing as a positive is actually a **huge red flag** for why TRV's current margins are unsustainable.

**The reason claims frequency is low right now:**
- Labor market is tight (fewer accidents, fewer unemployment-driven property crimes)
- No major catastrophic events recently (we're overdue for a major hurricane, wildfire season)
- Economic resilience means fewer business failures and bankruptcy-related claims

**But none of this is permanent.** The bull case requires believing that:
1. Labor markets stay tight indefinitely (implausible given demographic headwinds and potential recession)
2. We continue to avoid major catastrophic events (variance will eventually assert itself)
3. Premium pricing stays stable (already contradicted by your own "pricing moderation" narrative)

**When claims severity normalizes—and it will—TRV's margins compress 300-500 basis points.** That wipes out 2-3 years of your earnings growth projection.
