**Recommendation**: Overweight

**Rationale**: Weighing both sides, the bull case rests on genuinely durable and well-evidenced strengths: Visa's two-sided network moat, 61%+ operating margins, 99% FCF return to shareholders, 14%+ revenue growth, and a valuation (31.7x trailing, 25x forward) that is actually below its own 5-year historical range and cheaper than its closest peer Mastercard. The bear case raises legitimate risks worth respecting—DOJ litigation overhang, fintech/API disintermediation, and stablecoin/crypto rails as long-term competitive threats—but these are largely probabilistic, multi-year tail risks rather than present-tense earnings impairments. Critically, the bear's own numbers (30% Visa wins, 40% modest settlement) put a combined 70% probability on outcomes that are neutral-to-modestly negative, not thesis-breaking, and the DOJ case is not even set for trial until 2028, giving ample time for the market to reprice gradually rather than via a shock. The bear's disintermediation argument (Stripe/PayPal, BNPL settling via ACH, stablecoins) is directionally correct as a monitorable risk, but the bull correctly notes most of these alternative rails (Affirm, Klarna) still ultimately settle a large share of volume over card networks, and Visa is actively defending share via new AI fraud/cyber and agentic-payments initiatives that could open high-margin software revenue streams. On valuation, the bear's deceleration table assumes multiple compression is inevitable, but a 15% CAGR cash-generative toll-collector business with pricing power and a fortress balance sheet does not need re-acceleration to justify holding here—it needs merely to not stumble, and current evidence shows margins still expanding, not compressing. Given real but non-imminent regulatory/disruption risk and a still-favorable growth/valuation/cash-return combination, I lean toward the bull side but temper full-conviction Buy given the legitimate tail risk from the DOJ case and structural fintech/stablecoin threats — hence Overweight rather than outright Buy, with instructions to size and monitor rather than go max-conviction.
