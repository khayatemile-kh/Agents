### **Near-Term Risks (Negative)**
- **Soft Pricing Persistence:** If the property-casualty market remains in soft-pricing mode for quarters beyond the current period, even strong underwriting won't fully offset margin compression.
- **Catastrophe Events:** Summer/fall 2026 is hurricane and wildfire season (Northern Hemisphere). Large cat losses could spike underwriting ratios across the industry and test WRB's discipline narrative.
- **Competitive Escalation:** If rivals slash underwriting standards further, WRB's discipline may become a relative liability (lost premium growth) rather than an asset.
- **Valuation Complacency:** If WRB stock has already priced in the "quality" discount to the market, sentiment shift could reverse quickly on any disappointment.
