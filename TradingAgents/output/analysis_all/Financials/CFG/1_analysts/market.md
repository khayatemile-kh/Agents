2. **Breakdown Below Key Support:** The loss of the 50 SMA is significant for intermediate-term traders; this typically leads to either:

4. **Volatility Compression:** The declining ATR suggests that once the current trend exhausts, volatility is likely to re-expand sharply. This favors breakout strategies over mean-reversion fade attempts.

5. **Three-Week Selloff:** The 8.77% decline over 2.5 weeks is substantial but not panic-level for a regional bank during a potential market corrective phase. This context matters for evaluating whether the decline is sector-specific, CFG-specific, or macro-driven.
