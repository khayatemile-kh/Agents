### Narrative Reconciliation:
This is a **classic institutional-vs.-retail divergence**:
- **Institutions (via news/strategist commentary)** are anchored to earnings power and macro tailwinds (higher rates).
- **Retail traders** are focused on chart technicals and are seeing deterioration: support breaks, low volume, rolling-over structure.

**This mismatch is often a contrarian signal:** When institutions remain bullish on fundamentals but retail is capitulating on technicals, it can indicate either:
1. **Institutional accumulation on weakness** (bullish setup if technicals stabilize).
2. **Institutional complacency** (retail sees problems first, institutions react later).
