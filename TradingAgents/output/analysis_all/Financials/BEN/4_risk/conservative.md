My critical point: **The Aggressive Analyst has weaponized a leverage ratio that's standard for financial services companies and applied a manufacturing-company risk lens to it.** The 24.81x D/E is elevated for Franklin Templeton historically, yes, but in the context of sticky rates, strong operational earnings, and growing digital asset revenue, it's not a refinancing crisis.
