More concerning: the volume profile during the selloff shows elevated volume on down days (7.79M on August 19, 8.21M on August 31). The aggressive analyst mentions this but dismisses it as "weak hands being flushed out." In professional risk management, elevated volume on down days is actually a *distributional signal*—it suggests institutional selling, not retail capitulation. When institutions exit positions, they're usually exiting for a reason.

Here's the critical distinction the aggressive analyst missed: **a bounce from oversold conditions is probabilistically likely, but the magnitude and sustainability of that bounce are not guaranteed.** A 1-2% mechanical mean-reversion bounce that takes us back to $62.50 doesn't change the fundamental risk picture. It just creates a better exit opportunity for disciplined traders.

The trader's recommendation to "trim 10-20% on any recovery above $62.75" is actually the prudent interpretation of this setup. We shouldn't be betting on a sustained breakout; we should be prepared to defensively trim into any temporary strength.
