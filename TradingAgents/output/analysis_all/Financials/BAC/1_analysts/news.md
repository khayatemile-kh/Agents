### MACROECONOMIC CONTEXT & IMPLICATIONS FOR BAC

#### A. Monetary Policy: The "No Rate Cut" Regime (Critical for BAC)

**Prediction Market Signal (89% probability of zero Fed cuts in 2026)**

This extraordinary consensus against rate cuts has profound implications for Bank of America:

- **Net Interest Margin (NIM) Support**: Higher-for-longer rates preserve wider lending spreads, a core driver of bank profitability
- **Deposit Funding Stability**: Flat yield curve reduces incentive for customers to move cash to money markets, helping banks retain low-cost deposits
- **Lending Pressure**: However, tight credit conditions and weaker loan demand may limit upside to NIM expansion
- **Trading Revenue**: Potential volatility as markets reprice expectations around inflation, geopolitics, and growth

**Context**: The market is pricing near-certainty of restrictive policy continuation, suggesting the Fed views inflation or growth risks as requiring sustained higher rates.

#### B. Banking Sector Stress Signals (64% Bank Failure Probability by YE 2026)

This is a **critical red flag** for the entire financial sector, including BAC:

- **Credit Risk Elevation**: Prediction markets imply meaningful tail risk in the banking system
- **Deposit Outflows/Funding Pressure**: Potential contagion from weaker institutions
- **Loan Loss Reserve Requirements**: Larger banks like BAC may face pressure to build additional reserves
- **Regulatory Scrutiny**: Heightened supervision of capital adequacy and stress scenarios
- **Competitive Dynamics**: Potential consolidation among troubled regional/mid-sized banks

**BAC Resilience**: As a systemically important institution (SIFI), BAC has stronger capital buffers and diversification than the median bank, but sector-wide stress will weigh on equity valuations and profitability.

#### C. Recession Risk Remains Contained (8% Probability by YE 2026)

- Markets price low probability of a 2026 recession
- Suggests underlying economic resilience despite policy tightness
- **For BAC**: Lower default risk on loan portfolio; moderate demand for credit facilities
- **Caveat**: The 8% still reflects non-trivial tail risk; forward guidance from corporates and labor data will be critical
