## **CRITICAL REBUTTAL 2: THE TOKENIZATION THESIS IS FANTASY—NOT STRATEGY**

Let me deconstruct why this is fantasy:

**1. The 5-10% Market Penetration Assumption is Completely Unrealistic**

- Current total U.S. equity trading volume: ~$50T annually
- 5-10% tokenized = $2.5-5T annually
- There is **zero evidence** that tokenized securities will capture this share within 5 years

**Real institutional adoption barriers:**
- Legacy custodians (State Street, BNY Mellon) have zero incentive to adopt tokenized settlement—it disintermediates them

- Geopolitical risk: China has aggressively tokenized assets; U.S. regulators are now *skeptical* of accelerating this trend

The bull admits this: *"a rounding error on current revenue, but incremental and high-margin."*

**Exactly.** So why is the stock rallying 32.6% on a strategic pivot that will contribute **less than 0.1% to annual revenue at 5-year maturity?** The answer: **it's a narrative that allows retail traders and momentum investors to justify fresh capital entry, not a fundamental value driver.**

At $10.56B in annual revenue, a $5-10M incremental revenue stream is immaterial. It's not moving the needle on valuation. The bull is dressing up a financial engineering exercise (buying exposure to tZERO's IP portfolio) as a transformational business strategy.
