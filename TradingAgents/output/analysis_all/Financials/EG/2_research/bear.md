## SECTION 1: THE VALUATION ARGUMENT IS BUILT ON QUICKSAND

### 1.1 The P/B Ratio "Discount" Is Actually a Deterioration Signal

The bull argues EG's 0.95x P/B is a "misprice" compared to its historical 1.10–1.15x. **This logic is backwards.**

Here's what's actually happening:

**Why P/B Compression Occurs:**

| Scenario | P/B Multiple | Market Signal |
|---|---|---|
| High-ROE business in growth mode | 1.30–1.60x | "Book value is growing faster than expected" |
| Stable high-ROE business | 1.05–1.20x | "Reliable, mature business; fair value" |
| Deteriorating ROE / Shrinking franchise | 0.80–1.00x | **"Return on equity is declining; book value is becoming worth less"** |
| Liquidation or distress | <0.80x | "Book value itself is at risk" |

**EG's situation:** The company is transitioning from category 2 (stable) to category 3 (deteriorating). Here's the evidence:

**Declining Underwriting Profitability Signals:**
- **Soft pricing environment** (explicitly stated in the research): EG is being forced to reduce premium volume to maintain margins
- **Q4 2025 earnings stall:** Net income of only $446M (versus $680M in Q2 2025) suggests claims are eating into profits
- **TTM EPS growth is anemic:** $47.33 (TTM) vs. modest forward estimates of $59.75 (which assumes a +26% earnings expansion that feels optimistic given soft pricing)

**The Bull's Fallacy:** Comparing EG's current 0.95x P/B to its 5-year average of 1.10–1.15x assumes the business quality hasn't changed. **But it has.** Soft pricing is a structural shift, not a cyclical blip. EG's ROE is under pressure, and the market is correctly repricing the stock downward.

**Real Bear Insight:** If EG's ROE compresses from 12.6% to 10% (which is plausible in an extended soft market), the "fair" P/B ratio drops from 1.10x to 0.85x. **At that point, EG could be trading at $338—another 11% downside from today's $378.61.**
