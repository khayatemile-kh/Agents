### KEY FINDINGS

#### **1. NEWS CORPORATION-SPECIFIC DEVELOPMENTS**

**Fox-News Corp Re-Merger Possibility (Aug 28, 2026)**
- Reuters reported that Fox (FOX) and News Corp. (NWSA/NWS) could still pursue a future re-merger
- This represents a significant catalyst for NWS stock, as consolidation could:
  - Create operational synergies in content distribution and production
  - Enhance negotiating power with streaming platforms and advertisers
  - Improve cost efficiency through eliminated redundancies
- Status: Still exploratory, but market recognition of this possibility is growing

**Analyst Outlook**
- Despite year-over-year underperformance vs. market indices, analysts maintain **moderately optimistic** views
- This suggests analysts see value not yet reflected in current price
- Potential drivers: valuation attractiveness, dividend yield, strategic optionality (re-merger)

- **Implications for NWS:**
  - Higher interest rates pressure consumer spending and advertising budgets
  - Makes refinancing of corporate debt more expensive
  - However, dividend income becomes more attractive in higher-rate environment

**US Economic Growth Expectations**
- Market consensus shows bifurcated growth view:
  - Most likely scenarios: **2.0-2.5% growth (38%)** or **1.5-2.0% growth (38%)**

- **Interpretation:** Moderate growth likely supports advertising activity, though below pre-pandemic trends

**Recession Risk: Minimal**
- Only 8% probability of US recession by end of 2026

- Suggests NWS unlikely to face demand destruction from macro downturn
