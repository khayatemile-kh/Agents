   - T-Mobile closing retail stores and laying off workers (63 new layoffs across 13 Washington state locations announced).
   - Shift toward app-led (T-Life app) customer interactions and store closures.
   - **Institutional read:** Efficiency play, but signals headcount pressure and branch rationalization—typical of mature telecom under margin compression.

3. **Valuation Lag vs. Peers**
   - "While T-Mobile has trailed its communication peers over the past year, Wall Street analysts maintain a strongly optimistic outlook on the stock's prospects." (Barchart)
   - YTD performance down **9.12%**, materially underperforming AT&T (+?) and Verizon comparables.
   - Verizon narrative contrasts: "The market has crowned Verizon the winner in its telecom peer group, but its operating report card tells a different story" (Trefis)—suggesting institutional skepticism about crowd consensus, but no TMUS upgrade follows.

4. **Competitive Silence & Macro Uncertainty**
   - News flow focuses on SpaceX threat analysis, copper-network depreciation (AT&T), and margin trends—not TMUS-specific catalysts.
   - No earnings announcements, guidance updates, or management commentary in the 7-day window.

#### News Sentiment Rating: **Neutral to Mildly Bearish (4.5 / 10)**
   - Absence of positive catalyst, retail retrenchment signal, valuation lag, and satellite "threat" (even if overstated) create a cautious tone.
   - Positive: Strategic positioning (satellite, app retail) and BofA validation that satellite is complementary, not disruptive.
