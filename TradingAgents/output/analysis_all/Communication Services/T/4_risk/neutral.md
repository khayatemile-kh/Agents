But what happens in Q4 when we move into 2027 and the Fed starts considering cuts more seriously? What if corporate earnings reports in September and October begin signaling margin pressure from consumer spending slowdown? The aggressive case treats the current macro moment as permanent, when prediction markets suggest rates stay elevated through December and then *gradually* normalize. Bearish divergence *can* precede reversals, but divergence can also persist for weeks or months in healthy bull markets. The fact that volume is lower doesn't automatically mean distribution is occurring—it could mean patient accumulation by long-term holders. Lower volume on a rally can also reflect reduced short covering or simply less trading activity during a consolidation phase.

More problematically, the conservative analyst uses the technical warning signals as justification to downgrade positioning entirely. But they never adequately address what happens if AT&T breaks above $26.56 on increasing volume. If the bullish case resumes momentum confirmation, the entire technical risk framework shifts. The conservative view essentially bakes in a single scenario—pullback and reversal—without accounting for the probability that the uptrend simply continues with a healthy pause.
