## **4. ADDRESSING THE COMPETITIVE "THREAT" FROM OPENAI ADS**

Now, I know the bears will point to OpenAI launching ChatGPT ads and say "This is disruption! Traditional agencies are doomed!"

**Let me be direct: This is not the threat it appears to be.**

Here's why:

**Scale of Threat**: OpenAI's initial launch is *only in India* with *50 brands*. This is a pilot program, not a market-wide cannibalization event. OMC operates at a different magnitude entirely—$9.1B in global billings vs. a few million in OpenAI's pilot phase.

**Different Service Layers**: Yes, self-serve ad platforms exist and will grow. But they serve a *different customer segment*—small brands, startups, and cost-conscious players. OMC's core business is *strategic media planning, creative excellence, integrated campaigns, and account management* for Fortune 500 clients. 

You can't replace the strategic expertise that OMC brings with a self-serve tool. A self-serve ad platform is like saying "Amazon has e-commerce, so management consulting firms are obsolete." Wrong. Different value propositions.

**Opportunity**: If anything, **OMC should be *integrating* AI-powered tools into its service stack**—which is exactly what sophisticated agencies are doing. This becomes a *competitive advantage*, not a threat.
