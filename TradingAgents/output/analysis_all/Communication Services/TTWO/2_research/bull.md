**The bear case assumes current losses persist. I'm arguing they're temporary—and the inflection is 11 weeks away.** Once GTA VI launches and drives Q4 revenue north of $2B (vs. recent $1.5-1.7B), and R&D normalizes, net income swings to profitability. That's not speculation—it's math.
