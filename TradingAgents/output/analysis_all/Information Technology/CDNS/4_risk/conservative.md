## THE TECHNICAL SETUP IS NOT A REVERSAL PATTERN—IT'S A WARNING SIGN

RSI at 39.59 is not a reliable reversal signal. It's simply neutral-to-slightly-weak. The data shows RSI doesn't become predictive for reversal until it hits extreme oversold territory (typically 20 or below) or extreme overbought territory (80+). At 39.59, we're in the noise zone where RSI tells us nothing reliable about forward price action. The aggressive analyst is fitting a narrative to an ambiguous technical signal, which is the exact opposite of disciplined trading.

More importantly, what should concern us is the *direction* of RSI. It's declining. RSI has momentum too—momentum of momentum, if you will. When RSI is declining and falling through 40, that's typically a sign of continued weakness, not the setup for reversal. The aggressive analyst wants us to believe that RSI at 39 is "stealth" accumulation. What if it's actually early-stage deterioration? What if RSI continues to 30? Then we have a capitulation setup that justifies waiting.

**On MACD histogram turning positive at +2.02:** This is perhaps the most dangerous misreading in the aggressive case. The analyst claims the positive histogram is "the reversal signal" and that "momentum is shifting." This is technically inaccurate.

A MACD histogram of +2.02 when both the MACD line (-4.61) and signal line (-6.63) are deeply negative means exactly one thing: the MACD line is rising relative to the signal line, but both are still in bearish territory. This is *not* a reversal signal. This is the *very beginning* of potential momentum stabilization. Reversals occur when the histogram crosses through zero *and* turns positive—meaning both lines cross above their centerlines. We are nowhere near that point.

The technical data shows MACD and signal both deeply negative, which means the prevailing trend is bearish. Yes, the histogram is turning positive relative to the signal, but that's like saying a stock is "recovering" when it's down 40% from the highs. Recovery and reversal are not the same thing.
