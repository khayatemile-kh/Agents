- Customers are downgrading to cheaper tiers
- Customers are shortening contract lengths (shifting from 3-year to 1-year agreements)

**The bull wants you to celebrate the absolute dollar amount of deferred revenue ($4.46B) while ignoring that its *growth rate* has collapsed to 2%.** That's not reassuring. That's terrifying.
