**Validation:** Cadence (CDNS) and Synopsys (SNPS) are *also* raising guidance and mentioning AI tailwinds. This isn't KEYS getting lucky—this is a **sector-wide phenomenon**. If anything, it validates KEYS' positioning.

**The Math:**

- Semiconductor companies are investing $30B+/year in advanced fabs and design
- Embedded AI (IoT, automotive, edge) is adding another $10B+/year

A tiny fraction of these budgets ($500M–$1B/year across the EDA sector) flows to KEYS. There's *room to run* for many years.
