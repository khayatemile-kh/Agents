#### **Bullish Factors**
1. **Structural Secular Tailwind**: AI chip proliferation requires advanced EDA and testing solutions; KEYS is a critical enabler
2. **Momentum & Execution**: Beat-and-raise trajectory; orders accelerating; margins expanding with operating leverage
3. **Macro Resilience**: No recession expected; Fed holding rates stable (not cutting aggressively, but not hiking further either)
4. **Analyst Consensus**: 30% upside to price targets; strong earnings revisions
5. **Board Upgrade**: Hiring industrial software expertise signals confidence in new growth initiatives

#### **Headwinds / Risks**

2. **Supply Chain**: With orders >$2B, execution risk exists if supply/manufacturing scales don't meet demand
3. **Competitive Pressure**: Cadence and Synopsys are also benefiting from AI wave; KEYS must maintain differentiation
4. **Cyclical Semiconductor Exposure**: While near-term demand is strong, peak capex cycles in prior years have led to sudden demand reversals
5. **Rate Sensitivity**: If rates rise further or recession probability ticks up unexpectedly, capex could decelerate
