Given genuinely two-sided but not equally weighted evidence - the bear's rebuttals expose specific, concrete flaws in the bull's quantitative assumptions (segment mix math, margin bridge, single-quarter extrapolation) that the bull did not adequately counter - the more defensible near-term stance is cautious. But the preponderance of evidence, especially the technical breakdown coinciding with the fundamental uncertainty, argues against adding exposure here.
