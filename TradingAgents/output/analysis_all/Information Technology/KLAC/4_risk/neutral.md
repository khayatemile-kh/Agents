The Aggressive Analyst conflates "fundamentals are strong" with "price will recover shortly." But let me push back: fundamentals being strong doesn't mean price has to recover in 18 months. It could take 36 months. It could take 5 years. And critically, the path to that recovery doesn't have to be smooth or directional. The Aggressive Analyst assumes that once MACD crosses and technical conditions align, we'll see a "violent squeeze higher." But what if we don't? What if we get a tedious, sideways grind where price oscillates between $165-$185 for months while macro conditions clarify? Now, here's where I really need to challenge the Aggressive Analyst's macro analysis.
