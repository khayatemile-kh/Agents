- KLAC is winning in this space (proven by earnings beat)
- Unlike commodity memory inspection, advanced packaging commands premium pricing and margins

**Services Revenue Growth = Stickiness:**
One often-overlooked strength: KLAC's services business is growing alongside product sales. This reflects:
- Deeper integration into customer workflows
- Recurring revenue stream (less volatile than equipment sales)
- Higher margins than pure hardware
- Creates switching costs for customers
