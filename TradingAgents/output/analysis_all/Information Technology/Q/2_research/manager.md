Because the bull's upside case depends on guidance still being 'unproven and at-risk' (per the bear's own framing) while the bear's downside case depends on leverage and margin risks that haven't yet shown up in trailing results either, neither side has the stronger evidentiary footing today — this is a genuine 'wait for confirmation' situation. The debt is serviceable today but the trajectory needs one more quarter (Q3 2026 actuals) to confirm whether H2 EPS delivers anywhere near guidance and whether coverage ratios hold up as full-year interest kicks in.
