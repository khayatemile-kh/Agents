## THE AGGRESSIVE ANALYST'S CENTRAL FALLACY: CONFUSING CYCLICAL WITH STRUCTURAL

The Aggressive Analyst argues that ADI's margin expansion is "cyclical, not structural," driven entirely by supply constraints that will normalize. This is a reasonable starting hypothesis, but the evidence contradicts it more than it supports it. Let me be precise about this.

**First, the historical comparison is flawed.** The Aggressive Analyst invokes the COVID memory margin spike as an analogue, claiming that when TSMC and Samsung ramped capacity, margins "compressed back to trend within 12-18 months." But this ignores a critical distinction: the COVID shortage was an *artificial supply disruption*. Semiconductor capacity exists; it was simply offline. When factories came back online, supply normalized immediately.

What we're experiencing with ADI's margin expansion is fundamentally different. The demand driver is *structural*—AI infrastructure deployment is not a temporary shortage response. It's a multi-year capex cycle driven by hyperscalers (Microsoft, Google, Amazon, Meta, Tesla) that have made explicit, long-term commitments to AI infrastructure buildout. These are not speculative bets; they are strategic imperatives.

More importantly, the company has *diversified* its margin expansion across multiple end-markets, not concentrated it in a single constraint. Look at the data:

- **Data Centers (AI/Cloud):** Growing, but this is not the only margin contributor
- **Automotive:** ADAS and EV powertrains represent multi-year adoption cycles. The EV transition alone is a 10-15 year thesis
- **Industrial & Defense:** Both showing margin improvement independent of AI cycle dynamics
- **Energy:** Renewable infrastructure is just beginning its buildout phase

This is not one supply-constrained segment inflating margins artificially. This is *four distinct end-markets* all showing simultaneous margin expansion. The probability that all four are cyclical, temporary, and will normalize within 12-18 months is extraordinarily low. The more parsimonious explanation is that ADI has achieved genuine operational leverage through scale and product mix optimization.

Temporary pricing lifts gross margin but doesn't reduce OpEx ratios. Structural efficiency does.
