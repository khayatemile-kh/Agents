We're in 2026. Major hyperscalers (AWS, Google, Microsoft, Meta, Tesla) are *still in the early stages* of AI infrastructure buildout. Many announced plans extend to 2027, 2028, and beyond. The capex cycle for data center AI is **years from peaking**, not peaking now.

**The Margin Expansion Proof Point:**

Here's another data point that proves demand is durable: **gross margins expanded from 61.1% to 67.3% (620 basis points) year-over-year.**

Why does margin expansion matter? Because it indicates:
1. **Product mix is shifting toward higher-value offerings** (AI power delivery, optical solutions)

If the AI cycle were peaking or rolling over, margin compression would typically begin as customers negotiate harder and the company plays down inventory. Instead, we're seeing expansion. This is the *opposite* of a peak-cycle indicator.
