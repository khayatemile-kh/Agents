- Forward P/E of 21.77x
- Expected growth rate of approximately **41% annualized**

Now, let's ground this in reality:

**Is ADI realistically going to grow earnings at 41% annualized for the next 5 years?**

Let me reference the historical data:

- EPS growth from $1.14 to $2.40 = **110% growth over 5 quarters (22% compounded quarterly)**

So yes, recent growth is explosive. **But here's the problem: the bull is using recent growth rates as if they're sustainable forever.**

This is a classic error. **No company grows at 40%+ for years without market saturation, margin compression, or competitive pressure setting in.** The semiconductor industry particularly is prone to boom-bust cycles where explosive growth is followed by sharp deceleration.
