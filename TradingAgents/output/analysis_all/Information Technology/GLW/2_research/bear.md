### Demand Destruction, Not Growth Normalization

Let me challenge the bull's specific claim: "AI infrastructure buildout is shifting from 'acceleration phase' to 'normalization phase'—i.e., from 50% growth to 20-25% growth."

**This is backwards.** Cycles don't normalize downward while remaining healthy. Here's how actual tech cycles work:

**Acceleration Phase:** Growth accelerates, ROIC increases, capex yields positive returns → Stock rises
**Peak Phase:** Growth plateaus at unsustainable levels, margins peak, ROIC compressed → Stock peaks (June 30 at $271.78)
**Normalization Phase:** Growth slows *below* peak levels, but profits remain elevated → Stock consolidates
**Contraction Phase:** Growth turns negative, guidance misses, capex cuts announced → Stock crashes

**We are transitioning from Peak to Contraction.** Here's the evidence:

1. **AI capex cycle is 2-3 years, not 10 years.** Unlike fiber infrastructure (which is government-backed and multi-decade), AI data center capex is private sector, highly cyclical, and driven by incremental model training breakthroughs. Once Anthropic, OpenAI, and Google's models converge on capability, capex intensity *collapses*.

2. **Training capex is front-loaded.** The massive capex we see now is building out *initial training infrastructure*. Inference capex (serving models to users) is far less intensive on interconnects and optics. We're already shifting from training to inference monetization.

3. **The Nvidia $35B Anthropic deal is *not* a growth signal—it's a consolidation signal.** Nvidia is *investing in AI models directly* because chip demand is slowing enough that they need alternative revenue. This is a warning sign, not a bullish catalyst.

### Q1 2026 Earnings Miss Foreshadows Deterioration

**That Q1 miss is the canary in the coal mine.** The bull glosses over it, but a 30% sequential decline in earnings in a company supposedly experiencing "accelerating growth" is a red flag. 

What caused that Q1 miss? The macro report hints at it: "AI hardware trade cooling" started in Q1. Demand decelerated. The company guided down. Margins compressed.

**The bull is extrapolating from Q2's recovery and ignoring the structural deterioration that Q1 revealed.**
