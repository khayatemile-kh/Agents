2. **July 28–29 Crash:** MACD plunged to significantly negative (typical capitulation signature).
3. **August 4 Relief Rally ($518.58):** MACD bounced from deeply negative toward the signal line—this is a **classic momentum washout recovery.**

- It was during the summer lull (lower volume, lower conviction)

This is **exactly the pattern we see before sustained reversals**—multiple shallow bounces, each declining in conviction, until buyers return with fresh catalysts.
