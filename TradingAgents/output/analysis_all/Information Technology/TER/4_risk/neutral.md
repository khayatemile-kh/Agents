## The Real Question: What's Actually Happening at Teradyne?

Let me cut through both narratives and focus on what the data actually tells us.

Teradyne is experiencing a genuine cyclical upturn driven by AI infrastructure buildout. The 104% year-over-year revenue growth is real and structural in nature. The company is deliberately sacrificing margin percentage to capture market share and volume, which is the correct strategic move in a growth phase. The $378 million quarterly free cash flow is real and demonstrates that cash economics haven't deteriorated—management has simply chosen to invest more in capacity and sales effort.

*But*—and this is critical—the company is simultaneously showing signs of deceleration in both sequential earnings and margins. The 3.9% sequential revenue growth is materially lower than the prior two quarters' sequential growth. This could mean one of three things:

**First:** It could be a normalization after rapid growth, with the company now in a sustainable "new normal" of low single-digit sequential growth from an elevated base. That's the Conservative Analyst's interpretation, and it's plausible.

**Second:** It could be early evidence that capex demand is slowing due to rising financing costs, which would accelerate deterioration in Q3 and beyond. That's the Aggressive Analyst's interpretation, and it's plausible.

**Third:** It could be seasonal or timing-related—a product launch cycle completed, customers taking inventory builds. That's also plausible.

The problem is: we don't have enough information to distinguish between these scenarios with confidence. Which means the prudent response isn't to pick one narrative and go all-in on it. The prudent response is to reduce *directional conviction* while maintaining exposure.
