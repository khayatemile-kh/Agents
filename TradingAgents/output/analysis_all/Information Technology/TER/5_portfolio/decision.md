# FINAL TRADING DECISION: TER (Teradyne, Inc.)

## Rating: **Underweight**

---

## Synthesis of the Debate

The three risk analysts converge on more common ground than their rhetoric suggests, and the disagreements that remain are resolvable with discipline rather than heroics in either direction.

**What's undisputed:**
- Technicals have broken down: price ($335.46) below both the 50-day SMA ($377.57) and 10-day EMA ($363.16), MACD deeply negative (-6.59), RSI at 39. The Neutral Analyst correctly notes this is *not yet* confirmed capitulation (RSI hasn't hit 30, volume has been "moderate" not panicked) — but broken structure is broken structure regardless of whether it accelerates further.
- Sequential deceleration is real in the numbers: Q1→Q2 EPS ($2.53→$2.38), net margin (31.1%→28.2%), sequential revenue growth decelerating sharply (18.5%→3.9%). The Conservative Analyst's rebuttal — that this is mathematically inevitable off a larger base and that YoY comps (+104%) remain excellent — is a fair correction to the Aggressive Analyst's alarmism, but it does not erase the fact that the *trend line* the bulls were extrapolating (the $2.53 quarter, 31%+ margins) is not the current run-rate.
- Valuation is stretched, not absurd. Both the Aggressive and Conservative analysts land in the same neighborhood once you normalize: mid-to-high 30s to mid-40s forward P/E depending on assumptions. That is priced for continued strong execution, not for a business that just showed its first sequential wobble.
- Macro (10Y at 4.79%, near-zero cut odds through 2026) is a genuine, non-hypothetical headwind for a growth-multiple cyclical name, even if the Conservative Analyst is right that fab-level capex commitments are multi-year and won't reverse on a dime.

**Where I weight the disagreement:**
- The Aggressive Analyst's $280-305 cascade call is speculative — the Neutral Analyst's point that moderate (not panic) volume argues against an imminent capitulation cascade is well-reasoned and evidence-based, not just optimism.
- The Conservative Analyst's dismissal of technicals as pure "noise" is the weakest link in this debate — a 24% break of multiple moving averages with negative MACD is a real shift in institutional positioning, not something to wave away entirely. The dividend reaffirmation point is a legitimate positive signal, but it speaks to balance-sheet confidence, not to near-term multiple compression risk.
- No analyst produced evidence that Q3 bookings, hyperscaler capex commentary, or backlog data confirm either the bull or bear trajectory. We are deciding under genuine uncertainty about which of the Neutral Analyst's three scenarios (normalization / early capex slowdown / seasonal timing) is correct.

## Decision Rationale

This is a **high-quality franchise showing its first crack in an otherwise exceptional run, trading at a valuation that assumed no cracks, with technicals confirming that the market has already started to reprice that assumption.** That combination argues for reducing conviction-weighted exposure — not holding at full size waiting for clarity, and not panic-selling into unconfirmed cascade risk.

The Trader's proposal and Research Manager's plan are directionally aligned and well-calibrated: trim, don't exit; don't chase the dip; don't add on weakness; use technical bounce zones to execute rather than reactive selling into weakness.

## Final Action

**SELL (Partial — Trim Position)**

- **Size**: Trim 25–35% of existing TER position
- **Entry/Execution Zone**: Scale into strength toward $355–370 (near 10-day EMA/50-day SMA), not into current weakness
- **Stop Loss on remaining core position**: $385 (above the broken 50-day SMA — invalidates the breakdown thesis if reclaimed with conviction)
- **Downside watch/re-entry zone**: $280–305, using limit orders, not market chases
- **Residual core position retained**: Yes — do not fully exit. The AI-test-equipment secular tailwind, fortress balance sheet, and $378M quarterly FCF are real and validated by all three analysts.

## Monitoring Triggers (next 1–2 quarters)
- **Upgrade back to Hold/Overweight if**: Q3 bookings re-accelerate sequentially, hyperscaler/foundry (TSMC, Samsung, Intel) capex commentary firms up, RSI reclaims >50 with price back above the 50-day SMA.
- **Further trim/downgrade to Sell if**: Q3 shows continued sequential EPS/margin deterioration, guidance cuts accompany capex commentary, or price breaks $330 with a volume spike (confirming the Aggressive Analyst's cascade risk rather than the Neutral Analyst's "orderly correction" read).

## Bottom Line

The franchise is not broken; the setup is. Priced-for-perfection valuation + confirmed technical damage + first sequential deceleration in an admittedly cyclical semicap business = reduce, don't hold at full size, don't exit. **Underweight, executing via the Trader's 25-35% trim.**
