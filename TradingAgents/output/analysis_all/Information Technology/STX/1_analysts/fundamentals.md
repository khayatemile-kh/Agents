## COMPANY PROFILE

**Seagate Technology Holdings plc** is a global leader in data storage solutions, headquartered in Dublin, Ireland (with significant operations in the United States). The company designs, manufactures, and sells hard disk drives (HDDs), solid-state drives (SSDs), and related storage systems for enterprise and consumer markets. Seagate competes in the highly competitive computer hardware sector alongside rivals like Western Digital and industry consolidation has been ongoing for years.

**Key Market Position:**
- Leading provider of enterprise storage solutions
- Significant player in PC and gaming markets
- Presence in cloud infrastructure and data center markets
- Focus on data recovery and advanced storage technologies
