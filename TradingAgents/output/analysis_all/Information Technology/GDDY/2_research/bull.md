## PILLAR 3: THE WELLS FARGO "AI OVERVIEWS" DOWNGRADE IS OVERBLOWN

Alec Brondolo at Wells Fargo downgraded GDDY to Underweight with a $76 price target (24-26% downside). The rationale: **AI Overviews now appear in ~48% of searches (up from 10% in Dec 2025), eroding GDDY's domain registration competitive moat.**

This is a **structurally sound concern** that deserves scrutiny. Let me address it head-on.

### The AI Overviews Threat: Real, But Overstated

**What are AI Overviews?** Google's AI-powered summaries that answer queries directly, potentially reducing click-throughs to domain-based websites.

**The bear thesis:** Domains are less valuable if users get answers without visiting websites.

**The bull counter:**

#### 1. **Domain Registration ≠ Search Discovery**

GDDY's domain business isn't dependent on Google search driving traffic. **Domains are foundational infrastructure:**

- **Business registration:** Companies need domains to establish legitimacy (brand registration, legal requirement in many countries).
- **Email hosting:** Enterprise email (name@company.com) still requires domain registration.
- **Recurring revenue:** Domains renew annually; once acquired, they generate predictable MRR.
- **Developer tools:** Developers need domains for API endpoints, testing, staging environments.

**Google search is ONE channel for domain discovery.** It's not THE channel. If anything, **AI Overviews might increase domain registrations** as users click through to AI-recommended websites and then purchase domains to build their own.

#### 2. **The Data Contradicts the Thesis**

Wells Fargo claims AI Overviews went from 10% to 48% of searches in 9 months (Dec 2025 → Aug 2026). Yet GDDY's Q2 2026 results show:
- Revenue growth accelerating to 7% YoY
- Customer acquisition continuing
- No guidance reduction

**If AI Overviews were cannibalizing domains, we'd see it in the numbers already.** We don't.

#### 3. **Competitive Moat: Durability**

GDDY's moat isn't "people discover domains on Google." It's:
- **Brand recognition** (GDDY is synonymous with domain registration for SMBs)
- **Network effects** (hosting + email + DNS all integrated)
- **Lock-in** (migrating domains is friction-heavy)
- **Switching costs** (customers don't want to move email infrastructure)

**AI Overviews threaten SEO services. They don't threaten domain infrastructure.**

#### 4. **The Market Disagrees With Wells Fargo**

Simply Wall St. simultaneously noted that **GDDY screens as undervalued on key metrics** despite the AI Overviews narrative. Institutional valuation models aren't panicking about this—Wells Fargo appears to be an outlier.
