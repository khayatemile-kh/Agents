## Where the Aggressive Analyst is Overreaching

But here's the problem: they're interpreting a normal post-panic volume normalization as evidence of weakness rather than what it actually is—price discovery completing after an extreme event. The conservative analyst is right on this point. When you have 6.67 million shares dumping during a panic capitulation, you're not going to see that volume sustain during recovery. That's not how markets work. Panic selling is abnormal; normal market volume is, well, normal. The aggressive case has inverted the interpretation.

More importantly, let's think about what would actually need to be true for the 20-24% downside scenario to play out. You'd need a cascade of analyst downgrades, institutional selling pressure, and evidence that the litigation or refinancing risks are worse than currently modeled.
