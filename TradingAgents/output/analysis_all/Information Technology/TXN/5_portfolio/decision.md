# FINAL TRADING DECISION: TXN

## Rating: **Underweight**

## Synthesis of the Debate

The three analysts converge more than they diverge once the rhetoric is stripped away, and the disagreement that remains is about **magnitude and timing, not direction**.

**Where all three agree (the load-bearing facts):**
- Gross margin compressed 590bp from 2023 (62.9%) to 2025 (57.0%); operating margin fell from 41.8% peak to 34-35% norm — this is the multi-year baseline, not a one-quarter artifact.
- Q2 2026 (gross margin 61.4%, op margin 42.6%) is genuinely strong, but it is **one data point** against a two-year deterioration trend. Nobody — bull, bear, or neutral — can point to a second confirming quarter.
- Current price (~$253) at ~24x forward P/E is a bet that Q2 2026 is the new baseline. If it isn't, fair value sits materially lower (bear/neutral math: $117-$198 range depending on scenario).
- Technicals are unambiguously weak: 12% below 50-SMA, negative MACD, oversold RSI without confirmed bullish divergence, price down 13.4% from highs, testing the lower Bollinger Band on unremarkable volume.
- Competitive erosion (Vicor in power management, ADI's AI-driven margin expansion, hyperscaler/Broadcom vertical integration) is real and unresolved by one quarter of TXN margin recovery.

**Where they diverge:**
- The Aggressive Analyst treats margin compression as irreversible and pushes for active short-side pressure into $280-290.
- The Conservative Analyst treats Q2 2026 as an inflection and argues consensus $10.50-11.00 EPS is "eminently achievable" — but this leap is **unproven**; it requires margin expansion beyond even the Q2 print, which no one has demonstrated.
- The Neutral Analyst correctly frames this as a probability-weighted set of outcomes, with the **base case (40-45% probability) landing at $170-198** — well below spot — and assigns only 25-30% probability to the bull case that justifies current pricing.

The Neutral Analyst's probability-weighted framework is the most rigorous synthesis here: even giving the bull case its fairest hearing, the *modal* outcome is still meaningfully below the current price. That asymmetry — limited upside probability, substantial downside probability, confirmed by both fundamentals (margin trend) and technicals (downtrend structure) — is what tips this to Underweight rather than Hold. Balance-sheet quality (strong FCF, 35%+ ROE, moderate leverage) is real and is exactly why this is Underweight rather than outright Sell/short — the Conservative Analyst's stabilization signals are not nothing, they just aren't yet confirmed, and a name with this balance-sheet quality doesn't warrant full capitulation on a single ambiguous quarter.

## Decision Rationale

1. **Valuation asymmetry is unresolved in the bulls' favor.** Bull case requires accepting Q2 2026 as structural with no second-quarter confirmation. Base/bear cases (65-70% combined probability per the Neutral Analyst's own framework) put fair value well below spot.
2. **Technical structure confirms distribution, not capitulation.** Lower lows on unremarkable volume is consistent with continued de-rating, not a flush-out bottom — this argues against adding here and supports trimming into strength.
3. **Structural competitive risk (Vicor, ADI, in-house silicon) is a monitorable, not yet falsifiable or confirmable trend** — it warrants a defensive tilt and close tracking, not a full exit given balance sheet quality.
4. **One quarter of margin re-expansion is insufficient evidence** to override two years of deterioration. The bar for moving back to Hold/Overweight (two consecutive quarters of gross margin >59-60%, operating margin >38-40%) has not been met.

## Action Plan

- **Trim existing TXN exposure by 20-30%**, using any relief rally toward the **$280-290 zone** (50-SMA resistance) as the trimming venue rather than selling into current weakness at $253.
- **Do not initiate new long positions** at ~$253; margin of safety is inadequate against a normalized fair value range of $117-198.
- **Do not add outright short exposure** — the balance sheet quality, FCF generation, and lack of confirmed capitulation argue against escalating to a full Sell/short posture at this time.
- **Hard risk trigger:** a decisive break below the 200-SMA (~$235) escalates the stance toward full Sell.
- **Reassessment trigger:** two consecutive quarters confirming gross margin >59-60% and operating margin >38-40% would justify moving back to Hold/Overweight.
- **Retain core position** for existing holders given ROE/FCF quality, but consider covered calls to monetize the elevated implied volatility while the margin-trend question remains unresolved.
- **Monitor** Vicor/ADI share-loss data points and hyperscaler/EV in-house silicon design wins each quarter as leading indicators.
