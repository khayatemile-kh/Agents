### **1. TEXAS INSTRUMENTS (TXN) — COMPANY-SPECIFIC ANALYSIS**

#### **Recent Performance & Sentiment**
- **Outperformance**: TXN outpaced the broader semiconductor index over the past year, despite recent session weakness tied to macro headwinds (surging crude oil, rising Treasury yields).
- **Valuation Paradox**: Wall Street maintains a **cautiously optimistic outlook**, yet valuation models present conflicting signals:
  - **DCF-based fair value**: Approximately at current levels
  - **Market multiples**: Suggest premium pricing (higher risk/reward asymmetry)
  - 3-year return of 68.5% may have priced in meaningful growth expectations

#### **Competitive Positioning**
TXN faces **intensifying competitive pressure** in high-margin segments:
- **Analog Devices (ADI)**: Posted "monster quarter" with AI-fueled growth; strong margin expansion
- **Vicor (VICR)**: Challenging TXN & ADI in power management and AI-driven data-center applications with Gen 2 VPD technology
- **Broadcom (AVGO)**: Leveraging custom AI silicon (TPUs) to maintain profitability despite gross margin compression
- **Industry Mix Shift**: Custom AI silicon (TPUs) is creating a bifurcated market—high-margin for custom designers, pressure on standard analog/power ICs

#### **Key Competitive Vulnerability**
TXN's exposure to **standard analog, embedded processors, and general-purpose chipsets** may face **margin pressure** as the industry shifts toward custom AI solutions. Unlike NVIDIA/Broadcom (concentrated AI revenue), or ADI (growing AI exposure), TXN's more diversified product mix could lag in the current AI supercycle.
