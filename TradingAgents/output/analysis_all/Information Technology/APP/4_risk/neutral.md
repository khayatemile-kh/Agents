This commits a classic error—conflating technical stabilization with directional conviction. Yes, ATR has collapsed 42%. But ATR measures *magnitude of swings*, not *direction*. A stock can have normalized volatility and still drift lower for months. In fact, that's exactly what happened in 2022 with many mega-cap tech stocks: volatility normalized, but prices continued declining for months.

The positive MACD histogram is a real signal, but it's a *minor* one. Both MACD and its signal line are deeply negative at -27.88 and -32.78 respectively. The histogram turning positive just means downward momentum has *slowed*. It does not mean upward momentum is arriving. For a genuine MACD reversal signal, you'd need both the MACD line *and* its signal to be moving toward the zero line or above it. They're not. They're still deeply negative. The aggressive analyst is reading tea leaves and seeing what they want to see.
