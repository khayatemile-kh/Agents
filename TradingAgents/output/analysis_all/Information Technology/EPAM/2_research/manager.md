While the balance sheet is solid in absolute terms (large cash balance, low traditional debt) and the company remains profitable, the trend—not the static snapshot—is what matters for a forward-looking rating, and the trend here is deteriorating. Given the concrete evidence of operational cash burn, debt-funded buybacks, and stalled margin recovery, the bear case is more grounded in current data, while the bull case relies on optimistic extrapolation and activist sentiment as a proxy for fundamental value. This tips the balance toward caution rather than a full sell, since the company is still profitable and has real liquidity cushion (though shrinking), but the risk/reward skews negative until cash flow trends reverse.
