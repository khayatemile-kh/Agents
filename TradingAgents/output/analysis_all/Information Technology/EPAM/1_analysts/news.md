## COMPREHENSIVE NEWS & MACROECONOMIC ANALYSIS REPORT
**EPAM Systems, Inc. (NYSE: EPAM) — September 1, 2026**

### EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

EPAM Systems is facing near-term headwinds from a combination of company-specific challenges and broader macroeconomic uncertainties. Recent developments signal market concerns about valuation, capital allocation, and sector performance in a tightening monetary environment.

---

### I. COMPANY-SPECIFIC DEVELOPMENTS (Week of Aug 25 – Sep 1, 2026)

#### **A. JPMorgan Downgrade — Neutral Rating**
- **Event**: JPMorgan analyst Puneet Jain downgraded EPAM from **Overweight to Neutral** with a price target of **$120**
- **Impact**: Stock traded lower by 3.5% following the downgrade
- **Significance**: The shift to Neutral suggests JPMorgan expects EPAM to perform in line with the broader market rather than outperform—a notable shift in sentiment. The $120 price target may indicate limited upside from current levels.

#### **B. Engine Capital Activism — Capital Allocation Concerns**
- **Event**: Activist investor Engine Capital has urged EPAM to launch a **$750 million share buyback** or consider a strategic sale
- **Implication**: This activist pressure suggests current shareholder base views EPAM's valuation as depressed relative to intrinsic value and/or that management is not effectively deploying capital
- **Context**: The fact that an activist is pushing for buybacks indicates:
  - Strong cash generation capabilities (positive)
  - Management may not have sufficiently attractive organic growth opportunities
  - Potential undervaluation, though this conflicts with the JPMorgan downgrade

#### **C. Cash Generation Commentary**
- Recent analysis noted EPAM as a "cash-producing stock," but highlighted that not all cash-rich companies are effective capital allocators
- This underscores market concern about how well EPAM deploys its substantial cash reserves
