They tell you what traders have been doing recently and sometimes give you probabilistic hints about what they might do next. But they're not predictive in an absolute sense. Let me break this down:

**What the technicals actually tell us:**

1. RSI at 63-64 means the stock has seen strong buying pressure but isn't at extreme overbought levels (70+). That's consistent with an uptrend in consolidation mode. It doesn't tell us if consolidation breaks up or down.
