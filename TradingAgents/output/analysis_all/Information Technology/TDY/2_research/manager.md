**Recommendation**: Hold

**Rationale**: Both sides make legitimate points that roughly offset each other, so I can't in good conscience commit to a strong directional call here. The bull's strongest argument is the operational quality of the business: double-digit revenue growth accelerating sequentially, best-in-class 20%+ operating margins, 25% EBITDA margins, and a forward P/E/PEG (22.7x / 1.4x) that isn't demanding for a company growing earnings at a high-teens-to-20s clip. The defense exposure (DUTCH thermal imaging), the emerging AI-infrastructure validation angle, and the Varex bolt-on all represent real optionality, and the fact that the deal was funded all-cash without stretching leverage is a genuine sign of balance-sheet discipline.

But the bear case lands real punches that the bull doesn't fully rebut. The 30% forward EPS growth number doesn't reconcile with the trailing four quarters of actual EPS ($4.65, $5.84, $4.85, $5.37 = ~$20.72 annualized), meaning consensus estimates require a ~29% sequential jump in the next four quarters that isn't yet visible in reported numbers — this is analyst optimism, not demonstrated acceleration. The balance sheet composition is a legitimate concern: with goodwill/intangibles at ~70% of total assets, the debt-to-tangible-equity picture (~62%) is far less comfortable than the headlined net-debt/EBITDA of ~1.06x suggests, and Varex specifically carries integration and impairment risk given it was acquired at a price well below its IPO level, historically a red flag for a "struggling asset" narrative. The AI-validation opportunity is unproven — no disclosed customer wins, no revenue guidance, and established competitors (Keysight, Teradyne) already operate in adjacent validation markets, so the $500M/60%-margin figure the bull cited is speculative. On top of that, the technical picture (RSI collapse from ~69 to ~33, negative MACD) confirms the market is actively repricing the stock lower right now, and "oversold" alone isn't a reliable buy signal without a fundamental catalyst to reverse sentiment.

Net-net: the underlying franchise quality (margins, defense recurring revenue, disciplined M&A funding) argues against an outright Sell, but the EPS growth assumption embedded in the bull's valuation call is not yet supported by trailing results, and the goodwill-heavy balance sheet plus unproven AI-validation upside make me unwilling to add exposure into a deteriorating technical trend. This is a genuine stalemate between fundamental quality and valuation/estimate risk — that's exactly the situation the Hold rating exists for.
