### Company-Specific Developments (Past Week)

#### **1. Strategic Acquisition: Varex Imaging ($1.1B Deal)**
- **Event:** Teledyne announced an all-cash acquisition of Varex Imaging Corporation (VREX) for $18.90/share on August 10, 2026, valued at approximately **$1.1 billion**.
- **Significance:** This acquisition extends TDY's capabilities into X-ray imaging and components, a critical technology for medical imaging, security scanning, and industrial applications. The deal reflects TDY's strategic pivot toward complementary sensor and imaging technologies.
- **Market Reaction:** VREX shares surged 50% on the announcement, demonstrating investor confidence in the strategic rationale.

#### **2. Defense Contract Wins: U.S. Army DUTCH Initiative**
- **Partnership:** Teledyne FLIR OEM selected to support the U.S. Army's DUTCH (Domestic Uncooled Thermal Imaging Capabilities for the Halo) initiative.
- **Technology Focus:** Advanced uncooled thermal infrared sensing for next-generation defense applications.
- **Strategic Implication:** Positions TDY as a key player in modernizing U.S. military thermal imaging capabilities, with potential for sustained recurring revenue.

#### **3. Technology Infrastructure Expansion**

- **AI Infrastructure Play:** These developments indicate TDY's positioning in the AI infrastructure buildout, capitalizing on growing demand for advanced data center validation and testing solutions.
