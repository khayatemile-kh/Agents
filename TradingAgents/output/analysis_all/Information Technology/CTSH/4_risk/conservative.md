The Aggressive Analyst threw up their hands and said "nobody can explain it"—but that's lazy analysis. IT services companies regularly tap debt markets for refinancing, capital structure optimization, or strategic acquisitions. The fact that the debt *exists* doesn't mean it's destructive; it means we need to understand the *purpose*.

More importantly, look at the actual debt-to-FCF ratio the trader calculated: 1.6x on a trailing two-quarter run-rate. The Aggressive Analyst called this "not terrible in isolation" but then contradicted themselves by calling it "catastrophic." Which is it?
