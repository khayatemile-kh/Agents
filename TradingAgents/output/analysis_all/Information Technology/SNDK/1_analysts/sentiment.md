## Dominant Narrative Themes

1. **AI-driven earnings upside:** SNDK is positioned in the memory/storage segment benefiting from AI infrastructure capex and demand for high-bandwidth memory (HBM).

2. **Valuation re-rating:** After a 30% decline from peak (likely reflecting prior cycle concerns or earnings misses), analyst consensus has upgraded estimates. The headline question "Is Now the Perfect Time?" frames a value-trap vs. value-opportunity decision.

3. **Cycle inflection:** References to Micron and memory chip cycles suggest the broader memory sector may be inflecting (Micron article: "memory cycle that crushed shareholders for three decades may have just broken permanently"). SNDK's upgrade may reflect a view that this cycle inflection benefits memory suppliers.

4. **Limited retail attention:** Contrasts with the massive price move; suggests this is an institutional reallocation, not a speculative retail pile-in.
