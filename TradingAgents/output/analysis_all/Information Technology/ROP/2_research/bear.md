- **Reported Net Income:** $1,168.5M
- **Operating Income:** $584.7M

Strip out that non-operating income, and Q2 2026 net income would be approximately **$333M**—not $1,168.5M.

**But wait—there's worse. Operating cash flow was only $469.5M, while operating income was $584.7M.** That's a $115.2M gap. **Operating income is $115M higher than actual cash generated.** That's not profitability—that's **accounting accruals masking cash shortfalls**.
