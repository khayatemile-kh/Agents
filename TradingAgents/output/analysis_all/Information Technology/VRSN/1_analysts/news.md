## IV. RISK ASSESSMENT

### Downside Risks to VRSN

1. **Regulatory Risk:** ICANN contract negotiations; potential price regulation caps
2. **Alternative Domain Expansion:** Growth of new gTLDs (.io, .app, .cloud) fragmenting registry value
3. **Macroeconomic Slowdown:** If recession emerges (89% no rate cuts suggests restrictive conditions), domain registrations could contract
4. **Geopolitical/Cybersecurity Incidents:** Increased regulatory scrutiny on DNS infrastructure
5. **Interest Rate Volatility:** If rates finally decline, growth tech could re-rate higher vs. steady-state cash flows

### Upside Catalysts to VRSN

1. **Domain Re-Registration Cycle:** Q4 2026 / Q1 2027 (identified in analyst note)
2. **Pricing Power Realization:** Rate increases under ICANN contracts
3. **AI & Cyber Infrastructure Investment:** Expanding enterprise reliance on .com/.net
4. **Defensive Positioning:** Flight-to-quality in higher-rate environment favors VRSN's cash generation
5. **M&A or Strategic Partnerships:** Potential consolidation in infrastructure plays
