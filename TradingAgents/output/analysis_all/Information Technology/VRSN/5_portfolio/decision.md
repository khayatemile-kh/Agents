Net: this is a quality business trading rich for its growth rate with a levered, zero-buffer balance sheet — good enough to hold a reduced position for the cash-generation and dividend characteristics, but not good enough, at this valuation and with unresolved balance-sheet risk, to justify fresh buying or even an unchanged full-size Hold.
