## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

**International Business Machines Corporation (IBM)** is a global technology and information technology services company trading on the New York Stock Exchange (NYQ). As of September 1, 2026, IBM presents a mature, dividend-paying technology company with a market capitalization of **$218.3 billion**. The company demonstrates strong cash generation capabilities, consistent profitability, and strategic positioning in hybrid cloud, AI, and enterprise software markets. However, the company carries significant debt levels relative to equity and shows volatile quarterly earnings patterns.
