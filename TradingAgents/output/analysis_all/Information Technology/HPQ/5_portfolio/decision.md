Net: fundamental deterioration is current and accelerating, not merely a monitoring trigger (unlike the NVDA precedent where flagged risks never materialized)—here the risk (margin compression) is already in the reported numbers across three consecutive quarters, so downgrading conviction now is warranted rather than premature.
