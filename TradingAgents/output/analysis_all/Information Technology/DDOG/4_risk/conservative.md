- 2024: 7.9% operating margin
- 2025: 4.0% operating margin (decline of 390 basis points)

The PEG ratio of 1.13 is telling you something the Aggressive Analyst wants to ignore: DDOG's valuation is *reasonable* relative to its growth rate. The market is not demanding 60% perpetual growth; it's demanding growth sufficient to justify a reasonable return on capital deployed at that multiple over a normalized time horizon (typically 3-5 years).

For context:
- **Current earnings growth:** 35.6% revenue growth YoY in Q2 2026
- **Net income growth:** 1,616% YoY (Q2 2025 to Q2 2026)
- **Free cash flow:** $915M annually at 26.7% of revenue

The Aggressive Analyst cherry-picks the 53.9% growth from 2022 and says 35.6% is "deceleration." But that's comparing a hypergrowth phase to a normalized growth phase. Every mature growth company goes through this transition. Salesforce, ServiceNow, Okta—all of them show similar patterns. The companies that successfully navigate this transition see *expanding* multiples because the deceleration is priced into normalized valuations.
