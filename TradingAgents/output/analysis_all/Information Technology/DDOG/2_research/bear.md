My colleague triumphantly highlighted a 1,616% net income increase from Q2 2025 ($3M) to Q2 2026 ($45M).

**This is mathematically dishonest.**

| Period | Net Income | Operating Margin |
|--------|------------|------------------|
| Q2 2025 | $3M | 0.3% |
| Q3 2025 | $34M | 3.8% |
| Q4 2025 | $47M | 6.4% |
| Q1 2026 | $53M | 6.1% |
| Q2 2026 | $45M | 4.8% |

**What actually happened:** Q2 2025 was an outlier quarter where the company was barely profitable—likely due to one-time charges, restructuring, or accounting adjustments. The subsequent quarters show a return to normalized profitability levels in the $34-53M range with operating margins between 3.8-6.4%.

**Q2 2026's $45M profit is not an acceleration—it's a return to the normalized level the company reached in Q1 2026 ($53M).** We didn't get stronger; we got weaker compared to Q1. The bull is disguising a sequential decline as a glorious inflection by comparing it to the worst quarter from the prior year.
