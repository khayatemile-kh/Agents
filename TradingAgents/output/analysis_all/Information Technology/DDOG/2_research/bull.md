The bear will absolutely harp on valuation. So let's address it head-on.

### **PEG Ratio: The Real Valuation Metric**

**DDOG's PEG Ratio: 1.13**

For those unfamiliar: the PEG ratio divides the P/E ratio by the expected earnings growth rate. A PEG of 1.0 or below is considered *undervalued*. A PEG above 2.0 is generally considered *overvalued*.

**At 1.13, DDOG is essentially fairly valued to slightly undervalued relative to its growth rate.**

This makes sense when you consider:
- **35.6% revenue growth (Q2 2026)**
- **1,616% net income growth (Q2 2025 to Q2 2026)**
