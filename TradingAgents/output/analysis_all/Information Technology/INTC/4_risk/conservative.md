What actually matters: Can Intel service its debt from operations? Yes. Free cash flow is $4.87 billion TTM. Total debt service on $50.54 billion of debt is roughly $2-3 billion annually at current rates. That's entirely manageable from FCF. The company is not in distress territory.

Would I prefer Intel had lower absolute debt? Sure. But in the context of a semiconductor company's business model, the current leverage is within normal parameters. The current ratio of 1.604 is healthy. Interest coverage remains positive. The company is not headed for a credit spiral—that's fearmongering.
