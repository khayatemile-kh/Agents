## The Valuation Trap: "Growth Justifies 75x" Is a Dangerous Assumption

### 1. **The PEG Ratio Argument Falls Apart Under Scrutiny**

The bull claims MPWR at 75x earnings with 17% growth is actually a *discount* on a PEG basis compared to a 20x multiple with 5% growth. Let me dismantle this:

**The math:**
- MPWR PEG = 75 / 17 = **4.41**
- Industry median PEG = 20 / 5 = **4.0**

So MPWR's PEG is actually *higher*, not lower. The bull is wrong. MPWR is trading at a premium even on a growth-adjusted basis.

**But here's the deeper problem:** The PEG ratio assumes:
1. **The 17% growth rate is sustainable.** Is it? The bull offers no evidence. Q2 2026 showed 47.6% YoY growth, but that's a *single quarter*, likely inflated by AI capex peaks. Extrapolating from one exceptional quarter to years of 15-20% growth is dangerous.
2. **Macro conditions remain stable.** The prediction market shows 89% probability of no rate cuts through end-2026. That means *higher-for-longer rates*, which depresses growth stock multiples. As rates stay elevated, semiconductor capex cycles typically cool.
3. **Competitive dynamics remain favorable.** The bull doesn't address whether MPWR can maintain market share against TI, Analog Devices, or new entrants. If competition intensifies, margins compress, and the growth story derails.

**Historical precedent contradicts the bull:** During the 2000 tech bubble, many semiconductor companies traded at 80-100x earnings with similar "growth justifies the premium" arguments. Most crashed 60-80% when growth didn't materialize as expected.
