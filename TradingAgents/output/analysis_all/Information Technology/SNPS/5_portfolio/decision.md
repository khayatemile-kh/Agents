The bull never rebuts the EBITDA leverage comparison or explains why margins are still compressing sequentially over a year into integration rather than inflecting — this is the single most important unresolved question in the entire debate, as the Research Manager's plan also flags. The Neutral Analyst's key insight — that "expected temporary compression" and "guaranteed recovery to 42% margins" are not the same claim, and that hyperscaler royalty adoption is unproven and 2-3 quarters from visibility — is the most balanced read of the evidence. Valuation at 72.5x GAAP P/E already assumes successful integration and margin recovery; if that recovery is delayed, all three of the Conservative Analyst's downside scenarios (macro slowdown, integration delay, royalty underperformance) point to $350-390, while upside to consensus targets is a comparatively modest 25%. Applying the cross-ticker lesson from NVDA — that prospective risks flagged as monitoring triggers should be weighted by probability/timeline rather than treated as either dismissed noise or confirmed impairments — the correct stance is to hold pending one more earnings cycle of margin data rather than pre-committing to either an aggressive add or an exit.
