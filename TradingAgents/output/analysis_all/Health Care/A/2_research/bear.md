The bull says: "PEG of 1.24 is reasonable. Companies above 2x are overvalued."

- **PEG: 1.24x**
- **Implied growth rate: 29.57 / 1.24 = ~23.8% annual growth expected**

Now here's the problem: **Agilent is NOT a 23-24% grower. Not even close.**

Let's look at actual historical growth:

**TTM Revenue Growth:** 8% to 10% (based on Q2 2026 data showing 10% YoY, but company guidance is $7.49B-$7.51B for FY2026)
**Pharma Revenue Growth:** 12% (this is the ONE bright spot, but it's ONE segment)
**Advanced Therapeutics:** 30% (this is also ONE segment, and 30% growth rates NEVER sustain)

**The company-wide growth rate is ~8-10%, not 23%.**

So the bull's "reasonable PEG" is built on the fantasy that:
1. The 30% growth in advanced therapeutics will persist (it won't)
2. The 12% pharma growth will accelerate to 24%+ (no evidence)
3. The base business slowdown will be offset by these segments (unsustainable math)

**That PEG of 1.24 assumes growth rates the company is not delivering.**
