**Analysis Date:** September 1, 2026 | **Reporting Period:** August 25 – September 1, 2026

---

### EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Molina Healthcare (MOH) presents a **mixed but opportunity-rich investment thesis** as of early September 2026. The company benefits from strong recent Q1 results, Medicaid clarity, robust cash generation, and a contrarian endorsement from high-profile investor Michael Burry, yet faces headwinds from elevated medical costs, portfolio restructuring pressures (Medicare Advantage exits), and structural valuation challenges. The broader macroeconomic backdrop shows low recession risk (8% market-implied probability by end-2026) and near-zero Fed rate cut expectations (89% probability of no cuts), which supports stable Medicaid funding but limits near-term rate-driven upside. MOH offers value investors an attractive risk-reward setup, particularly given its 38% 3-year underperformance and 12.2% 90-day recovery.
