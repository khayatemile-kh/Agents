More likely, HUM is exiting specific regional or product-level plans where underwriting assumptions didn't hold. This is *disciplined capital allocation*, not panic. In fact, it's exactly what sophisticated healthcare investors want to see: management willing to walk away from unprofitable business rather than chase volume.

The Aggressive Analyst is asking us to *pre-emptively sell in anticipation of a binary catalyst they cannot quantify*. That's not disciplined risk management—that's guessing about timing. And in my experience, the cost of being wrong on timing typically exceeds the benefit of being right.
