Net-net: there's no clear catalyst on the horizon that the market is mispricing in ABT's favor (bear's point stands), but there's also no balance sheet crisis or fundamental deterioration that justifies selling a diversified, profitable healthcare franchise with multiple long-duration growth options still in play (bull's core defensive point stands). This is a case where the debate is genuinely balanced — real optionality exists but is not being rewarded yet, and downside risk from leverage is more optics than substance. That combination argues for staying put rather than adding or trimming aggressively.
