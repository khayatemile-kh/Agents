## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Gilead Sciences, Inc. presents a mixed fundamental picture as of September 1, 2026. While the company demonstrates strong revenue generation and cash flow capabilities, recent financial results reveal significant headwinds. Most critically, the company reported a substantial **net loss of $10.5 billion in Q2 2026**, driven primarily by a massive $12.6 billion impairment charge related to intangible assets and goodwill writedowns. This extraordinary loss masks underlying operational performance and represents a critical event in the company's recent history.

**Key Takeaway:** Beyond the Q2 impairment charge, the normalized business shows reasonable profitability with strong free cash flow generation, but the asset writedown signals potential strategic challenges or underperformance in key product lines. The company maintains substantial debt (Debt-to-Equity ratio of 223.5%) and negative tangible book value (-$10.5B), requiring careful scrutiny.

## COMPANY PROFILE & BUSINESS OVERVIEW

Gilead Sciences is a biopharmaceutical company focused on the discovery, development, and commercialization of novel medicines in therapeutic areas including:
- **Virology** (HIV, Hepatitis B, Hepatitis C)
- **Oncology** (Cancer treatments)
- **Inflammation & Immunology**
- **Cell Therapy** (CAR-T cell therapies)

The company operates globally with significant revenue contributions from both developed and emerging markets. As of mid-2026, Gilead has a diversified product portfolio but appears to be facing challenges in some key therapeutic areas, as evidenced by the recent asset impairments.
