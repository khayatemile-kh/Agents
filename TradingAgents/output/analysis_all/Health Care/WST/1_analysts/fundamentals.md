### EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

West Pharmaceutical Services, Inc. is a leading global manufacturer of drug delivery and containment solutions for the pharmaceutical and biopharmaceutical industries. The company has demonstrated resilience and growth through 2026, with strong operational momentum, improving profitability margins, and solid cash generation. However, the valuation metrics suggest the stock is trading at a premium relative to historical norms, with elevated leverage requiring monitoring.

---

### COMPANY PROFILE & BUSINESS OVERVIEW

West Pharmaceutical is a critical infrastructure provider in the pharmaceutical supply chain, manufacturing:
- **Primary Packaging Containers:** Vials, cartridges, and syringes
- **Drug Delivery Systems:** Injectable delivery systems and components
- **Containment Solutions:** High-barrier products for sensitive pharmaceuticals
- **Elastomer Components:** Closures, stoppers, and seals

The company serves both traditional pharmaceutical and emerging biopharmaceutical markets, benefiting from structural tailwinds including:
- Rising demand for prefilled syringes and injectable delivery systems
- Biosimilar and biologic expansion
- Specialty pharmaceutical growth
- Global healthcare spending increases
