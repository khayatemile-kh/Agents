### **Positive Catalysts (Forward-Looking):**
1. **GLP-1 Drug Adoption Acceleration:** WST is a key provider of specialty drug-delivery systems (prefilled syringes, cartridges, self-injection devices) to GLP-1 manufacturers. Continued market expansion would drive volume and pricing.
2. **Sector Momentum:** Healthcare outperforming; investor appetite for defensive, secular-growth exposure remains strong.
3. **Execution Visibility:** 43% YTD return reflects demonstrated operational delivery; company has shown ability to capitalize on trends.

### **Negative Catalysts & Risks:**
1. **Valuation Reset Risk:** If market sentiment shifts from growth to value, or if growth expectations modulate, WST could face P/E compression given current premium multiples.
2. **GLP-1 Margin Compression:** As GLP-1 market matures, competitive pricing pressure on drug-delivery components could emerge; WST margins could face headwinds.
3. **Competitive Consolidation:** Larger healthcare peers (Stryker, GEHC, McKesson) are acquiring adjacent capabilities. WST may face pressure to consolidate or risk being bypassed.
4. **Macro Headwinds:** Healthcare spending constraints, legislative price controls, or economic slowdown could dampen drug volumes.
5. **Operational Risk (Sector-Wide):** Boston Scientific cyberattack (Aug 26) highlights supply-chain and operational security risks; WST is not immune.
