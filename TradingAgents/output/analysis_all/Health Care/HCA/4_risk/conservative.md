Now, the Aggressive Analyst introduces a hypothetical: "What if rates rise?" Okay, let's stress-test this scenario:

Even in a stressed rate environment, HCA remains solvent and cash-generative. This is not a "time bomb"—it's a manageable leverage profile for a cash-generative business. **Healthcare REITs and leveraged healthcare operators carry similar profiles and have performed through multiple rate cycles.**

The negative equity position that the Aggressive Analyst flags? That's a *feature of HCA's capital structure*, not a bug. HCA was taken private in a leveraged buyout. The company optimized its capital structure to maximize returns to shareholders by increasing leverage. This is economically rational for a stable, cash-generative business. Negative equity is *expected* in this model, not a red flag.
