## COMPANY PROFILE & BUSINESS OVERVIEW

**Boston Scientific Corporation** is a multinational medical devices manufacturer and distributor focused on treating cardiovascular and other diseases. The company operates across multiple therapeutic areas including cardiovascular, rhythm management, neuromodulation, oncology, and endoscopy. With operations in over 160 countries, BSX serves hospitals, physician offices, and other healthcare facilities globally.

**Industry Classification:** Healthcare / Medical Devices (Diversified medical device manufacturer)  
**Key Markets:** United States, Europe, Emerging Markets  
**Primary Products:** Stents, cardiac rhythm management devices, electrophysiology systems, neuromodulation devices, and endoscopy equipment.
