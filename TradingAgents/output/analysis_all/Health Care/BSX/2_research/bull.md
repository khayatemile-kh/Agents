This is where I need to directly challenge the bear narrative.

### **Why the Cyberattack Impact Is Likely Contained**

**Bear Argument:** "It's been going on for a week with no resolution! Revenue will crater! Customer relationships damaged!"

**Bull Response:** Let me provide context from recent precedent:

1. **Similar incidents resolve in 2-4 weeks, not months.** When healthcare companies (and broader industrials) experience cyberattacks, the median recovery time is 10-14 days for core operational restoration. We're at day 7-8 as of September 1. A 2-week recovery window means:
   - October revenues minimally impacted
   - Full operational restoration by Q4 2026
   - Any revenue delay is likely pushed to October/November, not permanently lost

2. **Revenue recognition timing, not destruction.** The critical distinction: delayed revenue is not *lost* revenue. Hospitals and clinics aren't going to switch to Abbott LAA devices or competitor stents because BSX's order processing was down for 2 weeks. They'll backlog orders and place them when systems are online.

3. **This is actually a *bullish* long-term setup.** Once systems are restored and backlogs clear, Q4 2026 and Q1 2027 could actually see *elevated* revenue as pent-up demand clears. This could lead to a significant positive earnings surprise when management provides guidance.

### **Historical Precedent: Ransomware Recovery**

Looking at other major healthcare/industrial ransomware incidents:
- **Universal Health Services (2020):** 2-3 week downtime, full recovery, no permanent customer loss
- **Sony Pictures (2014):** ~1 month recovery, business continued
- **Brenntag (2021):** ~3 weeks, no permanent damage to customer relationships

**BSX is not unique. This is a known playbook, and companies recover.**

### **Customer Stickiness**

Here's a fact bears ignore: **BSX's customers (hospitals, clinics) are locked in by regulatory approval, clinical training, and supply chain integration.** A 2-week order processing delay does not cause customers to:
- Switch to competitors (FDA approval takes years)
- Damage relationships with BSX
- Redirect purchasing long-term

In fact, customers will likely *be more reliant on BSX* post-recovery as they rush to rebuild inventory that was delayed.
