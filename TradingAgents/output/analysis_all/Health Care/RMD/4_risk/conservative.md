**First: Is this margin expansion sustainable, or is it already baked into current expectations?** The forward P/E of 17.47x has already been calculated by the market *assuming* this 27% EPS growth will materialize. If that margin expansion proves to be a one-year anomaly driven by temporary cost controls, cloud transition accounting benefits, or operating leverage that doesn't persist, earnings will miss forward guidance. And when a stock trading at 17.47x forward P/E misses on a 27% growth expectation, the repricing is violent—not to the upside, but downward.

**Second: What happens if cloud adoption slows or faces price competition?** The aggressive case posits that cloud transition is "non-discretionary" and commands "pricing power." But here's what the data actually shows: the sentiment report explicitly flags "low confidence" in retail conviction. Only 2 StockTwits messages. Zero Reddit posts in major investing communities. That's not a structural tailwind that's "underpriced by the market." That's a narrative that barely exists in the market's consciousness yet.

And that matters, because if the narrative doesn't exist, there's no base of conviction to support the stock if execution stumbles. We have a company trading at 17.47x forward on 27% earnings growth. That assumes flawless execution. One quarter of guidance miss—even a modest one—and this valuation compresses by 15-20% immediately.
