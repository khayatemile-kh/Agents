Balance sheet is not distressed (2.49x current ratio, positive FCF), so this isn't a hard 'Sell' where balance-sheet risk or insolvency is a factor. But the core growth driver—Type 2/GLP-1 dynamics and credibility of forward guidance—has real structural uncertainty, and management's own guidance cut post-beat is the single most damning fact in this debate: it directly contradicts the market's ability to trust the acceleration narrative the bull leans on. Given that the burden of proof on the pivotal issue (durability of Type 2 pump demand) favors the bear's structural-reallocation thesis over the bull's 'temporary pause' assertion, and management's own actions (cutting guidance right after a beat) validate caution over conviction, I'm landing on a cautious stance rather than a full sell (balance sheet isn't broken, pipeline optionality has some real value, valuation has already compressed significantly) or a full buy (too many open questions on demand durability and margin trajectory to add confidently here). This is an Underweight: trim/reduce conviction-weighted exposure, don't add on weakness until Type 2 retention trends and Q3/Q4 bookings visibly stabilize.
