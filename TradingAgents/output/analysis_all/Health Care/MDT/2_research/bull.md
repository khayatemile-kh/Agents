**Historical Benchmarking:**

This represents a **2x acceleration** over historical norms for a company of this scale. You don't achieve that through accounting gimmicks or one-time tailwinds—you achieve it through **operational excellence, market share gains, and portfolio optimization**.

The bull case here is that growth is **diversified across multiple therapeutic areas**:

2. **Diabetes Care** (MiniMed spinoff momentum)

3. **Neuroscience & Surgical Solutions**
   - Broad-based, non-concentrated growth
   - Reduces risk of reversal; if one area slows, others can offset
