## **THE ROIC ARGUMENT IS MISLEADING—AND ABOUT TO IMPROVE**

Now, the HOLD recommendation flags "ROIC concerns (~6.75% vs. ~7-8% WACC)" as a reason to avoid chasing the rally. This is where the analysis becomes stale.

Let me be clear: **ROIC is backward-looking.** The calculation is based on *historical* earnings against current invested capital, much of which was deployed years ago in acquisitions that hadn't yet matured. But MDT's recent growth inflection means the earnings power of that capital base is *accelerating*.

Here's the math:

**Current ROIC: ~6.75% (trailing)** – this reflects TTM earnings of $4.80B on invested capital.

**Forward ROIC trajectory:** If MDT delivers on the raised guidance and maintains even 10% organic growth (well below the 13.7% Q1 just achieved), earnings could grow to $5.5-$5.8 billion within 12 months. That's a 15-20% earnings expansion. Deployed against the same capital base, ROIC would tick up to **7.5-8.0%**, which either meets or exceeds the WACC threshold.

The conservative analyst will say, "That's speculative." I say that's ignoring the data. The company just demonstrated it can grow at 13.7% in a single quarter. Assuming even a *partial normalization* to 9-10% for the remainder of the year is not aggressive—it's actually conservative relative to the stated guidance raise.

**The $52.7 billion goodwill sensitivity is also overstated.** Yes, a major impairment would hurt shareholder equity. But goodwill impairments occur when *business performance deteriorates*—and MDT just demonstrated the opposite. The risk of impairment is *lower* today than it was three months ago when the stock was trading at $73. Why? Because the underlying cash generation power of those acquisitions is being validated in real time by organic growth acceleration.
