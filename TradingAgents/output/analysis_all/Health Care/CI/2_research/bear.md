## **MARGIN COMPRESSION IS NOT SECTOR-WIDE NOISE—IT'S STRUCTURAL AND ACCELERATING**

The bull says: *"Margins are compressing across the sector, so CI is fine because it's navigating well."*

**I directly challenge this.** Let me show you the specifics:

### **CI's Operating Margin: 3.99% Is Razor-Thin and Deteriorating**

From your fundamentals report: **Operating Margin = 3.99%**

For context on healthcare insurance:
- A 1-2% margin means the company is barely profitable on core operations
- A 3-4% margin suggests the company is dependent on non-operational items (investment gains, one-offs) to maintain net profitability
- A 5%+ margin is healthier and more sustainable

**CI at 3.99% is on the precipice.** Here's why this matters:

When you have a 3.99% operating margin and medical costs rise 5-7% (typical during inflationary periods), **you have zero cushion**. You either:
- Cut costs (impossible in healthcare insurance—you can't reduce claims)
- Raise premiums (invites competition and loses enrollment)
- Accept margin compression (which is what's happening)

**The recent Q2 negative OCF tells us the company has already hit the wall on this trade-off.**
