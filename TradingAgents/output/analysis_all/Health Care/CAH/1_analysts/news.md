## **COMPANY-SPECIFIC DEVELOPMENTS (CAH)**

### **Q4 FY2026 Earnings & Capital Allocation**
Cardinal Health's Q4 2026 results reveal:
- **Revenue**: $63.7 billion in Q4 sales
- **Net Income**: $398 million in Q4
- **Non-GAAP Diluted EPS**: $2.91 (+40% YoY) — though a **one-time tariff refund** significantly inflated this figure
- **Full-Year Adjusted Free Cash Flow**: $5.0 billion
- **Share Repurchase Authorization**: Fresh $5.0 billion authorization (on top of $2.08 billion already spent, retiring ~5.7% of shares)
- **Revolving Credit Facility**: Expanded to $4.0 billion
- **Quarterly Dividend**: $0.5158 per share (reaffirmed)

**Key Insight**: The 40% EPS growth masks structural challenges — a significant portion came from a one-time tariff benefit, not underlying operational improvement. Adjusted free cash flow of $5B indicates solid underlying cash generation.

### **Operational Headwinds & Competitive Dynamics**
Recent analysis highlights several material risks:

1. **IRA Pricing Pressure**: Inflation Reduction Act-driven pricing changes pose a headwind to pharmacy margins
2. **GMPD Challenges**: Generic Manufacturer Price Drop (GMPD) related margin compression
3. **Heavy CapEx Burden**: Significant capital expenditure requirements may limit future shareholder returns and margin expansion
4. **Specialty Segment Growth**: The company enters FY27 with strong specialty distribution growth (oncology, GLP-1 therapies), which are higher-margin, higher-volume drivers
5. **Rite Aid Integration**: Linked to CVS Health's pharmacy strength into H2 2026, suggesting healthcare retail consolidation continues

### **Insider Trading Activity — Major Red Flag**
Between late August 2026, **multiple senior executives sold substantial stock positions** following the company's 58% one-year stock surge:
- **CEO Jason Hollar**: Sold 124,529 shares (~$29 million) over two days
- **CFO**: Divested $9.9 million in stock
- **Medical Segment Head**: Sold $8 million in stock
- **Pharma Chief**: Reduced position (net +47% holdings despite selling due to option exercise)
- **CIO Michelle Greene**: Sold 11,650 shares
- **Chief Accounting Officer**: Reduced equity stake

**Trading Implication**: While option exercises and diversification are normal for executives, the **coordinated, large-scale selling across the C-suite** following a 58% stock run-up signals potential concern about valuation or near-term headwinds. Insiders retaining stakes at lower valuations prefer to sell at peaks.
