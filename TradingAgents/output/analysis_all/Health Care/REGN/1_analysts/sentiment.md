## Executive Summary

REGN sentiment across the three-source dataset is **Bullish**, driven by FDA approval of Pasatru (garetosmab) for fibrodysplasia ossificans progressiva and strong retail conviction on the social platform. Overall sentiment is constructive but not euphoric, with near-term price tension evident.
