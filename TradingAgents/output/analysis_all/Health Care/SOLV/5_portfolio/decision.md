Net assessment: reduce, don't eliminate. A full exit (Conservative) overweights a single unconfirmed bad quarter and ignores the Q2 improvement; holding 70-80% (Aggressive) is inconsistent with the same analyst's own bearish fundamental case. Applying a lesson from prior cycles: prospective/monitoring-stage risks (here, credit cascade risk that "could" accelerate) should not be treated as confirmed impairments without at least one more confirming data point — hence Underweight rather than Sell, with a explicit trigger structure rather than an outright exit.
