1. It locks in the 13% gain and reduces your capital at risk
2. It right-sizes your exposure to a company with uncertain cash flow trajectory and elevated leverage
3. It leaves you with meaningful upside exposure if Q3 and Q4 data show the recovery is real
4. It prevents you from being overexposed if the deterioration resumes

The Aggressive Analyst's $82.69 stop assumes technical support will hold. The Conservative Analyst's $87-88 stop assumes things could fall apart quickly. The $86 level splits the difference—it's tight enough to acknowledge elevated risk, but not so tight that normal pullbacks shake you out.

- Operating cash flow is positive again
- Receivables are stabilizing quarter-over-quarter
- Management is guiding for sustainable FCF

If those answers are yes, you might actually add back at lower prices. If they're no, the 40-50% trim you've already done protects you while the remaining position gets stopped out at $86.
