- **Retail (StockTwits):** Mildly bullish on technicals (breakout noted); no strong conviction; profit-taking observed
- **Retail (Reddit):** Silent (zero posts)

**Interpretation:** Institutions are signaling that upside is limited; retail is neither excited nor alarmed. This mismatch is a sign that **sentiment is transitioning**, not that retail is front-running a bullish move. Retail watchers are tracking price, but conviction is low.

### **Dominant Narrative Theme: Post-Run Valuation Concerns**
The strongest recurring theme across both sources is that SOLV has already had a strong 6-month rally (22% vs. S&P 500 +11.6%) and the stock is no longer obviously cheap. The question now is whether further upside is available or if the stock has "run out of gas." No new catalyst (product launch, acquisition, earnings surprise) is highlighted in the news flow, which suggests sentiment is consolidating rather than building.

## Confidence Assessment

**Confidence: LOW to MEDIUM**

### Rationale:
1. **News sample:** Only 2 articles, and one is a generic framework piece. Limited institutional signal density.
2. **StockTwits:** 3 messages is a very small sample. No bullish/bearish tags means users are not attaching conviction language. Sentiment here is observational, not sentiment.
3. **Reddit:** Zero posts. While silence can be a signal, it is not as strong as active bearish discussion.
4. **Overall:** The data set is sparse. If SOLV were generating strong institutional or retail interest (bullish or bearish), we would expect denser coverage. The sparsity itself is mildly bearish—it suggests the stock is not front-and-center for either institutional or retail traders at this moment.

**Confidence would upgrade to MEDIUM-HIGH if:**
- We had 5+ news articles with varied framing
- StockTwits had 20+ messages with clear bullish/bearish split
- Reddit had 2+ threads with >100 upvotes and substantive discussion
