### Rationale

- **Why Bullish (7.5)?**
  - Strong institutional news flow, centered on genuine earnings beats and raised guidance.
  - Retail sentiment is uniformly positive, even if low-volume.
  - No bearish countercurrent in any source.
  - Forward narrative (AI leadership, acquisition integration) supports momentum.
  - Stock price action (+21.7% in a month) reflects conviction.

- **Why Not Higher (8–10)?**
  - Retail engagement is minimal (3 StockTwits posts, zero Reddit posts). This limits confidence in broad-based enthusiasm.
  - Risk factors (pricing, liquidity) are mentioned but not elaborated; potential downside not fully priced in retail dialogue.
  - Post-earnings rally is already 5–21% into the books; near-term upside may be limited without new catalysts.
  - No analyst downgrades or macro headwinds visible, but neither is there evidence of a buildup of retail conviction that could drive further rallies.

- **Why Medium Confidence?**
  - News source is robust and fact-driven (earnings results are verifiable events).
  - StockTwits sample is very small (n=3); not enough to anchor a strong bullish signal in retail.
  - Reddit silence is uninformative (could mean IQV is off-radar or low-volume on those platforms).
  - Overall, institutional framing is clear and positive, but retail validation is weak. A medium-confidence rating reflects strong news but weak retail confirmation.
