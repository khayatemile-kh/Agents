You claim IQV has "near-monopolistic market penetration" with 19 of 20 largest pharma companies using their AI platforms. Let me deconstruct this claim.

**First, using a platform ≠ switching costs:**

- Yes, 19 of 20 pharma companies use IQV's AI. But they also use:
  - **Veeva Systems** for clinical trial management
  - **IBM Watson** for drug discovery
  - **Tempus AI** and **Insitro** for AI-driven biomarker discovery

**Second, the data moat is weaker than you think:**

- Clinical trial data is **regulated and compartmentalized**. IQV can't simply recombine data across trials without explicit client consent and regulatory approval

- The competitive landscape isn't IQV vs. boutique competitors—it's **IQV vs. big tech** that's entering healthcare. That's a losing war for margin and market share

**Third, 19 of 20 clients using your platform is actually a concentration risk for IQV, not a moat:**

- If those 19 clients decide collectively to reduce trial volume (during a recession or pharma consolidation), IQV's revenue compresses by 40-50% overnight
- You're not selling to a diverse base—you're selling to oligopoly clients in pharma who have enormous negotiating power
- This is why pricing pressure is *real*, not hypothetical
