The data explicitly states: "improving... Optum results" (from Wall Street). This division includes:

The forward P/E of **17.81x** (vs. TTM P/E of 25.44x) is the market's implicit forecast that earnings will grow significantly. **If margins recover as the cycle indicates, that forward P/E *compresses further* and stock outperforms.**
