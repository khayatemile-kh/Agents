## COMPANY PROFILE & BUSINESS CLASSIFICATION

Stryker Corporation is a diversified, globally leading healthcare company that specializes in medical devices, serving the orthopedic, neurotechnology, and spine markets. The company operates across multiple divisions providing surgical instruments, implants, and technologies for reconstructive and trauma surgery, as well as neurosurgical and endoscopic systems. Stryker's business model is characterized by:

- **Geographic Diversification:** Strong presence in North America, Europe, and emerging markets
- **Product Portfolio:** Comprehensive offerings in orthopedics, neurotechnology, trauma, and surgical instruments
- **End Markets:** Hospitals, ambulatory surgical centers, and healthcare systems globally
- **Growth Drivers:** Aging demographics, minimally invasive procedures, and emerging market expansion
