**Rationale**: The bull case rests on three pillars: (1) FY2026 margin recovery to 27.9% proving the FY2025 collapse was one-off, (2) a "fortress" balance sheet with low leverage, and (3) technical consolidation (Bollinger squeeze, RSI 62, MACD still positive) setting up for another leg higher. Critically, the bear points out that the most recent quarter (Q1 FY2026: $311.4M vs $316.2M in Q1 FY2025, -1.5% YoY) confirms the FY2026 full-year -0.4% YoY decline is not noise but an actual deceleration trend following FY2025's peak. That's a real, data-backed rebuttal — the bull never addressed the Q1 quarterly deceleration, only hand-waved the annual number. The bear's valuation argument is also hard to dismiss: 62x trailing P/E against essentially flat-to-negative revenue growth is a steep multiple with no room for error, especially versus peers (TMO, ILMN, CRL) growing mid-to-high single digits or better at meaningfully lower multiples. Even if TECH's margins are superior, a 2x P/E premium on negative growth is asking the market to pay for a turnaround that hasn't shown up in revenue yet.

On technicals, the bull's "coiling for a breakout" thesis is plausible but speculative — Bollinger compression can resolve in either direction, and the bear correctly notes the MACD has collapsed ~87% from its July peak and is now in a bearish crossover (line below signal), while RSI has fallen 15 points from its June peak. The bull's rebuttal that "MACD line is still positive" is weak — what matters for near-term momentum is the crossover and the decaying histogram, both of which favor the bear's short-term caution.

Where the bull is stronger: balance sheet quality (12.7% debt-to-capital, minimal net leverage) and multi-year revenue growth (+6.9% cumulative, or ~2.3% CAGR) are real positives that argue against a "value trap" characterization and against an outright Sell. The company is not fundamentally broken — it generates real cash, has optionality for M&A/buybacks, and the margin recovery, even if partly a function of easier restructuring comps, is directionally positive. But the price has already run 42% and sits at 52-week highs while trading at a rich ~62x multiple against a business whose top line just went negative in the most recent quarter — that combination materially skews the risk/reward to the downside from here. This isn't a broken company, but it is a fully priced (arguably overpriced) one showing early cracks in the growth narrative just as momentum indicators roll over.

Given the fresh quarterly deceleration, the extreme valuation versus decelerating growth, and momentum indicators turning down from euphoric levels, the stronger, most current evidence favors trimming exposure rather than adding into strength or just holding at these valuation extremes. This isn't a full Sell — balance sheet strength, cash generation, and long-term margin trajectory keep this from being a broken thesis — but the risk/reward at 62x P/E with decelerating revenue and rolling-over momentum warrants a cautious, Underweight stance until either (a) revenue reaccelerates for two consecutive quarters, or (b) the multiple compresses meaningfully to better reflect the growth profile.
