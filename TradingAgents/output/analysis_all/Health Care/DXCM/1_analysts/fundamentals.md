## COMPANY PROFILE & BUSINESS OVERVIEW

**DexCom, Inc.** is a premier medical device manufacturer focused on real-time glucose monitoring systems. The company's core product portfolio serves diabetes management, with particular strength in continuous glucose monitoring technology that has become essential for Type 1 and Type 2 diabetes patients requiring insulin management.

**Key Business Characteristics:**
- **Primary Market:** Diabetes care management (continuous glucose monitoring)
- **Geographic Presence:** Global operations with significant U.S. market presence
- **Business Model:** Device-as-a-Service with recurring revenue streams
- **Regulatory Environment:** FDA-regulated medical devices
