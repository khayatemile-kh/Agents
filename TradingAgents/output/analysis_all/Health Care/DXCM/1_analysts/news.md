### **EXECUTIVE SUMMARY**

DexCom (DXCM) is trading at **$91.17**, up **23.6% year-to-date** with significant momentum relative to the S&P 500 (+11.5% outperformance over the past 6 months). As of early September 2026, DXCM is navigating critical inflection points: Medicare coverage expansion potential, competitive pressure from Abbott Diagnostics, and favorable macro conditions with very low recession risk priced by markets.
