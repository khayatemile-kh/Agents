### CONCLUSION

International Paper Company (IP) faces **mixed near-term headwinds balanced against medium-term tailwinds**. The historic mill closures and timber economy collapse represent structural supply challenges that could compress margins in the short run. **Macro backdrop is supportive**: No Fed rate cuts and low recession risk create a stable environment for consumer cyclical demand. However, elevated rates increase IP's cost of capital, a structural headwind for the equity.
