On balance, the bear's argument that this earnings quality is policy-contingent and could reverse sharply is more analytically rigorous than the bull's assumption that tariffs are quasi-permanent. That said, the balance sheet strength argument is real and not really disputed by the bear—Nucor's low leverage genuinely reduces downside risk if margins do compress, and the dividend aristocrat status provides some support. The stock isn't a table-pounding buy at peak-cycle multiples on policy-contingent earnings, but the fortress balance sheet and dividend support argue against an outright sell/exit call.
