### Industry Tailwinds & Headwinds

#### **Tailwinds:**
1. **Supply Constraints:** Tight global nitrogen capacity benefits incumbents like CF with pricing power
2. **Capital Intensity:** High barriers to entry protect CF's market position; new entrants require multi-billion-dollar capex
3. **Commodity Cycle:** Elevated grain and agricultural commodity prices support farmer purchasing power for inputs
4. **Energy Transition:** Low-carbon ammonia (Blue Point One) addresses ESG mandates and regulatory pricing premiums
5. **Shareholder Returns:** Demonstrated commitment to returning cash suggests confidence in sustained profitability
6. **Domestic Supply Focus:** Political priority on U.S. supply security supports permitting and offtake agreements

#### **Headwinds:**
1. **High Capex Requirements:** $4B investment increases balance sheet leverage; execution risk on 2029 ramp
2. **Interest Rate Persistence:** 89% probability of no Fed cuts means financing remains expensive through 2026–2027
3. **Commodity Price Volatility:** Fertilizer prices are cyclical; a sharp collapse in agricultural commodity prices could compress demand
4. **Agricultural Cycle Dependency:** Farmer purchasing tied to crop profitability; extended drought or surplus could weaken near-term demand
5. **Execution Risk:** Large construction projects face supply chain, labor, and cost inflation risks
