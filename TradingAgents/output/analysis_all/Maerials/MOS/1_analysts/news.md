### **SECTION 3: INDUSTRY DYNAMICS & SUPPLY-DEMAND BALANCE**

#### **Supply-Side Catalysts (Bullish for MOS)**

1. **Middle East Production Disruptions**: Ongoing conflict/instability reduces global potash supplies, supporting price floors for Mosaic's core potash exports and phosphate products.
2. **North American Capacity Response**: $4B+ in announced capex by competitors and partners signals industry recognition that demand will remain strong, justifying massive investment despite high rates.
3. **Strategic Positioning**: Mosaic's existing North American assets position it to benefit from regional supply tightness and customer preference for stable, non-disrupted suppliers.

#### **Demand-Side Uncertainties (Mixed for MOS)**

1. **Farmer Affordability**: High interest rates constrain farm financing for fertilizer purchases; this could dampen volume demand near-term.
2. **Commodity Prices**: Grain (corn, wheat, soybeans) prices determine farmer ROI and nutrient-spending decisions—no directional signal in this week's news.
3. **Global Agricultural Conditions**: No detailed crop reports in the review period; normal seasonal factors in H2 2026 (post-harvest planning) may reduce near-term nutrient demand.
