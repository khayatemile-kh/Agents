Costco at 50x earnings in 2019 looked like a bubble. It's now at 60x. Netflix, Adobe, ServiceNow—all have sustained premium multiples precisely because they keep beating expectations.

And here's where your argument starts to wobble: you're essentially saying "premium multiple + one macro headwind + one missed quarter = crater." That's plausible. But it's also just... one scenario.

What you're not adequately weighing is the *conditional probability* of each component. Let me lay this out:

**The odds you're implicitly betting on:**
- APD misses guidance: Maybe 30-40% (reasonable)
- If it misses, multiple compresses 20%+: Maybe 60-70% (reasonable)
- Combined = 18-28% downside probability

But here's what you're *underweighting*:
