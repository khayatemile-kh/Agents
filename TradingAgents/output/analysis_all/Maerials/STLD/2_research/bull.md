### Why This Matters for STLD's Margins

Canadian steelmakers compete on *price*, not differentiation. They have lower labor costs and favorable currency dynamics. Without tariff protection, they undercut U.S. domestic producers like STLD by 5-10% on commodity grades.

**With a 50% tariff in place:**
- STLD gains **pricing power** to raise prices or defend margins against Canadian competition
- Customers face a 50% premium on Canadian imports, making STLD's product economically attractive *even at higher prices*
- This translates directly to **gross margin expansion**—exactly what we're seeing in Q2 2026

Look at the margins in our financial data:
- **Q2 2026 Gross Margin: 15.7%** (vs 14.6% in Q1, vs 12.0% in Q4 2025)

That's **110 basis points of gross margin expansion in a single quarter**. This isn't accounting magic—it's operational leverage from reduced Canadian competition.
