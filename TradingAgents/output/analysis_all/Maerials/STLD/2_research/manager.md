**Recommendation**: Hold

**Rationale**: Both sides agree on the core fact pattern: STLD's recent margin/EPS surge (Q2 2026: 15.7% gross margin, 12.4% operating margin, $3.69 EPS, +32.7% QoQ) is closely tied to the 50% Canadian steel tariff, and the durability of that tariff is genuinely uncertain per the Polymarket data (only 22% probability of resolution by Oct 2026, 48% by Dec 2026, with probabilities having swung sharply week-over-week). The bull's strongest point is that operating leverage is real and structural EAF assets provide a baseline cost advantage even in normal cycles - STLD isn't going back to being a marginal producer. The bear's strongest point is more decisive, though: the bull's entire valuation case (PEG of 0.21x, 62% forward growth) is built by extrapolating a single peak quarter into a full-year run-rate, ignoring STLD's own recent history of a 570bps margin collapse from Q3 2025 to Q4 2025 with no tariff catalyst needed - that's just normal cyclical/seasonal volatility. The bear also correctly flags that the market's own tariff-resolution odds are trending toward resolution, not away from it, undermining the bull's "5-6 quarter runway" framing. Critically, using a peak-quarter annualized run-rate to justify a "screaming cheap" PEG is an analytical error - the growth rate embedded in consensus already assumes tariff persistence, so if tariffs are removed or watered down (which the prediction market gives real, rising odds to happening within 2-4 quarters), the earnings base that justifies the current multiple could evaporate quickly, and the stock, having already run up on this narrative, is vulnerable to a sharp de-rating. That's a meaningfully asymmetric risk: modest further upside if tariffs persist and normalize into structural pricing power, versus a larger downside (peers with similar EAF technology can also expand capacity, eroding any "structural" advantage) if the catalyst fades on the currently-favored timeline. Given the binary, event-driven nature of the primary catalyst and the historical evidence that STLD's margins are highly cyclical even absent tariff swings, I don't think this is a high-conviction Buy. But the bear case doesn't fully invalidate the current fundamental improvement either — Q1 and Q2 2026 already show real sequential strength, and EAF assets do give STLD better relative positioning than older integrated-mill competitors, so this isn't a Sell either. The prudent call is to trim the aggressiveness embedded in the bull's tariff-dependent thesis without abandoning the position, given genuine tariff-driven earnings uncertainty and peak-quarter extrapolation risk on one side, balanced against real sequential fundamental improvement on the other.

**Strategic Actions**: Maintain current STLD exposure rather than adding aggressively or cutting the position outright. Specifically: (1) Do not chase the stock higher on the tariff-driven margin story alone — avoid initiating new capital at current levels given the PEG/valuation case rests on annualizing a peak quarter (Q2 2026) that the company's own history (Q4 2025's 570bps margin collapse) shows is not guaranteed to repeat. (2) If already long, hold the position through Q3 2026 earnings to see whether margin expansion persists without further tariff escalation, using that print as the decision point to add (if margins hold >14% gross despite tariff-resolution odds rising) or trim (if margins revert toward the 12% Q4-2025 level). (3) Set a mental stop/trim trigger tied to tariff-news flow: if Polymarket-type resolution odds for the Canada tariff spike materially higher (i.e., market pricing faster removal), proactively trim size ahead of the potential guidance reset, since the bear's asymmetric downside argument (20-25% de-rating risk on tariff removal) is credible. (4) Conversely, use any near-term pullback tied to tariff-headline volatility (not fundamentals) as an opportunity to average in modestly, given the underlying EAF cost-structure and demonstrated operating leverage are real positives, just not sufficient on their own to justify a full Buy/Overweight while the primary catalyst remains this binary and this uncertain. (5) Size the position at a neutral, benchmark-weight level — not underweight, not overweight — until either the tariff outcome clarifies or STLD demonstrates margin durability through a full seasonal cycle (Q3/Q4 2026 prints).
