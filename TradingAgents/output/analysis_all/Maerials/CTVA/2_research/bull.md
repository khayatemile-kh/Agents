## ADDRESSING THE BEAR'S LIKELY ARGUMENTS (PRE-EMPTIVELY)

Before the bear even opens their mouth, let me dismantle the three arguments I know are coming:

**My Response:**

**My Response:**

**My Response:**

1. **Global agricultural consolidation is a feature, not a bug** — it creates stronger players with better R&D capabilities

3. **CTVA has significant R&D advantages** in biotechnology and precision agriculture—this is *not* a commodity business

The regulatory risk is real but **priced into the valuation** already. And the long-term growth drivers (climate-adaptive seeds, precision agriculture, global population growth) are *far more powerful* than consolidation headwinds.
