## **Why Not Buy?**

1. **You are buying into resistance**, not support. The $67–$68 zone has rejected price twice in late August; proof of breakout is required first.
2. **Momentum is deteriorating** (negative MACD histogram), suggesting the rally may be consolidating rather than accelerating.
3. **Better entry opportunities exist** at the 50 SMA support (~$59.50) if you miss the breakout; waiting costs nothing.
4. **Bollinger Band congestion** limits upside reward relative to downside risk if $67.40 fails again.

---

## **Why Not Sell?**

1. **Trend is intact** — price is above all major moving averages and support levels have held.
2. **Support is strong and well-defined** — a drop to $59.50 or even $58.37 would still be within a controlled downside scenario.
3. **No technical reversal signals yet** — MACD is still positive, RSI is in a healthy zone, not extreme overbought.
4. **Exiting into support** (if you hold) sacrifices the risk-managed upside potential for no clear gain.

---

## **Conclusion**

**LYB is in a technical setup that demands patience, not action.** The chart shows a functioning uptrend with real support, but also clear resistance and early momentum degradation. The asymmetric risk at current prices ($65–$67) does not justify fresh buying, and the intact structure does not justify selling or panic trimming.

**Recommended stance:**
- **Holders:** Maintain position; reset stops at $58.37 (hard support) and trail gradually higher.
- **Prospective buyers:** Wait for either a pullback to $60–$60.50 or a confirmed breakout above $68 with volume. Current zone is a "no trade" zone.
- **Next catalyst:** Watch Q3'26 earnings and EBITDA trend (per the research plan); if reported EBITDA holds or exceeds $1.5B normalized levels, an upgrade to BUY becomes justified.

**Hold. Monitor. Execute at better odds.**
