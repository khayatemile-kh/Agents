## COMPANY PROFILE & BUSINESS OVERVIEW

CRH plc operates as a diversified building materials supplier with a global footprint. The company serves the construction, infrastructure, and manufacturing sectors through multiple business lines including:

- **Aggregates & Concrete:** Production and distribution of key construction materials
- **Asphalt & Paving Products:** Materials for road construction and maintenance
- **Readymix Concrete & Construction Products:** On-site concrete services
- **Architectural Products:** Specialty building materials
- **Distributed Products:** Wholesale distribution of building materials

The company operates manufacturing facilities and distribution networks across North America, Europe, and other markets, positioning it as a cyclical play on construction activity and infrastructure spending.
