1. **Short-covering bounces** create technical oversold signals that mechanical systems mistake for reversal

The Aggressive analyst is cherry-picking a single technical signal and extrapolating full confidence from it. This is precisely backward. A positive MACD histogram in isolation is worth monitoring, not acting on. You need *confirmation* from other metrics. Let's look at what those confirm:
