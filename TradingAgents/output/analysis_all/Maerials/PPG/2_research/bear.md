The bull makes a deceptively simple argument: *"PPG trades at 12.83x forward P/E, which is cheap relative to its 15-18x historical range and 14-16x peer average."*

- Annualized basis: **~$7.80 EPS**
- Stock price at $112: **14.4x trailing P/E** (not 12.83x)

**But wait—let's look at the actual earnings trajectory:**

| Period | Diluted EPS | YoY Change | Trend |
|--------|------------|-----------|-------|
| Q2 2025 | $1.98 | Baseline | — |
| Q2 2026 | $1.95 | **-1.5%** | ⬇️ DECLINING |
