3. **Margin Expansion:** This is *not* a zero-sum game—rising prices expand industry margins, not just shift share

PKG, as a **vertically integrated producer** with both containerboard mills and corrugated box plants, captures margin at multiple levels:
- Sells containerboard to external customers at rising prices
- Uses that same containerboard in boxes (internal transfer at cost, protecting margin spread)
- Captures the pricing benefit both ways
