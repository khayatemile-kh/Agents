Net: fundamentals (declining EBITDA quality, leverage fragility, uncertain refinancing costs) outweigh the technical setup and moat narrative, consistent with the Research Manager's Underweight call. This is not a Sell because absolute cash generation remains solid (~$2B+ TTM EBITDA) and the structural moat is real, but conviction should be reduced and exposure trimmed rather than held at full size or added to.
