Let me be specific:

**The "Structural Demand" vs. "Cyclical Pricing" Disconnect**

The bull claims copper prices are "sustainably elevated" based on structural demand. But look at what the data actually shows:

- **FCX's gross margin in Q4 2025 collapsed to 18.1%** (down from 30-34% in surrounding quarters)
- **This wasn't operational failure—it was copper prices tanking** in Q4, then recovering in Q1-Q2 2026
- **Net income swung wildly: $406M (Q4 2025) → $881M (Q1 2026) → $984M (Q2 2026)**

If we truly were in a structural copper shortage with permanently elevated prices, why would FCX's profitability be *this volatile quarter-to-quarter*? The answer is simple: **FCX's earnings are still fundamentally tied to commodity prices, not to structural demand.**

The bull's error is conflating "long-term demand growth" with "immediate price stability." Yes, copper demand will grow for 10-15 years. **But copper prices cycle every 3-5 years.** The bull is asking you to buy at the peak of a cycle because the long-term trend is up. That's not investing—that's getting caught in a momentum trap.
