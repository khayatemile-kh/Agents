**Q1 2026 operating cash flow was $186 million.** The aggressive analyst attributes this to "working capital timing," but that's precisely the problem. If the company is struggling to convert operations into cash in a supposedly strong demand environment, that's not a feature—it's a bug.

And here's the critical point the aggressive case glosses over: **the debt increased despite operating cash flow.** That means the company is either:

1. **Burning cash to maintain operations** (suggesting underlying profitability may be weaker than reported earnings indicate)
