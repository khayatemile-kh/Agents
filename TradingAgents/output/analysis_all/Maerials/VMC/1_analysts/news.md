### KEY INSIGHTS FOR TRADERS

1. **Valuation Concern Dominates:** VMC trades at a premium to intrinsic value despite strong historical returns. There is limited upside unless earnings growth accelerates materially or rates decline (low-probability scenario).

2. **Structural Headwind from Rate Environment:** The 89% conviction that the Fed will hold rates steady through 2026 implies construction financing remains expensive, potentially capping demand growth and aggregates consumption.

3. **Downside Protection Weak:** With overvaluation and structural sector headwinds, downside protection appears limited if macroeconomic growth surprises to the downside (though current recession odds are low).

4. **Earnings Momentum Fading:** Post-earnings performance of +1% over 30 days suggests market digested results without enthusiasm—a bearish signal for near-term continuation.

5. **No Clear Catalyst:** Given low recession risk, no Fed cuts expected, and stable (not accelerating) inflation, there is minimal near-term catalyst for multiple expansion or strong earnings surprises.
