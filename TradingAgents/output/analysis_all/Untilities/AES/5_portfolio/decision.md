The neutral analyst's synthesis — that technical strength (bullish MA alignment, RSI 61.66) and fundamental deterioration can coexist over different time horizons — is the most useful reconciliation: it supports trimming into current strength as tactical risk management, not distress selling.

Net: the structural leverage gap, negative FCF, and declining forward guidance collectively outweigh the optically cheap multiple and the single positive catalyst, but genuine mitigants (dividend intact, coverage ratios fine, CFIUS resolved) prevent this from being a distress/exit case. Underweight — trim into strength, keep a residual stake, set explicit re-rating triggers — is the position that best reflects the asymmetry between capped near-term upside and real downside tail risk (refinancing stress, potential dividend pressure) if deleveraging doesn't materialize.
