The valuation is not stretched (18.6x forward P/E, in-line-to-cheaper than peers), and RSI oversold plus dividend support provide some near-term cushion against further downside.

Net: this isn't a clean bull or clean bear case. The fundamental business quality is intact and the stock isn't expensive, but the pace of debt growth outstripping cash flow generation, the negative working capital, and the real risk of margin compression during rate-case lag periods are legitimate reasons for caution that outweigh a full "Buy" stance. This looks like a name to hold and monitor for confirmation that rate-case recoveries are keeping pace with financing costs, rather than to aggressively add to. I'm not raking it to Sell/Underweight because the core regulated-utility model, valuation, and technical oversold setup provide real downside protection and dividend support — but conviction isn't strong enough for Buy/Overweight given the leverage trajectory and cash flow mismatch the bear correctly flagged.
