## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

FirstEnergy (FE) finds itself at an inflection point. After a robust **45.5% three-year run**, the stock has recently pulled back 4.9% post-earnings. The company is gaining substantial tailwinds from **AI-driven data center demand** and has positioned itself favorably within the utilities sector through its ambitious **$36 billion Energize365 capital investment program** through 2030. However, recent analyst commentary suggests the valuation is approaching "perfection," warranting careful consideration of entry points and growth sustainability.

- **Growth Driver:** Rate-base expansion through infrastructure modernization

### 4. **Data Center & AI Infrastructure Demand**

- **FE Exposure:** Positioned to benefit from hyperscaler demand for reliable, rapidly deployable power
