Think about that in the context of the Energize365 program. FE is committing to $36 billion in capex through 2030 while generating *negative* free cash. How does that math work?

When existing debt matures, FE has to roll it over at higher rates. That increases interest expense, further pressuring operating margins and cash flow.

This is a doom loop: **negative FCF → higher debt → higher interest expense → lower cash available for debt service and capex → refinancing pressure → higher leverage ratios.**
