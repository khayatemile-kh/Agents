## COMPREHENSIVE NEWS RESEARCH REPORT: EDISON INTERNATIONAL (EIX)
**Analysis Date: 2026-09-01**

---

### EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Edison International (EIX) experienced a **catastrophic week**, declining approximately **27% in August**, placing it as the **worst-performing S&P 500 stock for the month**. The primary catalyst was the **failure of California Governor Gavin Newsom's wildfire liability protection bill** for utilities, which collapsed at the last minute during state legislative negotiations. This regulatory disappointment has exposed EIX to potentially massive wildfire-related liabilities and created substantial uncertainty around the company's earnings trajectory and shareholder equity.

---

### KEY FINDINGS & MARKET DYNAMICS

#### 1. **CRITICAL REGULATORY SETBACK: Wildfire Liability Legislation Failure**

**The Core Issue:**
- Governor Newsom's proposed wildfire legislation, which would have **limited utilities' liability exposure** from climate-related disasters, **fell apart at the last moment** during negotiations between the governor and state legislators.
- The failed bill would have protected both PG&E and Edison International from catastrophic legal judgments related to wildfires.
- Without liability protections, utilities remain exposed to **class-action lawsuits and damages claims** stemming from future wildfire events.

**Market Reaction:**
- EIX shares **tumbled sharply**, recovering only modestly when negotiations temporarily stalled (suggesting procedural delays, not a breakthrough).
- PG&E shares fell ~20%, marking their **biggest drop since the COVID-19 selloff of March 2020**, indicating the severity of this regulatory blow.
- Edison International followed a similar trajectory, signaling that both California utilities face identical regulatory risk.

**Why This Matters:**
California has become increasingly vulnerable to wildfires due to climate change, longer drought cycles, and aging infrastructure. Utilities are held liable for damages caused by faulty equipment or poor vegetation management. Without legislative shields, EIX could face **multibillion-dollar liability claims** that would directly reduce earnings and shareholder equity.

#### 2. **DIVIDEND IMPACT & VALUATION RESET**

- Despite the market turmoil, **Edison International's board declared a semi-annual dividend** on its 5.00% Fixed-Rate Reset Cumulative Perpetual Preferred Stock, Series B, plus preference dividends for Southern California Edison operations.
- The dividend declaration highlights a sharp **valuation reset** — the stock is down **27% over the past week and 26% over the past month**, putting dividend coverage ratios and payout sustainability under question.
- This is a critical signal: the board is signaling confidence in the company's stability, but the market is pricing in **increased downside risk** that overwhelms confidence in dividend security.

#### 3. **MACROECONOMIC HEADWINDS: INTEREST RATES & INFLATION**

**Current Macro Environment (as of early September 2026):**
- **10-Year Treasury yields remain elevated** near **4.7%**, after Federal Reserve official Kevin Warsh stated that **"inflation has not slowed meaningfully."**
- **Macro data retrieval unavailable**, but market signals indicate inflation concerns are resurging, which pressures utility valuations through higher **discount rates** (WACC).
- Higher long-term borrowing costs directly impact utilities' financing costs for capital expenditures (grid modernization, infrastructure upgrades), reducing return on invested capital.

**Implication for EIX:**
- Utilities are highly capital-intensive and rely on debt financing. Rising rates increase the cost of financing grid infrastructure, squeezing net margins.
- **Inflation + liability risk** is a toxic combination: EIX must invest in modernization while facing higher borrowing costs *and* uninsured catastrophic downside from wildfires.
