The Neutral analyst's own base case (60% probability) still allows for a modest common dividend cut and continued litigation overhang, which is inconsistent with the Aggressive thesis that the 6% yield is safely underwritten.

Net: the wide valuation discount looks like the market correctly discounting unresolved tail risk and earnings degradation rather than an obvious mispricing. This supports trimming/underweighting—capturing dividend income and bounce optionality with a reduced position while protecting capital against the more probable path of further earnings pressure, tightening credit metrics, and an equity-unfriendly liability resolution—rather than holding full size or buying the dip.
