The Neutral Analyst correctly frames this as a genuinely ambiguous multi-scenario setup rather than a binary call, and that ambiguity itself argues against adding risk into an oversold bounce — it argues for reducing size while retaining income exposure. Net: the weight of disclosed, hard evidence (leverage trend, interest coverage trend, flat EPS) exceeds the weight of the unsubstantiated margin/backlog narrative, supporting a trim/de-risk stance rather than either aggressive accumulation or full exit.
