1. **Capex is incurred** (2024-2025): NEE spends $9.27B on grid upgrades, renewable capacity, etc.

3. **Rate case review** (2026-2027): State commissions examine the investment, approve cost recovery and return
4. **Rate adjustment implementation** (2027-2028): New rates go into effect, earnings benefit materializes

So if capex was deployed in 2024-2025 and rate-case filings happened in 2025-2026, we'd expect to see EPS inflection in late 2026 through 2027, not right now in Q2 2026.

The Aggressive Analyst is betting the regulatory environment is favorable. The Conservative Analyst is betting it's hostile. **The truth is probably somewhere in the middle, and we should size positions accordingly.**
