## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

American Water Works Company, Inc. (AWK) is the largest publicly traded water and wastewater utility in the United States, serving approximately 14 million people across 24 states. As of September 1, 2026, AWK operates as a regulated utility generating predictable, inflation-protected cash flows. The company exhibits the characteristics of a defensive dividend-paying utility stock with moderate growth prospects driven by infrastructure investment requirements and regulated rate recovery mechanisms.

**Key Investment Highlights:**
- **Market Position:** Leading regulated water utility with stable, recurring revenue streams
- **Valuation Metrics:** Trading at a TTM P/E of 23.80x with forward P/E of 21.24x

## COMPANY PROFILE & BUSINESS DESCRIPTION

### Corporate Overview
American Water Works operates a diversified portfolio of water and wastewater utility systems. The company's business model is characterized by:

1. **Regulated Utility Operations:** Provides essential water services under utility commission oversight, enabling cost-plus rate recovery mechanisms
2. **Geographic Diversification:** Operates in 24 states with concentration in the Midwest and Mid-Atlantic regions
3. **Infrastructure Criticality:** Provides essential services (water, wastewater treatment) with predictable demand

### Regulatory Framework
- Rates are set by state Public Utility Commissions (PUCs) through regulatory proceedings
- Cost recovery mechanisms protect the company from commodity price volatility
- Allowed return on equity (ROE) typically ranges from 9-10% across jurisdictions
- Infrastructure investment requirements support continued capex programs
