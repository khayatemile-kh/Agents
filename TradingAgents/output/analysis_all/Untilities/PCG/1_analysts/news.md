### EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

PG&E Corporation faces a critical near-term headwind from regulatory uncertainty surrounding California wildfire liability legislation. While the utility sector broadly benefited from energy price momentum this week, PCG's stock performance is heavily constrained by legislative risk around catastrophe coverage frameworks. Bank of America's downgrade to Neutral (from Buy) reflects market concern that recent California wildfire reforms fall short of providing durable liability protection and the capital markets framework necessary to support PCG's multi-billion dollar infrastructure investment plans.

### SECTOR & REGULATORY ANALYSIS

#### Wildfire Liability Crisis as Structural Risk
- **Root Issue:** California utilities face potential catastrophic wildfire-related litigation and settlements that could exceed equity values
- **PCG's Specific Challenge:**
  - Company emerged from bankruptcy in 2020 but remains vulnerable to wildfire claims
  - Capital markets access (critical for $20B+ infrastructure modernization) depends on predictable liability framework
  - **Current Bill Risk:** Legislation without liability caps = open-ended downside

#### Capital Investment Needs vs. Capital Access
- PG&E's multibillion-dollar grid modernization (electrification, resilience, grid hardening) is essential to:
  - Meet California's climate goals
  - Reduce ignition risk / wildfire exposure
  - Support demand from residential electrification & data center buildout
- **Bottleneck:** BofA's downgrade signals market skepticism about whether PG&E can raise sufficient capital under unfavorable regulatory conditions

#### Competitive & Relative Position
- Edison International (EIX) similarly exposed to regulatory risk but potentially better positioned geographically in Southern California
- Sempra Energy (SRE) benefits from San Diego Gas & Electric diversification
- **PCG's Disadvantage:** Northern California concentration + historical wildfire legacy
