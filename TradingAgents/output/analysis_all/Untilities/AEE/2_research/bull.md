**1. Utilities operate under regulatory agreements that *guarantee* rate recovery.** AEE isn't a discretionary business—it's a public utility. **What's actually happening:** AEE is *managing working capital efficiently* by using short-term commercial paper (lower-cost) backed by committed credit facilities and OCF generation. This is *textbook utility finance*, not a sign of distress.
