# COMPREHENSIVE FUNDAMENTAL ANALYSIS REPORT: HILTON WORLDWIDE HOLDINGS INC. (HLT)

## Executive Summary

As of September 1, 2026, Hilton Worldwide Holdings Inc. (HLT) presents a **mixed fundamental picture** with strong operational performance offset by significant leverage concerns. The company demonstrates solid revenue growth and positive cash generation, but an inverted balance sheet with negative stockholders' equity and mounting share buybacks warrant careful consideration for investment decisions.

## COMPANY PROFILE

**Company Name:** Hilton Worldwide Holdings Inc.  
**Ticker:** HLT  
**Exchange:** NYQ (New York Stock Exchange)  
**Sector:** Consumer Cyclical  
**Industry:** Lodging  
**Market Capitalization:** $70.1 billion (as of Sept 1, 2026)  

Hilton operates one of the world's largest hospitality portfolios, featuring multiple brands across luxury, upscale, and mid-scale segments. As a primarily asset-light operator, the company franchises most of its properties rather than owning them directly, which explains the low PPE (Property, Plant, & Equipment) relative to total assets.
