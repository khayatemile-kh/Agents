1) Margin cyclicality is confirmed, not merely alleged. The Conservative Analyst's "stressed" 2.8-3.2x leverage scenario under an EBITDA contraction was rightly tempered by the Neutral Analyst, who noted CarMax and peers operate comfortably at 2.5-3.0x. Extrapolating this trend to 20-25% growth by 2027 flips the bull's "cheap" 0.77 PEG into an expensive ~1.95 PEG, a scenario neither analyst fully disproved. A 33-39x forward P/E priced for sustained 40%+ growth is vulnerable to simultaneous earnings and multiple compression if margins revert further.

Countervailing bull points — sequential net income improvement (Q1→Q2 +24%), $2.63B cash cushion, low near-term recession risk (8%), and technical consolidation just below the 200-SMA — are acknowledged as legitimate but insufficient to offset the cyclical margin/earnings volatility and unresolved regulatory overhang (federal probe). Net: legitimate current profitability and revenue scale argue against a full Sell, but cyclical margin normalization, leverage-driven fragility in a downturn, decelerating growth undermining the valuation case, and an unresolved regulatory tail risk argue against Hold or any bullish stance — Underweight, trim into strength, is the appropriate calibrated response.
