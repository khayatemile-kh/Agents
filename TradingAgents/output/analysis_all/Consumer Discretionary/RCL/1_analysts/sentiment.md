## Executive Summary

RCL exhibits **mixed-to-mildly-bearish** sentiment across the three-source dataset, with a notable divergence between institutional and retail perspectives. Near-term headwinds (oil price rally, post-earnings decline) dominate news framing, while retail traders show fragmented conviction—some identifying oversold technical conditions and bottoming signals, others describing the stock as a "POS" and projecting further downside. Reddit data is absent, limiting engagement-weighted insights. The dominant narrative is cyclical pressure on cruise operators amid macro oil-driven travel-cost anxiety, paired with technical capitulation that is beginning to attract contrarian/oversold buyers.
