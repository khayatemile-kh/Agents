So your MACD argument isn't wrong, but it needs a qualifier: yes, convergence could lead to explosive upside, but the *current environment* (low volume, RSI divergence, price trapped in narrow bands) suggests the explosive move is more likely downward unless we see immediate confirmatory signals.
