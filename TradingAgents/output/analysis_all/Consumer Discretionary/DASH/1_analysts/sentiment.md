   - Context: Published August 31, highlighting double-digit August gains for DASH despite broader consumer weakness and deteriorating confidence data. This is a sentiment positive.

   - **Kohl's (KSS) same-day delivery partnership** (Aug 28): DASH announced expanded partnership with Kohl's for rapid on-demand delivery, framed as strategic retail penetration in the department store vertical.
   - **Carter's (CRI) back-to-school partnership**: Carter's designated DASH as its "largest kids' apparel assortment" for rapid delivery — ecosystem expansion theme.

   - Insider Monkey article (Aug 20): Amazon expanding Prime Air drone delivery to **500 US cities by end of 2026** (6x expansion from 11 locations).
   - **Framing:** While positioned as competitive threat, it also validates on-demand/drone delivery as a strategic battleground — implicitly endorsing DASH's relevance. The fact that DASH is explicitly named as a competitor to Amazon's expansion suggests institutional recognition of DASH's position as a key rival, not a marginal player.

#### Negative/Risk Signals (News)

- **Headwind:** No direct criticism of DASH in the news corpus; however, **indirect exposure** to consumer confidence weakness (multiple references to "August gloom" in the headline corpus despite DASH bucking the trend).
- **Governance concern** (mentioned in StockTwits, not news, but worth flagging): Schedule 14C statement indicating Delaware-to-Nevada legal structure shift — one retail poster raises concerns about liability limitation. This is not mainstream news coverage but reflects emerging corporate governance scrutiny.
