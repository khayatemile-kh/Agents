The Aggressive Analyst presents this as "structural problems" when it might actually represent **temporary investment spending to fix those structural problems.** If Tesla's management is spending now to optimize Berlin/Texas operations, we should expect margin recovery in Q4 and Q1 next year—not sustained deterioration.
