But the Neutral analyst's sizing critique and the absence of confirmed structural deterioration (only one quarter of data, no management commentary yet) argue for a more moderate trim than the Trader's 20-30%, and a wider, more consistent stop-loss that doesn't contradict the technical basing pattern also cited in the bull/neutral arguments. This is not a Sell — the fortress balance sheet, positive FCF, and genuine long-duration optionality in energy/autonomy are real assets that argue against full liquidation, as all three analysts and the Research Manager acknowledge.
