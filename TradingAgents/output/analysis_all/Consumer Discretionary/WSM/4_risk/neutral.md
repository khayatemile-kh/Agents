So what the Conservative Analyst is actually saying is: "In a seasonally weaker quarter that historically runs 16-17% margins, Q2 2026 maintained 17.3% *despite* not having the seasonal revenue peak." 

That's not margin deterioration. That's margin stability through operational discipline. Yes, when tariffs eventually cycle through, there will be a headwind. **The neutral position:** Olive is a legitimate competitive advantage *if* adoption scales. The conservative approach is to treat it as optionality (it either works or it doesn't) rather than a core valuation driver. The aggressive approach of assuming it drives future earnings is premature. The right framework is: *maintain core exposure to capture upside if Olive proves durable, but trim into strength to de-risk if quarterly comps start deceleration.*

This is *exactly* what the Hold recommendation with 20-30% trimming does. You're not betting the farm on Olive; you're maintaining exposure while hedging execution risk. That's the intellectually honest position.
