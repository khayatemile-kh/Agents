**Direction:** Bullish with embedded caution  
**Dominant Themes:**
1. **Earnings Beat & Guidance Raise (PRIMARY POSITIVE)**
   - WSM reported Q2 revenue of $1.96B (+6.7% YoY), beating consensus of $1.93B.
   - Non-GAAP EPS of $2.10 (+1.2% above consensus $2.08).
   - Net income up 36.6% YoY; operating income up 36.8% YoY.
   - Full-year guidance raised to +4.7% to +7.2% revenue growth (vs. prior guidance).
   - Comparable brand revenue growth accelerated to 6.2% from 3.7% YoY.

2. **Operational Strength & Innovation (SECONDARY POSITIVE)**
   - AI-powered shopping assistant ("Olive") driving 3x higher conversion rates for users vs. non-users.
   - All-brand positive comps; record B2B demand noted.
   - Broad-based growth across Williams-Sonoma, Pottery Barn, West Elm segments.
   - Beat driven by "real comp sales and traffic gains, not tariff refunds" — differentiates from peers using one-time tailwinds.

3. **Tariff Windfall & Stakeholder Distribution (TACTICAL POSITIVE)**
   - Tariff refunds boosted Q2 profitability; company allocating $10M to employee retirement accounts and vendor reimbursement.
   - Signals financial confidence and stakeholder management acumen.

4. **Valuation & Margin Concerns (EMBEDDED RISK)**
   - Trailing P/E of 23.3x; EV/EBITDA of 17.0x; EV/Revenue of 3.5x all described as elevated for a retailer.
   - Non-GAAP operating margin fell to 17.3% (down from prior quarters), despite strong topline.
   - Margins under pressure despite tariff tailwinds — underlying cost pressures suggested.
   - StockStory headline: "Here's the Catch" — editorial framing signals diminishing margin operating leverage.

5. **Macro Headwinds (NEAR-TERM RISK)**
   - Crude oil surge to ~$90/bbl (U.S.-Iran Hormuz disruptions) stoking inflation concerns.
   - Rising oil/freight costs pressuring discretionary retailers broadly.
   - DIY demand weakness noted in Home Depot comp (consumer deferral of big projects).
   - Secular e-commerce pressure noted: retail industry flat over past 6 months vs. S&P 500 +12.1%.
   - K-shaped consumer trends acknowledged (strength in premium, weakness in middle market).

6. **Analyst Reaction**
   - **UBS:** Reiterates positive outlook; sees continued strong performance.
   - **RBC:** "Solid Q2 results" with full-year guidance raise as supporting factor.
   - **Evercore:** Upgraded price target to $245 from $240 but maintained HOLD rating (notable divergence).
   - **BofA:** Raised to $263 from $261 (more bullish).
   - **Jim Cramer (CNBC):** Criticized post-earnings 1-day selloff as "wrong" after strong beat; positioned as contrarian buy on weakness.

**News Sentiment Vector:** Bullish (60%) — Earnings fundamentals and execution merit justify price strength. Bearish (40%) — Valuation, margin pressure, macro uncertainty warrant caution.
