## **COMPANY PROFILE & BUSINESS OVERVIEW**

Airbnb, Inc. is a leading online marketplace for lodging and tourism experiences. The platform connects property owners ("Hosts") with travelers ("Guests") seeking alternative accommodations to traditional hotels. The company operates globally with a highly scalable, asset-light business model that generates revenue primarily through service fees rather than property ownership.

**Key Business Characteristics:**
- **Platform-Based Model:** Commission-based revenue structure with limited capital requirements
- **Global Reach:** Operates across multiple countries and regions, providing geographic diversification
- **Market Leadership:** Dominant player in the short-term rental market with substantial competitive moat
- **Seasonality:** Revenue exhibits significant seasonal variation (peak Q3, Q4; trough Q1, Q2)
