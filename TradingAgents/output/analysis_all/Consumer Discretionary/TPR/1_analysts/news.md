## COMPREHENSIVE MARKET & MACROECONOMIC ANALYSIS REPORT
**Analysis Date:** September 1, 2026 | **Instrument:** TPR (Tapestry, Inc.)

---

### EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Tapestry Inc. (TPR) faces a complex market environment characterized by recent stock underperformance, competitive pressures from both established and emerging luxury players, and execution risks that overshadow positive operational momentum. Over the past week, news flow has been decidedly bearish, with the stock extending a significant 30-day decline while underlying business fundamentals show mixed signals.

---

### I. COMPANY-SPECIFIC DEVELOPMENTS (Past Week)

#### **A. Competitive Threats & Market Pressure**
- **Gap Inc. Entry into Luxury Handbags:** Gap has tapped Coach architect Reed Krakoff to develop a $29.95-$498 handbag line, directly targeting the accessible-luxury segment that Coach dominates. This represents a significant competitive threat, as Coach accounts for the majority of Tapestry's revenue. Any meaningful share capture by Gap would directly erode Coach's customer base.
- **Shein IPO Valuation Milestone:** Shein's recent IPO valued the ultra-fast-fashion competitor above H&M, signaling aggressive competition at lower price points and younger demographics. This reinforces structural pressure on the mid-to-premium market positioning.

#### **B. Financial Performance & Guidance**
- **Fiscal 2027 Outlook Tensions:** Tapestry targets margin expansion and double-digit EPS growth for FY2027, but faces execution headwinds:
  - **Tariff Risk:** Incoming tariffs threaten margin guidance and present a significant execution risk
  - **Kate Spade Losses:** Persistent underperformance of the Kate Spade brand remains a drag on consolidated profitability
  
- **Coach Momentum:** Despite broader company challenges, Coach brand shows strong momentum with:
  - Targeting Gen Z and emerging markets for growth
  - Management's $10 billion brand ambition with a credible runway
  - International expansion and men's business growth initiatives

#### **C. Stock Performance**
- **Recent Selloff:** TPR down ~17.6% over the prior 30 days (as of late August), extending the 13.2% monthly decline referenced in recent analysis
- **Intraday Weakness:** Stock indicated down ~1.9% ahead of trading following the Gap/Krakoff news
- **Valuation Premium Concerns:** Multiple sources note a premium valuation coupled with elevated risks, despite Coach strength and margin improvement

### II. MACROECONOMIC ENVIRONMENT

#### **A. Fed Policy & Interest Rates**
- **No Rate Cuts Expected in 2026:** Prediction markets show 89% probability that the Fed will NOT cut rates in 2026 (highest traded volume of $7.86M), with recent 1-week movement +1.6pp toward higher probabilities. This implies:
  - Sustained higher funding costs for luxury discretionary spending
  - Consumer debt servicing pressures remain elevated
  - Reduced financial flexibility for rate-sensitive investors

#### **B. Economic Recession Risk**
- **Very Low Near-Term Recession Risk:** Prediction markets price only **8% probability** of a US recession by end of 2026 (resolves Jan 2027), down -1.0pp over the prior week. However:
  - Unemployment threshold risks remain elevated
  - 8% probability of US unemployment reaching 5.0%+ in 2026
  - These metrics suggest a stable but potentially tightening labor market

#### **C. Consumer Spending Environment**
- **Luxury Market Resilience Hypothesis:** Per analyst commentary, the luxury market is rewarding brands with:
  - Pricing power and brand loyalty
  - Recognition and perceived quality
  - International growth capability
  - NOT volume-driven growth
- **Gen Z Focus:** Coach's positioning toward younger cohorts is strategically sound given their demonstrated willingness to spend on recognizable luxury brands, but execution risk remains

#### **D. Broader Macroeconomic Context**
- **Sticky Inflation/No Fed Cuts:** The Fed's hold on rates combined with tariff pressures suggests persistent cost-push inflation, which:
  - Constrains real consumer purchasing power
  - Increases input costs for luxury goods manufacturers
  - May limit Tapestry's margin expansion despite pricing power
  
- **Global Luxury Softness:** Nike's recent decision to "ship less on purpose" coupled with margin guidance acceleration suggests inventory corrections and consumer caution across discretionary spending
