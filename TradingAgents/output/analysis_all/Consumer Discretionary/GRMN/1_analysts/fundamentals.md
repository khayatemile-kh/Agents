## COMPANY PROFILE & BUSINESS OVERVIEW

Garmin Ltd. is a leading global provider of navigation, communication, and information solutions for the automotive, aviation, marine, outdoor, and fitness segments. The company's product portfolio spans GPS-enabled devices, wearables, automotive infotainment systems, and specialized aviation and marine electronics. With headquarters operations and significant manufacturing presence, Garmin serves both commercial and consumer markets worldwide.

### Key Business Characteristics:
- **Diversified Revenue Streams:** Multiple end-market segments reduce concentration risk
- **Hardware + Software Integration:** Proprietary mapping and software ecosystems drive customer lock-in
- **Geographic Diversification:** Global operations with hedged currency exposure
- **Recurring Revenue:** Subscription services and software licensing provide revenue stability
