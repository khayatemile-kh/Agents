## Detailed Narrative Analysis

### 1. News Sentiment (Institutional Framing)
**Direction: Bullish**

The dominant institutional narrative centers on **portfolio optimization and capital unlock** via the completed Pizza Hut sale:

- **Primary Event:** Yum Brands completed the sale of Pizza Hut (ex-China) to LongRange Capital for **$1.5 billion**, plus up to $75M in earn-outs by 2030 (QSR, Nation's Restaurant News, Restaurant Dive, GuruFocus).
- **Framing:** Described as a strategic pivot away from legacy, slower-growth assets toward a "leaner, faster-growing restaurant portfolio" (GuruFocus). Pizza Hut is now "sheltered from immediate pressures of public markets" under private equity, implying improved autonomy.
- **Secondary Catalyst:** Taco Bell's return to UAE via Americana Restaurants deal signals brand-expansion momentum, framed as a 14-year re-entry and new geographic territory (TheStreet, Nation's Restaurant News, Verdict Food Service).
- **Macro Tailwind:** Trump's 90-day beef tariff waiver mentioned as cost-benefit for fast-food chains (Zacks), though not YUM-specific.

**Valuation Caution:** Simply Wall St. article flags YUM stock as potentially **9% overvalued** on a DCF basis, citing fair-to-expensive positioning despite 28.4% 5-year returns. This injects a modestly cautious note into otherwise bullish news flow.

**Peer Context:** News includes comparative framing (Wendy's turnaround prospects, McDonald's cash returns, Buffalo Wild Wings COO hiring) but no direct YUM criticism — positioning Yum as the active portfolio manager among peers.
