- **A pure-play on high-growth brands:** Taco Bell (fastest-growing QSR concept in North America) and KFC (global powerhouse with emerging market upside)

**The Math:**
If Pizza Hut represented ~$1.8B in annual revenues (2025 levels) with lower margins than the system average, removing it actually *improves* YUM's blended growth rate and margin profile going forward. The remaining portfolio grows faster, commands higher multiples, and generates better ROIC.
