## Dominant Narrative Themes

1. **Asset-Light Resilience**: Marriott's franchise model continues to be a key institutional selling point; franchisee portfolio expansion (Hoi Hup, Highline) validates the model.
   
2. **Experiential Luxury Expansion**: Luxury Collection Expeditions represent a strategic pivot beyond room inventory toward high-margin, culturally immersive travel experiences—a differentiation play in luxury travel.

3. **Seasonal Cyclicality**: Summer winding down is reducing guest check-ins; Q3 seasonality is a known headwind. Institutions acknowledge this explicitly.

4. **Macro Headwind in Consumer Discretionary**: Consumer discretionary sector has underperformed S&P 500 over 6 months (5.5% vs. 10.5% YTD); MAR is grouped in this cyclical cohort. Uncertainty about macro spending appetite is a risk.

5. **Valuation Caution**: One headline flags MAR among "3 Inflated Stocks We Approach with Caution"—suggests price may have run ahead of near-term earnings visibility.

6. **Retail Indifference / Low Conviction**: Minimal StockTwits engagement and zero Reddit posts suggest retail has not built significant conviction (bullish or bearish) on MAR in the past week.
