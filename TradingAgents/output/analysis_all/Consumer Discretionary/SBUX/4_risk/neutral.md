It's not pretty, but it's a feature of the restaurant franchise business model, not a bug specific to Starbucks. You see the same negative equity among mature QSR operators like McDonald's.

The real issue you're identifying is that Starbucks has limited margin for error. If the core business deteriorates, there's less cushion to absorb shocks. That's a legitimate risk.
