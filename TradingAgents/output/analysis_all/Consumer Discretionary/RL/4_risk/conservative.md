**Current State (TTM):**

- Net Income: $983M

- Diluted EPS: $15.88

**Forward State (Implied by $20.91 EPS Guidance):**

That's **16% revenue growth**, not 200%. And here's the thing: **RL has already demonstrated it can achieve this.** Look at the quarterly trajectory:

- Q2 2025: $1.719B revenue

- Year-over-year growth: **13.9%**

So the forward guidance isn't a fantasy. It's the company extrapolating from *actual current performance*.
