Net synthesis: the weight of company-guided forward evidence (yield decline outpacing cost cuts) and the seasonality-adjusted cash flow deterioration justify reducing exposure now, but valuation support, deposit-funded working-capital normalcy, real (if insufficient) cost-cutting execution, and notable value-investor positioning (Klarman/Singer) argue against zeroing out the position.
