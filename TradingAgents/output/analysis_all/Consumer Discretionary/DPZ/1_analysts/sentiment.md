#### Overall News Tone
- **Bullish signals:** China growth, marketing initiative, analyst caution optimism, turnaround precedent.

- **Net assessment:** **Mildly bearish-to-neutral**. Institutions are hedging; bright spots (China, marketing) are being noted but not driving conviction. The ownership news is neutral but underscores consolidation risk.
