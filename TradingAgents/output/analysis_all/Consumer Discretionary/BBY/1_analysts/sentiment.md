- Tariff uncertainty, rising costs mentioned as headwinds

**Headline Tone:** "Turnaround gains steam," "hot summer quarter," "raises outlook" — **predominantly positive framing**, but with caveats about margin composition and macro headwinds.

**News-Layer Conclusion:** **Bullish on fundamentals**, cautiously optimistic on valuation (several articles flag that BBY no longer trades at a discount after 26.8% 3-year return).
