## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

D.R. Horton (DHI), the nation's largest homebuilder by volume, is navigating a period of conflicting signals. While macro conditions suggest headwinds (elevated mortgage rates, Fed holding rates steady, industry-wide soft guidance), the company benefits from strong contrarian institutional support (Berkshire Hathaway's recent accumulation) and maintains a valuation discount to intrinsic value. However, mounting litigation risks, softer guidance, and deteriorating housing market sentiment present material downside risks.
