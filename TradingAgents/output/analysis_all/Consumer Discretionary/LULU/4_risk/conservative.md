## On The "Airtight" Technical Case: You're Reading The Tea Leaves, Not The Price Action

Let me start with the most dangerous claim: that the MACD death cross and RSI rollover constitute a "leading indicator" that price will cascade lower. This is technically false, and it reflects the cardinal sin of technical analysis—retrofitting narrative to recent price action.

The MACD deteriorated from +2.35 on August 11 to -0.33 on September 1. That's accurate. But what does that *mean*? The Aggressive Analyst interprets this as "institutional capitulation" and "buyers throwing in the towel." 

That's speculation. What the MACD actually tells us is that the rate of upward momentum has slowed relative to the long-term trend. MACD measures the relationship between two moving averages. A negative MACD after a positive spike simply means the short-term trend has weakened relative to the medium-term trend. It doesn't predict future price direction—it describes what *already happened*.

More critically: LULU bounced from $115 on August 27 to $120.26 on August 31, despite the negative MACD. Price rallied *while* momentum indicators were deteriorating. That's not the behavior of a stock about to "cascade lower." That's the behavior of a stock finding support and recovering.
