### C) Gross Margins at 90%+ = Moat Strength

EXPE's **90.3% gross profit margin (TTM)** is exceptional for a platform business. Why?

- **Minimal COGS:** Expedia doesn't own hotels or flights—it's a pure technology/marketplace intermediary
- **Scalability:** Once the platform is built, incremental bookings flow through at near-100% margin contribution
- **Pricing Power:** These margins suggest Expedia has leverage with both suppliers (hotels) and customers, and can grow without margin compression

**Compare this to:**
- Traditional travel agencies (15-25% margins, capital-intensive)
- Full-service hospitality operators (20-35% margins, labor-intensive)
- Software SaaS companies (typically 60-75% margins)

Expedia sits at the **intersection of platform efficiency and travel's sticky demand**, making it a rare asset.

**Prediction markets assign only 8% probability to a US recession by end of 2026.** This is extraordinarily low. For context:
- In 2022, when rate hikes began, recession probability peaked at 30-40%

This asymmetry favors bulls: **Minimal downside if recession comes; significant upside if not.**
