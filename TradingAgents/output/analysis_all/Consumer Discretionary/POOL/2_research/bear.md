## **1. REFUTING THE "CYCLICAL NORMALIZATION" NARRATIVE – THIS ISN'T JUST NORMALIZATION, IT'S DETERIORATION**

| Metric | 2022 | 2023 | 2024 | 2025 | **2025 vs 2022** |

**Here's what the margins hide:**

Yes, gross margins have held at ~29-30%. **But that's because POOL has likely been forced to cut costs and reduce discretionary spending to protect margins as revenue declines.** This is a **defensive margin strategy**, not evidence of pricing power or operational resilience.

**In a true cyclical recovery, revenue grows first, then margins expand.
