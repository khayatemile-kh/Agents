2. **Consumer Spending Resilience Uncertain**: While recession risk is low, the prediction markets do not contain specific data on consumer discretionary spending trajectories. Consumer confidence, credit growth, and wage growth will be critical tracking points.

3. **International Exposure**: LVS has significant exposure to Macau and Singapore gaming markets. Any geopolitical or regional economic weakness (e.g., China slowdown) would be a downside risk not reflected in US macro data alone.

4. **Seasonal Dynamics**: September marks the start of autumn travel season; Labor Day weekends (early September in the US) are historically strong for Las Vegas tourism. Watch for early September occupancy data.
