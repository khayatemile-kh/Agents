### The Pro Channel: The Real Revenue Driver

Here's the critical insight bears overlook: **HD isn't relying on DIY to drive growth. It's pivoting to professional contractors and builders—a segment that is far less rate-sensitive and carries significantly higher margins.**

The data tells the story:

- **Pro sales are strong and growing**, creating revenue diversification away from cyclical DIY spending.
- **Pro customers spend 5-10x more per transaction** than DIY consumers, driving higher-margin revenue.

- Unlike DIY, the Pro channel creates **recurring, high-touch relationships** with sticky customer acquisition costs that drive lifetime value.

The market is treating HD like a DIY-dependent retailer. It isn't anymore. **This is a secular strategic shift that will drive superior profitability for years.**
