## **TREND ANALYSIS: MACRO PERSPECTIVE**

### **1. The July-August Boom & Bust**

The period from **July 29 to August 3 marked an extraordinary rally**:
- **Low:** $226.65 (July 29)
- **High:** $284.02 (August 3)
- **Rally:** +25.3% in just 4 trading days
- **Volume spike:** Massive intraday volumes (77M+ on June 26, 129M+ on July 31) confirmed panic buying

This explosive move suggests either a capitulation bottom followed by short-covering, or a major positive catalyst (earnings, corporate action, sector rotation). However, **the inability to hold above $280 is a red flag**. The stock failed to sustain the peak and has been in a grinding downtrend since August 3.

### **2. Post-Peak Deterioration (August 4 – September 1)**

Since the August 3 high:
- **Decline:** $284.02 → $254.92 = **-10.26%** in 20 trading days
- **Character:** Lower highs (Aug 10: $280.14 → Aug 14: $265.81), lower lows (Aug 18: $257.73 → Aug 27: $255.02)
- **Chop pattern:** Multiple false rallies (Aug 19: $265.84, Aug 28: $266.43) followed by fresh lows
- **Volume:** Declining into recent weakness (31.6M on Sept 1), suggesting retail participation fading

**Implication:** The August spike appears to be a **bull trap**. Momentum has exhausted, and the market is now repricing expectations lower. This is a distribution phase, not accumulation.
