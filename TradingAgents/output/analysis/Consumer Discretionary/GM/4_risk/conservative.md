Here's what the aggressive analyst conveniently ignores: Q2 is not always a stronger seasonal quarter for auto manufacturers. That's a broad generalization that doesn't hold consistently. Looking at the five-quarter history available—and this is critical—Q2 2025 posted 9.07% gross margin, and Q3 2025 posted 6.41%. So Q2 2026 at 7.6% is *between* those two historical points.
