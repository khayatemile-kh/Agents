That's the single most concrete, hard-to-refute data point in the whole exchange, and the bull's rebuttal to it hasn't fully addressed it (the debate cuts off mid-bear rebuttal, but the bull's Part 5 preemptive defense doesn't get to address this specific point in the transcript).
