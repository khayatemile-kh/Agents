## COMPREHENSIVE TRADING ANALYSIS: EXPEDIA GROUP (EXPE)
**Analysis Date: September 1, 2026**

---

### EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Expedia Group (EXPE) is currently benefiting from strong operational momentum and positive macroeconomic tailwinds in the travel sector. The company raised its full-year 2026 guidance (revenue: $16.05B–$16.22B, +9–10% YoY; gross bookings: $129.5B–$130.8B), reflecting accelerated B2B expansion and cost discipline. However, market-implied probabilities suggest a low-recession environment with near-zero probability of Fed rate cuts in 2026, creating a stable but constrained growth backdrop.

### KEY FINDINGS

**Positive Catalysts:**
- **Raised Full-Year Guidance**: EXPE increased 2026 revenue guidance to $16.05B–$16.22B (9–10% growth), exceeding Wall Street consensus of $16.01B. Gross bookings now forecast at $129.5B–$130.8B.
- **Strong B2B Growth**: Accelerated expansion in B2B channels is a key driver of the guidance increase, signaling diversification beyond consumer bookings.
- **Operational Efficiency**: Demonstrated cost discipline alongside revenue growth, improving operating leverage.
- **Stock Performance**: EXPE stock rallied ~54–59% over the past 12 months, recently hitting new highs alongside competitor Airbnb (which surged 21% in a month).

**Risk Signals:**

  - Executive disposed of 3,133 shares for ~$1M (August 26)

- **Valuation Concerns**: StockStory flagged "3 Large-Cap Stocks with Questionable Fundamentals," suggesting size and growth trajectory may raise valuation questions post-rally.

### ACTIONABLE INSIGHTS FOR TRADERS

1. **Near-Term Bullish Bias (Next 4–8 weeks):**
   - Strong operational momentum, raised guidance, and low recession risk support continued strength
   - PMI data and sector tailwinds favor travel demand through Q4 2026
   - Consumer confidence likely remains elevated into year-end travel seasons (Q4 bookings)

2. **Valuation Caution (Medium-term Risk):**
   - 54–59% 12-month rally has pushed EXPE to new highs; insider selling suggests smart money is trimming exposure
   - StockStory's mention of "questionable fundamentals" among large-caps warrants scrutiny of forward P/E ratios
   - Consider taking partial profits or reducing position size above recent highs

3. **Monitor Fed Communications:**
   - 89% probability of no rate cuts in 2026 is a ceiling for rate stability
   - Any shift in Fed messaging (e.g., rate-cut expectations for 2027) could reignite upside
   - Conversely, hawkish guidance would dampen discretionary spending

4. **Watch Geopolitical Risks:**
   - Iran tensions mentioned but not yet material; monitor for escalation that could disrupt Middle Eastern and European travel flows

---

### CONCLUSION

**Current State**: EXPE is well-positioned with strong guidance, accelerating B2B growth, and a benign macroeconomic backdrop (low recession risk, stable rates). However, the stock's substantial 54–59% rally and insider profit-taking suggest caution at current valuation levels. The market is pricing in sustained travel demand and operational excellence, which appears well-founded given PMI strength and booking trends.

**Trading Stance**: **Hold for long-term growth investors; consider trimming on further strength** for momentum traders. The fundamentals remain solid, but valuation risk and insider selling warrant defensive positioning above current levels.
