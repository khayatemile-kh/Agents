- **Lower customer acquisition costs** (enterprise relationships)
- **Pricing power** in corporate segments

The forward P/E of **12.70x** and PEG ratio of **0.8** tell us the market is *still pricing in modest growth expectations* despite these accelerating B2B tailwinds. This is a **classic valuation disconnection**—the stock has rallied on momentum, but the *earnings growth is only now catching up*.

### **PILLAR 2: FORTRESS CASH FLOW + STRATEGIC DELEVERAGING = FINANCIAL FLEXIBILITY**

Now let's talk financial strength—because this is where bears typically miss the story.

**TTM Free Cash Flow: $3.43 billion.** On $15.7B of revenue, that's a **21.8% FCF conversion rate**. For a platform business at scale, this is exceptional.

But look at the *trajectory*:
- **Q2 2026 FCF:** $1.279B
- **Q1 2026 FCF:** $3.747B (exceptional)
- **TTM FCF:** $3.43B

The reason Q1 was so strong? **Deferred revenue jumped to $15.60B in Q2** (up from $10.59B in Q4 2025). This is advance customer payments sitting on the balance sheet—essentially an interest-free loan from customers funding operations. As these advance bookings convert to revenue in Q3-Q4, we get another wave of cash flow.

**The capital allocation story is smart, not reckless:**
- $2.6B in share repurchases over the past 4 quarters
- Strategic debt issuance ($986M in Q2) at today's reasonable 4.71% 10-year yield
- Cash position grew from $5.41B (Q4 2025) to $6.68B (Q2 2026) *despite* buybacks

This is a management team executing a **disciplined deleveraging strategy while returning capital**. The Debt-to-Equity ratio of 230x sounds alarming in isolation, but it's a function of the asset-light model—what matters is debt *relative to cash flows*, and at **1.66x Net Debt-to-TTM-EBITDA**, EXPE is in healthy territory for a cyclical business.

**Bears will say:** "They're running up debt!" 

**Reality:** They're managing a **highly predictable, high-FCF business** and smartly utilizing leverage when 10-year rates are reasonable. This is textbook capital allocation.
