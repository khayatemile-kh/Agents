APTV doesn't bounce back to $5.1B quarterly revenues just because the market "accepts higher rates are here to stay." OEMs adjust their production capacity downward permanently when demand falls. They don't rebuild capacity waiting for a recovery that might not come on the timeline investors hope for. **But here's the structural component the aggressive analyst completely ignores:** From the company fundamentals report, APTV's gross margin in Q2 was 23.7%, but in Q1 it was 18%, and in Q4 2025 it was 18.7%. That's not noise—that's the signature of OEM pricing pressure. When OEMs themselves face margin compression from EV transition costs and rate-induced demand destruction, they cut prices for components. APTV has no pricing power; it's a Tier 1 supplier competing against other suppliers globally.

**Translation:** Even if revenue partially recovers, margins won't. The company could see $4.5B quarterly revenues with 15% gross margin instead of $5.1B with 20% gross margin. That's a permanent profitability hit, not a cyclical one.
