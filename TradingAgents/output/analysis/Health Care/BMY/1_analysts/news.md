### **KEY DEVELOPMENTS - PAST WEEK (August 25 - September 1, 2026)**

#### **1. CAR-T Safety Concerns & Trial Pause**
**Critical Issue:** Bristol-Myers Squibb voluntarily paused its **zola-cel CAR-T program** due to transient side effects reported in study participants. This follows Novartis halting rap-cel development after three patient deaths in autoimmune indications.

- **Impact:** CAR-T is a high-growth segment; safety pauses undermine competitive positioning against J&J (who recently partnered with Sail Biomedicines to strengthen CAR-T capabilities)
- **Timeline Risk:** Clinical delays typically add 12-24 months to development timelines
- **Competitive Implication:** J&J's partnership move suggests accelerating competitive consolidation in the immunotherapy space

#### **2. Positive Atrium Therapeutics Milestone ($15M)**
**Bright Spot:** BMY paid $15 million milestone to Atrium Therapeutics (RNA) for a lead RNA compound targeting a cardiology indication, demonstrating:
- Active pipeline investment beyond patent-cliff vulnerabilities
- Focus on adjacent therapeutic areas (cardiovascular RNA)
- Global collaboration framework generating value

**Mixed Signals:**
- **YTD Performance:** 47.9% one-year return (strong recovery)
