**Recommendation**: Hold

**Rationale**: Both sides make legitimate points that roughly offset each other, so I can't back a high-conviction Buy or Sell here. The bull is right that Q2 was genuinely strong: 12.1% adjusted EPS growth, 8.7% revenue growth, a guidance raise (management doesn't do that lightly), $360M quarterly FCF, and near-universal enterprise penetration (19 of 20 top pharma) that does create real switching costs. Macro backdrop (low recession odds, stable rate expectations) is also a plausible tailwind for pharma R&D spend, which flows through to IQV's bookings.

But the bear's rebuttal lands hard on two fronts that the bull never adequately closes out. First, leverage: 4.4x net debt/EBITDA is the highest among stated peers (Charles River ~2.5x, Syneos ~3.2x), interest expense of $788M annually is eating roughly a quarter of EBITDA, and $2.3B of debt rolls over in the next 12 months into a 'no cuts' rate environment—so the bull's claim that stable rates reduce refinancing risk is backwards; stable-but-elevated rates just lock in a higher cost of capital on that refinancing. This leaves very little covenant cushion if EBITDA softens even modestly. Second, the AI/data moat claim is asserted but not proven — 'using IQVIA's AI' is not shown to be exclusive, contract durations aren't specified, and the bear's point that pharma companies own their own trial data (IQVIA is the executor, not the owner) is a real structural weakness in the 'network effect' story that the bull doesn't rebut with hard evidence. The bear's observation that the stock is already up 40.6% since June and 21.7% in the past month means a lot of good news (including the AI narrative) is already priced in — that raises the bar for the next earnings print and skews risk/reward less favorably at current levels even if the underlying business is fine.

Net: strong operating momentum and management credibility argue against a Sell; unresolved leverage risk (highest in peer group, near-term maturities, thin covenant cushion) and an unproven, over-extrapolated AI moat combined with a large recent run-up argue against adding aggressively here. This is a case where the debate is genuinely balanced between 'good business, priced for perfection, financed with more risk than peers' and 'strong momentum with real competitive entrenchment' — so Hold is the right call rather than forcing a directional bet.

**Strategic Actions**: 1. Maintain current position size; do not add or trim materially until either (a) Q3 2026 earnings clarify whether AI-driven revenue/pricing is actually accelerating (not just being mentioned), or (b) there's clarity on refinancing terms for the $2.3B of debt maturing within 12 months.

2. Set a watch-list trigger to move to Underweight/Sell if: net debt/EBITDA moves toward or above 4.5-5x, interest expense as a % of EBITDA rises further, or if EBITDA growth decelerates below high-single digits (any of these would validate the bear's leverage thesis).

3. Set a watch-list trigger to move to Overweight/Buy if: management provides concrete evidence of AI-driven pricing power (e.g., quantified AI-attached revenue, expanding win rates against Charles River/Syneos, longer contract durations/renewals), and if leverage is de-risked via debt paydown or EBITDA growth outpacing debt growth.

4. Hedge/protect gains from the recent 21.7% one-month rally — consider trimming into strength opportunistically (e.g., scaling out a modest portion, 5-10% of position) to lock in some of the run-up without abandoning the core thesis, given valuation is now pricing in a lot of good news.

5. Avoid using margin or adding leverage to increase IQV exposure given the company's own balance sheet leverage is already the highest among stated peers — don't compound balance-sheet risk with position-level risk.

6. Reassess at next earnings print (Q3 2026) specifically for: (a) any covenant language changes, (b) updated debt maturity schedule/refinancing rates achieved, and (c) any disclosed metrics on AI-attributable revenue growth or customer contract duration — these are the key facts missing from this debate that would resolve the standoff between bull and bear.
