## COMPANY PROFILE & BUSINESS OVERVIEW

IQVIA is a clinical research organization (CRO) and healthcare intelligence company that provides contract research, development, and commercialization services to pharmaceutical, biotech, and medical device companies. The company operates across:

- **Drug Development & Trials:** Clinical trial services, patient recruitment, data management
- **Sales & Marketing Solutions:** Real-world evidence, physician engagement
- **Technology & Analytics:** Healthcare data analytics and business intelligence

The company has grown substantially through strategic acquisitions, which explains the significant goodwill and intangible assets on its balance sheet ($21.35B as of Q2 2026, representing ~71% of total non-current assets).
