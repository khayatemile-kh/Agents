## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Moderna, Inc. is navigating a critical inflection point in its business trajectory. As of Q2 2026, the company is experiencing severe profitability headwinds following the post-pandemic revenue normalization that began in 2024-2025. Despite maintaining a substantial cash position of $5.1 billion and a healthy balance sheet structure, MRNA faces significant operational challenges with persistent net losses, negative EBITDA, and deteriorating margins. The company's TTM revenue of $2.23 billion represents a substantial decline from peak pandemic-driven revenues, while the TTM net loss of $3.15 billion reflects the company's inability to achieve profitability despite maintaining operational scale.

## COMPANY PROFILE & BUSINESS CLASSIFICATION

**Company Name:** Moderna, Inc.  
**Ticker:** MRNA  
**Sector:** Healthcare  
**Industry:** Biotechnology  
**Exchange:** NMS (Nasdaq)  
**Market Capitalization:** $59.91 billion (as of Sept 1, 2026)

Moderna is a clinical-stage biotechnology company based on messenger RNA (mRNA) technology. The company pioneered mRNA vaccine development and achieved massive commercial success with its COVID-19 vaccine (Comirnaty/Spikevax). However, the company is now transitioning through a challenging period as COVID-19 vaccine demand normalizes and the company attempts to bring new mRNA-based therapies to market for other indications including influenza, respiratory syncytial virus (RSV), cancer vaccines, and other diseases.
