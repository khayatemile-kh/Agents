- August 20 (continued breakdown): 3.88M shares

Now, the Aggressive Analyst argues this moderate volume means "institutional profit-taking meeting retail forced selling"—implying that the forced sellers are exhausting and a bounce is imminent.

But I read it differently: moderate volume on declining price suggests *lack of conviction on either side*. When a stock truly bounces from oversold extremes, you typically see an accumulation day with volume spike on the upside. We haven't seen that yet. What we see is a stock drifting lower on declining interest, which is often *more dangerous* than panic selling because it suggests a slow, grinding repricing lower rather than a sharp V-bottom.

The MACD histogram at -1.34 and *widening* (not stabilizing) confirms this. If the selling were exhausting, we'd expect to see the histogram flatten or start narrowing. Instead, it's accelerating downward. That's not the technical signature of a near-term bottom—that's the signature of a *trend extension*.

This isn't risk management—it's *capitulation through tranches*. You're increasing your exposure as conditions worsen, which is the opposite of prudent risk discipline.
