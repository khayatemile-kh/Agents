## Catalysts and Risks

### **Positive Catalysts (Forward-Looking)**
- **Ericsson wallet partnership:** Accelerated digital wallet launches, lower integration costs, recurring revenue streams.
- **Fintech infrastructure demand:** Ongoing shift to digital payments and issuing services post-COVID normalization.
- **Valuation reset:** Post-downgrade price may attract value-oriented institutional capital if fundamentals stabilize.

### **Risks and Headwinds (Forward-Looking)**
- **Wells Fargo downgrade rationale unknown:** Lack of clarity on why Wells Fargo downgraded (valuation, growth guidance, margin pressure, competitive threat, macro) creates uncertainty. This is a near-term overhang until the thesis is clarified.
- **Execution risk on Ericsson deal:** Partnership announcements are common; revenue realization and margin accretion timelines are key.
- **Fintech sector competition:** Visa, Mastercard, Shift4, and other payment processors are also pursuing digital wallet and issuing services; no evidence of FIS competitive moat in the data.
- **Macro headwinds:** If macroeconomic slowdown pressures transaction volumes, FIS (fee-based on transaction/user activity) could face revenue headwinds.
