1. **Debt refinancing** (which depends on capital markets remaining open and rates not spiking further)

If capital markets tighten even slightly—which is entirely possible if we see economic weakness, credit spreads widen, or sentiment deteriorates further—this CP rolls off and suddenly FIS faces a $4.2 billion payment due in 30 days.

In a stressed scenario, that becomes forced asset sales or desperate debt restructuring. We've seen this movie before (see: 2008 financial crisis and the commercial paper market freezing).
