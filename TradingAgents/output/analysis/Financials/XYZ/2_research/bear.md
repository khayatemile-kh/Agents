**Total Debt: $7.17 billion**

- Long-term debt: $6.31 billion

**Debt-to-Equity Ratio: 32.58x**

For a software infrastructure company, this is alarming. Here's why:

1. **Leverage vs. Asset Quality:**

   - This means $7.17B in debt is servicing largely intangible assets
   - If the business deteriorates or M&A synergies don't materialize, there's impairment risk

2. **Refinancing Risk:**

3. **Earnings-to-Debt Coverage:**

**For context:** Even with "net cash" on the balance sheet, the gross debt load relative to profitability is concerning. The cash is partially restricted ($821M) and needed for operations, so the real liquidity cushion is smaller than it appears.
