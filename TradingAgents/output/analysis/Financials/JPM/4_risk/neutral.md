It's not expensive either, but let's be precise: the market has already incorporated strong earnings expectations. **My challenge to both of you:** You're treating 2027 as binary—either the Fed doesn't cut and NII stays elevated, or the Fed cuts and earnings collapse. Reality is more nuanced. The outcome depends on magnitude, timing, and what replaces NII compression in the earnings mix (e.g., capital markets activity, wealth management, trading revenue). JPM's earnings aren't a single-product story.
