The aggressive analyst's claim that this is a 'permanent structural improvement' is unsupported speculation — the conservative analyst correctly notes tax rates are contingent on legislative and geographic-mix factors outside the company's control, and even the neutral analyst, while pushing back on the conservative analyst's more extreme tail-risk framing, still concedes a normalization to 19-20% is a plausible baseline that removes the 'windfall growth' narrative. There is no evidence of business breakdown, only a mismatch between a rich valuation (27.81x trailing P/E) priced for margin expansion and a trend that is currently moving the opposite direction. The trader's Hold stance undersells this mismatch by treating the fundamental deterioration as merely a monitoring item rather than an active reason to reduce risk now, and the research team's Underweight call is the best-supported synthesis: reduce exposure to reflect deteriorating fundamentals and earnings-quality concerns while preserving optionality on the strategic pivot if margin data validates the bull thesis in coming quarters.
