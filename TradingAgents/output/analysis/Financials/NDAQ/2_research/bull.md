### **PILLAR 2: STRATEGIC PIVOT TO RECURRING, HIGH-MARGIN DATA & ANALYTICS REVENUE**

This is the bull case the market hasn't fully priced in yet, and frankly, it's the most important story.

Nasdaq is **deliberately moving away** from pure transaction-based revenue (which is cyclical and dependent on market volatility) toward **recurring software and data subscriptions**. Here's why this matters:

**The Business Transformation:**
- Market data and analytics = recurring annual subscriptions
- Technology platforms for capital markets = sticky, multi-year contracts
- Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) margins = 60-80% gross margins with high retention rates

The company has explicitly positioned itself as a "**global technology, data, and analytics provider**"—not just an exchange operator. The Helsinki event announcement in August 2026 signals continued international product innovation. The "around-the-clock US stock trading" initiative demonstrates platform expansion capacity.

**Why This Matters for Valuation:**
- Recurring revenue is valued at 8-15x revenue multiples in SaaS (compare to transaction-based revenue at 2-4x)
- If NDAQ can grow its data/analytics revenue to 40-50% of total revenue (from current ~35%), the blended multiple should expand significantly
- This is a *structural* re-rating opportunity, not dependent on market cycles

**Evidence from Recent Quarters:**
- Gross margin compression in Q2 2026 (40.3% from 44.4%) is *temporary* and driven by integration costs and platform expansion investments
- Once data/analytics reach scale (which management clearly targets), margins will re-expand and *exceed* historical levels
- EBITDA growth (+17% YoY) already outpacing gross profit growth (+17.2%) signals operational efficiency gains

**Fed Rate Hold Through 2026: Actually *Bullish* for NDAQ**

The bears will say "rates stay high, that's bad for financial stocks." But NDAQ isn't a traditional financial stock—it's a *technology platform* that benefits from *stable* market conditions where institutions feel confident trading and publishing data.

**Broader Market Tailwinds:**

- **ESG and compliance tech:** NDAQ's regulatory and compliance solutions are in higher demand as regulations tighten worldwide
- **Data monetization:** Financial firms are increasingly willing to pay for decision-making analytics and market intelligence
- **Technological advancement:** AI and machine learning applications in finance *require* high-quality market data and infrastructure—Nasdaq's core offering

Now, let me proactively tackle the three main bearish arguments before they're leveled:

   - A PEG of 1.0x suggests fair value relative to growth

C) **The earnings growth trajectory justifies the premium:**
   - 60% net income growth in 2025
   - 21.9% EPS growth in Q2 2026 YoY

**The Real Story:** NDAQ isn't a boring exchange operator anymore; it's a **high-growth software-data hybrid** that Wall Street hasn't fully re-rated. When analysts update their models to reflect the recurring revenue mix (which is accelerating), multiples will likely expand, not contract.
