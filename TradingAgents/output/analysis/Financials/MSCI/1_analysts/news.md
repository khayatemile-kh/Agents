### **COMPANY-SPECIFIC DEVELOPMENTS: MSCI (MSCI)**

#### **1. Index Methodology Controversy – Michael Saylor / Strategy Inc. Delisting Dispute**

**The Issue:**
MSCI proposed a new delisting criterion that would remove three companies from its global indexes in November 2026, with **Strategy Inc.** (Michael Saylor's company) being the largest target. This controversy has escalated significantly in the past week:

- **Michael Saylor's Strong Opposition:** Saylor has publicly urged MSCI to withdraw the proposal, calling it "discriminatory" and "fundamentally flawed." Strategy Inc. has requested MSCI either withdraw the rule entirely or apply it only prospectively (to future listings, not retroactively).
- **Reputational Risk:** The controversy highlights concerns about MSCI's index methodology credibility and independence. Critics argue the proposed rule threatens MSCI's reputation as an objective, neutral index provider.
- **Stakeholder Pressure:** The high-profile nature of this dispute (involving a crypto-adjacent publicly traded company) increases visibility and potential regulatory scrutiny of MSCI's governance processes.

**Implications for MSCI:**
- **Short-term:** Risk of public backlash and potential pressure from index constituents to reconsider methodology changes.
- **Medium-term:** Potential client dissatisfaction and reputational damage affecting index license fees and subscriptions.
- **Regulatory exposure:** Possible scrutiny from securities regulators regarding index governance standards.

#### **2. Elevated 2026 Operating Expense Outlook**

**The Challenge:**
MSCI raised its 2026 operating expense forecast earlier in the year, citing:
- Acquisition-related costs
- Higher employee incentives
- Increased investment spending

**Market Reaction:**
- Analysts have raised concerns about near-term profitability pressures despite acknowledging long-term positive prospects for the company.
- This expense guidance suggests the company is in investment/acquisition mode, which may pressure near-term earnings.

**Implications for MSCI:**
- **Earnings Headwind:** 2026 earnings could face compression relative to consensus expectations.
- **Strategic Investment:** The spending reflects MSCI's commitment to product development and market expansion, which could benefit long-term growth.
- **Valuation Pressure:** Higher near-term expenses may pressure P/E multiples or require market to extend valuation multiples based on long-term growth prospects.
