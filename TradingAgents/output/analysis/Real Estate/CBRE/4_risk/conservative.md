Do you see what's happening here? Even as revenue has grown 27% from FY 2023 to FY 2025, net income has grown only 17%. The margin compression story is not about "pricing power" or "mix shift." The data center narrative is being positioned as the savior, but even if it accelerates, the consolidated impact is limited given its likely revenue mix.
