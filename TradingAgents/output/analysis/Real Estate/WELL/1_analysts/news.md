- **October 2026 Meeting:** 4% probability of rate cut (extremely low)

  - Increased cost of capital for debt refinancing

### SECTOR DYNAMICS: HEALTHCARE REAL ESTATE

#### **Why Senior Housing is Positioned for Growth**

1. **Demographic Tailwinds**
   - Baby boomer aging continues to drive structural demand for senior living facilities
   - This fundamental demand driver is largely independent of macro cycles
   
2. **Occupancy Recovery Narrative**
   - Post-pandemic occupancy stabilization and growth is now materializing
   - Operators have adjusted pricing and operations, improving margins
   - New supply is entering the market but at measured pace

3. **REIT Capital Access**
   - While interest rates remain elevated, the breadth of healthcare REIT coverage suggests capital markets remain open for refinancing
   - Quality operators like Welltower have strong balance sheets and refinancing capacity

#### **Headwinds to Monitor**

1. **Interest Rate Sensitivity**
   - REITs are highly sensitive to Treasury yields and discount rates
   - Elevated 10Y rates compress valuations even with improving operations
   - Any surprise rise in long-end yields would pressure WELL stock

2. **Medicare/Medicaid Reimbursement Risk**
   - Senior housing revenue is partially dependent on government reimbursement rates
   - Policy changes to Medicare/Medicaid could compress operator margins
   - Currently no market-priced events for healthcare policy changes, suggesting low near-term concern

3. **Economic Slowdown Risk**
   - While recession probability is low, economic slowdown would affect middle/upper-middle income residents' ability to afford premium senior living
   - Job market deterioration could pressure occupancy growth
