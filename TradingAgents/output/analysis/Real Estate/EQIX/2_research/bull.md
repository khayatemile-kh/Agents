### Why Leverage Analysis Matters in REIT Context

**1. The D/E Ratio is a Red Herring for REITs**
- REITs are structurally leveraged businesses—that's the model. The stated purpose of REIT legislation is to allow real estate companies to lever up against tangible assets without double taxation.
- Comparing EQIX's 162x D/E to industrial companies (4-8x D/E) is **apples to oranges**. You wouldn't compare a bank's leverage to a software company's for the same reason.
- The relevant metric for REITs is **Net Debt-to-EBITDA**, not D/E.

**2. The Real Leverage Story: Net Debt-to-EBITDA**

Here's the key metric:
- **TTM EBITDA: $4.54 billion**
- **Net Debt: $18.73 billion**
- **Net Debt-to-EBITDA: 4.12x**

This is **healthy and improving**. Here's why:

| Leverage Metric | EQIX (Current) | Industry Benchmark | Assessment |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Net Debt-to-EBITDA** | 4.12x | 3.5–5.0x (REITs) | **Within healthy range** |
| **Interest Coverage (EBITDA/Interest)** | ~2.8–3.0x* | 2.5–3.5x | **Comfortable** |
| **Debt Maturity Profile** | Well-laddered, avg. 5–7yr | Typical | **No refinance cliff** |

*Estimated from Q2 2026 data; EQIX paid ~$1.6B annual interest on $23.36B debt at blended rate ~6.8%

**Why This Matters:**
- At 4.12x Net Debt/EBITDA, EQIX has meaningful but sustainable leverage
- With EBITDA growing 20%+ YoY (as shown in quarterly trend), this ratio **will compress**
- If Q3 and Q4 2026 continue momentum, full-year EBITDA could reach $4.7–4.9B, driving the ratio toward 3.8–4.0x
