- Q2 2026 Operating Cash Flow: $1.067B
- Q2 2026 Capex: $1.578B
- Q2 2026 Free Cash Flow: **-$511M**
- Q2 2026 Dividend Paid: ~$510M (estimated quarterly dividend based on TTM)
