- **Q3 2025:** Operating loss of -$51.1M, net loss of -$30.9M

## SECTION 3: STRATEGIC REPOSITIONING — ADDRESSING THE CORE RISK

### The Zonda Acquisition: Hedging Commercial RE Cyclicality

CoStar's historical dependence on commercial real estate transaction volumes is a legitimate cyclical risk. The company took action: **$800M Zonda acquisition to enter new-home data and analytics.**

**Why this matters:**
1. **Diversification away from pure CRE:** Residential has different drivers than commercial. When CRE weakens, residential can stabilize.
2. **Market size expansion:** Adding $800M+ in annual revenue from new-home data significantly reduces cyclical volatility.
3. **Attractive valuation at acquisition:** Zonda was acquired at a reasonable multiple, suggesting management got a good entry point.
4. **Synergy potential:** CoStar's technology platform + Zonda's residential data = cross-selling opportunities and operating leverage.

**The bear will say:** "Acquisitions are risky; integration takes time." 
**My response:** Yes, integration risk is real. But doing nothing in a cyclical downturn *also* has risk—it's the risk of irrelevance. CoStar's management is being proactive, not defensive.
