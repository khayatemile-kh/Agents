**Now let's address the dangerous conflation of "support levels" with "downside protection."**

The Aggressive Analyst cites the golden cross alignment and argues there's "13.5% cushion to the 200 SMA" as if this provides downside protection. This is a fundamental misunderstanding of how support works in volatile markets. Support levels are descriptive, not prescriptive. They describe where *past* demand emerged, not where *future* demand is guaranteed to emerge.

When the market turns, support levels collapse. Fast. The 2008 financial crisis, the March 2020 COVID crash, and most recently the 2022 energy sector volatility all demonstrated this: support levels that looked "robust" beforehand evaporated in days. If crude oil experiences a geopolitical de-escalation shock (Iran tensions ease, for instance), WTI could fall $10-15 in a single session. At that point, the "robust" $150.42 support level becomes a speed bump, not a guardrail.

The Aggressive Analyst is essentially arguing that past uptrend structure guarantees future downside protection. That's not risk management; that's narrative construction.
