**Let me break down what actually happened in Q2 2026:**

**This is textbook commodity cycle behavior:** When oil prices spike, integrated producers show massive earnings spikes because:
1. Refining margins widen
2. Exploration costs are fixed, so higher commodity revenues flow straight to the bottom line
3. Accounting treatment of hedges can create lumpy earnings

**The critical question the bull ignores:** Is Q2 2026 net income of $12.1B *repeatable*?

Historical context from the data provided:

4. **Fed keeping rates higher for longer = slower growth:** The bull correctly notes zero rate cuts through 2026 (1-10% probabilities). But higher rates *reduce* long-term GDP growth. The economy isn't "resilient"—it's being squeezed by restrictive monetary policy. That pressure builds.
