### **B. The Synergy Math Doesn't Work**

The bull confidently asserts: *"Cost synergies worth $200-300M annually (5-7% of combined EBITDA)"*

Let me challenge this directly:

**Where exactly are these synergies coming from?**

1. **Procurement Savings:** SLB already has global scale and supply chain leverage. Kelvion is a *thermal engineering company*—its supply chain (aluminum, copper, advanced materials) is *completely different* from SLB's (oilfield services, chemicals, wireline equipment). **Overlapping suppliers: minimal. Realistic savings: $20-30M max.**

2. **Manufacturing Footprint Consolidation:** SLB primarily manufactures oilfield equipment (wireline tools, downhole sensors). Kelvion manufactures thermal exchangers and cooling systems. These require *different facilities, different expertise, different quality standards*. **The bear trap: Rushing to consolidate manufacturing could disrupt Kelvion's customer relationships with hyperscalers (who demand dedicated supply chains). Realistic savings: $30-50M, offset by disruption costs.**

3. **R&D Consolidation:** What R&D overlap exists between oilfield wireline technology and data center thermal management? **Virtually none.** Combining these teams would dilute focus, not enhance it. **Realistic savings: $0.**

4. **G&A Reduction:** Yes, back-office consolidation could save $50-100M. But this assumes SLB can execute organizational change while integrating a mission-critical acquisition. **Historical reality: M&A-related org restructuring is messy and often results in talent loss, customer disruption. Typical realization rate: 60-70%.**

**Bottom line:** Realistic synergies = **$100-150M, not $200-300M.** That's a 50% miss from the bull's base case.

**Impact on valuation:**
- Bull's thesis: $4.1B / 11x EBITDA = ~$380M EBITDA target
- Post-synergy reality: $380M (standalone) + $125M (realistic synergies) = $505M EBITDA
- **Effective entry price: $4.1B / $505M = 8.1x EBITDA**

Still high, but my real concern isn't the multiple—**it's what happens when AI capex slows.**
