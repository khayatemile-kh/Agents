- Suppliers *must* advertise on Walmart's platform because Walmart owns the shelf and the customer relationship

- **Walmart+ membership** is growing with double-digit YoY penetration
- Unlike transactional retail, membership revenue is **recurring, predictable, and high-margin**
- Bundled benefits (free shipping, fuel discounts, pharmacy benefits) create **moat-like customer stickiness** similar to Amazon Prime
- **Conservative estimate:** $2-3B in incremental membership revenue by 2028, with 60%+ gross margins

#### **E-Commerce & Fulfillment Services** (Diversified Revenue)
- Marketplace model allows third-party sellers to reach Walmart's customer base, generating **commission-based revenue** with minimal incremental cost
- Fulfillment-as-a-service opportunities for smaller retailers needing distribution
