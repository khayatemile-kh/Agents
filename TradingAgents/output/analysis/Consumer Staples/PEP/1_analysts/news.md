## COMPREHENSIVE MARKET & MACROECONOMIC ANALYSIS
### PepsiCo (PEP) - Analysis Date: September 1, 2026

---

### EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

PepsiCo (PEP) enters early September amid a mixed narrative: the company is facing near-term operational headwinds in North America while benefiting from defensive characteristics and strong dividend positioning. Sector-wide, PepsiCo is underperforming the broader Consumer Defensive sector and significantly lagging competitor Coca-Cola (KO), which has gained 26% YTD 2026. However, current market dynamics—stable inflation expectations, no Fed rate cuts anticipated, and low recession probability—create a backdrop favoring dividend-yielding consumer staples. The key tension centers on PEP's structural North American challenges versus its long-term growth positioning in protein snacks and international markets.

### COMPANY-SPECIFIC DEVELOPMENTS (Past Week)

#### **Positive Catalysts:**

1. **Dividend Aristocrat Recognition** (Multiple sources)
   - PEP maintains a 54-year consecutive dividend growth streak
   - Current 4% dividend yield highlighted as attractive relative to peers (Coca-Cola ~2.5%)
   - Recently raised dividend in 2026; positioning as inflation hedge
   - Multiple financial media outlets (Motley Fool, 24/7 Wall St.) feature PEP as core holding for income-focused investors

2. **Protein Snacks Growth Positioning**
   - PepsiCo identified in new global protein snacks market report as major beneficiary of 2030 expansion
   - Key tailwinds: metabolic health trends, preventive nutrition focus, convenient snacking formats
   - Areas of opportunity: plant-based proteins, biotech proteins, AI-supported personalized nutrition

3. **Analyst Commentary & Valuation**
   - Jim Cramer (Mad Money, Aug 26) highlighted PEP as defensive opportunity in consumer headwinds
   - Falling oil prices cited as tailwind (relief in transportation/distribution costs)
   - Value-oriented analysts noting reasonable valuation vs. Coca-Cola

#### **Negative Catalysts:**

1. **North America Weakness** (Zacks Report)
   - PEP's North America segment reflects softer consumer spending and category pressure
   - Convenience store headwinds tied to elevated gasoline prices (conflicting with oil price narrative)
   - Described as "temporary or structural" challenge requiring monitoring

2. **Geopolitical Risk: Ukraine Facility Damage**
   - PepsiCo confirmed "damage" to Ukraine plant; no injuries reported
   - Assessment of full damage extent ongoing
   - Supply chain and operational implications unclear at this stage

3. **Competitive Pressure**
   - Coca-Cola's 26% YTD 2026 gain + record highs overshadowing PEP performance
   - Relative weakness in consumer defensive sector vs. S&P 500 overall
   - Market sentiment clearly favors KO over PEP despite PEP's higher dividend yield

4. **Product Safety Concerns (Non-Accusatory)**
   - Pepsi and Coca-Cola products found in alleged India relabeling scheme
   - **Key note:** PepsiCo and Coca-Cola explicitly stated "not accused of wrongdoing"
   - Reputational risk minimal but worth monitoring for regulatory follow-up
