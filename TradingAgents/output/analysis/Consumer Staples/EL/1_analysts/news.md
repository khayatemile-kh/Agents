### CONSUMER & LUXURY MARKET DYNAMICS

#### **Positive Drivers**

1. **Luxury Fragrance & Skincare Strength**: The "megawatt launches" across prestige brands indicate robust seasonal demand entering autumn
2. **China Turnaround**: EL's China exposure—a major headwind in prior years—now contributing materially to growth
3. **Premium Positioning**: Luxury goods have historically been more resilient than mass market during moderate economic slowdowns
4. **Omnichannel Innovation**: R&D collaboration (University of Leeds on color science) and digital reach investments positioning EL for sustained competitive advantages

#### **Headwinds & Risks**

1. **Consumer Confidence**: Reported as "weaker" in August, despite consumer stocks' relative outperformance—suggestive of bifurcated spending by income cohort
2. **Makeup Category Lag**: Despite fragrance/skincare strength, EL's makeup business "still hasn't caught up" (Insider Monkey), indicating portfolio imbalance
3. **Competitor Pressure**: Ulta Beauty's strong earnings (+9% revenue) and Bath & Body Works' guidance raise suggest vibrant competition; Coty's continued weakness signals industry consolidation dynamics
4. **Valuation Reset Dependency**: Much of EL's recent gains hinge on margin recovery and China stabilization—reversal of either could trigger sharp pullback
