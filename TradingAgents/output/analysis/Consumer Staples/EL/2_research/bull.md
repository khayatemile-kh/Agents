## ADDRESSING THE ELEPHANT IN THE ROOM: LEVERAGE & BALANCE SHEET

The bear will absolutely hammer me on debt. Total debt is $9.25B against equity of $3.81B. Debt-to-equity of 243%—that's objectively high.

**Here's my counter:**

1. **EL is generating $1.3B in free cash flow per year.** At that rate, **net debt is ~2.9x FCF.** For a mature company in the beauty sector with 75%+ gross margins, that's serviceable. It's not pristine, but it's not distressed either.

2. **The company is paying down debt:** Net debt declined from $4.40B (FY2025) to $3.81B (FY2026)—a $590M reduction in one year. That's meaningful progress.

3. **Most importantly: EL's business model doesn't require heavy capex.** Capital intensity is only 3% of revenue. That means FCF conversion is high. Compare that to capital-intensive businesses that need heavy reinvestment—EL can actually *reduce* debt while investing in innovation.

4. **The leverage is being serviceable because of margin expansion.** As operating margin grows from 5.2% toward normalized levels (8-9%+), FCF growth will accelerate. The trajectory matters more than the snapshot.

**The debt isn't ideal, but it's not a bankruptcy risk either.** And with FCF growth kicking in, leverage *improves* from here.
