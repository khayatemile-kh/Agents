Some might point to the **35.72x TTM P/E** and cry "overvalued!" But here's the critical context:

2. **Forward P/E tells the real story:** At **22.09x forward P/E**, CL is trading at a **reasonable multiple for a defensive compounder** with proven volume growth, pricing power, and 61.8% expected forward earnings growth.

3. **Historical context:** Consumer staples leaders like CL trade at 20-25x P/E in normal markets. At 22.09x forward, this is **fair value for the quality and resilience on offer.**

4. **Dividend yield cushion:** The 2.37% dividend yield provides downside protection. Even if equity valuations compress, the dividend floor supports the stock.
