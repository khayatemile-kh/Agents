Let me be precise about what the data actually shows. We're now at the point where MACD has been negative for roughly 10-12 trading days (since the Aug 18-20 crossover). In that period, the price has fallen from $93.49 to $89.63—that's a $3.86 drop captured. The question the Aggressive Analyst refuses to answer is this: **if MACD is so predictive of future moves, where's the next leg down?** We've already had the capitulation. The widening histogram they're citing as evidence of "acceleration" is not new information—it's *lagging evidence* of the move that's already occurred.

Moreover, and this is critical, **MACD operates on exponential moving averages that respond to price history.** When price stops falling as sharply, MACD begins to flatten and eventually reverses. We're now seeing price consolidation near the lower Bollinger Band at $89.27—that's not weakness continuing, that's support forming. The Aggressive position assumes the histogram will continue widening indefinitely, but that's not how momentum works. Momentum that widens eventually exhausts.

The Aggressive Analyst also cherry-picked a historical comparison: "August 10th saw a similar MACD setup before a 4.1% collapse." But they're ignoring the context. August 10th was a peak with MACD at +0.525—massively overbought momentum. We're not in that situation now. We're in a consolidation zone with MACD modestly negative. These are categorically different setups. Comparing them suggests either analytical sloppiness or intentional misrepresentation.
