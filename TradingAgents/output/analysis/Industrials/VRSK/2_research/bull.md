**Evidence from the numbers:**

**Why this matters:** Subscription revenue is *invisible* in headline revenue growth because it's recorded ratably over time, but it *explodes* in margin expansion and cash flow. **The AI Kicker:** VRSK has invested heavily in AI-driven analytics capabilities. These are exactly the tools enterprise clients will pay *premium* subscription fees for. We're in the early innings of seeing this monetize.
