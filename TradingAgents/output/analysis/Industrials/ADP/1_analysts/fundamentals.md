## **Executive Summary**

Automatic Data Processing, Inc. (ADP) is a major player in the enterprise software and business process outsourcing sector. As of September 1, 2026, ADP demonstrates solid profitability and operational stability with a market capitalization of approximately **$111.9 billion**. The company maintains strong cash generation capabilities with trailing-twelve-month (TTM) free cash flow of **$5.24 billion**, supporting consistent dividend payments and share repurchases. However, the company faces elevated leverage and a valuation that reflects growth expectations into the mid-20s forward PE range, suggesting traders should be cognizant of valuation risk in any market downturn.

---

## **Company Profile and Business Overview**

ADP is a global leader in cloud-based human capital management (HCM), payroll processing, and talent management solutions. The company serves millions of clients worldwide across various industry segments, from small businesses to large enterprises. ADP's business model is characterized by:

- **Recurring Revenue Model:** Strong subscription and service-based revenue provides predictable cash flows
- **Market Position:** Market leader in payroll and HCM services with high switching costs
- **Diversified Services:** Payroll processing, benefits administration, talent management, time tracking, and recruitment solutions
- **Global Reach:** Multi-jurisdictional operations with exposure to various geographies and industries
