These aren't one-time margin spikes—they've been consistent across all five quarters shown in the data. ADP's business generates predictable, durable profits, not volatile feature cycles.

The payroll and HCM market is consolidating. ADP's $21.95B in revenue dwarfs competitors, giving it:

## **IV. THE TECHNICAL SETUP VALIDATES THE FUNDAMENTAL CASE**

Now, let me address the bears' technical concerns head-on, because they're legitimate short-term warnings, but they don't change the long-term thesis.

### **Yes, RSI is at 64—Here's Why That's Not Bearish**

The bear will say: *"RSI at 64 is overbought; divergence with MACD; pullback coming."*

**My counter:** 

1. **RSI of 64 is *approaching* overbought, not *in* overbought.** Overbought is >70. RSI of 64 means there's still room to run before a true reversal. Moreover, RSI can remain elevated in a strong uptrend without signaling exhaustion.

2. **The MACD "divergence" is a 30-day lookback artifact.** MACD peaked at 9.33 on Aug 11, but price hit new highs on Aug 31. This is a *normal retracement* in momentum, not a reversal signal. **MACD divergences are most predictive when they occur at *falling* prices (bearish divergence)—not rising prices.** Here, we have a classic "momentum cooling" while price strength persists, which often precedes a consolidation, *not* a collapse.

3. **The real signal in the MACD?** The histogram turned negative (MACD below signal). That's worth watching, but it's a *warning to watch for weakness*, not a confirmed reversal. The 50-day SMA at $256.83 is $26.66 below—plenty of cushion.

### **Bollinger Bands: A Sign of Strength, Not Weakness**

The bear will say: *"Price is at the upper Bollinger Band; it will snap back down."*

**My counter:**

Bollinger Bands are *descriptive*, not predictive. Trading at the upper band in an uptrend is **normal and expected**. In fact, strong uptrends *should* hug the upper band. The real tell is the *width* of the bands:

- **Band width has narrowed from ~$40 (early Aug) to ~$24.5 (now)**, signaling *volatility compression*
- **Volatility compression before a move often precedes a *bigger* move**, not a reversal

If ADP were topping, we'd see *expanding* volatility and price *reversal* from the band
