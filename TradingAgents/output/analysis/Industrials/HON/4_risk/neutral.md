Now, here's the critical part: the aggressive analyst is betting that upside scenarios are significantly more likely than they actually are. They're treating Morgan Stanley's upgrade as evidence that the market will eventually agree. But Morgan Stanley's upgrade came *before* the 43 percent selloff accelerated, which suggests the market disagreed with the thesis. The conservative analyst, conversely, is treating the downside scenarios as almost certain, which discounts the realistic probability that a company with HON's asset base can stabilize operations.
