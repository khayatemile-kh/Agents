**The bull says "10.6x EV/EBITDA is cheap." I say it reflects the market's correct assessment that HON has operational challenges peer companies don't have.**

When EMR trades at 18x EV/EBITDA and HON at 10.6x—a **41% discount—that gap exists because:**

1. **EMR's EBITDA is more durable** (higher margins, software exposure, data center tailwinds)

If HON "re-rates" to peer multiples, it would first need to demonstrate earnings power and growth comparable to peers. **That hasn't happened, and the data suggests it won't.**

**This is where the bull's narrative breaks down.** Let me show you the actual trajectory:

**HON's EBITDA Trend (Normalized, ex-one-time gains):**

At 4.0x Debt/EBITDA with deteriorating cash conversion, **any recession or market stress could force covenant violations or credit rating downgrades.**
