**Possibility 2: One-time items or accounting adjustments** — Without the detailed income statement, a 6.3 percentage point quarterly swing suggests either:

**Possibility 3: Unsustainable cost-cutting** — If BR cut costs in Q2 to hit targets, that's not sustainable and will reverse.

**The risk:** The bull is assuming this 38.4% margin is the new baseline. If it reverts to 32-34% (a more typical range for the industry), the forward EPS estimate of $11.58 **evaporates.** That's a $1-2 per share earnings miss waiting to happen.
