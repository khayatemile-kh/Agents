## 1. THE VALUATION DEFENSE IS CIRCULAR LOGIC DRESSED IN FORWARD EARNINGS

### Let's Dissect the Bull's Multiple Argument

My colleague just claimed the 28.1x forward P/E is "justified" because of 40%+ EPS growth and superior margins. This is textbook **earnings optimism bias**. Let me show you why:

#### **The Problem: Forward EPS of $14.85 Is Not Consensus; It's Management's Best Case**

Here's what the bull didn't emphasize:

| Metric | Current | Bull's Assumption | Reality Check |
|--------|---------|-------------------|---------------|
| **TTM EPS** | $10.52 | Baseline | Actual delivered |
| **Forward EPS (Bull's 41% growth)** | $14.85 | Assumed | Requires flawless execution |
| **Historical 3-Year EPS CAGR** | ~12% | Normalized | Not 40%+ |
| **Q2 EPS Growth** | +40.4% | YoY comp | Built on depressed Q2 2025 base |

**Critical Point:** That 40.4% EPS growth in Q2 2026 is a *year-over-year comparison*. Look at the comparison year:

- **Q2 2025 EPS: $2.60**—this was *during* the demand normalization trough
- **Q2 2026 EPS: $3.65**—this is recovery from a depressed base

This isn't acceleration of a healthy trend; **this is a recovery bounce off a low base.** It's like showing someone a 90% stock price recovery from a crash and claiming that proves the company is soaring. It doesn't.

#### **The Real Question: Can ROK Actually Sustain 40%+ EPS Growth?**

Let's look at the math required:

**To justify 28.1x forward P/E on $14.85 forward EPS, ROK needs:**

1. **Revenue growth of 12-15% CAGR** for next 3 years (from $8.97B base)
   - Current run rate: 7.9% YoY
   - Requires acceleration despite industrial sector down 2.5% YTD
   - **How?** The sector is headwind, not tailwind

2. **Operating margin expansion of 300+ basis points** to 24-26%
   - Current: 21%
   - Bull claims this comes from AI/software mix shift
   - **But:** Legacy business still 80%+ of revenue; hardware margins aren't dramatically expanding

3. **No multiple compression even if growth slows**
   - 28x P/E is already stretched
   - If growth disappoints to 15-18%, multiple should compress to 20-22x
   - That's **25-30% downside** in a normal scenario

**The Circular Logic:** The bull is saying the valuation is justified *because* growth will be 40%, and growth will be 40% *because* the valuation is priced for it. This is backward-looking optimization, not forward-looking discipline.

- **+380 basis points net margin expansion**

This is *technically* true but **profoundly misleading**. Here's why:

#### **Q2 2025 Was an Anomaly—Not a Normal Baseline**

Look at the full history:

| Period | Operating Margin | Net Margin | Context |
|--------|-----------------|-----------|---------|
| **Q2 2024** | 19.3% | 15.2% | Pre-demand normalization |
| **Q2 2025** | 17.6% | 13.8% | Demand trough / capacity digestion |

**What's Actually Happening:**
- Q2 2025 represented a **temporary margin compression** (not a baseline)

- Q1 2026 already showed 20.8% operating margin, so Q2's performance is *consistent*, not accelerating

The bull is presenting a **recovery from depressed margins as evidence of structural improvement**. This is a classic trap: When a company bounces off a trough, one quarter of recovery looks dramatic in year-over-year comparisons, but it doesn't prove the improvement is sustainable.
