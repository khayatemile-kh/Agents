### The Problem: Baird's Thesis Rests on Unproven Commodity Assumptions

1. **Commodity Price Forecasting Has a Terrible Track Record**

2. **Farmer Cash Flow Recovery Is Structural, Not Cyclical**

   You claim "farmer cash flows have normalized" and "debt levels have stabilized." But let me offer a different interpretation of the data:
   
   - **Farmers aren't recovering—they're consolidating**. Smaller farms are being absorbed by larger operations
   - Farm debt *as a percentage of farm equity* has deteriorated, even if nominal debt levels stabilized
   - The farmer who can't afford to replace equipment isn't "waiting for 2027"—they're going out of business or merging
   - **DE's addressable market is *shrinking*, not waiting**

4. **Equipment Age Isn't a Forcing Function—It's a Cost Cutting Measure**

   Your claim that "farmers can't defer maintenance forever" misses the economic reality:
