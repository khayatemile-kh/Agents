### TRADING IMPLICATIONS & RISK FACTORS

**Bullish Drivers for EXC:**
1. **Defensive Shelter Trade**: Weak consumer confidence and macro uncertainty drive capital toward utilities.
2. **Dividend Income**: Stable, predictable cash flows appeal to income investors in a higher-rate environment.
3. **Regulated Model**: Insulated from commodity price volatility; revenue tied to consumption and regulated tariffs.
4. **Grid Reliability Support**: Easing emergency risk in EXC's core regions (Midwest, Mid-Atlantic) may reduce regulatory pressure and support investment thesis.

**Bearish Risks:**
1. **Rate Sensitivity**: No rate cuts expected; if long-term rates rise further, dividend yields become less competitive vs. bonds.
2. **Valuation Compression**: Utilities typically trade at lower multiples in high-rate environments.
3. **California Regulatory Spillover**: Elevated grid emergency risk in California could trigger sector-wide regulatory scrutiny (e.g., wildfire liability reforms) affecting utilities broadly.
4. **Economic Recession**: While defensive, utilities are not immune to severe recessions; weak consumer confidence bears watching.
