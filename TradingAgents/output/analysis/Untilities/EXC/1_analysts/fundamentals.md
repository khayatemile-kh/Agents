## Executive Summary

Exelon Corporation (EXC), a major regulated electric utility operating on the NASDAQ, presents a mixed financial profile as of September 1, 2026. The company operates in a capital-intensive, regulated utility sector that generates relatively stable but moderating returns. ### Business Profile
Exelon is one of the largest electric utilities in the United States, delivering regulated electricity and gas services to millions of customers across multiple states. The company operates a diversified portfolio of generation assets and manages extensive distribution networks. As a regulated utility, Exelon's operations are subject to regulatory oversight that typically ensures stable, though modest, returns on invested capital.
