These are **multi-year, CPUC-approved capex programs** that will:

- Command higher allowed ROE (8-9.5% typical for California utilities)
- Generate long-term earnings accretion

**2. Operating Cash Flow Is Strong and Growing**

- TTM OCF: **$7.45 billion** (excellent)
- This covers the dividend ($0.43B annually on 3B shares) **17x over**
- Capex is *deliberate and funded*, not a sign of distress

**3. The "Negative FCF" Narrative Confuses Debt with Distress**

Here's the reality: PCG is **funding capex with a combination of:**
- Operating cash flow ($7.45B)
- Debt markets ($6-7B annually)

This is how *every major utility* funds modernization. It's not a sign of financial stress—it's a sign of a company investing in long-term value creation.

**The debt-to-equity ratio of 189.45x looks insane until you understand:** That's a utility accounting convention where regulators cap leverage ratios based on capitalization (debt + equity), not total assets.
