### EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Duke Energy (DUK) faces a **mixed near-term outlook** driven by regulatory headwinds, shifting utility sector dynamics, and an accommodative rate environment. While the broader utilities sector benefits from AI-driven data center demand and stable dividend yields, DUK is navigating specific regulatory challenges in Florida and Washington that create uncertainty around cost allocation and future cash flows. The macro environment signals continued rate stability, with markets pricing in **89% probability of NO Fed rate cuts in 2026**, supporting stable valuations for dividend-paying utilities. However, DUK's valuation discount relative to peers may reflect sector-specific risks requiring careful monitoring.

### KEY FINDINGS

#### **1. DUKE ENERGY COMPANY-SPECIFIC DEVELOPMENTS**

**Regulatory Challenges - Critical Concern:**
- **Florida Large Load Customer Rate Dispute**: Duke Energy Florida's proposal to punt decisions on rate treatment for large load customers faces scrutiny from Florida's Public Counsel. The proposal was criticized for not complying with Florida's new data center law (SB 484), specifically regarding how tariffs treat large energy-intensive customers. This is significant as data centers represent growing, high-margin revenue opportunities.
- **FERC Intervention**: DUK has moved to intervene in a Federal Energy Regulatory Commission proceeding involving PJM Interconnection, suggesting potential cost allocation disputes at the transmission level.
- **Valuation Implication**: One analyst noted DUK could be **13% undervalued** as regulatory questions build, suggesting the market is pricing in uncertainty. This represents both downside risk (if regulatory decisions favor customer interests over shareholder returns) and upside potential (if favorable cost-recovery decisions emerge).

**Dividend & Income Appeal:**
- Analysis highlighted DUK as part of a cohort of utility dividend stocks offering structured income strategies (e.g., collecting $7,100/month on $850k portfolios without selling shares), suggesting strong yield support and institutional interest.

**Competitive Positioning vs. Sector Peers:**
- **PPL Corporation (PPL)**: Rising operating income and $23B investment outlook support 6-8% annual EPS growth through 2029—outpacing DUK's historical performance.
- **Xcel Energy (XEL)**: $70B+ investment plan targeting 11% rate-base CAGR and 6-8% earnings growth through 2030—more aggressive capital deployment than DUK.
- **NextEra Energy (NEE)**: Outperforming over 12 months due to strong operations and clean-energy growth; premium valuation reflects market preference.
- **Constellation Energy (CEG)**: Benefiting from clean-energy demand and nuclear strength.
- **Vistra (VST)**: Power producer chasing data center boom despite recent stock pullback; represents indirect competition for high-value load customers.

**AI/Data Center Exposure - Moderate Risk:**
- Unlike peers such as Constellation and NextEra capturing more significant data center demand, DUK's exposure appears more constrained, reflected in selective analyst articles dismissing XLU holdings in favor of companies with "more exposure to AI."
