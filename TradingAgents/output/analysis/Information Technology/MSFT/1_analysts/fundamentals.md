## COMPANY PROFILE & BUSINESS OVERVIEW

**Company:** Microsoft Corporation  
**Sector:** Technology  
**Industry:** Software - Infrastructure  
**Exchange:** NMS (NASDAQ)  
**Market Cap:** $3.68 trillion  

Microsoft is the world's leading provider of cloud infrastructure, enterprise software, gaming, and productivity solutions. The company operates across three primary segments:

1. **Productivity & Business Processes** (Office 365, Dynamics, LinkedIn)
2. **Intelligent Cloud** (Azure, server products, enterprise services)
3. **More Personal Computing** (Windows, gaming, devices)

The company's competitive moat stems from deep enterprise customer relationships, strong switching costs, recurring software licensing models, and dominant positions in cloud infrastructure (Azure) and productivity software (Microsoft 365).
