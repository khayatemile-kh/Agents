## WHERE THE AGGRESSIVE ANALYST WINS—BUT OVERSELLS IT

A company can have excellent fundamentals and still be poorly timed as an entry point. The Aggressive Analyst keeps saying "the market is mispricing Microsoft," but the data doesn't support that claim with conviction.

Let me challenge their specific argument: they say a 21x forward P/E is a "15-20% discount relative to peers with inferior growth profiles." Okay, let's grant that. But here's the critical question they dodge: **at what point in the cycle are we, and what happens to those multiples if growth disappoints?** The Aggressive Analyst assumes the 17.7% revenue growth persists or accelerates. The macro data and the technical deterioration suggest this is not a safe assumption. This actually validates a moderate entry strategy: not aggressive, not defensive, but structured for earnings-driven returns rather than speculative multiple expansion.
