Given the bull's specific growth catalysts (sovereign AI, enterprise refresh) are either immaterial in size or already priced in, and the Fire Ant disclosure introduces real near-term uncertainty around customer trust and potential vendor share loss to Arista, the stronger arguments tilt bearish/cautious rather than genuinely balanced. This isn't a full sell case, though, because the cash-flow generation, capital returns, and market leadership in enterprise networking are still intact and provide a floor. Net conclusion: this is not a name to add to aggressively right now; it's a name to trim into strength while the security and competitive risks work themselves out, revisiting once there's clarity on Fire Ant remediation costs/customer retention and evidence the enterprise refresh cycle is translating to accelerating (not merely steady) revenue growth.
