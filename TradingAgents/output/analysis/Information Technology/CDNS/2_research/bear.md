## **PART 1: THE VALUATION IS INDEFENSIBLE — EVEN WITH GUIDANCE**

### **1A: 60x P/E in a Rising-Rate Regime is Suicidal**

Let me start with the core problem that undermines the entire bull thesis:

**The market is in a monetary tightening cycle, not an easing cycle.**

From the macro research:
- **Fed rate cuts through end of 2026: 89% probability of ZERO rate cuts**
- **Global bond yields are rising**, not falling
- **10Y yields are sticky above 4.0-4.5%**, creating persistent headwinds for high-multiple growth stocks

Now, here's the mathematical reality the bull glossed over:

**Valuation = (Earnings × P/E Multiple) / Discount Rate**

When discount rates rise (which they have, and will continue to), P/E multiples *must* compress to maintain equilibrium. CDNS's 60x trailing P/E was justified in a 2.0% 10Y yield environment. At 4.5% yields, it's not.

**Let me do the math:**

If CDNS's trailing earnings are $5.04 per share:
- At 60x P/E = $302.40 stock price (roughly where it was before the drop)
- This assumes a 1.67% earnings yield ($5.04 / $302.40)
- A 1.67% earnings yield is *less* than the 4.5% 10Y Treasury yield

**This is mathematically irrational.** You can get a riskless 4.5% in Treasuries, yet CDNS asks you to pay 60x earnings for a cyclical software business? 

The bull will counter: "But forward earnings are $9.54, making it 31.9x forward!"

**Exactly—and that's where my second argument crushes the bull thesis.**

## **PART 2: THE "STRUCTURAL AI SUPER-CYCLE" IS OVERHYPED — HERE'S WHY**

### **2A: Custom AI Chip Design is a Cyclical Phenomenon, Not a Structural Shift**

The bull made a bold claim: **"Custom silicon is becoming the competitive moat for AI, and this is irreversible."**

Let me directly challenge this narrative:

**It's not irreversible—it's a cyclical capex boom that will end.**

Here's the historical pattern:

1. **2023-2024:** Large tech companies (Meta, Google, Amazon) announced aggressive custom chip programs because off-the-shelf Nvidia GPUs were expensive and capacity-constrained
2. **2025-2026:** These programs ramped, creating peak demand for EDA tools (CDNS's backlog spike)
3. **2027-2028:** What happens next?
   - Custom chips are deployed and performing well
   - Design refinements decrease (not every year needs a new design)
   - Capex spending normalizes
   - EDA tool demand slows

**This is the classic boom-bust cycle in semiconductor capex.**

The bull wants you to believe it's different this time because "AI is structural." But here's the reality: **AI chip design is just another semiconductor design cycle, albeit a larger one.**

And we know from history that semiconductor cycles are *deeply cyclical*. Look at:
- 2007-2008: Capex boom in mobile chips, then bust
- 2012-2013: Capex boom in cloud data centers, then normalization
- 2017-2019: Capex boom in autonomous vehicles, then... nothing materialized as fast as expected

**Why should this cycle be different?**
