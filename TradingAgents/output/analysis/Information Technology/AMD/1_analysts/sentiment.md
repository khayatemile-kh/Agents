### 5. **Catalysts & Risks Identified**

#### Bullish Catalysts:

- **Near-term (Q3 2026):** Continued Saudi Arabia and international AI infrastructure deployments; potential earnings beat if Data Center segment accelerates
- **Medium-term:** Broader AI chip ecosystem maturation; custom silicon alternatives may fail to ship on time, consolidating demand back to AMD/NVIDIA
- **Long-term:** Lisa Su's assertion of "barely started" AI buildout suggests multi-year runway

#### Bearish Catalysts / Risks:

- **Immediate:** Fed rate trajectory; any further yield shock could trigger multiple compression across high-growth semis
- **Near-term (Q3-Q4 2026):** Export control tightening (China, advanced node restrictions); if AMD revenues exposed to China are impacted, guidance could disappoint
- **Medium-term:** Custom silicon (Google TPU, Amazon Trainium, Broadcom custom) could displace AMD in hyperscaler segments; competitive intensity rising
- **Structural:** Valuation at 2x YoY growth (rough estimate from doubling) leaves limited margin for error; any growth deceleration could trigger sharp drawdown
