**Why margins are temporarily compressed:**

1. **Integration redundancy elimination** (duplicate corporate functions, IT systems consolidation)
2. **Synergy investment costs** (IT spend, training, reorganization)

**These are temporary, not structural.** Once integration completes (which we're clearly entering), margin expansion is inevitable.

### **The Healthcare Tailwinds Are Real**

CVS operates in an aging demographic that structurally drives demand:

- **U.S. population 65+ is growing at 3-4% annually** (vs. 0.6% total population growth)
- **Healthcare spending per capita grows 2-3% above inflation** (structural cost inflation in medical delivery)
- **Pharmacy utilization is secular, not cyclical**—even in recessions, people fill prescriptions

These tailwinds aren't priced into the valuation. A company with $412B revenue growing in a secular tailwind market should trade at a premium, not a discount.
