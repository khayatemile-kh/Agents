## RISK FACTORS & CONSIDERATIONS

1. **Economic Sensitivity:** As a credit services company, AXP is sensitive to economic cycles, consumer spending patterns, and credit quality deterioration during downturns.

2. **High Leverage:** The extremely high debt-to-equity ratio, while normal for the industry, makes the company sensitive to interest rate changes and refinancing risks.

3. **Interest Rate Risk:** Changes in the interest rate environment could impact both lending returns and the cost of borrowing.

4. **Consumer Discretionary:** The core credit card business is dependent on consumer willingness to spend and travel.

5. **Competitive Landscape:** The financial services sector is highly competitive with established players and emerging fintech competitors.
