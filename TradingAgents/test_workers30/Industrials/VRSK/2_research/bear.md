
Bear Analyst: # Bear Case: Verisk Analytics (`VRSK`) — Why I'm Passing at This Price

Let me be direct with my colleague across the aisle: the bull playbook for `VRSK` never changes. "Fortress subscription model. Insurance data moat. Defensive compounder." I've heard it before, and it's a genuinely good business story. My problem isn't the story — it's the price tag attached to it, and the macro machinery quietly working against it. Let me walk you through why the risk/reward here is skewed the wrong way.

## 1. The Rate Repricing Just Broke the Valuation Math

Here's the single hardest piece of data in this debate, and it's tool-sourced, not opinion: **prediction markets now price a 93% probability of *zero* Fed rate cuts in 2026** — that repricing moved +4.0 points in just the past week on $8.2M of volume, with essentially 0% odds of a cutting cycle of 6+ cuts.

Why does that matter for a company like `VRSK`? Because Verisk is a long-duration, premium-multiple defensive name. Its entire investment case rests on "certainty today is worth paying up for." When the market concludes rates are staying higher for longer, that certainty premium gets compressed — mechanically, mathematically, through discounted cash flow.
