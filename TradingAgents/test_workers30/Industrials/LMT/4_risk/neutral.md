Now here's my problem with this math: Scenario 1 only works if your pullback prediction is *both* correct and precise on timing. The Aggressive Analyst would argue that Scenario 1 and 2 together still provide better risk-adjusted returns than holding the full position, because you're reducing tail-risk exposure in Scenario 3.
